# Inventory, Nov. 14, 2026: the queue, the unread, the canvassed

The owner, Nov. 14: "What does our queue look like? What's in the hopper for when we resume? I would like something like an inventory of what we haven't read yet, what we haven't read for our current scope, and what extensions we have canvassed but haven't yet queued. I'm particularly interested in RMN and associates for the period after January 20, 1961 ... I want to be sure we have comprehensive coverage of RMN 'in the wilderness,' and as a 'chronic campaigner,' in the period leading up to January 20, 1969."

How it was made: the calendar's entries counted by year and tag; every card's cites counted across `lists/cal`; the Drive inventory (`notes/drive-additions-2026-11.md`, 312 titles, Nov. 5–12) matched to cards by title words (a first cut: a few read books match no card and show 0, e.g. Beschloss); the proposals and the handoff queue. "Read" below means cited ten times or more; "touched", one to nine (mostly through other books' notes); "unread", none. The owner's own `never-read-works` sheet (Drive, Nov. 5) was not opened; the catalogue now answers the same question.

## 4. Nixon after Jan. 20, 1961: the gap and the plan

**The gap.** Entries tagged Nixon by year: 1960, 445; 1961, 34; 1962, 30; 1963, 32; 1964, 51; 1965, 23; 1966, 52; 1967, 56; 1968, 433. A third of the 1961–67 entries are polls. What has been read: *RN* (its "Private Citizen 1961–1967" is 59 pages, read in full; 76 cites for those years), *Six Crises*, White's three *Making of the President* volumes, Chester, Hodgson and Page, Hess and Broder (1964–67), Perlstein's *Before the Storm* (to 1964), Persico's Rockefeller (12). The landmarks are in (the 1962 concession, the Manila exchange with Johnson, Nov. 3–4, 1966, the Waldorf planning meeting of Jan. 1967), the life between them is not: the law firm, the travels abroad, the speeches for other candidates, the writing, the staff, the fund-raising.

**Readers (on the Drive), in order of yield for 1961–Jan. 1969:**
1. Ambrose, *Nixon: The Triumph of a Politician, 1962–1972*, chs. 1–10 (to the inauguration), with vol. I's last chapter (1961–62): the spine, dated, sourced to the Nixon papers.
2. Witcover, *The Resurrection of Richard Nixon* (1970): 1962–68 by a reporter who covered it, day by day for 1966–68.
3. Buchanan, *The Greatest Comeback* (2014): 1966–68 from inside, Buchanan from Jan. 1966; memoranda quoted.
4. Farrell, *Richard Nixon: The Life* (2017), and Aitken, *Nixon: A Life* (1993), the 1961–68 chapters: Farrell for the Chennault channel and the papers opened since 1990, Aitken for his interviews with Nixon.
5. The staff memoirs, the 1961–68 chapters: Price, *With Nixon* (from 1967), Klein, *Making It Perfectly Clear* (1962 and 1968), Safire, *Before the Fall* (1965–68), Garment, *Crazy Rhythm* (the firm, 1963–68), Whalen, *Catch the Falling Flag* (1967–68).
6. 1968 as a campaign: Wills, *Nixon Agonistes*; McGinniss, *The Selling of the President*; Nelson, *Resilient America*; Cohen; Goudsouzian; Nichter.
7. For context, lightly: Black, *A Life in Full*; Wicker, *One of Us*; Rarick's Pat Brown (the 1962 race).

**Needed, not held:** Earl Mazo and Stephen Hess, *Nixon: A Political Portrait* (1968); Herbert Parmet, *Richard Nixon and His America* (1990; card held, unread); James Rosen, *The Strong Man* (Mitchell, 2008; card held); Maurice Stans, *One of the Presidents' Men* (1995; 1968's money); Kevin Phillips, *The Emerging Republican Majority* (1969; written for the campaign; card held); Harry Dent, *The Prodigal South Returns to Power* (1978; Thurmond and Miami); Ehrlichman, *Witness to Power* (the 1968 tour; card held); Ken Hughes, *Chasing Shadows* (2014; Chennault; card held, 3 cites); Witcover, *Very Strange Bedfellows* (Agnew; card held, 1 cite).
**Nixon's own record, 1961–68:** his articles (*Reader's Digest*, "Cuba, Castro and John F. Kennedy," Nov. 1964; *Saturday Evening Post* pieces; "Asia After Viet Nam," *Foreign Affairs*, Oct. 1967: card held, 1 cite); his syndicated column (1963–64); the 1968 compilations (*Nixon on the Issues*; *Nixon Speaks Out*); the Nixon Library's Wilderness Years Collection (speech files, correspondence, travel and appointment records, 1961–68) and its online finding aids; the *Times* and the wires for his trips (Europe 1963; Asia 1964, 1966, 1967; Moscow 1965 and 1967; Latin America, Africa and the Middle East 1967) and his campaigning for others (1962, 1964, 1966).
