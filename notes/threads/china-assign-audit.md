# China threads: the audit of the assignment

Nov. 25, 2026. An audit by reading of the assignment in `notes/threads/china-assign.tsv`, made under `notes/briefs/china-threads-audit.md`. The assignment puts each of the 26,347 entries now under `china-leadership` in one of 38 proposed threads, or leaves it where it is. The rules are in `tools/threads/china/measure.py` and `systems.py`. Nothing in `lists/`, `th.yaml` or `sources/` was changed. The proposal it audits is `notes/proposals/china-threads-measure.md`.

## 1. What I read

I drew my own random sample, with a seed of my own: 25 entries from each of the 38 proposed threads and 50 from the leftovers, 1,000 in all. 190 of them were also in the first reader's samples; the rest were new. For each I read the line whole and the note. In the note I left out the Chinese characters, the "Made public" and "The words" sentences and the "To check" lists, since they say nothing of what the entry is about; a note longer than 900 characters after that I read to 900 characters and its names. I judged each entry against the thread statements in the proposal. An entry counts as right where it belongs to the thread it was given, and as arguable but right where two threads could hold it and the rule chose one of them. It counts as wrong where another thread fits it better, or none does.

I then read five more samples, outside the first, for the rules the brief named: 40 entries placed by the book they were read from, 40 placed by the office of the person named, 30 placed by the rule for the fallen leaders, 20 placed by Yang Shangkun's post in Shaanxi, and 20 placed by a conference's days. For these I read the line whole and the first 250 characters of the note.

After mending the rules I read two fresh samples under the new rules: 25 from Shanghai and Beidaihe, 1960, and 20 from Four Cleanups. I also read the 128 stations in the proposal's draft statements against the entries of their dates.

## 3. The rules the brief asked about

The book rule (1,640 entries). In the two samples, 79 of 86 were right, about 92 percent. The wrong ones were a department store's prices, a mine blast, the fight against "back-door" sales, two plots uncovered by the police and Zhou Enlai's request for Soviet petrol: matters the famine books report that are not the famine. Many of the right ones are arguable, since a famine book's report of prices, disease or riots in 1960–61 could also go to a system. The rule holds.

The office rule (1,890 entries, 1,499 of them naming Yang Shangkun). In the two samples, 60 of 77 were right, about 78 percent. Most wrong ones were Yang Shangkun's own private day that the rule took for General Office business: a cold, a hearing test, a lunch, meeting his wife at the station, a memorial service. A second fault went the other way: "Li Fuchun with Yang Shangkun" went to Li Fuchun's office, Finance and economy, though ruling 58 (5) puts a caller on the director of the General Office in the Center's daily work. Mends 7 and 8 below deal with both. About 115 such callers now go to the Center's daily work.

The rule for the fallen after their fall (75 entries). 32 of 33 read were right by the rule's own design: what Peng Dehuai, Peng Zhen, Liu Shaoqi or Deng Xiaoping does or suffers in custody goes to the stage of its month. The one wrong was Peng Dehuai at a third-front conference in Chengdu in September 1966, before he was taken. Whether a prisoner's days of writing, illness and weeping belong in the stages at all is a question for the owner, not a fault of the rule.

Yang Shangkun's post in Shaanxi (231 entries before). 19 of 29 read were right. The rule took his whole stay, October 1964 to May 1965, including his tour of south Shaanxi and Yan'an, his sightseeing, his illness and his evenings. Mend 6 narrows it to the post itself; 152 entries keep the rule.

A conference's days (257 entries before). 29 of 38 read were right. The wrong ones were meetings that only fell in a conference's weeks: a CPPCC anniversary, the General Political Department's evening in the Great Hall, a session of the National People's Congress, a writers' congress, the streamlining group. The word list took 大会 in 人民大会堂 and 小组 in 精简小组 as words for a meeting. Mend 4 deals with these; 237 entries keep the rule.

## 4. The faults found and the rules mended

Each mend is marked `AUDIT MEND n` in the code. The first reader's limits on its own test were borne out: its second reading of Shanghai and Beidaihe, 1960 found 27 of 30 right, and mine of the same rules found 13 of 25, because the words of the Great Leap placed the whole of 1960's business there.

1. Shanghai and Beidaihe, 1960: the Leap's words ("Great Leap", "technical revolution", "urban communes" and the like) now count only on the two conferences' days, Jan. 7–17 and July 5–Aug. 10, or in their months where an entry has no day. The thread fell from 134 to 86.
2. Two episodes at a seam: in July 1962 the seam rule for Xilou and the Tenth Plenum stopped the ranking, so a third episode first in the list, Famine, took the entry. Now the seam only puts one episode last, and the rest are ranked.
3. A conference's seat alone (the Xilou, Beidaihe, Lushan), outside that conference's days, now ranks after an episode found by a strong word. The Politburo's sittings in the Xilou room on the Soviet letter in March 1962 go to Polemic.
4. A conference's days: the Great Hall, the National People's Congress, a mass body's or a province's congress or conference, a ceremonial meeting and a named small group no longer count as the conference's meeting.
5. Third Front: "the Southwest Bureau" alone no longer places an entry.
6. Yang Shangkun's post: Chang'an county and Xi'an only; not his journeys, his tour of south Shaanxi and Yan'an, his illness, films and dinners.
7. A lesser figure's private day: more words (a cold, palpitations, swelling, a medical examination, electrotherapy, a wedding, the zoo, storytellers, a lunch), and a line set in a hospital.
8. The office rule: a caller on Yang Shangkun, to November 1965, is the Center's daily work (ruling 58, 5).
9. A building is not its system's business: the CPPCC's auditorium, the Overseas Chinese Building, the Nationalities Palace.
10. Bomb: the movement for banning atomic and hydrogen bombs is taken out of the line, as the conferences against them already were.
11. A foreign guest at a performance is foreign business, not propaganda and culture, as the first reader already ruled for Opera reform.
12. Xilou and the retreat: the May work conference of 1962 (May 7–11) is a station of the thread's statement and was not among the conferences' days; it now is. The thread rose from 135 to 147.

Faults found that I did not mend, because no rule I could write would not do more harm than good:

- Propaganda and culture (15 of 25) and Army (18 of 25) hold many lesser figures' private days and foreign guests: an exhibition visited, an opera watched, a notebook on philosophy, a Vietnamese army troupe's performance. They also take business that belongs to an episode but does not use its words: an editorial on Peng Dehuai in August 1967, a broadcast of the Central Committee's statement on the Moscow talks, Jiang Qing's army forum on the arts, Deng Xiaoping's division of the central departments in June 1966. A bare "army" in a list of cadres is enough to give Army the line.
- A line that opens with a phrase and no comma ("During the Congress", "From Nov. 3 to 25", "By Zhou Bingde's account") hides its actor from the person rule.
- A line headed "Zhu De, with Mao, Liu Shaoqi and others" goes to Mao or Liu. I counted these as arguable; where Mao is plainly the host ("Deng, with Liu, accompanies Mao") it is wrong.
- Four Cleanups still takes Yang Shangkun's private days at Niujiao by the word "Niujiao": reading, listening to the radio.
- Political-legal work (20 of 25) takes the National People's Congress's business of every kind, as the owner's default (7) asks; the wrong ones are a speech to the Congress on the Sino-Soviet dispute, an NPC delegation's speech at Hanoi and the like.
- Two mends moved three entries the wrong way: Li Zongren's press conference in the CPPCC's auditorium now goes to Foreign affairs; a meeting at the Academy of Military Science during the May work conference of 1962 now goes to Xilou and the retreat; Yang Shangkun deciding to bring his journey forward now goes to Agriculture.

## 5. The stations

All 128 stations now have an entry of their thread inside their dates. Under the old rules the May work conference of 1962 had none in Xilou and the retreat; mend 12 gives it its entries. I read each against the entries of its dates. Nine did not match and are corrected in the proposal:

- Bucharest and Moscow: Peng Zhen at Bucharest, from June 22, not June 24 (the entries run from his greeting to the Romanian congress on June 22).
- Polemic: Zhou at the Twenty-second Congress, from Oct. 15, the day he arrives.
- India war: July 22, 1962 is the post in the Chip Chap valley, not Galwan; Jan. 5, 1963 is Mao with Bandaranaike at Hangzhou, not Zhou's talks.
- Lei Feng, Dazhai, Daqing: December 1964 is Zhou having Dazhai's results checked again; the thread holds no entry of Dazhai in the Government Work Report.
- Opera reform: April 1965 is the end of the Ministry of Culture's rectification, not its reorganization.
- Afro-Asian conference: Nov. 4, 1965 is Zhou on the postponement, not China's withdrawal; the station is now Zhou's letter of Oct. 22 that it is better not to meet.
- Border war: Order No. 1 of Oct. 18, 1969 is in Army, as "the orders to the General Staff"; the station is now the border talks, Oct. 20.
- United front: July 27, 1965 is the General Office on Li Zongren; his return is in Zhou Enlai's thread (July 18–20) and Mao's (July 27).

The Opera reform statement does not name the philosophy campaign ("two combine into one"), though the rule puts it there; the owner may want a word for it in the statement or the entries elsewhere.

## 6. Which threads are fit to apply

I take a thread as fit where 22 or more of 25 read are right under the rules as they now stand.

Fit to apply as they stand: Bucharest and Moscow, Famine, Sixty Articles, Seven Thousand Cadres, Xilou and the retreat, Beidaihe and the Tenth Plenum, India war, Polemic, Four Cleanups, Lei Feng, Dazhai, Daqing, Opera reform, Zhou in Africa, Third Front, Afro-Asian conference, Bomb, Hai Rui, Luo Ruiqing, the six stages of the Cultural Revolution, Border war, Mao, Zhou Enlai, Liu Shaoqi, Center's daily work, Foreign affairs, Agriculture, Organization, United front and nationalities, and the leftovers. Four Cleanups is the weakest of these: 22 of 25 on the old sample and 17 of 20 on a fresh one, its faults Yang Shangkun's days at Niujiao.

Not fit as they stand:

- Shanghai and Beidaihe, 1960: 23 of 25 on the old sample but 21 of 25 on a fresh one. It still takes Soviet business of September 1960 by the word "experts", and Mao's letter of July 19 on the writers' congress by the conference's days.
- Twelve Articles: 21 of 25. The words "urgent directive" and "emergency directive" take other directives of the winter, on grain imports and on markets.
- Propaganda and culture: 15 of 25, for the reasons in section 4.
- Army: 18 of 25, for the same reasons.
- Political-legal work: 20 of 25.
- Finance and economy: 21 of 25; the office rule still gives it Chen Yun's rest days and journeys where no word marks them as private.

## 7. Is the assignment ready

Not yet as a whole. 923 of 1,000 entries read are right under the mended rules, and 32 of the 38 threads with the leftovers are fit to apply. The six threads above need another pass at their word lists, or a reading of their entries, before they are applied. The tsv is in step with the rules as I leave them: `python3 tools/threads/china/measure.py` makes it again.

Nothing in the brief narrowed what I read, and I disregarded nothing in it.
