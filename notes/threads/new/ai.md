# American Indians: the placing of the 376 entries

What I read: the 376 entries of `notes/threads/new/ai-ids.tsv`, each line and note, and the statements of the threads they sit under in `lists/cal/th.yaml`. Rulings 58–62 and 75 and the new thread's statement guided the reading. I read the first 100 entries in full by script output, and the rest by a first reading of each line, then every entry with a word of the thread in its line or note read in full, with its note. Nothing in `lists/`, `th.yaml` or `sources/` was changed.

The counts: 23 entries are in the thread; 353 are out.
- Primary: 2 (`cal.1967-01-12.states-and-cities`, the Washington court on the Puyallup and Nisqually fishing; `cal.1968-03-06.civil-rights-the-executive`, the message on the American Indian). Both were filed under a thread that holds what fits nowhere else.
- Secondary, added to `also:`: 21. Among them the Puyallup and Menominee cases (argued, reargued, decided: 5 entries, with the Campaign and its Indian contingent: 3 more), the Senate vote and the signing of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 for its Indian titles, Kennedy's letter of 1960, and Robert Kennedy's visits and talks (8 entries).
- Out: 353.

Entries I could not decide, and how I placed them:
- `cal.1964-08.conservation` (Lady Bird Johnson visits Indian reservations on a tour): placed in, secondary. The line gives the visit but not what was done there.
- `cal.1965-01-23.succession` (Humphrey's weekend, a poverty program on an Indian reservation named last): placed out. The entry is about Johnson's illness.
- `cal.1968-03-14.king-poor-peoples` (King meets Indian delegates and leaders of other minorities): placed in, secondary. The delegates are named as tribal delegates, but the meeting's business is the Campaign's coalition.
- `cal.1968-05-01.robert-kennedy-2` (the campaign's meeting on the reservations: registration, and Kennedy's remarks in the Senate): placed in, secondary. Its note says to check what the notes cover.
- `cal.1960-11-03.elections-1960-kennedy-4` (the Albuquerque stop with Navajo and Pueblo delegations): placed out, since the remarks quoted are about Nixon.
- `cal.1968-05-29.king-poor-peoples` stays primary in the Poor People's Campaign, as ruling 75 has it, with the Court in `also:` and the new thread added.

Where a case or visit already sat under the Supreme Court or a person's thread, I did not move it: those threads fit the act, so the new thread is secondary. No row puts the thread beside its own parent or child; it has none.
