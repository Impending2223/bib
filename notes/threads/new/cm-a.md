# Comecon, batch A: placing of 307 entries

What I read: all 307 entries of `cm-a-ids.tsv`, the whole line and the whole note of each, in `lists/cal/*.yaml`, with the thread's statement in `th.yaml` and rulings 58–62. All 307 are 1960 entries, Jan. 6 to Nov. 29, and most are Chinese-leadership entries.

I first decided many rows from the line and a clipped note. After the coordinator's message I read every note whole, to its last character. No note changed a row. Three notes added detail that bears out the row: the Chen Yi speech at the Polish reception of July 22 (Poland's western border), the Hungarian delegation's draft of Nov. 21, and the Czechoslovak and Polish delegates at the Moscow committee. One row I changed on a second look, for consistency, and not because of a note: the Kennedy statement of Aug. 6 on aid to Poland is out, like the other campaign entries.

The rule I used. An entry is in where a government, party or church of the eight states, or one of its officials, is a party to the act or its subject: a visit, a treaty, a telegram to its leaders, a call by or on its ambassador, a speech, a party's stand, a court's act, a state's arms sale or Moscow's dealing with it. An entry is out where one of them is named in passing, is one of many delegations or speakers, is only the place, or where the act is Chinese, Soviet, American or another state's business and the eight are only an occasion.
