## What I read

All 297 entries of `notes/threads/read/batch-a-ids.tsv`, each line and each note whole, nothing cut short. I read `th.yaml` for every China thread and its parent, ruling 59 and the rest of the brief's sources, and the audit `notes/threads/china-tree-audit.md`. I changed nothing in `lists/`, `th.yaml` or `sources/`. The entries are all of 1960: Jan. to Dec., from `cal.1960.china-leadership-3` to `cal.1961.china-leadership-21`.

I decided each entry by ruling 59: an episode first, then Mao (Zhou and Liu had no entry here as the one acting), then the system whose business it is. I took the episodes to include the Famine, whose entries start in January 1960 by the book rule, and Shanghai and Beidaihe, 1960. A parent is never put beside its child, so where Famine is the primary thread Agriculture is not in `also`.

Rules I used where the line did not settle it:

- A leader's visit to a school, an exhibition, a studio or an opera, with no instruction given, is not the work of Propaganda and culture. It stays in `china-leadership` with the thread in `also`. A speech or instruction on the subject, or a reception of a conference's delegates, goes to the system.
- A leader's inspection of a commune or a farm goes to Agriculture, because its statement names it. A visit to a plant goes to Finance and economy, with Agriculture second where the plant makes farm goods or the line is of a reservoir.
- Where Mao is the one acting (a talk, a comment, a circular he drafted, work on a text), his thread is primary and the system is secondary. Where he only hears a report, the system is primary and he is not added.
- The Center's daily work is never a secondary thread, as ruling 59 says. It is primary on five entries.
- Science, health, sport, education and the press go to Propaganda and culture, as ruling 58(7) says.
- Entries about the grain shortage, the oil and grain directives, food substitutes and the relief go to the Famine.
