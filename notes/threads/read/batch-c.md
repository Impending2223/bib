## What I read

I read the brief, `CLAUDE.md`, the rulings 30, 58, 59 and 60, the proposal `china-threads-revision.md`, the audit `china-tree-audit.md`, and every China thread and its statement in `th.yaml`. I read all 297 entries whole, the line and the whole note, Chinese characters and the "To check", "Check" and "Made public" sentences included. I cut nothing short. I printed the entries with a script to a scratch file, with `c` and `n` complete, and read the file through in order. To place an entry beside its neighbours I also looked at the primary threads of the entries around it (dates, thread and the first 200 characters of the line) and read the lines of the threads' own entries for the same weeks, but I did not read those whole.

The batch runs from Aug. 18, 1961 to Oct. 29, 1962. The word lists had proposed, for the 297: Agriculture 129 (kept in `china-leadership` with the thread in `also:`), Propaganda and culture 58 and Army 48 (the same), Organization 62 (held, unchanged).

## The rule I applied

Ruling 59: the primary thread is the episode whose business it is, else Mao, Zhou or Liu where he is the one acting, else the system whose business it is. Every other thread the entry plainly fits goes in `also:`. Two further points I held to.

- A thread never stands beside its own parent or child. Famine is a child of Agriculture, so an entry about hunger or grain takes Famine alone, or Agriculture alone where the matter is farming. The entries 1961-11-03-2 and 1961-12-20-15 have a clause on hunger inside a village inquiry; I kept Agriculture.
- A thread already in `also:` is kept where it fits and dropped where it does not. I dropped `china` (the parent of every China thread) in 7 entries, `farm` in 6, `defense` in 1 and `press-and-broadcasting` in 3, because those calendar threads are the American threads of those names (U.S. farm policy, U.S. defense, the President and the press). I kept `korea` (Deng at a farm in Korea), `japan` (a Japanese edition of Mao's *Selected Works*), `vietnam` and `laos` (two Xinhua reports), since the line is that nation's business with China.
