# China threads: batch D, read whole

I read all 297 entries of `notes/threads/read/batch-d-ids.tsv`, each with its whole line and whole note, from `lists/cal/*.yaml`. I did not cut any entry short. I also read `th.yaml`'s China threads, ruling 59, the revision proposal and the tree audit. The entries run from Oct. 29, 1962 to Jan. 1, 1964. The decisions are in `notes/threads/read/batch-d.tsv`, one row an entry, using only slugs that `th.yaml` has. Nothing in `lists/`, `th.yaml` or `sources/` was changed.

## The rule I applied

The primary thread is the episode whose business the entry is; else the person thread where one of Mao, Zhou or Liu is the one acting; else the system whose business it is. Where a person is one of several named as the source of an act (a joint instruction, a directive among others), I made the system or episode primary and the person secondary. A person thread is secondary wherever the line names him acting inside an entry. Three kinds of entry fit no thread and stay in `china-leadership`: a leader's private day, a family death or a hobby, and a call with no business stated. Where a thread was already in `also:`, I kept it if it fits. I dropped `farm` and `press-and-broadcasting` because they are the American farm-policy and press threads, not China's. I dropped `china` and `china-agriculture` or `china-propaganda` wherever the primary thread is that thread's child or parent. A system goes into `also:` only where the line plainly treats its business.

## Where the word lists were wrong

Organization (87 held): 71 are right. 16 are not: 6 are private or family matters that fit no thread (a death in a family, a leader's day at home, an obituary-like call), 2 are the Center's daily work (a General Office matter, a proposal on who attends the Standing Committee), 2 belong to the Polemic (the Soviet talks and the two parties' letters), 2 to the Four Cleanups, and one each to Agriculture, United front (the death of a Democratic League chairman), Foreign affairs (a condolence cable) and Opera reform.

Army (53 kept): 38 are right. 15 are not: 3 are Agriculture (Zhu De's farm and flood visits where a garrison or the militia is only heard), 3 Propaganda and culture (sport, the Welfare Institute's party, and a war history that is the Academy's own work, which I put back in Army), 2 Foreign affairs, 2 private or no business stated (an orchid gift, a call on Chen Yun), and one each to the Models, the India war, the Polemic, Hai Rui and United front.

Agriculture (80 kept): 54 are right. 26 are not: 8 are the Four Cleanups (Peng Zhen's inspection tour of May to June 1963 and the rural-work decision), 8 are Finance and economy (plants, plans, trade, procurement, exports), 5 are private days of Peng Dehuai, Zhu De and others, 2 are Propaganda and culture, and one each to Famine, Political-legal work and United front.

Propaganda and culture (77 kept): 35 are right. 42 are not: 20 are Opera reform (ghost plays, the thirteen-years slogan, modern plays, Mao's comments on the arts, the East China drama festival), 8 are the Polemic (editorials, broadcasts and articles of the quarrel with Moscow), 5 are Army, 3 are private days, and the rest are spread over Hai Rui, Zhou Enlai, Four Cleanups, Economy, Organization and Agriculture. The largest fault is that the word list put every play and every editorial in Propaganda and culture, though Opera reform and the Polemic already hold the campaigns.

I put plays, ghost plays, modern repertoire and the same kind of attack there. Three that could as well stay in Propaganda and culture: `cal.1963-11-11.china-leadership-2` (Yang Xianzhen's lecture), `cal.1964.china-leadership-9` (Jian Bozan), `cal.1963.china-leadership-15` (Jiang Qing meets Zhang Chunqiao; the line states no business).

Others: `cal.1964.china-leadership-37` (Hunan's land under grain, 1958 to 1962: Agriculture, or Famine); `cal.1963-06-14.china-leadership-10` (the Welfare Institute's party: Propaganda and culture, or United front); `cal.1962-12-22.china-leadership-7` (Zhu De at a radar research institute: science, so Propaganda and culture, though the institute is military); `cal.1963-04-06.china-leadership-6` (a county committee heard at a reservoir guesthouse: the line states no business, so I followed the tour's matter, Agriculture); `cal.1962-12.china-leadership-6` (the Organization Department on reaction to Mao's speech: Organization, with the Seven Thousand Cadres conference secondary); `cal.1963-04.china-leadership-7` (Henan's counts of counter-revolutionary cases: Political-legal work, though it may be the Four Cleanups' run-up).
