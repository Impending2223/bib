## Rules I applied where the order gave no answer

- Callers on Yang Shangkun with no business stated, and his office's telephone calls, are the Center's daily work (ruling 58, point 5). Yang hearing a ministry on farming, or his meetings with Wang Yuming on the unions and the Youth League, take the thread of that business.
- Deaths of leaders, appointments, the control commissions, the unions, the Youth League and the Women's Federation are Organization (ruling 58, point 7).
- Where a line reports Mao's comment, talk or instruction as the thing forwarded or relayed, Mao Zedong's thread is primary and the system second. Where a man reads or answers Mao's comment, the system is primary and Mao second.
- The Central Work Conference of May and June 1964 is in Third Front (its statement names it).
- A thread is never put beside its parent or its child. So where an entry is in Opera reform or Luo Ruiqing, Propaganda and culture or Army is not added.
- Soviet Union was in `also:` on three entries and Vietnam on one; I dropped them, since the lines are about domestic education against revisionism, a Romanian guest's remark, and an American fleet, and none states that nation's business with China.
