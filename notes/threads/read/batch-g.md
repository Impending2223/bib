## How I decided

The rule was ruling 59. An entry of the army forum summary, the work teams, the Red Guards and the like goes to the episode whose business it is. A person's thread comes second, only where Mao, Zhou or Liu is the one acting. A system comes third. Where a person acts inside an episode, his thread is secondary.

Three rules of my own, taken so that like entries go the same way:

- A visit, a telephone call or a family matter of a leader other than Mao, Zhou and Liu, with no business stated, is a private day and stays in `china-leadership`. Peng Dehuai's confinement log, Ye Jianying's barber, Deng's household in Jiangxi are of this kind.
- A leader's inspection of a farm, a plant or an exhibition goes to the system it inspects (Agriculture, Finance and economy, Propaganda and culture). Where the visit is only the setting of a private act, the entry stays private.
- An entry that prints or publishes (an editorial, a notice, a bulletin) is Propaganda and culture, with the system of its subject as a secondary thread. Where the line is what Mao instructed, the first announcement of an instruction is Mao's.

Two threads cannot sit beside their parents. Hai Rui is under Propaganda and culture, Luo Ruiqing under Army and Third Front under Finance and economy. So the entries I put in Hai Rui or Third Front carry no Propaganda, Army or Economy beside them. Where the lists had put Propaganda beside an entry in `china-leadership`, the entry now stands in Hai Rui and the secondary thread is gone because of this rule, not because it was wrong.
