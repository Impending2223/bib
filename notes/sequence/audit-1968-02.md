## Links checked: 7. Kept: 6. Struck: 1. Turned: 0.

### Kept

- Feb. 4, Mao's note (`china-leadership`) after Zhou's liaison meeting and letter (`china-leadership-2`). The reader called this weak, as resting on the nianpu's order. It does not rest on that order. The nianpu (III, 212–213) gives Mao's words as a 批示, a comment written on Zhou's letter: "Fully agreed, back to the Premier to handle." A comment on a letter comes after the letter. The first entry's own line says as much ("send him ... Mao: fully agreed"). The two entries overlap: both hold the sending of the letter (see "What is left").
- Feb. 5, the *Renmin ribao* and *Jiefangjun bao* editorial on the founding of the Hubei Revolutionary Committee (`china-leadership-5`) after the founding (`china-leadership-4`). A comment comes after the act it is on, and the line says "on the founding". But Yang (I, 474) dates the founding Feb. 5 and gives no day for the editorial. I added a Check on the editorial's day. If the editorial proves to be of Feb. 6, the link goes.
- Feb. 7, Westmoreland's conference at Đà Nẵng (`vietnam-khe-sanh-2`) after the overrunning of Lang Vei (`vietnam`). The conference is "to bring out Làng Vây's survivors", and is "soon after daylight"; Lang Vei fell before dawn. The words are enough.
- Feb. 8, the state dinner for Wilson (`europe-and-the-alliance-2`) after the talks (`europe-and-the-alliance`). The reader called this weak. Lady Bird Johnson's diary (629–30) has the conference set for 11:30 to one, then lunch on the second floor, "talking and working until past 3:30", and the dinner "tonight". The hours stand in order and nothing goes against them. The talks entry runs Feb. 8–9; the link holds for its first day, which is where it sorts. I wrote the basis into the dinner entry's note by an edit.
- Feb. 26, Taft's insertion in the Record (`republican-party-congress`) after William S. White's column (`press-and-broadcasting-3`). Taft puts in "White's column of the day": the column came first.
- Feb. 27, Califano and McPherson riding back from the meeting at State (`vietnam-205000-2`) after the meeting (`vietnam`). The words "riding back ... from the meeting" show it.

### Struck

- Feb. 7, Zhou's talk with Hồ Chí Minh (`china-leadership-2`) after Mao's meeting with Hồ (`china-leadership`). The link rested on the words "after the meeting with Mao" in the line. Neither source bears them out. The nianpu (III, 213) says Zhou went with Mao to meet Hồ and, "the same day" (同日), made his proposal in talking with Hồ. The Wilson Center text (doc. 30) gives the place and the day, no hour. Zhou's proposal (one to three field corps) is close to Mao's (several field armies), and it may have been made in the same meeting. I struck the link and added a Check on the words "after the meeting with Mao".

## Links already in the tree

I read the 11 links of the month already in the tree: Feb. 7 (the National Security Council after Lang Vei; Peng's three entries), Feb. 8 (Peng; Kennedy's luncheon after his breakfast with Daley), Feb. 12 (Peng; the 1:45 meeting after Westmoreland's cable), Feb. 28 (Rockefeller and Nixon after Romney's withdrawal) and Feb. 29 (Peng's three sessions). All agree with the lines or their sources. None is struck.

The reader thought one of the two hours on Feb. 12 was in doubt: the cable at "2:12 p.m." and the meeting at 1:45 p.m. There is no doubt. The cable's time is 0612Z, which is 2:12 p.m. in Saigon and 1:12 a.m. in Washington. The meeting at 1:45 p.m. in Washington came about twelve hours later. FRUS doc. 69 has McNamara telling Johnson of the cable at 8:29 a.m.

## Links added: 0

I read every day of the month for links the reader did not write. I found none the record shows. The days where an order seemed possible, and why I wrote no link:

- Feb. 12: the Joint Chiefs' answer to McNamara (JCSM-91-68, `vietnam-205000`) and the 1:45 meeting. At 8:29 a.m. McNamara told Johnson "The Chiefs are meeting now" (FRUS doc. 69). At the meeting Wheeler gave the Chiefs' view (doc. 70). Nothing says whether the written memorandum went to McNamara before or after the meeting.
- Feb. 13: McNamara's order for the brigade and the landing team (`vietnam-205000-5`, Drea, 183) and the Joint Chiefs' direction to fly them out (`vietnam-205000-3`, MACV Command History, 244–46). Neither source names the other act. MACV says only that the Joint Chiefs directed the deployment after Westmoreland's request.
- Feb. 17: Zhou's instruction to Wei Guoqing (`china-leadership`), his talk with the Vietnamese at 1:40 p.m. (`china-leadership-3`) and the business group on Guangxi's rail traffic (`china-leadership-4`). In the talk Zhou says he told Wei Guoqing "yesterday" to hold a meeting on Guangxi's railways. So the instruction may be of Feb. 16, not Feb. 17. I added a Check on its day. The business group follows the talk only in the nianpu's printed order, which is no basis (owner-rulings 55).
- Feb. 3, 9, 18, 19, 28: the pairs the reader left (Wheeler's two messages of Feb. 3; JCS 1589 and MAC 1858 of Feb. 9; Lộ Tẻ and An Bình of Feb. 18; Mao's reception and Chen Yi's party of Feb. 19; Romney at Boston and in Washington on Feb. 28). I agree with the reader: nothing in the entries or their sources gives an order.
