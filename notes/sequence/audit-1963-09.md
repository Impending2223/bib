# Audit of the sequence pass, September 1963 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1963-09.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1963-09.md`. I read all 30 days of the month in the order the site now gives them (`day_order.py 1963 --from 1963-09 --until 1963-09 --full`). For each link in the patch, and for the days the reader left unlinked, I read the entries in full with `./bib show`. Where the lines did not settle the order, I read the sources. The corrected patch is in place: 12 `follows` ops (12 links: 8 kept, 1 turned, 3 added; none removed) and 8 `edit` ops. `./bib patch-check` gives 0 errors and 6 warnings, one for each edit that sets only `n`; that is expected, since the basis of a link goes into the note by an edit. I disregarded nothing under R0.

## Links checked: 10. Kept: 8. Struck: 1. Turned: 1.

### Kept

- Sept. 9, Dirksen in the Oval Office (`testing`) after the Huntley–Brinkley interview (`vietnam`). The reader asked that it be struck if the interview's act was the evening broadcast. The Public Papers' note says the interview was videotaped in the President's office "on the morning of the same day" and broadcast that evening. The interview entry is the interview, and Reeves (593–94) puts Dirksen after it. Kept, with that basis written into the note of `testing`.
- Sept. 10, the Celler markup at 2:30 p.m. after Ways and Means approves the tax bill: the markup line's own words ("The tax bill out of Ways and Means, Celler resumes").
- Sept. 11, Goldwater at the women's dinner after the day's stops: the stops line's words (a stranger drives him "to the women's dinner").
- Sept. 15, Peng Zhen sees Lukman's delegation off for the DRV (`-5`) after meeting it at the station back from Korea (`-4`). The Peng Zhen nianpu, IV, 290, read on the page: 中午 at the station, then 下午 to the airport to see the guests off.
- Sept. 15, Liu Shaoqi's speech at the evening state banquet in Pyongyang (`-3`) after his arrival there (`-2`): the banquet is the evening's, and he cannot speak at it before he arrives.
- Sept. 17, Kennedy's cable to Lodge, 9:28 p.m. (`vietnam`), after the recorded meeting (`vietnam-3`). The reader called the link an inference to strike if the record showed another meeting. It does not. The recordings' catalogue has one recording of the day: Meeting Tape 111.3, Cabinet Room, 5:45 p.m., Kennedy with Rusk, McNamara, Robert Kennedy, Harriman, McCone, Bundy and Taylor. The cable reports what the "Highest level meeting today has approved" (doc. 125). The next morning Bundy has Lodge's answer sent "to those present yesterday p.m." (doc. 126 n.3), and Harriman speaks of "people at table yesterday" (doc. 127). Harriman's 4:20 p.m. call is on the draft for that meeting (doc. 124 n.2). The basis is now in the cable's note, and the meeting entry has its hour from the catalogue (`time`, 5:45 p.m. Eastern).
- Sept. 20, Chen Yi writes the quatrains at Conghua (`-6`) after going there to stay (`-5`): the lines' words, and the nianpu's 是日.
- Sept. 30, Zhou's speech at the reception (`-7`) after Mao's revision of it (`china-leadership`): "for that evening's National Day reception". The revision entry carried the evening range (7 to 10 p.m.). That range came from the words "that evening's", which are the reception's, not the revision's. I took the range out and tagged the entry `ok:fields-time`. The link still puts the revision first.

### Struck

- Sept. 13, the printing of the second commentary (`-3`) after Mao's revisions (`china-leadership`). The revision entry records no act of Sept. 13. Its note (*Jianguo yilai Mao Zedong wengao*, XVII, 63–68) has the last revision and Mao's note to Kang Sheng, which agrees to print on the 13th, both on Sept. 11, at 7 a.m. The entry stands on the 13th only because the nianpu dates the revisions "early to mid-September", and its own Check says so. A link runs within one day. Across days the dates give the order, once the revision entry's date is settled by its Check.

### Turned

- Sept. 24, the Defense appropriation (`defense`). The reader linked it to the treaty's consent (`testing`), by the words "hours after consenting". That order holds, but it is not the entry directly before. The Senate's Record of the day has the consent at 17,832, the bill resumed at 17,865, McGovern's speech for his amendment at 17,881–85, the amendment refused 2 to 74 at 17,889, and the bill passed 77 to 0 at 17,894. So `defense` now follows McGovern's speech (`vietnam-congress`): the vote comes after its debate. A new link puts `vietnam-congress` after `testing`. Both notes give the Record's pages.

## The link in the tree on Sept. 3

`china-leadership-7` (Mei Xing and Yang Bo report to Yang) follows `-5` (Liu Lantao's talk). The diary, II, 311, read on the page: 下午3时到6时，刘澜涛同志来谈话，梅行、杨波同志来汇报. One window holds both, Liu Lantao named first. The hours of the day's entry run in order (morning, 3 to 6 p.m., evening, to 2 a.m.), and nothing goes against them. So the order of the diary's sentence is a basis, the weakest kind (R25c). I kept the link and wrote that basis into the note of `-7`, which had not named it.

## Days the reader left unlinked, read again

- Sept. 30, the delegations Mao and Zhou receive (`-8`) against the reception (`-7`). The two sources do not point opposite ways. The Peng Zhen nianpu, IV, 293, read on the page: in the evening, with Mao, Liu, Soong, Dong, Zhou, Zhu and Deng, Peng meets in turn Lukman's delegation and Ouzegane's, and 随后 ("then") attends Zhou's National Day banquet. Yang's diary, II, 317, read on the page: the reception at the Great Hall from 7 p.m., over 1,800 foreign guests; 主席宴会后到了场 (Mao came on the scene after the banquet), to cheers; over at 8:45. Both put the meetings before Mao and Peng came to the banquet. Neither says whether Zhou's speech came before the meetings or after them, and Zhou was at both. No link, since the entry `-7` is Zhou's speech.
- Sept. 15–16, the Birmingham bombing. The one link (King's telegram after the bombing) stands. On Sept. 16, King's statement and wire, Morgan's speech (its day in Check), the FBI's recommendation, Rusk's 11 a.m. meeting and Forrestal's note give no order among themselves.
- Sept. 24, the test-ban vote: above. The letter to Diệm at 9:25 p.m. already follows the Senate vote and the House debate; it is placed by its hour and the link is not wrong. Dillon with Byrd, "on the eve of the House's vote", has no hour and nothing to place it.
- Sept. 24–28, the western tour. It is one entry of five days, with its first day's hour (Milford, 1 p.m.). The signing of the health professions act at 9:30 a.m. in Washington comes first by the clock. On Sept. 25–28 no other entry is of the President's tour.
- Sept. 21, Zhou's call on Hong Xiannü (`-2`). I looked for a link after the flight to Guangzhou. The Zhou nianpu, II, 581, read on the page, puts the call (△) under "Sept. 21: flies to Guangzhou, then to Conghua; on the evening of the 25th back in Guangzhou", so the call may fall on any day of the stretch. No link. The entry's day should carry "Check: the day" (left, below).
