# Audit of the sequence pass, May 1963 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1963-05.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1963-05.md`. The reader wrote 29 `follows` ops holding 32 links. I read all 31 days of the month from the day sheet (`day_order.py 1963 --from 1963-05 --until 1963-05 --full`, 969 lines), each day's entries together. I held every one of the reader's links against its two entries in full (`./bib show`), and where the lines did not settle the order, against the pages. I also read the links already in the tree on the days I read, where a page could test them. The corrected patch is in place: 28 `follows` ops (one of them a removal from the tree) and 16 `edit` ops. Each edit sets only `n`: it adds one sentence that names the source for a link whose basis is not in the line. I disregarded nothing under R0: the batch brief set no limit.

## Links checked: 32. Kept: 25 (two of them narrowed to one leader). Struck: 5. Turned: none. Taken out of the tree: 1. Added: 4.

### Struck

- May 3, Marshall's calls to King (`civil-rights-the-executive-2`) after the hoses (`alabama-birmingham`). The entry holds two calls. Branch (762) has the first "that morning," before the hoses, and only the second after them. The entry sorts by its first act, so the link would put the morning call after the hoses. The entry should be split in two (R17, R25b); then the second call can follow the hoses.
- May 8, King's release from jail (`alabama-birmingham-5`) after the night's mass meeting (`-4`). Branch tells the meeting (785) and then the release (786), but no word says which came first: both are "that night." The order of telling is not an order of the day. The release still follows the jailing (`-3`), a link already in the tree.
- May 12, Chen Yi at Liu Shaoqi's dinner for Hồ Chí Minh (`china-leadership-7`) after his visit to the machine works and the park (`-6`). On the page (966–67) the dinner is "晚上" and the visit has no time word at all. The link rested on the nianpu's printing alone, which is no order (R25c; owner-rulings 55).
- May 30, Yao Zhen's report from Hankou (`china-leadership-4`) after Mao's second talk with Kim Il Sung (`-2`). The diary (II, 283) has "姚溱报告汉口谈话情况" with no hour, after the afternoon's talk with Ma Chungu. The talks at Wuhan ran from May 29, and the report may cover the first day's. Wu Lengxi (572–74) gives the second talk no hour. Nothing orders the two.
- May 31, Yang's telephone call with Hankou (`china-leadership-3`) after Kim's departure from Wuhan (`-2`). The diary (II, 283): "上午处理文电，与汉口通话。中午金已安抵平壤。" The entry holds the morning call and the noon news of Kim's arrival. The news comes after the departure, but nothing places the call against it, and the entry sorts by the call. The entry should be split; then the noon news can follow the departure.

### Narrowed to one leader

- May 7, the night's mass meeting (`alabama-birmingham-2`): now after the hosing of Shuttlesworth (`-3`) alone, not also after "The jails full; Wallace sends state troopers" (`alabama-birmingham`). Branch (779) has the hoses "by three o'clock," Shuttlesworth among their targets, and the meeting that night. The hosing came after the midday hour when the jails were found full (777), so it is the entry the meeting comes directly after. The troopers, by King's words at the meeting (780), were moving in "that very hour," which is the same hour and no order.
- May 8, King and Abernathy jailed (`alabama-birmingham-3`): now after the President's press conference (`middle-east`) alone. Branch (784): jailed "shortly after President Kennedy's optimistic press conference." The press conference came "half an hour" after Shuttlesworth's announcement of the truce (783), and its hour already places it after the morning entry (`alabama-birmingham`). So the conference is the entry the jailing comes directly after.
