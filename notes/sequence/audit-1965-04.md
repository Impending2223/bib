# Audit of the sequence pass, April 1965 (R25c, R33b)

The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1965-04.yaml`. A Sonnet reader wrote it; its note is `notes/sequence/1965-04.md`. I checked each of its 14 links against the two entries it joins, and against the source where the lines did not settle the order. I then read every day of the month myself on a fresh sheet (`day_order.py 1965 --from 1965-04 --until 1965-04 --full`). The patch now has 19 ops, and `./bib patch-check` shows 0 errors and 0 warnings. Under R0 I disregarded nothing: the brief narrowed nothing.

## The reader's links: 13 kept, 1 moved, none struck or turned

- Apr. 3, china-leadership-8 after -7 and -9 after -8: kept. Read on the image of the Chen Yi nianpu, 1094–95. In the morning he leaves Kathmandu; "途经" he stops at the base; in the afternoon he leaves the base for Kunming.
- Apr. 11, intelligence after school-aid: kept. The line says "after the signing". Lady Bird Johnson's diary, 258, read in the text layer, agrees: he signs, then announces Raborn.
- Apr. 13, china-leadership-14 after -13: kept. Read on the nianpu's image, 1096. The nianpu dates Mao's reception of Sabri at Wuhan to the 13th, and Sabri was seen off from Wuhan to Guangzhou on the morning of the 13th. The reception therefore came first.
- Apr. 13, vietnam-force-levels-2 after -3: kept, and now on a page read. The reader took the cable's date-time group from the entry's citation, without reading the cable or Cosmas's page. I read Cosmas, 276, and the note on 581, n.11, in archive.org's DTIC text (DTIC_ADA557393). On 276: "On 13 April, after the Joint Chiefs of Staff had endorsed Admiral Sharp's request, Secretary McNamara approved". Note 11 cites "Msg, CINCPAC to JCS, 132235Z Apr 65" and a JCS paper of Apr. 14. 22:35Z is 5:35 p.m. in Washington, after the midday lunch. Drea, 29, also read in DTIC_ADA552481's text, has Johnson concur in the brigade at the Tuesday luncheon. A JCS message sent on the 13th then told Taylor of "this latest decision". So the approval follows the lunch. One step rests on inference: note 11 does not say in so many words that the CINCPAC message is Sharp's request. It is the only message the note gives for the paragraph, so I took it to be the request. The cable itself is not read.
- Apr. 16, china-leadership-8 after -2: kept. The nianpu, 1096, read: "到达雅加达。之后，作《水调歌头·访问雅加达》" ("arrives at Jakarta; after that, writes the poem"). -2 is the statement made on arrival.
- Apr. 18, china-leadership-2 after china-leadership: kept. Read on the image of Zhou waijiao huodong, 447: the Senayan ceremony was in the morning (上午), and Sukarno's reception at noon (中午12时).
- Apr. 19, south-carolina after program and vietnam-support after program: kept. The diary, 261–62, read: Johnson spends three hours on the telephone with Bundy at the Ranch, then lunch, then the helicopter to Bergstrom and Air Force One. He talks to the pool "on the way up", and the stop at Columbia comes after. The diary does not say which leg of the flight the talk was on. So the link to the Bundy call stands, and the talk is not linked to Columbia.
- Apr. 22, china-leadership-10 after -9 and -11 after -10: kept. The nianpu, 1098, read: a stop at Rangoon at noon; in the afternoon "离开缅甸时" he cables his thanks; in the afternoon he arrives at Kunming.
- Apr. 29, china-leadership-2 after china-leadership: kept. Zhou waijiao huodong, 450, read: Zhou lands at Peking at noon with Mrs. Touré, and the first Ghana talk is at 5 p.m.
- Apr. 29, china-leadership-4 after -3: kept. The line says "after the banquet"; the book, 450, says "宴会后".
- Apr. 29, -9 after -11: moved to -8 after -11. -11 is Yang's call to Yao Zhen "今天在丈八沟时" (today, while at Zhangbagou). -9 says "5时半由医院直接返村" (at 5:30, straight from the hospital back to the village). He was at the hospital from 3:30 p.m. (-8) and left it directly for Niujiao. So his time at Zhangbagou came before 3:30, and the call comes before the hospital visit, not just before the drive home. The order of -9 after -8 is already set by their clock times (3:30 and 5:30).

## The links in the tree
