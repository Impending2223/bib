I read the reader's brief whole first, then CLAUDE.md, the paragraph on the day's order and the copy guide. I read every day of the month from the day sheet (`day_order.py 1962 --from 1962-11 --until 1962-11 --full`), each day's entries together, and held each of the reader's links against the two entries and, where the lines did not settle the order, against their sources.
