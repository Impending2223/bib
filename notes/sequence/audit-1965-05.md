## Kept, with what I read

- May 2, the report to the nation (`latin-america`) after the evening with the congressional leaders (`vietnam-congress`). The report's line says "after meeting the congressional leaders". Drea, 304, puts the meeting with the leaders "earlier that evening", before Johnson's call to Bennett a few minutes before 9 p.m.; it is the meeting at which Johnson said "if I send in Marines I can't live in the Hemisphere", which the other entry's note cites from Dallek.
- May 2, Vance's courses to the advisers (`latin-america-dominican-republic-2`) after Masterson's 2:14 a.m. reply (`-3`). Kept, but not on the reader's ground. The reader said the courses were the ones in Masterson's reply. They are not quite: the figures Drea gives on 304 (12, 8 and 16 battalions) are Moorer's, as CINCLANT, not Masterson's (18, 15 and 12, Poole, 141). The order still holds. Drea, 303, has Wheeler ask the field commanders on May 1 for their courses "by 8:00 a.m. on 2 May", for a conference later; Poole, 141, has the same query go to CINCLANT, Masterson and Palmer, and Masterson answer at 2:14 a.m. Vance put the commanders' answers to the meeting; Masterson's was one of them, and came in the night before. The link is to the first act of the entry; the entry also holds Johnson's approval late in the day and the Chiefs' order of about 11 p.m. (Drea, 304), so it should be split under R25b (left, below).
- May 6, Ervin's amendment refused (`voting-rights`) after the Senate passes the $700 million (`vietnam`). The reader's basis was the roll-call numbers and the order of the Record's pages; R25c says a serial number alone is no order. The Record itself settles it: at 9772, after the vote on H.J. Res. 447 (No. 64), "So the joint resolution ... was passed", then "The Senate resumed the consideration of the bill (S. 1564)", Ervin's amendment is called up, and its vote (No. 65) follows at 9807.
- May 12, Peng Zhen's speech at the rally (`china-leadership-10`) after Mao's statement (`china-leadership`). The line's own words: the people "fully support Chairman Mao's" statement. The statement's entry has it printed in the *Renmin ribao* of May 12, the rally in the afternoon.
- May 14, the arrival at Zhangbagou at 12:30 (`-4`) after the hour's talk at Ximawang (`-7`). Yang, II, 603–604: at Ximawang at 10, spoke an hour, at Zhangbagou at 12:30. The tree's older link of `-4` after `-3` is not wrong and stays.
- May 16, leaving Niujiao at 3 p.m. (`-5`) after the talk with Hang Shangzeng (`-4`). Yang, II, 605: the talk of over an hour at his lodging, then "3时正式离村". The diary's order, with the hours agreeing.
- May 17, Zhou and Deng with Hồ in Beijing (`-3`) after Zhu De meets Hồ at the airport (`-8`). Hồ was at Changsha on the 16th; a talk in Beijing comes after his arrival there.
- May 21, Peng Zhen's call on Aidit, afternoon (`-6`), after the delegation's flight to Jakarta (`-2`). The ranges already place them; the link records an arrival before a call and does no harm.
- May 26, the Senate passes the bill (`voting-rights-2`) after Thurmond's speech (`voting-rights`). The line's words: "before voting against the voting rights bill".
- May 28, Peng Zhen meets the East Java governor in the evening (`-11`) after the flight to Surabaya (`-10`). A meeting at Surabaya follows the flight there; the flight had no hour.
- May 29, Zhou "back in Beijing after seeing Subandrio off" (`china-leadership`) after the 10:40 a.m. leave-taking at Guangzhou (`-3`). The line's own words.
- May 30, the Baita hill (`-7`) after the arrival at Lanzhou at 3:30 (`-6`). Yang, II, 628: 3:30 at Lanzhou, the guesthouse, "稍事休息之后" the Baita hill, back at 7.

## Added

- May 1, Palmer's request for four more airborne battalions and a third of Marines (`latin-america-dominican-republic`) after the paratroopers' move into the city about 10 a.m. (`-2`). Drea, 302: "Only after the paratroopers began to move into the city did Palmer officially notify his military superiors", and then "Palmer again requested thousands more troops", "we should not send a boy to do a man's job", the words Poole, 141, gives to the request the entry records. The entry cites Poole only; the order rests on Drea.
- May 13, Yang with An Yumi and He Tongqing in the rain to 11:30 p.m. (`-3`) after Wang Kuancai's wedding (`-2`). Yang, II, 603: the wedding seen "早饭后" and the hour at the groom's house "下午"; then "晚间在大雨中" the talk. The wedding entry has no hour or range, so the sheet put it last in the day.
- May 19, Fong's amendment taken (`voting-rights-2`) after Mansfield's third request refused (`voting-rights`). 111 Cong. Rec. 11,018–19: Mansfield's request for a limit on debate, Ellender's objection, then "Mr. FONG. Mr. President, I call up my amendment"; its roll call, No. 77, at 11,027. This is the pair the reader left for the Record.
- May 25, Ye Qun tells Luo late at night that Lin's instruction is withdrawn (`china-leadership-14`) after Lin's instruction on the bulletin (`-13`). Bu Weihua, 24–25: the instruction of May 25 on bulletin no. 63, then "當天深夜" Ye Qun calls Luo and says "林的「指示」已全部收回". The same instruction, the same day.
