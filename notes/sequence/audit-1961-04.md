## A link already in the tree that does not hold

- Apr. 19, `cuba-3` (Berle and Schlesinger fly to Opa-Locka; the Council is brought to Washington and received) follows `cuba-2` (the beachhead falls; San Román's last message, 2:32 p.m.). Schlesinger's journal (113–17) has the flight leave after the White House meeting broke up about 2 a.m. and reach Opa-locka about 8:15 a.m. Washington told them the operation was "substantially over" soon after 10 a.m. So the entry's first act, and the word that the beachhead was lost, both came before 2:32 p.m. Only the reception at the White House, late in the afternoon, came after it. The `follows` op can only add a link, so taking this one out needs an `edit`. As the earlier audits did, I left it for the merger or a later pass. The entry holds several acts and wants R25b's split: the flight and the meeting at Opa-locka in the morning, the reception in the late afternoon.
