### Kept

- Jan. 7, the opening of the enlarged Politburo (`china-leadership`) after Mao's 11 a.m. word setting the plenary for 11:30 (`-3`). The diary (I, 468) has the plenary from 11:30 a.m. to 2 p.m.
- Jan. 14, the 432d NSC meeting (`cuba`, 9 a.m. in Washington) after Khrushchev's speech to the Supreme Soviet (`soviet-union`). The speech entry says Dulles reported it to the NSC that morning. FRUS X, pt. 1, doc. 140 bears it out: Dulles had to change the briefing he had prepared the night before "in the light of the latest news" of the speech.
- Jan. 18, Zhou's reception of Rau (`-2`) and the treaty signing (`-4`), both at 4 p.m. in Peking, each after "Zhou Enlai back in Peking" (`china-leadership`). The links are sound. The Zhou nianpu (II, 279) has "1月18日 回到北京" and then the reception. Zhou was in Shanghai at the meeting until Jan. 17, and an act in Peking at 4 p.m. on the 18th comes after his return that day, whatever the hour of the return. Without the links the return, which has no hour, would sort after the 4 p.m. events. No order is written between the reception and the signing: the dashiji gives both in one sentence at 4 p.m. (269), and a compiler's sentence is no order (owner-rulings 55, the first default).
- Jan. 24, Zhou's going to the airport to welcome Ne Win (`-4`) after "Zhou Enlai back in Peking" (`-2`). The Zhou nianpu (II, 281) has the return and then the welcome, and the Mao nianpu (Jan. 23) has Zhou return the next day "and go to the airport to welcome" Ne Win.
- Jan. 28, the signing at 6:50 p.m. (`-4`) after the State Council's 95th plenary (`china-leadership`), which adopted the drafts and named Zhou plenipotentiary to sign. The Zhou nianpu's note (II, 285 n.1) adds that the NPC Standing Committee's 13th meeting decided the same day. That meeting has no entry, so the plenary is the entry the signing comes directly after.
