## What else the reading showed

- Three entries claim the last talk with Kennedy: Ball's call on Nov. 20, 9:20 p.m. (trade, "Their last talk"), Sorensen at the helicopter on Nov. 21 (assassination-2) and Albert in the Oval Office on Nov. 21 (congress-2: "I was the last person to visit with him in the White House"). Each is its author's account (R24), but the two of Nov. 21 are one departure, the helicopter at the lawn, and cannot both be last. The order between them is not shown, so I wrote none; an auditor with Sorensen 360 and Albert 280–81 could settle it, or the entries could take "Check:" sentences.
- Nov. 21 democratic-party and elections-1964-3 give the same remark (Johnson "incapable of telling the truth") from two accounts, Jacqueline's to Robert Kennedy and Robert Kennedy's later one. One event, two entries; the merge's duplicate test may not catch it.
