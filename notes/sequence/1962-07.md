## The links written

- July 6: china-leadership (Tian Jiaying to Mao at the pool residence) after china-leadership-7 (Mao back in Peking in the morning). Basis, the notes: the Chen Yun chronology (III, 135) has Mao "just back that morning", and the Mao biography (V, ch. 70 n.22) calls July 6 "the day Mao returns". Mao had to be in Peking first.
- July 9: china-2 (Zhang Hanfu "warmly welcomes the Laotian government's declaration of neutrality") after laos (Souvanna presents the statement, accepted by the conference). A reply to the statement, at the same plenary.
- July 10: king (Dora McDonald's report to King "in the Albany jail") after albany (King and Abernathy choose jail over fines). The report is to a man already in jail; the lines show it.
- July 18: china-leadership-6 (Yang tells Zhou at 9 p.m.) after china-leadership (Mao to Yang in the afternoon). The first line says "that evening he reports it to Zhou Enlai"; the second's note quotes "at 9:00 p.m. that evening". The link also stops the sheet printing the 9 p.m. entry above its unplaced cause.
- July 20: albany (Elliott enjoins the marches) after georgia-albany (King at the night's mass meeting). Basis, the lines: King says "the city attorney has gone to Atlanta to get an injunction", so none was yet granted; the albany note gives Garrow, 206–07: the order issued early Saturday, July 21. The albany entry's own date, July 20 (Young), is therefore in doubt. See below.
- July 22: china-leadership-13 (Zhou, by the General Staff, to the Xinjiang command: still not the first shot) after china-leadership (Mao hears Zhou at 1:30 a.m. on the Indian attack of July 21). The instruction answers that attack, which Zhou first had at 1:30 a.m.; nothing in either note times the instruction itself, so the basis is the entries' words only.
- July 23: china-leadership-8 (Chen Yi at the co-chairmen's lunch "marking the signing") and china-leadership-11 (Souvanna's reception "for the signing") after laos (the Declaration signed). Both follow the signing in their own words. Laos has only a kind range (09:00 to 17:00) and the lunch begins at noon; the link brings laos ahead of both.
