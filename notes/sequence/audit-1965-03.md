### Struck

- Mar. 24, the counts of Celebrezze and O'Brien (`school-aid-4`) after the opening of the House debate (`school-aid`). Bernstein, 197, gives the counts as "the next day" after Mar. 23, with nothing to say whether they came before or after the debate opened. Only O'Brien's "It's been hell today" is clearly said after the day's debate. The entry holds two acts, so the link would put the counts after the opening without a record. I wrote no link in its place. Splitting the entry would let O'Brien's report take the link.

## What is left

- Pairs that the sources do not order, so no link was written:
  - Mar. 22, Zhou's departure for Romania (`china-leadership-2`) and his seeing off of Chen Yi (`china-leadership-4`). The *Waijiao huodong*, 442, lists the seeing-off first, both "morning". The order would follow, since Zhou could not see anyone off after he had flown. But the Chen Yi nianpu has Chen Yi leave Peking on Mar. 19, and Yang's diary has him at Xi'an that evening; the seeing-off on Mar. 22 is itself in doubt (its Check). Settle that first.
  - Mar. 22, Zhou's departure and the PLO joint statement (`china-leadership-3`). The Zhou nianpu, II, 720, says only that the statement was signed in Peking the next day in the names of Zhou and Zhang Xiruo as the Institute's officers. It does not say Zhou signed it in person. The line "Zhou signs in Beijing" reads more into the page than it gives; a Check on that line is due. No link.
  - Mar. 23, Zhou's arrival at Bucharest at 5 p.m. and wreath at 8:30 p.m. (`china-leadership`) and his condolence at the Central Committee at 8 p.m. (`china-leadership-2`). *Waijiao huodong*, 442, gives the three hours. The first entry holds two acts, so a link would be half wrong. Splitting it, the arrival and the wreath each with its hour, would place all three.
  - Mar. 6, Deng's return to Peking with Hill (`china-leadership-11`) and his welcome of Williams at the airport (`china-leadership-12`): both afternoon at the airport, by the Peng Zhen nianpu; perhaps one occasion.
