## Links checked: 30 ops (31 links). Kept: 29. Struck: 1. Narrowed: 1. Turned: none.

### Struck

- Oct. 5, Zhou at Ishibashi's reception (china-leadership) after Peng Zhen's ribbon-cutting (china-leadership-5). The dashiji (373) gives the reception "celebrating the opening" of the exhibition, with no hour; the Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 294) gives the opening ceremony in the afternoon and says nothing of the reception. A reception for an opening may come before or after the ribbon. Nothing on either page gives the order, so this was likelihood, not an order.

### Narrowed

- Oct. 29, the committee's vote (civil-rights-bill): now after the White House meeting (civil-rights-bill-2) alone. Whalen (62) gives the day's order: the 8 a.m. breakfast, "after breakfast" the Republicans in Arends's office, "Then Charlie Halleck and Bill McCulloch drove down to the White House." The Arends meeting is therefore not the entry the vote comes directly after; it now leads the White House meeting instead (added below).

### Kept

- Oct. 5, McCone's cable (vietnam-saigon-2) after the station's cable (vietnam-saigon). The reader called it the weakest of the month and had not opened the report. The report settles it: "Upon being informed, Director McCone sent two cables" (217), and the cable "responded" to the station (221). I added that sentence to the cable's note.
- Oct. 5, the station's cable (vietnam-saigon) after Conein's talk with Minh (vietnam): the cable is "on Conein's talk".
- Oct. 7, Mollenhoff's calendar note (baker-2) after Baker's resignation (baker). Thomas (265): "His calendar for October 7 shows these cryptic notes: ... Baker Resigns." A note that records the resignation comes after it. Kept as the weakest link of the month: nothing says when the calendar was written.
- Oct. 16 to 23, Zhu De's flood tour, 17 links. I read the three pages (III, 1886–88). Every item is printed as the reader says: Oct. 16 "离京乘汽车" first, then Baoding, Shijiazhuang, Xingtai and Handan, "夜宿邯郸" last; Oct. 17 Anyang, Xinxiang, Zhengzhou; Oct. 18 Kaifeng, Shangqiu, Xuzhou; Oct. 19 the Qiligou orchard, Su county, Bengbu; Oct. 21 Jining, Taian, Jinan; Oct. 22 Dezhou, Cangzhou, Tianjin; Oct. 23 "回到北京", then the airport welcome of Thapa, with "晚，宴请塔帕" in the same item. The printed order agrees with the route each day: south along the railway through Hebei and Henan, east to Xuzhou, south to Bengbu, then north through Shandong and Hebei to Tianjin. The items' own words (leaving Peking, the night at Handan, the return to Peking, the evening banquet) agree with it, and nothing goes against it. Kept under owner-rulings 55, first default. The two Hefei items of Oct. 20 rightly have no link.
- Oct. 12, Peng Zhen with Zhu De at the meeting and welcoming banquet (china-leadership-2) after Zhu's airport welcome (china-leadership-3). The Zhu De nianpu (III, 1885) has the airport, then "晚上举行欢迎宴会"; the Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 294) has Peng there "晚".
- Oct. 28, Kennedy's 4 p.m. reading of the compromise (civil-rights-bill-3) after its drafting (civil-rights-bill-2). Whalen: by lunch only details were left, and "A summary of the compromise bill was hurriedly delivered to the White House. At 4:00 p.m. ..." The drafting entry also holds the delivery to the members' homes by midnight, but its act, the drafting, comes first.
- Oct. 29, Kennedy's call to Halleck, Robert Kennedy's statement and Wilkins's statement, each after the vote: the entries' words ("after the committee's vote", "of the bill reported", "the committee's bill"); Whalen (66) gives all three reactions after the adoption. I did not point Robert Kennedy's statement at the counsel's visit with the news (civil-rights-bill-3): nothing says he spoke only after that visit.
- The rest, on the entries' words: Oct. 2 (three hours after the subcommittee's vote), Oct. 23 (the dinner "after the leaders' meeting"), Oct. 26 (the call "on Mollenhoff's story"), Oct. 27 (the flight to Dalat after the 7 a.m. send-off).
