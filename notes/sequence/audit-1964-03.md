## What else the reading showed

- The Salinger days. Salinger's own sequence puts the lunch, the Goldberg calls, the resignation, the Reedy call, Goldwater's remark and the call to Brown on one day, "the next day" after Mar. 17, and the landing at San Francisco at 4 a.m. on the day after that (345–49); his p. 125 makes Mar. 19 his last day. The tree splits the one day between Mar. 18 (lunch, leaving, Goldberg, Brown) and Mar. 19 (resignation, Reedy, Hatcher, Goldwater, the delegation), and puts the San Francisco arrival on Mar. 19 too. Johnson's call to Salinger as press secretary at 4:02 p.m. on Mar. 18 (on the tape) and Robert Kennedy's "Thursday" (Mar. 19) both point to Mar. 19 for the resignation, so the four Mar. 18 entries likely belong to Mar. 19 and the San Francisco entry to Mar. 20. I moved no date (a correction for a reader of the book, R22). The links among the Mar. 18 entries hold whatever day they take; if they move to Mar. 19, the resignation should then follow the Goldberg call, Reedy follow the resignation as now, and the Brown call follow the Reedy call.
- Hours the sources give that the entries lack: Mar. 1, the arrival at Kunming, 9:25 a.m. (Chen Yi nianpu, 1018) or 10 (Yang, II, 356), on `china-leadership-5`'s line; Mar. 24, the landing at Huntsville "a little past 9" (diary, 98), `space`; Mar. 25, the talks with the Japanese party, 3:30 to 6:30 p.m. (Yang, II, 363), `china-leadership-2`; Mar. 18–19, Salinger's 3:30 meeting, 5 p.m. letter and 6 p.m. briefing (346–47).
- Entries whose line says "evening" or "morning" and that have no `time_range`: Mar. 5, 6 and 9, `china-leadership` (Mao's evening meetings); Mar. 16, `china-leadership` (afternoon); Mar. 24, `civil-rights-bill-2` (morning). Mar. 7, `civil-rights-bill` could take its conference's time (3:30 p.m., as `vietnam-force-levels`).
- Mar. 11 and 12, the Athens entries (`succession`, `cyprus-2`, `cyprus-3`) show their hours in Eastern time on the sheet: they lack `time_zone: Europe/Athens`.
