## Links checked: 15. Kept: 12. Struck: 3. Turned: none.

### Struck

- June 2: the middle-school students rushing to the Tsinghua middle school (`china-leadership-5`) after the poster of that afternoon (`china-leadership-6`). The reader took "hearing the news" to mean the news of the poster. MacFarquhar, 104, says otherwise. The news is the founding of the Red Guards (May 29) and the posters of the next day. The students came on June 2 and 3, and the poster of June 2 is given as "one of the posters" put up then, perhaps one of their own. Nothing shows that it came first.
- June 6: the work team sent into the Propaganda Department (`china-leadership-3`) after the Secretariat meeting (`china-leadership`). Pu, 153, says the Secretariat "decided on June 6" to send the team. Deng's chronicle, in the meeting's note, has the same meeting discuss it. So the decision was taken at the meeting, not after it. Neither entry says when the team went in. The suspension of the department's deputy directors (`china-leadership-5`) is the same case. The meeting, the decision and the suspension may be one sitting entered three times (R17).
- June 27: Chen Yi's evening banquet for the Afro-Asian writers (`china-leadership-9`) after his speech opening their meeting (`china-leadership-4`). The nianpu (1153–54) gives no hour for the opening. It prints the opening first, then the morning reception of the delegation heads, then the evening banquet. A printed order is no basis by itself (owner-rulings 55), and the items' words do not speak to when the opening was. That the opening came before the banquet is likely, but nothing shows it.

### Kept, with the basis written into the later entry's note

- June 3: the new committee's announcement reaching the city (`china-leadership-3`) after Mao's approval of the two Xinhua dispatches (`china-leadership`). The Mao nianpu (June 3) has him approve the drafts of that day's dispatches ("已阅。同意。"). MacFarquhar, 42, has the appointments become public that afternoon. A sentence naming both is added to the note.
- June 21: Wang Guangmei's words that Kuai Dafu means to seize power (`china-leadership-18`) after Kuai's note on the poster (`china-leadership-5`). Pu, 163, gives her words right after his note, and they answer it. A sentence is added.

One of the two may be a duplicate. Also, the words that `cal.1966-06-10.china-leadership-2` gives to the evening meeting of June 10 ("work groups need not be sent") are in the nianpu under June 10 evening, but the nianpu has more on work groups under the afternoon of June 12 ("sending work groups so fast is not good").
