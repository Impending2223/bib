# Audit of the sequence pass, October 1962 (R25c, R33b)

The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1962-10.yaml`. The reader's note is `notes/sequence/1962-10.md`. I read every day of the month from the day sheet (`day_order.py 1962 --from 1962-10 --until 1962-10 --full`), each day's entries together. I held each of the reader's links against the two entries, and against their sources where the lines did not settle the order. Under R0 I disregarded nothing: the batch brief set no limit. The patch now has 50 ops and passes `patch-check` with 0 errors. It has 7 warnings, one for each `edit` that sets only `n`. No additive op adds a plain sentence to a note, so those edits are needed.

## What I did not link, and why

- Oct. 16: missile-crisis-5 (O'Donnell's desk) comes after Bundy's news at breakfast and before the 11:50 meeting. But missile-crisis holds both the breakfast and the meeting, so a link would place the scene after the meeting. R25b would split that entry. The reader saw this too.
- Oct. 27: missile-crisis holds the U-2 shot down in the morning and Robert Kennedy's meeting with Dobrynin at night. So excomm-3 (the Rose Garden, "after the word that Major Anderson's U-2 had been shot down") cannot follow it. And the Dobrynin meeting cannot follow missile-crisis-8 (the 7 p.m. Oval Office meeting that sent Robert Kennedy). Splitting the entry by R25b would allow both links.
- Oct. 27: soviet-union-2 approves "Pavlov's proposal from Havana." The entry's own Check leaves open whether Pavlov is Alekseyev or Pliyev, so I did not link it to Pliyev's cable (soviet-union).
- Oct. 22: soviet-union-2 (Khrushchev sleeps in his office "the night after the address") has an open Check on the day, so I wrote no link. The reader left Oct. 20 china-2 unlinked for the same reason.
- Oct. 22: the address already follows soviet-union-leadership (Protocol 60). `patch-check` counts the Presidium entry soviet-union as part of that session, so no second link is needed.
- Oct. 23: three entries are "approved by the Presidium" (Khrushchev's reply, the government's statement, and the cable to Castro). The resolution is P 60/I. The tree's soviet-union entry and its Check do not show at which sitting of Oct. 23 the resolution was taken, so I wrote no link.
- Oct. 20: china (Mao's 7 p.m. meeting) has its own Check: the nianpu gives no subject. So I did not tie it to the attack.
- The reader flagged two links already in the tree that cross a day: cal.1962-10-22.soviet-union-3 follows cal.1962-10-23.soviet-union, and that entry follows cal.1962-10-22.soviet-union. I agree they break R25c's rule that a link runs within one day. They are not in the patch, and the follows op can only add, so taking them out needs an `edit`. I left them for the merger or a later pass.
