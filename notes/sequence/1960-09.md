The basis most links rest on: the Senate Commerce Committee's *Freedom of Communications* (Part 1) prints Kennedy's remarks in the order given, and the notes of his stops cite its pages, which run on from stop to stop (78 to 416 for the month). ## The days

- Sept. 1: no link. The Chinese entries are placed by their hours or ranges; nothing else in the day answers anything else.
- Sept. 2: Kennedy in Maine: Presque Isle, Bangor, the Portland television press conference, the Portland Stadium speech (pp. 80–95). The press conference has Kennedy say "we are going to speak tonight" (p. 83), so the stadium follows it. Manchester, N.H., is left out: the volume prints it first (pp. 78–79), the Presque Isle entry's Check on the order of the stops stands, and nothing settles it. The existing link of Castro's speech to Alekseyev's warning ("the night's rally") stands.
- Sept. 3: San Francisco speech (the entry's own noon, 19:00Z), then the San Francisco press conference, Anchorage airport, Palmer (the evening), the Edgewater (late at night), pp. 95–105.
- Sept. 4: Detroit "on arrival from Alaska" follows the Anchorage press conference (pp. 105–09).
- Sept. 5: the Labor Day tour in Michigan, pp. 111–21: Cadillac Square, the State Fair, Pontiac, Flint, the Muskegon picnic, the Do Drop Inn, the airport "at the day's end". Tshombe's overtures follow Kasavubu's dismissal of Lumumba ("after hearing of Kasavubu's attempt"; the entry's own Check on the day stands). The Third Congress entries of Hanoi are left alone: their notes say the day of each speech is unknown.
- Sept. 6: the Moscow press conference of Mitchell and Martin comes first. Hagerty's word for Eisenhower, the two Defense Department statements, Walter's announcement of the hearings and Walter's denial all answer it and follow it (the *Times* of Sept. 7 reports them together). The raid freeing Bolikango is by "the soldiers from Thysville", who arrive in the entry before. Kennedy: Pocatello, the Spokane wheat statement, the Seattle speech (pp. 125–36).
- Sept. 7: Hoffa's remarks "after the hearing" and Robert Kennedy's reply "on the affidavits" follow the hearing at which the affidavits were presented. Kennedy: the Seattle press conference, Salem, the Multnomah Hotel dinner (pp. 142–58). The Eisenhower news conference chain was already in the tree and is unchanged.
- Sept. 8: Kennedy's train in California, Dunsmuir (six in the morning) to Oakland: Redding, Red Bluff, Chico, Marysville, Sacramento, Suisun, Martinez, Richmond, the Oakland speech (pp. 163–74). White puts Red Bluff an hour after Dunsmuir and Richmond in the late afternoon (pp. 257), and the line runs south.
- Sept. 9: Stockton, Modesto, Merced, Madera, Fresno, Tulare, Bakersfield (pp. 175–81, 188–89), then Burbank, the hotel room, the Shrine speech. O'Donnell has the train trip end at Bakersfield and Kennedy fly to Los Angeles (the note of the hotel-room entry), so Burbank follows Bakersfield, the hotel room follows Burbank, and the Shrine speech follows the hotel room ("before the evening's speech at the Shrine Temple"). The volume prints Burbank (pp. 187–88) before Bakersfield (188–89); I took O'Donnell's order. Turlock, which the volume prints after Tulare (p. 182), is left unlinked. The Burbank press conference (the afternoon) is left unlinked: nothing says whether it came before or after the Burbank remarks.
- Sept. 10: Touré's arrival. Zhou, "waiting" at the airport, comes before Chen Yi's welcome (2:15 p.m.); Liu Shaoqi's meeting with Touré (4 p.m., by Peng Zhen's chronology) follows the arrival. The banquet and the Politburo are placed by their ranges and times. The Politburo and Mao's changes to the Reply carry hours.
- Sept. 11: San Diego airport, the U.S. Grant Hotel, then El Paso "late at night" (pp. 195–97; O'Donnell, 230–31).
- Sept. 12: Texas, pp. 197–206: the Cortez breakfast, the plaza, Lubbock, the Alamo, the Coliseum, then the ministers at the Rice Hotel (pp. 206–18, "Monday evening"). The Alamo speech follows Goodwin's finishing its draft on the bus "that afternoon". The Rice Hotel suite (Rayburn watching) is the same hour as the speech and gets no link. Smith's memorandum has no hour and gets none.
- Sept. 13: Austin, Fort Worth, Grand Prairie, Dallas, the St. Louis airport (pp. 219–27); Arlington is left out (the volume's note, p. 222, out of the route's order). The two Chinese entries on Sino-Soviet relations (Zhou's report at Diaoyutai, and Deng and Peng at Zhou's) may be one meeting by the entry's own Check; no link.
- Sept. 14: St. Louis in the morning, then in New York the women's luncheon, City Center, the Waldorf rally and the Liberal Party dinner "in the evening" (pp. 227–41). Zhou's Shanghai day is placed by its ranges.
- Sept. 15: New Jersey, pp. 242–49: Jersey City, Paramus, Paterson, Clifton, Newark, New Brunswick, Trenton, then the Harrisburg television speech (pp. 249–52).
- Sept. 16: Lebanon, Reading, Columbia, York, Towson, Pikesville (pp. 253–62).
- Sept. 17: North Carolina, pp. 262–77: the Greenville stadium, the tobacco warehouse, Greensboro, the Charlotte Coliseum, the Charlotte–Asheville telephone speech, Raleigh. Moscow: the talks open at 1 p.m. (the Andropov entry's own words); the delegation's meeting "after the session" and the third meeting (4 to 6 p.m.) follow them. The two may be one meeting (the third meeting's own Check); both are linked to the talks, not to each other.
- Sept. 18: the Moscow entries were linked already (Hu Qiaomu's drafting follows the delegation's decision); no new link.
- Sept. 19: Atlantic City, the Chemical Workers then the Steelworkers, then Charleston, the Civic Center and the Daniel Boone Hotel (pp. 279–95). The Moscow entries are timed.
- Sept. 20: no new link. The farm entry's Check on its day (Goodwin's "less than forty-eight hours") stands.
- Sept. 21: Tennessee: Bristol, Knoxville, the Nashville steps, the State Fair, Memphis, then Sioux City "the evening" (pp. 298–311). The night-before-the-contest scene at Sioux City, where Kennedy spent the night (the note of the 3 a.m. call), follows the evening speech.
- Sept. 22: Goodwin's dawn call to Humphrey follows the 3 a.m. call ("Call Hubert in the morning"). Then Sioux City, the Fort Dodge airport and City Square, the Plowing Contest at Sioux Falls (which also follows the dawn call, "hours away"), the Mitchell Corn Palace, Fargo, Billings (pp. 314–35).
- Sept. 23: Cheyenne, the Denver Civic Center, the Hilton luncheon, the Tabernacle, the Hotel Utah (pp. 336–57).
- Sept. 24: Chen Yi's poem is "after the Japanese company's folk-tale play", which his attending the first performance in the entry before records. The Bến Tre entries are placed by ranges, or say "the same time".
- Sept. 25: Cleveland, the Hollenden, then the Euclid Beach steer roast (pp. 358–63). The two entries on the Australian party's leaders are not ordered: Liu Shaoqi's and Mao's talks, no hour for Mao's.
- Sept. 26: the first debate. The nap (Harris finds him at the Peggy Lee records) comes before the holding room, "ten minutes before air time"; the debate follows the holding room (its link to Nixon's morning was in the tree). After the debate: Nixon's aside, the telephone call to the father; the *Caroline* to Painesville follows both ("Thanks, Dad, I've got to go to Ohio"). The governors' telegram and Nixon's night at the Pick-Congress were linked already. The entry "the first debate" with the makeup scene covers the time before and after air; no link either way.
- Sept. 27: Ohio, pp. 366–78: Painesville, the parking lot, Lorain, Mansfield, Akron, Canton, the Erie airport. The Lausche entry carries its own range and is not linked.
- Sept. 28: Erie breakfast and rally, Niagara Falls twice, Lockport, North Tonawanda, Rochester, Buffalo (pp. 379–400).
- Sept. 29: Albany, Troy, Schenectady, Amsterdam (pp. 400–08), then Ilion, Utica, Rome, Oneida and the Syracuse arrival, which the volume gives together as a note (p. 408), then the Syracuse dinner ("on his arrival" in the earlier entry). The order of those four stops rests on the line west from Amsterdam to Syracuse, not on a page.
- Sept. 30: the CIA headquarters' reply follows Devlin's request ("reply at once"). The Moscow entries are placed by hours.
