## Struck

- Dec. 8, the Standing Committee meeting (`china-leadership-7`) after Zhou's two airport welcomes (`china-leadership`, `china-leadership-2`). The meeting was in the afternoon (Mao nianpu, Dec. 8: 下午). It "听取陈毅访问印度尼西亚、缅甸，李先念、伍修权访问阿尔巴尼亚，廖承志、赵毅敏访问意大利的情况汇报": reports on the visits, but no source says that Chen Yi or Li Xiannian gave them in person. No source gives the hour of either landing. The *waijiao huodong* (430) and the Chen Yi nianpu (1072: 由昆明回到北京) give none, and the Zhou nianpu (II, 690–91) has no item for Dec. 8. The order is likely, but likelihood is no order (R25c).

## Pointed at another entry

- Dec. 10, `king-4` (the night's confidences and toasts). The reader linked it to the ceremony (`voting-rights`). Branch (541–42) has the press conference at "the receptions afterward" and the toasts "that night". So `king-4` comes directly after the press conference (`king`), and the press conference after the ceremony. Both links are now in the patch, with their basis in the notes.

## Kept, with what I read

- Dec. 1, Peng Zhen at Deng's (`-7`) after Peng Zhen at Liu's (`-6`). Peng Zhen nianpu, IV, 375: 下午，先后到刘少奇处、邓小平处谈话.
- Dec. 1, the removal of `press-and-broadcasting` after `king`. The line puts the offer to McCartney "during the Hoover–King meeting". It is of the same hour, so there is no order.
- Dec. 2, Peng Zhen's instruction (`-8`) after Mao's comment (`-3`). Peng Zhen nianpu, IV, 375–76: Mao's comment, then "当即批示".
- Dec. 10, the ceremony (`voting-rights`) after the quarrel over the cars "before the ceremony" (`king-3`). Branch, 541, bears it out.
- Dec. 16, Deng reads the draft (`-5`) after Peng Zhen sends it (`-6`). The line says "that Peng Zhen forwards".
- Dec. 18, the Justice Department meeting (`voting-rights`) after King with Rockefeller (`king-2`), and the White House (`king`) after the Justice Department. Young, 326: Rockefeller's plane from New York to Washington, "where we went to the Justice Department"; Humphrey leaves after about an hour and returns with Johnson's invitation. Both entries keep their "Check: the day". Young has the Rockefeller meeting "while in New York", so it may have been the 17th; the plane is the 18th's.
- Dec. 25, Throckmorton's calls (`vietnam-saigon-2`) after Taylor's cable (`vietnam-saigon`). FRUS doc. 467 is Saigon 1939, "December 25, 1964—9 p.m.". It has the calls still to be made: "I have asked Throckmorton to see individually"; "Throckmorton is also to kill the canard". FRUS doc. 471 n.2 puts the calls on Dec. 25. The basis is now in the note. The reader was wrong about the cable's `time` (13:00Z). That is 9 p.m. in Saigon (then UTC+8), the hour the cable was sent. The Washington receipt was 8:46 a.m., or 13:46Z. The field is right.
- Dec. 29, Coretta King's worry on the taps (`king`) after the *Times* story (`georgia`). The line says "After the New York Times's story".
- Dec. 31, the reissue as Zhongfa [64] 815 (`-6`) after Mao's order to reprint (`-5`). *Wengao*, XVII, 482–83: "照改的……文字，重印。请少奇同志阅后交机要室办"; 485 n.1: "同日，中共中央又以中发〔64〕815号文件印发这个经过修改的纪要".

## Added

- Dec. 10, the press conference (`king`) after the ceremony (`voting-rights`). Branch, 541–42: reporters "interrupted the receptions afterward" with the news of the Meridian hearing. The basis is in the note.
- Dec. 16, Zhou's talks with Ouzegane at 11 a.m. (`china-leadership-2`) after Chen Yi's welcome of Ouzegane at the airport (`china-leadership-7`). Chen Yi nianpu, 1073: 上午，到机场欢迎 the envoy; 中午 the talks with Zhou. The envoy is met on arrival, then talks with him. The airport entry's range (6 a.m. to noon) did not place it before 11. The basis is in the note.
- Dec. 30, the Supreme State Conference (`china-leadership-2`) after Zhou and Peng Zhen send the list (`china-leadership`). Zhou nianpu, II, 698: the list sent "当日" with word that more names "还可在最高国务会议上提出"; "当日还出席刘少奇召集的最高国务会议，讨论《政府工作报告》和上述名单". The covering word looks ahead to the conference, and the conference takes up the list just sent. The basis is in the note.
