## Links checked: 7. Kept: 6. Struck: 1. None turned round or repointed.

### Struck

- Nov. 10, JCSM-811-65 (`vietnam-2`) after JCSM-810-65 (`vietnam-air-war-2`). The link rested on the words "In a second memorandum" in the line of 810. Drea, 69, writes "In a second memo they urged...", which means "another memorandum", not "a later one". Gibbons, IV, 104 n.81, calls 810 "a separate memorandum to McNamara that day". Neither says which was written or sent first, and the serial numbers alone are no order (R25c). The words "a second memorandum" in the line say more than the sources; "another memorandum the same day" would say what they say (below).
