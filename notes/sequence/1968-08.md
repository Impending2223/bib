## Left unlinked on purpose

- Aug. 1: Clifford's order for a paper (vietnam-paris-2) and his remark to the 8:30 Group (vietnam-paris). Both may be one meeting; nothing says which came first. Johnson's naming of Thomas to the FAA (transportation-3) and Thomas at the Brooks hearing as "acting administrator" (transportation-2): the hearing may follow the naming, but the lines do not say.
- Aug. 3: the Bratislava declaration and Biľak's handing of the letter to Shelest. The note on the letter gives no hour.
- Aug. 4: Chen Yi's talks with Husain and his banquet. A banquet is usually after the talks, which is likelihood, not order. The nianpu would settle it; I did not open it.
- Aug. 5: the keynote (elections-1968-miami-12) and Brooke's speech (-11), against the second session's other entries. No hour, no order in the notes. Mao's mangoes, a gift of Husain (china-leadership-9), against Mao's receiving Husain: the gift may have come at another time.
- Aug. 20: Rostow to Mrs. Johnson that "the Russians have emptied their out-basket" (soviet-union-3), which cannot have been said after Dobrynin's 8:15 p.m. message, but the diary gives no hour in the entry. The build sorts it after the evening's placed entries; it should sort before them. A reader with the diary (Johnson, *White House Diary*, 703–04) can place it by `time_range` or the link `soviet-union-czechoslovakia-2` after `soviet-union-3`.
- Aug. 23: the Yippies' withdrawal of their suit (elections-1968-chicago-3) against Judge Lynch's dismissal of the Mobilization's (-2). The first line gives the reason, "lest a ruling bar even a festival by day", which fits after the dismissal; no source in hand says so.
- Aug. 24: Schlesinger's visit to McGovern (chicago-8) after Ribicoff's call (chicago-2): the note says the day of the visit is not stated.
- Aug. 26: the convention's first day. Daley's welcome and the Illinois parade (chicago-21), Albert's election as permanent chairman (chicago-27) and the rules votes (chicago-15) have no hours; they sort after the placed entries of the night. The Proceedings or a chronology would give their order.
- Aug. 27: the convention's refusal to add the State chairmen to the National Committee (chicago-15) has no hour; it belongs in the Rules report's sequence around the 12:38 a.m. vote.
- Aug. 29: Humphrey's choice of Muskie in his suite (elections-1968-chicago-14) must come before his naming of Muskie at 4:34 p.m. (chicago-7), but its own note says the day is not given by the work (O'Brien, 253), so I wrote no link. If the day is found to be Aug. 29, chicago-7 follows chicago-14.
