### Struck

- Mar. 13, the advisers at Stephen Smith's apartment (`elections-1968`) after the Cronkite interview (`robert-kennedy-campaign-decision-5`). Witcover, 70–72, has the advisers arriving from about 4 p.m. and watching the 6:30 broadcast together. Schlesinger's journal, 280–81, has an afternoon meeting. The meeting began before the interview aired, so the link runs the wrong way. I wrote no link in its place. The meeting needs its hour: about 4 p.m., Witcover, 70.
- Mar. 15, Johnson to Wilson (`economy-gold-2`) after Wilson to Johnson (`economy-gold`). Schwartz gives the two messages on different pages (199–200 and 202) and never calls Johnson's message a reply. Two messages on one day with nothing to order them get no link.
- Mar. 15, the shuttle from Washington to New York (`robert-kennedy-campaign-decision-2`) after the Hickory Hill party (`robert-kennedy-campaign-2`). This ran the wrong way. The party began on the night of Mar. 15, and its breakfast at seven was the morning of Mar. 16: Edward Kennedy came back from Green Bay, where he arrived about 2:45 a.m. on Mar. 16. Witcover's day runs: the shuttle to New York, the calls from Smith's apartment, the Kings Point coffee, the Garden City lunch, the stop with Hall, the flight back, and then the party (81–84). The links that replace it are listed under "Added".
- Mar. 12, the drive uptown to "21" (`robert-kennedy-campaign-decision`) after Kennedy's lunch with Hackett (`robert-kennedy-campaign-2`). The drive was Moyers's and Schlesinger's, not Kennedy's. The lunch now leads directly into Kennedy's next act of the day, the talk at "21".
