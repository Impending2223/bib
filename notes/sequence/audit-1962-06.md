The chronology (III, 1842) has him reach Tonghai and then, as a △ item, Jianshui; he was at Yuxi on June 7 and set out from Jianshui for Gejiu on June 9; Tonghai lies on the road between. The diary's order alone (owner-rulings 55, the third default): the day's hours stand in order on the page (II, 190: 8 a.m., morning, afternoon, 7:30 p.m.), and Tong's talk is the day's last sentence, after the swim, the television and work to 2 a.m.
