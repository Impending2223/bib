Baker's version has Mansfield opening that meeting at 3:10, the members choosing Monroney chairman, and Monroney handing round a worksheet "considered by the Democratic members at a luncheon today" (617). So there is no duplicate, and the link stands. The lunch entry now has its hour (below).
