## Links checked: 15. Kept: 13. Repointed: 2. Struck: 0.

### Repointed to the entry each comes directly after

- Aug. 6, Ford's address as permanent chairman (`elections-1968-miami-14`). The reader had it after the committees' reports (`-12`). The Proceedings has Dewey's address between them (183, 191, 204–05), so it now follows Dewey (`-13`). The basis sentence is added to the note.
- Aug. 20, Rusk reading Johnson's message to Dobrynin (`soviet-union-czechoslovakia`). The reader had it after Dobrynin's call on Johnson at 8:15 (`-2`). FRUS XVII, doc. 81, shows that at the National Security Council of 10:15 p.m. Johnson told Rusk to ask Dobrynin in, and Rusk read the draft reply. So the reading follows the Council (`-3`), which itself follows the 8:15 call. The basis sentence is added to the note.
