## Struck

- Apr. 16, Zhou's 3 p.m. call on Ne Win (`china-leadership-3`) after `china-leadership-2`. That entry holds four acts: a wreath at Aung San's tomb and calls on Win Maung, U Nu and Ne Win. The call on Ne Win is the act of `-3` itself. The *waijiao huodong*, 273, puts Win Maung early in the morning (晨), U Nu in the morning (上午) and Ne Win at 3 p.m.; it does not name the wreath. The Zhou nianpu, II, 303, puts the wreath on the 16th ("次日") with no hour. The Chen Yi nianpu, 809–810, gives the calls and not the wreath. So nothing places the wreath before 3 p.m., and the entry's last act is `-3`'s own. A link would say the whole of `-2` came first, which the record does not show. The same ruling as the May audit's on Zhou's arrival at Ulan Bator.

## Added

- Apr. 6, Robert Kennedy's call to his brother from Charleston (`elections-1960-primaries-2`) after the county chairmen's meetings (`elections-1960`). The call's line says "after the meeting at the Kanawha Hotel"; the only Kanawha Hotel meeting the tree has on Apr. 6 is the southern chairmen's that afternoon, the last act of `elections-1960`. White, 103: O'Brien and Robert Kennedy arrive the day after Wisconsin; the northern chairmen in the morning at Clarksburg, the southern "that afternoon at the Kanawha Hotel". O'Donnell, 180–81: they fly in the morning after the primary, go "directly" to the meeting at the Kanawha Hotel, and "When we left the meeting, Bobby went to a telephone booth and called Jack". The link holds as long as the call stays on Apr. 6. The call's own Check (and `cal.1960-04-08.elections-1960-2`) says the minutes put the Kanawha Hotel meeting on Apr. 8; if the call moves to Apr. 8, this link must come out.
- Apr. 21, the House passes the Mutual Security authorization (`foreign-aid`) after it agrees to the Senate's amendments to the civil rights bill (`voting-rights`). The Daily Digest, D206 (the file both notes link, its page 204, the page before the D207 they cite), cites the Record for the civil rights vote at pages 8497–8508 and for the Mutual Security bill at 8508–8545. The Record's pages within one chamber's day are the order of the sitting (R25c). The roll-call numbers agree (106, then 107) but are not the basis.
