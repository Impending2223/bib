# Audit of the sequence pass, June 1965 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1965-06.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1965-06.md`. The reader wrote 32 links. I read all 30 days of the month in the order the site now gives them (`day_order.py 1965 --from 1965-06 --until 1965-06 --full`, every day). For each link in the patch, and for each pair the reader left or doubted, I read the two entries in full with `./bib show`. About 20 of the links order the stops and acts of Zhou Enlai's, Chen Yi's and Peng Zhen's journeys; I held each of them to the words on the page, read as images. The corrected patch is in place: 38 `follows` ops, nothing else. I disregarded nothing under R0.

## Links checked: 32. Kept: 30. Struck: 2. None turned round or repointed.

### Struck

- June 6, Zhou's talk with Karume and the exchange of gifts (`china-leadership-4`) after his flight to Zanzibar (`china-leadership`). The dashiji, 461, prints the talk between the Zanzibar banquet and the afternoon flight back to Dar es Salaam, but it gives the talk no place and no part of the day. Karume had also met Zhou at Dar es Salaam on June 4 (460). Only the book's order puts the talk on Zanzibar, and a chronology's order alone is no order (R25c; owner-rulings 55). If the owner takes the printed order, the link can go back.
- June 24, Dirksen against a new resolution (`republican-party-congress`) after the Javits and Randolph resolution (`vietnam-congress`). The reader called Dirksen's word a reply. Neither line says so. Gibbons, III, 282, gives Dirksen's word among the views of early June, beside Mansfield's memorandum of June 9 and before Javits's speech of June 9, and dates it only by the *New York Times* of June 25 (n.16). The Javits and Randolph resolution is at 284–86 (n.21, 111 Cong. Rec. 14,636). Javits had urged a resolution for weeks. Nothing shows that Dirksen spoke after the resolution was offered, or of it.
