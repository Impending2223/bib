The audits left these pairs without a link because the volume that prints them puts the day's chief item first on other days. The owner ruled that a volume's habit is not evidence on a pair, so each pair was read for evidence of its own. I disregarded nothing under R0: the brief set no limit.

I read each page as an image: *Zhou Enlai nianpu* (1997), II, 595–96, 603, 604 and 656 (printed = PDF − 3); *Chen Yi nianpu*, 1000–1003, 1039 and 1200 (printed = PDF − 13); *Zhou Enlai waijiao huodong dashiji*, 376, 379, 380, 381 and 413 (printed = PDF − 13); *Zhonghua renmin gongheguo waijiao dashiji*, II, 310–11 and 349 (printed = PDF − 9); *Ye Jianying nianpu*, 975 (printed = PDF + 553); *Zhou Enlai nianpu* (2020), III, 216–17 (printed = PDF − 4); the Mao nianpu EPUB, Feb. 19, 1968 (by date).

- Nov. 16, 1963: `cal.1963-11-16.china-leadership-2` (Zhou's talk with Liao Chengzhi on the World Peace Council meeting) after `cal.1963-11-16.china-leadership` (the State Council's 138th plenary). The Zhou nianpu, II, 595–96, prints the plenary under the date and the talk as the "△" paragraph after it. Neither entry gives an hour. No other volume prints either item (the dashiji, 376, has no item for the day). Nothing bears on the pair. Link written, on the printed order alone.
- Dec. 21, 1963: `cal.1963-12-21.china-leadership-3` (the flight to Algiers and the visit) after `cal.1963-12-21.china-leadership` (the cable to the Center on the UAR visit). The Zhou nianpu, II, 603, prints the cable under the date and the arrival as the "△" paragraph after it. The cable is untimed. The dashiji (379) and the Chen Yi nianpu (1000) print the departure from Cairo and the 2 p.m. arrival but not the cable. The cable reports a visit that ended that morning, which fits its place before the arrival and does not prove it. Nothing goes against the print. Link written, on the printed order alone.
- Dec. 27, 1963: `cal.1963-12-27.china-leadership-3` (the China–Algeria joint communique) after `cal.1963-12-27.china-leadership-2` (the cable on Tunisia). The Zhou nianpu, II, 604, prints the cable under the date and the communique as the "△" paragraph after it. The Chen Yi nianpu (1002) and the dashiji (381) print neither. The 2001 *Waijiao dashiji* (II, 311) puts the communique under the span Dec. 21–27 with no hour. The Chen Yi nianpu puts a press communique on the Tunisia visit in the morning, which suggests the cable was sent early that day, but it says nothing of the Algerian communique's hour. Nothing goes against the print. Link written, on the printed order alone.
- July 11, 1964: `cal.1964-07-11.china-leadership-2` (Zhou signs the joint communique) after `cal.1964-07-11.china-leadership` (the talks with Ne Win, 8 a.m. and 5:30 p.m.). The Zhou nianpu, II, 656, prints the second talk and the order to the embassy, then "同日" and the signing. The earlier audits took "同日" for an addition with no place in the order. I read it as the nianpu's own word that the signing is of the same day, printed last, with the talks before it. The dashiji (413) puts "during the visit" under July 12 and gives no day or hour. The Chen Yi nianpu (1039) has only "July 11, the joint communique issued." The 2001 *Waijiao dashiji* (II, 349) puts it under July 10–11 with no hour. No source puts the signing before the talks, and a communique that ends talks would come after the last one. Link written, on the printed order alone.
- Feb. 19, 1968: `cal.1968-02-19.china-leadership-2` (Chen Yi at the China–Africa cocktail party) after `cal.1968-02-19.china-leadership` (Mao's reception of the garrison and "three supports, two militaries" units). The Chen Yi nianpu, 1200, prints the reception under the date and the party as the "△" paragraph after it. The Ye Jianying nianpu (975) has the reception with no hour. The Zhou nianpu (III, 216–17) has no item for Feb. 19. The Mao nianpu has no item for the reception; it has a different meeting that day, in the afternoon in Hall 118 of the Great Hall, which Chen Yi attended, and that does not place the two entries. The audit's ground was that the same volume prints Mao's reception first on Aug. 5, 1968 although it came later. That is the volume's habit on another day, not evidence on this pair. Link written, on the printed order alone.
