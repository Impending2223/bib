## Finding the links again

- A link on the printed order alone: `grep -c "the printed order alone" lists/cal/*.yaml`.
- A link on a narrative: the same words with "in a narrative".
- A link from one volume: the same words with the volume's card after them.
- The reception links and the "during" links are listed apart in `audit-watch.md`.

## What the audits found about the volumes

- Several chronologies do not always sort a day by the clock. The Chen Yi nianpu prints Mao's 5 p.m. reception before Zhou's of 2:30 p.m. on Aug. 5, 1968. The Zhou nianpu, II, prints the day's talks before visits that two other volumes put earlier, on Dec. 15 and 19, 1963, and is out of time order on Apr. 21, Sept. 5 and Sept. 30, 1963 and June 28, 1965. The Zhu De nianpu prints an item of the road after the day's main item on June 9, 1962 and Apr. 1, 1963. The Peng Zhen nianpu prints an evening before an afternoon on Mar. 3, 1966.
- Three auditors, working apart, read this the same way: these volumes tend to put the day's chief item, or the senior man's, first. The chronology of Zhou's foreign activities kept the clock on every day read.
- No link was written on a day where the volume is shown out of order (ruling 56, the third reading). On other days the presumption was kept, as the weakest. Two auditors went further and wrote no link from a volume in the weeks where it showed the habit; those pairs are named in ruling 56, the sixth reading.

## What is not done

- Group c's auditor swept every day of its ten months: it listed 207 groups of same-day entries that share a chronology and have no order, and read each against the page. That gave 171 of its links. No other group was swept; the others took up the pairs the notes had raised, and some days the auditors met in passing. A sweep of the other 70 months read, on group c's yield, would give something like a thousand more links of this weakest kind. It has not been started.
- The 171 links of group c's sweep were written by the auditor and have had no second reader.
- Entries that hold a reception or an arrival beside another act were left unlinked, for splitting first (R25b). The audit notes list them.
