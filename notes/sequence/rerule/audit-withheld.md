- Dec. 21, 1963. Kept: `cal.1963-12-21.china-leadership-3` (the flight to Algiers, arriving 2 p.m.) after `cal.1963-12-21.china-leadership` (the cable to the Center and Mao on the UAR visit). - Dec. 27, 1963. Kept: `cal.1963-12-27.china-leadership-3` (the China–Algeria joint communiqué) after `cal.1963-12-27.china-leadership-2` (the cable on Tunisia). The dashiji, 381, and the Chen Yi nianpu, 1002, give neither item. Nothing goes against the print. The basis stands.
- July 11, 1964. Kept, with a second page added to the basis: `cal.1964-07-11.china-leadership-2` (Zhou signs the communiqué) after `cal.1964-07-11.china-leadership` (the talks with Ne Win, 8 a.m. and 5:30 p.m.). - July 11, 1964, the dashiji's page 413. The reader is right and the earlier audit's words mislead. Page 413 prints the communiqué inside the item headed July 12 ("清晨" Zhou and Chen Yi leave Rangoon), as a closing sentence: "访缅期间，发表了中缅联合公报". That dates it only to the visit, July 10 to 12. It gives no day and no hour, and it puts the communiqué before nothing. It does not date the signing July 12. Every volume that gives a day gives July 11 (the Zhou nianpu, 656; the *Waijiao dashiji*, II, 349; the Chen Yi nianpu, 1039, "7月11日 发表中缅联合公报"). So no other day for the signing is in the record. Had one been, the link would fall as a link across two days; it would not turn round.

## The meeting in Hall 118

The reader reported a Mao nianpu meeting of Feb. 19, 1968, in the afternoon in Hall 118 of the Great Hall, with Chen Yi named to lead a study of international questions. The Mao nianpu has no item for Feb. 19, 1968. The meeting is printed under Feb. 19, 1969, in the chapter for 1969, the year Mao was 76. The tree already has it: `cal.1969-02-19.china-leadership` ("Mao, Hall 118, afternoon, with the Cultural Revolution Group and Chen Yi, Li Fuchun, Li Xiannian ..."). No entry is missing, and I added none. The reader's sentence that the meeting "does not place the two entries" is right, but for another reason: the meeting is of another year. For Feb. 19, 1968 the Mao nianpu has nothing, so it says nothing about the pair. Its chapter for 1967 has a Politburo meeting on the morning of Feb. 19, 1967, which is also of another year.
