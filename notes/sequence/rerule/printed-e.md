# Re-ruling the printed order, group e (Aug. 1964 to May 1965)

The rule: owner-rulings 56. Where a volume is otherwise in the order of time, an item printed after another of the same day follows it, unless something goes against it. The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-rerule-printed-e.yaml`: 11 links, 0 errors on `patch-check`. Each note will say "the printed order alone" with the page.

I read the reader's and the auditor's notes for the ten months, and the one patch that took a link out in them (Dec. 1, 1964, which turns on a reception, not a printed order, and belongs to the reception pass). No link in these ten months was struck on the ground of a printed order. The cases are pairs the notes left without a link. I took only those where the note gave the printed order as the ground, or said the volume prints two items of one day with no order between them.

## Links written

Woods dates the Mansfield meeting to Aug. 6 (a Check on that entry); that goes to the day, not to the order.
