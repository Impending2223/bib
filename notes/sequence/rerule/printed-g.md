# Re-ruling on the printed order, group G: June to Nov. 1966, Jan. to Mar. 1967, May 1967

The rule is R25c as the owner rewrote it on Nov. 25, 2026 (owner-rulings 56): where a chronology or a volume is otherwise in the order of time, an item it prints after another of the same day follows it, unless something goes against the printed order. The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-rerule-printed-g.yaml`: 51 links, `patch-check` 0 errors and 0 warnings. Each link's note will say "the printed order alone", with the volume and page.

## How the months were read

I read the sequence notes and the audit of each of the ten months, and the merged patches. In none of the ten months did an audit take a link out of the tree on this ground. The two links taken out (July 13 and Oct. 6, 1966) went on other grounds. The reader's links that an audit struck on this ground are two: June 27 and Aug. 3, 1966.

The days were printed from the day sheet (`day_order.py`, June to Nov. 1966 and Jan. to May 1967). The pages were read as images from the Drive copies: *Zhou Enlai nianpu* (2020), vol. III (offset: printed page + 4); *Chen Yi nianpu* (offset + 13); *Ye Jianying nianpu*, vol. II (offset − 553); and the Mao nianpu EPUB for June 10, 1966. Every copy and image is deleted.

Why each volume counts as otherwise in the order of time: the days run in date order, and where the items carry a part of the day or a word of sequence they stand in that order on every page read (for example Zhou III, 78: 凌晨 first; 84: the day's items in order; Chen Yi 1167: 上午, 上午, 下午, 晚上, 晚上; Ye II, 947: the group speech, then "晚"). Nothing in any pair below goes against the printed order: I read the two entries' words, their hours and ranges, and the route where there was one.
