# Re-ruling the printed order: group f (June to Sept., Nov., Dec. 1965; Feb. to May 1966)

The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-rerule-printed-f.yaml`: 41 links, each with the basis "the printed order alone" and the volume and page. `./bib patch-check`: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Nothing is merged. I disregarded nothing under R0.

What I did. For each of the ten months (June, July, Aug., Sept., Nov., Dec. 1965; Feb., Mar., Apr., May 1966) I read the reader's note and the auditor's note and the merged patch. I looked for links the reader wrote and the auditor struck, pairs left without a link, and links taken out, where the reason was that a chronology or a volume prints a day's items in no order. Then I opened each printed page as an image and read the entries' words, hours and places against it. The copies were fetched into a scratch directory of my own and deleted afterward.

Links already in the tree that an audit took out on this ground: none. The only removals in these ten months are Nov. 5, 1965 (`cal.1965-11-05.china-leadership-7` after `-6`, struck on a diary's sentence) and May 17 and May 26, 1966 (struck because the note's basis was the order of its own citations). Neither is a volume's printed order, so neither is touched here. Struck links that were not removals: June 6 (Karume's talk, now linked after the banquet, below) and Aug. 17 (Chen Yi's call on Pak Yong-sun, linked below). The other struck links (June 24 Dirksen, Sept. 15 Harriman, Nov. 10 JCSM-811, Dec. 24 Kennedy, Feb. 17 and 23) were struck on other grounds and are left.

A note on weight. The nianpus and the *dashiji* are in the order of time on every page I read, with one exception on one day: Peng Zhen nianpu IV, 471, prints the evening banquet of Mar. 3 before an afternoon item. That day is not in the patch. The Peng Zhen links below rest on pages where the hours and parts of the day do stand in order.
