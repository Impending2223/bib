The rule is R25c as the owner rewrote it on Nov. 25, 2026 (owner-rulings 56): where a chronology or a volume is otherwise in the order of time, an item printed after another of the same day follows it, unless something goes against it. The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-rerule-printed-d.yaml`: 34 links and one restored Check, 0 errors by `patch-check`. Each link says "the printed order alone" and the page in its basis.

What I read: the reader's and the auditor's notes for the ten months, the merged patches, the day sheets, and the pages themselves (read as images, nothing changed on Drive): the Zhu De nianpu III 1885–88, 1895–98, 1903–04, 1912–15; the Chen Yi nianpu 993–94, 1005–12, 1018, 1021–23, 1025, 1029–30; the Zhou dashiji 383–91, 404–05, 409, 413; the Peng Zhen nianpu IV 323, 330, 334, 336, 339; the Zhou nianpu II 606–11, 614, 656; FRUS 1964–68, I, docs. 33 and 34.

How a link was tested: I took the order from the page, read the entries' words and hours against it, and read the other chronology for the same day where the entries cite two. A link stands only where the volumes agree or only one prints both items, and nothing in the words, hours or route goes against it.
