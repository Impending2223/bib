## Struck

- Mar. 3, 1962, the hearing on the way to Ji'an (`-7`) after the letter to Deng (`-5`). The nianpu (III, 1827–28) prints the letter, then "△ 抵达吉安市。途经清江县东村人民公社和新淦县时，听取……". Within that item it prints the arrival before the hearing that came on the way to it. So on this day the volume is shown not to keep the order of the day, and R25c gives it no presumption on such a day. The letter says nothing of where it was written. No link.
- Mar. 21, 1962, Zhou's speech (`china-leadership`) after Liu at the Supreme State Conference (`-4`). The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 180) prints one item: Liu reports on the work conference, Zhou introduces the government report, and then "会上，刘少奇还提出" the three-year extension of the fixed interest. The earlier entry holds both of Liu's acts. The second is printed after Zhou's, with "also" and no order. A link to the whole entry would put Zhou after the fixed-interest proposal, which the page does not show. No link while the entry holds both acts.

## Added

- Feb. 11, 1961, Chen Yun's letter to Zhou (`-11`) after his evening return from Tianjin (`-10`). The Chen Yun nianpu (III, 73) prints "晚，由天津返回北京", then "△" the letter. The reader left it on the ground that the letter, being about Hebei and Tianjin, was probably written there. That is a likelihood, not a record, and nothing in the page or the entries places the letter. The basis says "the printed order alone".
- May 28, 1961, Deng's meeting on grain (`china-leadership`) after the meeting at Zhou's (`-7`). The audit of May 1961 left this pair because nothing placed the meeting at Zhou's. The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 131) prints the meeting at Zhou's, then "△" Deng's meeting; the Chen Yun nianpu (III, 89) prints them in the same order, "下午" the meeting at Zhou's, then "△" to Deng's. Yang's diary (as the entry cites it, II, 37) puts Deng's meeting at 4 to 7 p.m., which agrees.
