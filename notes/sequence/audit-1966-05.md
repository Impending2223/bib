## Links checked: 11. Kept: 11. Struck: none. Turned: none.

- May 3: Zhou at Daqing (`china-leadership`) after his morning flight with the Albanian delegation from Peking to Harbin (`china-leadership-2`). Kept on the route: he was in Peking on May 2, and the flight comes before any visit in the province that day. It holds only if the Zhou nianpu's day for Daqing stands; the Daqing entry's note already has the Check on that.
- May 4: Liu's approval (`china-leadership-6`) after Deng's letter proposing the notice (`-5`) and after Peng Zhen's word to Liu and Deng (`-8`). Liu's own words show both: he reads and approves the proposed notice, and "today Comrade Peng Zhen has told me and Xiaoping". Nothing shows which of the two came first, so both stay.
- May 10: the 557th NSC (`vietnam`) after the briefing paper on the rules of engagement for Cambodia (`vietnam-2`). Gibbons, IV, 360 n.18, calls the paper a briefing paper "for an NSC meeting on May 10, 1966". The same note showed that the line of `vietnam-2` held the words of another paper (below); the corrected line now says the paper was for that day's meeting, so the line shows the order.
- May 12: the Defense Ministry's protest (`china`) after the air fight over the border (`vietnam-air-war`). A protest comes after the act it protests. The air-war line also gives Peking's charge in its second sentence; the link stands on the fight, its first act.

- May 17: Deng Tuo's suicide that night (`china-leadership-4`) after his last letter to his wife (`-12`). The letter is his last, and says he had no time to write more; it already follows his letter to the city committee (`-11`).

## Other corrections

- May 10, `cal.1966-05-10.vietnam-2` (`kind: correct`): the line gave the briefing paper the words "fire across the border in self-defense only", "offensive operations could make Cambodia a belligerent" and "questions of law and of international and domestic opinion also weigh heavily with us". Gibbons, IV, 360 n.18, the entry's only source, gives those words to Rusk and McNamara's cable 3212 of Apr. 26, which the calendar already has (`cal.1966-04-26.vietnam`). The note gives the briefing paper the rules then in force: no immediate pursuit into Cambodia; land forces not to enter or fire into Cambodia without Washington's approval, or in self-defense; hostile aircraft pursued only when actually engaged. The line now says that, and that the paper was for that day's meeting; the note says what the correction rests on.
- May 15, `cal.1966-05-15.vietnam-struggle-3`: Gibbons, IV, 317, which the entry cites, gives the joint flash cable 3450 at 7:31 a.m., Washington time. The entry now has that time (11:31 UTC) in place of the workday range it had by kind, and the note keeps the words.
