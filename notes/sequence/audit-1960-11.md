# Audit of the sequence pass, November 1960 (R25c, R33b)

The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1960-11.yaml`. The reader's note is `notes/sequence/1960-11.md`. I read all 30 days of the month from the day sheet (`day_order.py 1960 --from 1960-11 --until 1960-11 --full`), each day's entries together, and held each of the reader's links against the two entries. The reader opened no source and built the chains of Kennedy's stops of Nov. 1–7 on the page order of *Freedom of Communications*, Part 1. So I read that volume's November texts for anything that shows the order of a day (a stop named as next or past, "this morning", "tonight", an hour), and read O'Donnell and Powers, *Johnny, We Hardly Knew Ye*, 246–53, which tells the same week stop by stop. Under R0 I disregarded nothing: the batch brief set no limit. The patch now has 75 ops and 78 links, and `patch-check` shows 0 errors.

## The question the audit turned on

The October audit found that the volume's printed order is not always the order of the day, and the default taken then (`notes/owner-rulings.md`, 55; R25c) is that the printed order is a basis only where the texts agree with it wherever they speak and nothing in them or in the route goes against it. In November the texts agree with the printed order on every day but one. On Nov. 5 they do not:

- At the 90th Street rally Kennedy says, "When we leave here tonight, we are going to have a rally at 2 in the morning in Waterbury," and near the end, "We will now move on to Waterbury" (pp. 906–07). He apologizes for being "about 5 hours late." The volume prints three more New York items after it: the Commodore, the remarks outside the Coliseum and the Coliseum speech.
- Outside the Coliseum he says, "I am going to leave here tonight and speak at 1 in the morning, in Waterbury" (p. 909). The later, later-running hour at 90th Street fits 90th Street coming after the Coliseum, not before it.
- O'Donnell (247) has the Coliseum rally "that night," and then "Kennedy couldn't get out of New York City fast enough." So the sources do not agree on which stop was last, and the printed order of the evening is not the order of the day.

On the other days the texts and the route agree with the volume, and O'Donnell's lists of the stops (248–49) run in the same order: Sunday, New Haven, Bridgeport, Commack, Newark, Jersey City, then Lewiston; Monday, Springfield, Hartford, Burlington, Manchester, Boston.
