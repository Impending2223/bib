Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1968-02.yaml`, 7 links. `./bib patch-check`: 0 errors. I disregarded nothing under R0. Where the words did not settle an order I opened the entries' notes and the FRUS text in `sources/frus-text/` (docs 51 and 63); I opened no book copy, so no link rests on a page I have not seen in the tree.

## The days

- Feb. 1: no link. The Prayer Breakfast, the Economic Report, the House vote and the Tet fights have hours or no bearing on each other; the Loan shooting is one entry.
- Feb. 2: no link. The Panmunjom meeting, the news conference and the correspondents' session are placed by hours.
- Feb. 3: no link. Wheeler's CM-2944-68 (FRUS 1964–68, VI, doc. 51) and his JCS 1272 to Westmoreland (doc. 51 n.5) are both of Feb. 3 and the note gives no order. Nixon's St. Anselm's reception, the Concord dinner and the Hillsboro taping (Saturday and Sunday mornings) have no stated order in their lines or in the White cites.
- Feb. 4: Mao's "fully agreed" follows Zhou's letter with the Railway Ministry report (china-leadership after china-leadership-2). The Zhou nianpu (III, 212–213) has the liaison meeting, then the letter, then Mao's note; the first entry's line holds the sending and the reply, the second the letter. Peng's second day of questioning is a different day from the first.
- Feb. 5: the editorial on the founding of the Hubei Revolutionary Committee follows the founding (china-leadership-5 after -4). The Peng session of 2:30 p.m. to 1 a.m. and the case-group head's notebook entry have no order between them; Zhou's two Zhejiang talks are one entry.
- Feb. 6: no link. The breakfast, the Tuesday lunch of the advisers and the evening events are placed by hours or are unrelated.
- Feb. 7: Zhou's talk with Hồ Chí Minh follows the meeting with Mao ("after the meeting with Mao" in the line; china-leadership-2 after china-leadership). Westmoreland's conference at Đà Nẵng, to bring out Làng Vây's survivors, follows the overrunning of Lang Vei (vietnam-khe-sanh-2 after vietnam). Westmoreland, by his own account (*Soldier Reports*, 341), puts the attack just after midnight on the 6th and the conference "the next day"; the order holds either way. The COFRAM firing in support of the camp is not linked: the entry does not say whether it came before or after the overrun. The Peng entries already carry their links.
- Feb. 8: the Wilson state dinner follows the talks and lunch (europe-and-the-alliance-2 after europe-and-the-alliance). The Johnson diary (629–30, cited in the first entry) has the conference set for 11:30 to one, then lunch, "talking and working until past 3:30"; the dinner is the evening. The first entry runs Feb. 8–9, so the link holds for the first day's talks only. Daley's talk with Kennedy and the luncheon speech already carry their link.
- Feb. 9: no new link. JCS 1589 (hold back the formal submission; FRUS 1964–68, VI, doc. 63 n.4: "in replying" to MAC 1812 of Feb. 8) and Westmoreland's MAC 1858 of 1633Z (the reply to JCS 1590 of 0021Z) have no stated order; the serial numbers alone are none. The Helms memorandum, Alsop's call and Fulbright's note on Kennedy's speech are separate acts with no order shown. The mediation panel and Lindsay's refusal are placed by hours.
- Feb. 10: no link. Zhou's talks, Mao's added paragraph (it follows Zhou's Feb. 9 draft, across days) and the Vance trip stand alone.
- Feb. 11: no link.
- Feb. 12: no new link. McNamara's talk with Russell, the Joint Chiefs' answer to McNamara's request of Feb. 9 and the two Johnson meetings have no stated order. The existing link of the 1:45 meeting after Westmoreland's cable is right; the cable's entry carries the hour 2:12 p.m., after the meeting's start, so the sort rests on the link (see below).
- Feb. 13: no link. McNamara's order for the brigade and the regimental landing team (Drea, 183) and the Joint Chiefs' direction to fly them (MACV Command History, 245, 247) read as one decision and its execution; the two lines do not say which came first, and I did not open either book. The Joint Chiefs' mobilization report (JCSM-96-68) is its own act.
- Feb. 14, 15, 16: no link.
- Feb. 17: no link. Zhou tells Wei Guoqing to take hold of the Liuzhou backlog; he convenes the business group on Guangxi's rail traffic; and at 1:40 p.m. he tells the Vietnamese he "told Wei Guoqing yesterday" and will go himself if it is unsolved. The three have no stated order. The night-of-Feb. 17 attacks on Saigon and in Nam Bộ are of one night.
- Feb. 18: no link. The 1 a.m. attack on the Lộ Tẻ airfield and the fight at An Bình and Long Tuyền, both at Cần Thơ, both from the Vietnamese history (V, 158): the second entry's note points to the first, but the history gives no order.
- Feb. 19: no link. Mao's reception of the Peking garrison and Chen Yi's cocktail party are probably day and evening; the lines give no hour.
- Feb. 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25: no link. The Zhou entries of Feb. 21 (the liaison meeting on the supplementary order and the evening talk to Tianjin cadres) have an hour only for the second.
- Feb. 26: Taft's insertion in the Record follows White's column "of the day" (republican-party-congress after press-and-broadcasting-3); the second entry's note cites the same page of the Record as the column "as Taft put it in". The open-housing entry holds the Feb. 28 vote as well (see below).
- Feb. 27: Califano and McPherson, riding back from the meeting at State, follow the meeting (vietnam-205000-2 after vietnam). The Ripon board's minutes are dated Feb. 27 and answer Romney's withdrawal of Feb. 28; the entry carries its own Check.
- Feb. 28: no new link. Romney's withdrawal, Rockefeller's and Nixon's remarks already carry theirs. Romney at Boston on Johnson's broadcasting holdings is said by the note to be the day of the withdrawal in Washington; no order is given. The compromise announced precedes the tabling vote on the same day, but that vote sits in the Feb. 26 entry.
- Feb. 29: no new link. Johnson's argument with Califano over the Kerner report and the Commission's report are not linked: Kerner had handed Califano an advance copy on Feb. 27, so the report was in hand before Feb. 29. The Peng sessions already carry theirs. Zhou's report-hearing and the Guangxi arms call have no order.

- Feb. 27: the Ripon board's minutes are dated the day before the withdrawal they answer; the entry says so.
- Feb. 7 and Feb. 9: the Wheeler messages of Feb. 7, 8 and 9 are dated differently by Longley, Barrett and FRUS; each entry carries the Check.
