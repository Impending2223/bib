# Sequence pass, December 1964 (R25c)

Read all 31 days of December 1964 as the site orders them (`day_order.py 1964 --from 1964-12 --until 1964-12 --full`, about 560 entries), each day's entries together, asking of each: does it come after another entry of the same day? Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1964-12.yaml`, 12 ops (11 links added, 1 link removed). I disregarded nothing under R0. Beyond the day sheet I opened only the entries named below (`./bib show`) and no outside source; where a link rests on a note, the day's line says so. The day sheet prints each time in the place's clock; I compared stored times where it mattered.
