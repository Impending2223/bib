## The reader's links: 14 checked, 14 kept

- Mar. 1, `peace-corps-2` (the special message) after `peace-corps` (the executive order): kept. The message, read at the American Presidency Project: "I have today signed an Executive Order establishing a Peace Corps on a temporary pilot basis."
- Mar. 9, `press-and-broadcasting` (Kennedy to White, the end of the day) after `farm` (the House passes the feed grain bill): kept. White, 376: the bill had passed "a few hours before, by only seven votes." I also link the same entry to the conference on the science budget (below); both are true, and the House's vote is not on White's chain of the President's day.
- Mar. 13, `china-leadership-5` (Deng and Zhou propose one conference) after `china-leadership` (Mao's 8 a.m. letter): kept. The entries' notes give the basis: the Deng Xiaoping nianpu (III, 1622) has Deng and Zhou propose after they are told of Mao's views, and the Mao zhuan (ch. 67 n.26) has the letter, then the proposal, then Mao's agreement. I did not read those two pages myself; I read Yang Shangkun's diary for the day (I, 662–63), which gives the proposal and Mao's agreement and does not speak to the letter.
- Mar. 13, `china-leadership-6` (the Three North conference, 3 to 6 p.m., told of the move to Guangzhou) after `china-leadership-9` (Mao agrees to one conference at Guangzhou): kept. The diary, I, 662–63: Mao agrees, it is decided all go to Guangzhou next day; then "3时到6时，继续开了会，宣布明天去广州."
- Mar. 23, `china-leadership-3` (the Center sends the letter on investigation) after `china-leadership` (the closing plenary): kept, as the weakest link of the month. The reader's basis was Mao's giving the draft its title. The firmer basis is in the plenary's own note: the Mao zhuan (ch. 67 n.33) has the conference close that day and the Center's letter adopted there, and the Jianguo yilai wengao (XV, 527 n.1) has the letter issued on Mar. 23, the day Mao dated it. A letter issued after its adoption. But Yang's diary (I, 667) has it sent to the printers only on Mar. 25 (the entry's note carries that Check); if the "sending" of Mar. 23 is only the letter's date, the link says nothing, and it should go.
- Mar. 24, `program` (the House votes on the minimum wage) after `jobs-and-wages` (the meeting in Rayburn's office): kept, on a condition. O'Brien (124–25) has the meeting before the floor debate. The same pages put Albert's speech "the next day," which would put the meeting on Mar. 23 and the link across two days. The entry carries a Check on its day; if the meeting moves, the link goes.
- Mar. 26, Zhu De: `china-leadership-5` (hears Zhao Wenfu at Zhengzhou) after `-4` (arrives at Zhengzhou); `-6` (arrives at Luoyang) after `-5`; `-7` (arrives at Sanmenxia) after `-6`: kept, all three. I read the Zhu De nianpu (xinbian), III, 1801, on the page image. The first is one item: 抵达河南省郑州市。听取……赵文甫汇报. The other two rest on the route, not on the printed order alone (owner-rulings 55): coming north from Guangzhou he reaches Zhengzhou first; Luoyang lies between Zhengzhou and Sanmenxia on the line west; he is at Sanmenxia on the 27th and goes on to Xi'an. Nothing in the page goes against it.
- Mar. 27, Zhu De: `china-leadership-12` (arrives at Xi'an) after `-11` (the Sanmenxia reservoir); `-13` (talks with Liu Lantao and Zhang Desheng) after `-12`: kept. The same page: the reservoir and the forum with its engineers, then △ 抵达陕西省西安市。和……刘澜涛、……张德生谈话. The route west, and the talk is at Xi'an, in the same item as the arrival.
- Mar. 27, `china-leadership-3` (Zhou cables Mao the draft "agreed by the Secretariat") after `china-leadership` (Zhou reports the draft at the Secretariat): kept. The line itself says the draft was agreed by the Secretariat.
- Mar. 28, `china-leadership-7` (Zeng Xisheng calls the Anhui standing committee that evening, "back from Guangzhou") after `-2` (Zeng returns to Hefei): kept. The line says "back from Guangzhou"; the Mubei's 當晚 (I, 333) ties the meeting to the return.
- Mar. 30, `china-leadership-9` (Zhu De's diary on Chengdu's flower market) after `-8` (he visits the flower fair): kept. The nianpu, III, 1801, gives both in one item: 参观成都花会和手工艺展览。在日记中记述：…… A comment written on a thing seen comes after the seeing (R25c: a comment after the act it is on).

The brief's count of seven links on the Zhu De nianpu: I count six (three on Mar. 26, two on Mar. 27, one on Mar. 30), all read on the page.

None struck, none turned round, none moved to another entry.

## Links I added: 15, in 14 ops

- Mar. 1, `school-aid-2` (the news conference, 10 a.m.) after `peace-corps` (the executive order). The conference, read at the American Presidency Project (1961 Pub. Papers 135), opens: "I have today signed an Executive order providing for the establishment of a Peace Corps." The order had sorted after the conference by its kind of event. The patch adds the sentence to the conference's note by an `edit`. (The same opening has "I am also sending to Congress a message", which does not say whether the message went before or after, so the message gets no link to the conference.)
- Mar. 9, the President's day by White's account (*The Making of the President, 1960*, 374–76, read on the copy's text layer). White gives the day as one run, each step marked "then", and the calendar has an entry for nearly every step. Twelve links, in eleven ops: `economy-2` (Seaborg, Wiesner, Bell) after `democratic-party` (Truman); `europe-and-the-alliance` (Finletter) after `economy-2`; `europe-and-the-alliance-france` (Gavin) after `europe-and-the-alliance`; `trade` (the cotton growers) after `europe-and-the-alliance-france`; `peace-corps` (the filming) after `trade`; `program-2` (the veterans' commander) and `transition-and-staff` (the Treasurer) each after `peace-corps`, White putting both after a private lunch and giving no order between the two; `laos` (the meeting of 3:30) after both of them; `defense` (the swearing in at the OCDM) after `laos` ("Then, from this meeting ... to another ceremony"); `economy-3` (the conference on the science budget) after `defense` ("Then another conference"); `press-and-broadcasting` (White's talk with Kennedy) after `economy-3` ("At the end of such a day"). The patch adds a sentence naming the step before, with White's word and page, to each later entry's note by an `edit`. The day itself is in doubt (each entry carries White's "a typical Thursday" Check); if it moves, the chain moves with it.
- Mar. 22, `china-leadership-6` (Yang arranges a plane to Guangzhou) after `-5` (Chen Yi's dinner, 5 p.m.). The diary, I, 666: 饭后看川剧……归来办公，并组织飞机去广州, after the meal and the opera. Note sentence by an `edit`.
- Mar. 25, `china-leadership-7` (Peng Zhen tells Yang of the conference) after `-4` (Zhou, Deng and Peng fly back from Hankou that morning). The diary, I, 666–67: the flight in the morning, then the memorial at 3 p.m., then Peng's telling, then the evening. This rests on the diary's own order of the day (owner-rulings 55), the weakest kind of basis; I did not tie the telling to the memorial, since both men were at it and the telling may have been there. Note sentence by an `edit`.
