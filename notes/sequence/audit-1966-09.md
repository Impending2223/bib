I read the four items at the American Presidency Project: the President spoke at 11:40 a.m. in Cobo Hall, Detroit; at 2:35 p.m. at the Battle Creek Sanitarium; at 5:08 p.m. at the Montgomery County Fair, Dayton; at 8:40 p.m. at the Fairfield County Fairgrounds, Lancaster. This is her account. It is still the only record, but it is her own statement of what came next, not the order of her sentences alone. The entries' pages (423–25, 425, 426–27) are right.
