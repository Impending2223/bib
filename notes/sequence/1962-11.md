- Nov. 19: china (Mao twice calls Zhou and others to meetings) after south-asia (Mao's afternoon note "to the Premier to read", 4 p.m. by the Wengao). Basis, the sources and the weakest link of the set: Jian, 482 (cited in the china note) has Zhou rush to Mao's residence half an hour after Mao's note. Jian and the Mao biography date the note Nov. 18 and Nov. 19 differently (Chen against the Wengao); the china note already carries "Check: the day". I take the order within the day on whichever day it was. The nianpu says Zhou went to Mao's twice; the link says only that the meetings come after the note.

Basis, the notes: the Mao biography (V, ch. 71) says "随后" (afterward) of the Banerjee talk, and the china note repeats it ("Zhou and Chen Yi then receive the Indian chargé"). The 8 p.m. time already put south-asia-2 first among the three, since the other two have no hour and sorted after it; the links bring the afternoon note and the meetings ahead of it.

The same notice: Zhou's revision of the departments' draft, Mao's approval. The Mao entry's own note gives the hour; Zhou's has none and sorted after it.
