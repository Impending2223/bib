## The basis that is a source: Dec. 27

Salinger, *With Kennedy*, 56: after the passage on Kennedy's agreement "on December 27, 1960," he writes "There was now no question that TV was willing ... I had met for five hours that day with the top news executives of the networks. All were enthusiastic." Then: "My next chore was to tell the newspapermen." The meeting is in the past perfect, so it comes before the scene in which Kennedy agrees. Kennedy's earlier "Start it moving with the networks, but let's hold off on the announcement until we know they'll buy it" is a first instruction, not the agreement. One account's grammar; the link rests on it alone. The entry for the agreement (`cal.1960-12-27.press-and-broadcasting`) now follows the meeting entry (`-2`), though its note says "See" the other way round.
