## Links checked: 3. Kept: 2. Struck: 1. None turned round or repointed.

### Struck

- Aug. 17, Chen Yi calling on "Piao Yongchun" (`china-leadership-13`) after "Piao Yongchun" calling on Chen Yi (`china-leadership-12`). The link rested on the line's words "returns the call". The nianpu (1114) does not say that. It prints two afternoon items, the Korean's call on Chen Yi and then "拜会朴永淳", a plain call, not a return call (回拜). Chen Yi's call is one of his rounds of calls on the heads of the delegations that week (Bitat and Stoica on Aug. 16; Nguyễn Duy Trinh and Aidit on Aug. 17). The order of a nianpu's items is no order by itself (R25c), and nothing else places the two calls. The words "returns the call" in the line go further than the page. A Check or a reword to "calls on" is due (below).

## Links added: 5

- Aug. 5, Cooper's memorandum "Another Pause" (`vietnam-pause`) after the raid on the Liên Chiểu fuel depot (`vietnam-hanoi-3`). The line says "after the night's attack on the fuel depot at Đà Nẵng".
