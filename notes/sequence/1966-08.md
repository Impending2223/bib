# Sequence pass, August 1966 (R25c)

Read all 31 days of August 1966 as the site orders them (`day_order.py 1966 --from 1966-08 --until 1966-08 --full`), each day's entries together, asking of each: does it come after another entry of the same day? Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1966-08.yaml`, 27 ops (the links below). I disregarded nothing under R0. I opened no outside source; where a link rests on a source it is the one the entry's own note already cites, and I say so. The times the sheet prints for Chinese entries without a `time_zone` are Washington's clock, so "10:00" for an event at 10 p.m. in Peking is correct.
