# Sequence pass, May 1968 (R25c)

Read all 31 days of May 1968 as the site orders them (`day_order.py 1968 --from 1968-05 --until 1968-05 --full`, the whole sheet, about 540 entries), each day's entries together, asking of each: does it come after another entry of the same day? Where a link was in doubt I read the entries in full (`./bib show`). Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1968-05.yaml`, 15 links, `./bib patch-check` 0 errors. I disregarded nothing under R0. I opened no source beyond the entries' own words and notes; every link rests on those, and the basis is given where the two lines do not show it. No `check:` or `to-check:` op was needed.

## The days

- May 1 to 5: no link. The Vietnam offensive entries of May 5 (a dozen places, each "at dawn" or "in the first week") say nothing of their order. Kennedy's May 1 entries and Califano's memorandum of May 2 are placed by the day or by a Check.
- May 6: soviet-union-leadership-2 (the Politburo, 3 to 8:30 p.m.) after soviet-union-leadership (the Defense Council, 11 to 3); the Politburo's line says "after the Defense Council". robert-kennedy-campaign-primaries-2 (the Holiday Inn bar) after robert-kennedy-campaign-primaries (the motorcade); its line says "after the motorcade". No link for -3 (dinner at Sam's Subway): Witcover prints it after the bar, but the lines give no hour for either.
- May 7: no link. The primary-night entries (Sheraton-Lincoln, CBS with Mudd and Cronkite, O'Brien on the couch, the airport coffee shop around midnight) do not say which came first. Eisenhower's briefing and the Zhou-Xuân Thủy talk (21:45) stand apart.
- May 8: no link. Kennedy's rabbis (the day) and the Synagogue Council (night): Witcover puts the Council "the same night"; the rabbis' hour is not given.
- May 9: no link. The two memoranda to Johnson on Kennedy and "law and order" look like one paper (see below). The two existing links on the conference report hold.
- May 10: robert-kennedy-campaign-primaries-2 (Nebraska City) after robert-kennedy-campaign-primaries (Beatrice, "the first rally of a motorcade"). No link for -3 (the Omaha dinner, night): the motorcade's last stop is not given.
