Four sources were opened where the lines did not settle an order, each from the owner's copy on Drive, each deleted after: Schlesinger, *Robert Kennedy and His Times*, 910–14 (text layer, printed pages checked against the running heads); Longley, *LBJ's 1968*, 163–64 (offset 18); Goodwin, *Remembering America*, 536–39 (offset 12). ## The days

- June 1: debate (elections-1968) after Lowell's morning call on Kennedy (robert-kennedy-campaign-california-2). Schlesinger 910–11 puts Lowell's call that morning and the "Rudolf Hess" remark to McCarthy before the debate ("reached McCarthy before the debate"), so the whole of the Lowell entry comes first, "Afterward" in its line meaning after the call. The all-day Fairmont entry and Sirhan's purchase of the ammunition get no link: nothing says which came first.
- June 2: no link. Kennedy's taped *Issues and Answers* and McCarthy's *Face the Nation* both speak to "joining"; the entries give no hour for either, and McCarthy's words may answer an earlier Kennedy line rather than the broadcast. Jiang Qing's note and Zhou's talk with Wang Dongxing and visit to Jiang's (china-leadership, -2, Zhou nianpu III, 233) are likely in that order, but the lines do not say so and I did not open the nianpu: left.
- June 3: china-leadership (Mao's evening talk, "after receiving the study class", by *Mao Zedong zhuan* n.16 as the entry's note has it) after china-leadership-3 (the receiving of the Nanjing and Shenyang study classes). Trinh's instruction to the Paris delegation and Lê Đức Thọ's arrival: no order.
- June 4: from Malibu to the Ambassador. The Malibu entry (robert-kennedy-campaign-2: lunch, the 3 p.m. CBS sample) comes first: Schlesinger 913 has Frankenheimer drive Kennedy to the Ambassador about 6:30. McGovern's call, "mid-evening" (california-3), follows it. The Goodwin bathroom entry, "toward midnight" (campaign-3), follows the mid-evening one (the entries' words, and Schlesinger 913 in that order). Goodwin's calls to Galbraith and Lowenstein (california-4) follow the bathroom talk: Schlesinger 913 ("Goodwin began calling") and Goodwin 536–39 (Goodwin on the telephone with a McCarthy supporter as Kennedy left for the ballroom, after the bathroom talk). Sorensen's bathroom talk (campaign-4) is no link: Goodwin writes that Sorensen "had joined us" in the same bathroom, so the two are one scene and no order. The Daley call, the O'Donnell call, the Mudd and Schoumacher interviews: no basis for an order (Schlesinger's page order alone is not one). The Dougherty call (robert-kennedy-campaign-california, range 23:30–00:15 from Peirce) is left alone; see below.
