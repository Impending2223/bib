## Links checked: 27 (in 26 ops). Kept: 25. Repointed: 1. Struck: 1.

### Struck

- June 17, the Secretariat meeting (`china-leadership`) after the work conference's closing plenary (`china-leadership-5`). The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 339) gives both in the afternoon and lists the Secretariat meeting first. It says that meeting decided "on the basis of the conference's discussions", not on matters the conference had settled. Yang's diary (II, 385) puts the Secretariat at 3:30 to 7 p.m. and does not mention the plenary. The Deng nianpu (in the entry's note) has the Secretariat on the questions the conference "is to settle". Nothing shows which came first, so there is no link. The entry's words "the main matters settled by the Central Work Conference" go further than its sources; that is left for a later pass.

### Repointed

- June 10, the cloture vote (`civil-rights-bill`) now follows Russell's last speech (`civil-rights-bill-10`), not Mansfield's (`-9`). Risen, 227, gives the order: Mansfield spoke, then ceded the floor to Russell, then Dirksen spoke (his words are in the vote's line), then the roll was called. A new link puts Russell after Mansfield (below). The tree's link from the vote to Humphrey's morning count (`-6`) stands.

### Kept, with the basis put in the note

- June 6, Russell chiding Humphrey (`civil-rights-bill`) after the agreement on Hickenlooper's amendments (`civil-rights-bill-2`). The lines alone do not show this: the chiding is "Saturday morning" and the agreement is "Saturday". Purdum, 297, has the Senate reconvene Saturday morning, the consent given with no objection, and then Russell's protest. Whalen, 193, does not settle it.
- June 9, Zhou at the treaty's signing (`china-leadership-2`) after the NPC Standing Committee meeting (`china-leadership-6`). The Zhu De nianpu (III, 1910) says the meeting "decided that Liu Shaoqi sign" the treaty (决定由刘少奇签订), and gives the signing ceremony next. So the decision came before the signing, not a ratification after it.
- June 16, the car ride (`civil-rights-the-executive`) after the St. Regis dinner (`robert-kennedy`). Lady Bird Johnson, 171: "After dinner we piled into a car." The line's words "the car in from the St. Regis" are hers misread ("in from the airport" refers to the earlier drive); the order holds. The wording could be corrected by a reword.
