# Sequence pass, December 1963 (R25c)

Read all 31 days of December 1963 as the site orders them (`day_order.py 1963 --from 1963-12 --until 1963-12 --full`, 1,111 lines), each day's entries together, asking of each: does it come after another entry of the same day? Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1963-12.yaml`, 24 ops: 23 links added and one removed, no `check` or `to-check` op. `./bib patch-check`: 0 errors, 0 warnings. I disregarded nothing under R0. Where the lines did not settle an order I opened the entries in full (`./bib show`) and, for five days, the source: Lady Bird Johnson's diary, pp. 14–27 (the Drive copy, fetched by curl, offset 14, text layer, deleted after), Schlesinger's *Journals*, pp. 210–15 (the Drive copy, offset 18, text layer, deleted after), and FRUS 1961–63, IV, doc. 374 (`sources/frus-text`). No clock-placed pair got a link unless the words or a source give an order the clock does not.
