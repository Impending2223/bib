## The link in the tree the reader took out (Dec. 17)

The record bears the removal out. `cal.1963-12-17.succession-2` (Johnson to Ball, 6:05 p.m.) followed `cal.1963-12-17.latin-america` (the Senate's Chamizal vote). The call is about the UN address: Beschloss's headnote (105) reads "At noon, LBJ addressed the United Nations in New York ... Now he tries to generate some favorable press coverage," and Johnson asks for "reactions from Stevenson up there at our speech today." The Miller Center's summary gives the call's subjects as Bunker's appointment to the OAS and the press reaction to the speech. Nothing ties the call to the Chamizal vote. The vote's own hour is not given, only the Senate's sitting from noon to 5:47 p.m., and the call's hour already places the call after the sitting. So the old link was no order of the day, only a restatement of the clock, and it named the wrong entry as the one the call comes after. It is not a judgment call. I kept the removal, gave it a fuller `why`, and kept the new link to the address. I also took the sentence "After the Senate rose at 5:47 p.m.: Daily Digest ..." out of the call's note, since it was the old link's basis, and put in its place a sentence giving Beschloss's headnote as the basis of the new link.
