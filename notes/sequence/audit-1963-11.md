# Audit of the sequence pass, November 1963 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1963-11.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1963-11.md`. I read all 30 days of the month from the day sheet (`day_order.py 1963 --from 1963-11 --until 1963-11 --full`), each day's entries together, and every one of the reader's links against its two entries. The reader opened no source; most of its Nov. 22 links rested on the lines alone, and it left the Air Force One entries and the Chinese entries of Nov. 3–8 and 14–17 unordered for want of a page. I read the pages listed below. The corrected patch is in place: 68 `follows` ops and 17 `edit` ops. `./bib patch-check` gives 0 errors and 16 warnings; each warning is an edit that sets only `n`, which is expected, since each adds one sentence giving the source for a link. I disregarded nothing under R0: the brief set no limit.

## Links checked: 53 ops. Kept as written: 40. Struck: 2. Pointed elsewhere or narrowed: 11. Turned: none.

### Struck

- Nov. 1, the five-minute ultimatum to Nhu (vietnam-saigon-7) after the 4:30 p.m. radio announcement (vietnam-saigon-6). The Church report (222) gives the announcement at 4:30 and then begins a new sentence, "According to Conein's report," with no hour and no word of sequence; the entry itself carries "Check: the hour." Only the report's order of telling puts it second. The link from the "half an hour later" offer (vietnam-saigon-8) to the ultimatum stands, on the entry's words.
- Nov. 22, the Sheraton-Carlton lunch of Kitchel and Tony Smith (goldwater-4) after the shooting. The entry's act is the lunch and its talk; only its last sentence, the taxi afterward, comes after the news. The lunch is not shown to follow the shooting. Splitting the taxi into an entry of its own would let that part follow.

## The Chinese days, Nov. 3–8 and 14–17

I read the Chen Yi nianpu (993–95), Yang's diary (II, 323–27) and the Zhou nianpu for these days. None of them orders a pair the entries leave unordered, except Nov. 4 (above).
