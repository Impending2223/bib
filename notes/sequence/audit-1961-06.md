## Struck

- June 26, Harriman's talk with Souvanna (`laos`) after Chen Yi sees Souvanna off to Paris (`china-leadership-4`). The reader reasoned from the route: Souvanna leaves Geneva in the morning, and the line has the talk in Paris. The record says otherwise. The entry's "To check" names Harriman's cable, Confe 287. That is FRUS 1961–63, XXIV, doc. 122: "Geneva, June 26, 1961, 10 p.m." It speaks of meeting Souvanna "last night", and its n.1 puts the meeting "late in the evening of June 25". Souvanna "was leaving the next morning for a 10-day stay in Paris". The Chen Yi nianpu (877) has him leaving Geneva for Paris on the morning of June 26. So the talk came before the departure, a day earlier, at Geneva. The entry keeps its date and place, which come from Kaplan (248). The patch adds a Check naming the cable.

## Added

- June 3, the luncheon (`berlin-and-vienna-3`) after the first talk (`berlin-and-vienna`). Salinger (177–78) has Kennedy and Khrushchev meet "that first morning" for an "hour-long conversation", and then come into the embassy dining room for lunch. O'Donnell (333–35) has the first talk at the embassy, then "lunch that day", then the walk in the garden, then the afternoon session. The talk is the entry's first act, which its time (12:45 p.m.) places. The reader's link to Salinger's morning meeting with Kharlamov (`-4`) is also kept. "The morning of Salinger's arrival" comes before lunch, and neither source says whether it came before or after the first talk. So the luncheon follows both. The basis is now in the luncheon's note.
- June 13, Liu Shaoqi's banquet for Sukarno (`china-leadership-5`) after Mao's reception of Sukarno (`china-leadership`). The Zhou diplomatic chronology (313) gives the reception at "下午6时20分" and then the banquet "晚上". The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 134) puts the banquet "当晚". The day's parts and the hour stand in that order, and nothing goes against it. Mao's entry has the hour in its note but no `time`, so the sheet printed it after the banquet. The basis is now in the banquet's note.

## Kept, with what I read

- June 3, the bathtub (`berlin-and-vienna-2`) after the talks (`berlin-and-vienna`). The line says "after the first day's talks". O'Donnell (335) has the talk in the tub "later", after the walk in the garden.
- June 4, Reston (`press-and-broadcasting`) and Salinger staying behind (`berlin-and-vienna-4`), each after the Soviet Embassy meeting with Mrs. Jarvis (`berlin-and-vienna-3`). Salinger (180) sets that meeting "the following morning", while the leaders met upstairs. Reston comes "ten minutes after Khrushchev". Salinger's own entry comes "after the last session".
- June 4, Kreisky (`berlin-and-vienna`) and the flight (`berlin-and-vienna-2`), each after Reston. The lines show it: Reston sees Kennedy at the embassy in Vienna, and Kreisky has seen Kennedy off and the plane has left Vienna. O'Donnell (337) bears it out: Reston asked to see Kennedy "when we were getting ready to go to the airport"; "that night on the plane" came after. Kreisky and the flight both follow the departure and are not ordered against each other.
- June 6, Souvanna and Souphanouvong arrive at Moscow (`laos-2`) after Zhou sees them off at Peking (`china-leadership-2`). The line says "from Peking". The Zhou chronology (312) has the send-off "晨".
- June 8, Chen Yi calls on the two princes (`china-leadership-11`) after he meets them at the Geneva airport (`china-leadership-10`). The Chen Yi nianpu (876) gives the arrival, then "△ 下午，分别拜会".
- June 12, Zhou explains the nine measures again (`china-leadership-2`) after Mao closes the conference (`china-leadership`). Yang Shangkun's diary (II, 41) has Mao speak at the 11 a.m. plenary, and "以后" Li Fuchun, Zhou and Li Xiannian. The note of `-2` already says so. Li Fuchun's speech has no entry of its own. It sits inside Mao's entry, so the link names that entry.
- June 24, the debate (`soviet-union-2`) after Mrs. Jarvis's lunch (`soviet-union`), and the barbecue (`soviet-union-3`) after the debate. Salinger (184–86): the lunch "before the debate"; the debate taped "that afternoon" and broadcast "late in the evening"; the party "that night", the guests waiting "for us to arrive from New York"; Kennedy's call five minutes after the show. Watching the broadcast at the barbecue is not the act that comes first here. The debate itself was taped in the afternoon, and the barbecue began after the debaters came back from New York. So R25c's rule on an act watched as it happens does not take the link out. The day is in doubt for all three entries, and each carries a Check. The order holds whichever day it was.
- June 27, the sows returned (`china-leadership-8`) after Chen Yun's inquiry begins (`china-leadership-7`). The Chen Yun nianpu (III, 96): "在养猪问题的座谈会之后，小蒸公社根据陈云的意见宣布". The forum is one of the inquiry's ten. The announcement's own day, somewhere between June 27 and July 11, is unknown, and its entry carries a Check. It cannot come before the inquiry began.

## Left unlinked, checked against the source

- June 4, Reston (`press-and-broadcasting`) and Salinger told to stay behind (`berlin-and-vienna-4`). Salinger (182) has Kennedy tell him to invite Reston, and to stay behind, in two sentences with no word of which came first.
- June 5, Zhou receives Chervonenko at the airport at 3 p.m. (`china-leadership-2`), and Zhou welcomes the princes at the airport "下午" (`-3`). The chronology (311–12) prints them in that order, with no word that orders them.
- June 6, the plenary. Yang (II, 39): "上午10时开大会，恩来同志报告粮食问题，到1时半才完". The Deng nianpu (III, 1643–44) and the Chen Yun nianpu (III, 92–93) put Deng's and Chen Yun's speeches "上午" and do not mention Zhou's report. Chen Yun answers Li Fuchun, not Zhou. The Zhu De nianpu (III, 1806) gives Zhu's words only "六月六日，在讨论农业问题与压缩城市人口问题时", with no hour. The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 132) says the plenary discussed Zhou's nine measures. But the interjection it records is Deng's, inside Peng's own speech, which has no entry. If Zhou's report ran until 1:30, morning speeches may have come before it, or within it. So none of the three is linked.
- June 13, the Cuba Study Group's report (`cuba`) and the 5 p.m. meeting (`cuba-2`). Taylor's covering letter of June 13 (FRUS 1961–63, X, doc. 230) says the group will "make our final report to you orally", supported by four memoranda. Its n.3 points to doc. 229, the 5 p.m. meeting. They are one occasion, so there is no order between them (see "What is left").
- June 9, the two receptions of the East German ambassador: Liu (`china-leadership-3`, no hour) and Zhou (`-2`, 11 p.m.). As with the Albanian ambassador that day, these are two receptions by two men. Nothing orders Liu's.
- June 21, Mao receives the Japanese Communist Diet members, and Peng Zhen's talks with them. Both are "afternoon", with nothing in the lines to order them. I did not reach the Peng Zhen nianpu's page for June 21 (IV, 136). The order in which it prints a day's items would be no basis by itself.
