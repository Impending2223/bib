### Kept, with the basis put in the note

- July 2, the Naples drive (`-in-4`) after the North American College (`-in-3`). O'Donnell, 425–26: the visit to NATO headquarters at Naples came "before his departure to the United States," and "From Naples we flew directly to Washington." The College was in Rome that morning, so Naples comes after it. The sentence is added to the Naples note.
- July 16, Kennedy's approval of the cuts (`europe-and-the-alliance`) after McNamara's memorandum (`economy-2`). The reader could not tell whether the recommendations approved were those of the memorandum. Poole, 210: "McNamara sent his recommendations to the White House with JCS caveats attached; Kennedy approved them on 16 July." FRUS IX, doc. 28, the memorandum of July 16, is the paper that makes them: the 23 B-47s and the 32 C-130s from France, and the Chiefs' concern. The sentence is added to the note.
- July 19, Goldwater's answer (`goldwater-2`) after Rockefeller's news conference (`elections-1964`). The reader's doubt was whether the answer was to the July 17 challenge to a debate. Novak, 215, settles it: the news conference's "real meat" was "his challenge to Goldwater to disavow right-wing support," and then "Goldwater's reply to Rockefeller's challenge." So the answer is to the July 19 news conference. The sentence is added to the note; the line's Check on the day stays, since Novak gives no day for the reply.

## Added: 1 link, and one hour set

- July 16, Kennedy's approval of the smaller steps in Japan (`japan`) after McNamara's memorandum (`economy-2`). Poole, 290–91: McNamara dropped the move of the 1st Marine Air Wing, and "On 16 July, President Kennedy approved the smaller steps." The memorandum of July 16 proposes the steps in Japan (the B-57 wing, the C-124s, the F-102s) and leaves the wing's move to a review. The sentence is added to the note.
- July 15, the sixth meeting (`china-leadership-4`) had no hour, so the site showed it after the delegation's meeting at the embassy from 4:30 p.m. (`-5`). Its own note already cites Yang's diary (II, 297) for the session from 10 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. The edit sets the hour, 10 a.m. Moscow time (07:00 UTC), and the day's order comes out right with no link.

## Removed: 1 link already in the tree

- July 12, Li Yan's talk with Yang (`-8`) after the delegation's meetings at the embassy (`-7`). The reader called it doubtful. Yang's diary (II, 296) reads: 8时晚餐后又去使馆，李岩同志来谈话 ("after dinner at 8, to the embassy again; Comrade Li Yan came to talk"). The "again after 8 p.m." of the `-7` line is that same evening at the embassy, so Li Yan's talk falls inside it, not after it. The link is taken out, with that reason; the 8 p.m. hour of `-8` still places it.
