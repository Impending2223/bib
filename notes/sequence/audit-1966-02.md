### Struck

- Feb. 17, the background session on the flight from India to Australia (`cal.1966-02-18.vietnam-humphrey-2`) after Humphrey's talk with Indira Gandhi. A link runs within one day, and the flight was probably not on Feb. 17. Humphrey saw Mrs. Gandhi at New Delhi on Feb. 17 (FRUS XXV, doc. 296). He writes of a fourteen-hour flight from India and a staff meeting the first night in Australia (335). Solberg's note dates that Canberra staff meeting Feb. 18 (508). So the flight left late on Feb. 17 or on Feb. 18, and most or all of it fell on Feb. 18. The sources do not settle the day beyond doubt, so I did not move the entry's date (R22). I added a `check` op that sets out the evidence. If the date is later moved to Feb. 18, the entry's id will match its date.
- Feb. 23, the enlarged Standing Committee on farm mechanization (`china-leadership-5`) after Liu's letter (`-4`). The page (Liu nianpu, 632) has Liu propose that the Center call a meeting "in a few days" (几天以后), and then says Liu "afterward" (随后) presided at it. "Afterward" does not put the meeting on Feb. 23, and the letter itself points to a later day. I added a `check` op that quotes the letter's words.
