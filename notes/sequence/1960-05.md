# Sequence pass, May 1960 (R25c)

Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1960-05.yaml`, 13 ops, 13 links added, none removed, no `check` or `to-check` op. `./bib patch-check`: 0 errors, 0 warnings. I disregarded nothing under R0. Where the lines did not settle an order I opened the entries in full (`./bib show`) and, once, the source (Fursenko, *Khrushchev's Cold War*, pp. 278–79, the Drive copy fetched by curl, offset 6, text layer, deleted after). No clock-placed pair got a link unless the words or a source give an order the clock does not. The month is mostly Chinese leadership entries from the nianpu and Yang Shangkun's diary, most already placed by an hour or a range.
