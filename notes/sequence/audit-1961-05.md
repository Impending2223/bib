## Struck

- May 19, Proclamation 18 (`korea-leadership-3`) after Proclamation 17 (`korea-leadership-4`). May 22, Proclamation 9 (`korea-leadership-3`) after Proclamation 6 (`korea-leadership-2`). May 27, Decree 17 (`korea-leadership-5`) after Decree 15 (`korea-leadership-2`). Each link rested only on the serial numbers. Each pair is printed in one issue of the Gazette, and nothing in either text says which was made or announced first. On May 27 both decrees take effect at the same hour, noon. A number in a series shows the order in which the office numbered its acts. It does not show the order of the day, just as the order in which a collection prints a day's items does not (R25c; `notes/owner-rulings.md`, 55). I took that as a default. If the owner rules that serial numbers do order a day, these three links go back, and the other Korean days need the same reading (May 16, Proclamations 1–8; May 18, 12–16; May 21, Decrees 5–7; May 22, Proclamations 5–10).
- May 31, Chen Yun's speech (`china-leadership`) after Zhou Enlai's report on grain (`china-leadership-3`). The reader rested this on Yang Shangkun's diary (II, 38). I read the page. It says the enlarged meeting sat from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. and from 3:30 to 6 p.m., heard Zhou's report, and heard Chen Yun, Deng, Zhu De and Liu speak. It does not say Chen Yun spoke after the report. The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 131–132) puts Zhou's report in the afternoon, so Chen Yun may have spoken in the morning, before it. Neither the Chen Yun nianpu (III, 90) nor MacFarquhar (III, 30–32, 62) gives Chen Yun's hour.

## Kept, with what I read

- May 1, Zhu De leaves Chengdu in the evening (`-13`) after the rally (`-8`) and the producers (`-9`). The Zhu De nianpu (III, 1804, read on the page image) has the rally and the reception under May 1, then "△ 晚，离成都赴陕西省视察". He left in the evening, after his acts at Chengdu.
- May 2, Zhu De arrives at Xi'an (`-9`) after Baoji (`-8`). The same page prints "抵达陕西省宝鸡市。视察二一二仪表厂" and then "△ 抵达西安市". He was coming from Chengdu, and the line from Chengdu reaches Baoji first. The page and the route agree, and nothing goes against them (owner-rulings 55).
- May 4, Zhu De hears the Henan heads (`-13`) after he arrives at Zhengzhou (`-10`). One item on the page: arrives, then hears.
- May 4, the Chinese statement (`china`) after Zhou approves it (`china-leadership`). Zhou nianpu II, 409: Zhou "审批" the statement.
- May 5, Zhu De's day. The page reads: Shijiazhuang (arrives, inspects, hears), then "△ 抵达保定市" (hears, inspects), then "△ 晚，回到北京". So Baoding (`-12`, `-13`) comes after Shijiazhuang (`-10`, `-11`), and the evening return (`-14`) after Baoding. The page prints the acts within each city in different orders. No time word orders them, so the reader rightly left them unlinked.
- May 7, Mao has the report sent round (`china-leadership`) after Zhou's telephone report (`-13`). Zhou nianpu II, 409–410: "清晨三时 ... 电话汇报", then Mao's comment.
- May 15, the riders arrive at New Orleans (`freedom-rides-2`) after they fly out of Birmingham (`-4`). Kept. Both entries are filed under May 15, though the plane left near midnight and the arrival may fall on May 16; `-2` already carries "Check: the day".
- May 16, the Dominican Republic. Church Committee report, 209 (the AARC copy): the State Department's cable to Dearborn was "a return cable ... sent the same day" (`-2` after the base entry). The CIA officer "referred to Dearborn's May 16 request" (`-4` after the base). "Devine responded to the CIA officer's memorandum on the same day" (`-5` after `-4`). The page does not say whether State's cable to Dearborn came before or after `-4` and `-5`. No link is added between them.
- May 16, Chen Yi's first speech (`china-leadership-2`) after the conference opens (`laos`). The entry's own note gives the Chen Yi nianpu (872–873): the speech came "after the opening". I did not open that page.
- May 16, Korea. Martial law (`korea-leadership`), the six pledges (`-2`) and the officers put over the police (`-4`) each come after the coup (`japan-and-korea`). Each is an act of the committee that the coup set up before dawn. The link runs to the coup entry's first act; that entry spans May 16–18.
- May 21, Peng Zhen telephones the Standing Committee's decision (`china-leadership-6`) after the conference entry (`china-leadership`). Yang's diary (II, 28) and the Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 131) both have Peng telephone in the evening that "常委会议决定" the order of business. The decision was made at the enlarged Standing Committee meeting, which the conference entry holds ("its order set by the enlarged Standing Committee that evening").
- May 21, King's call to Robert Kennedy (`robert-kennedy-2`) after the siege of the church (`freedom-rides`). The call is made from inside the siege. This matches the tree's own link for Patterson's call (`freedom-rides-2`).

## Added

- May 2, Zhou's evening reception of the Burmese (`china-leadership-4`) after his return to Peking (`-2`). Zhou nianpu II, 408 (page image): "当日，返回北京，立即主持研究 ... △ 晚，在接见 ...". Yang's diary (II, 1) has the Premier back by the 5 p.m. Secretariat. I did not link the Secretariat (`-3`) after `-2`. The study Zhou "at once" presided over may be that same Secretariat meeting, which by the Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 125, as the entry cites it) took up the Geneva conference.
- May 10, Zhou leaves for Handan at midnight (`china-leadership-2`) after his visit to Chen Jiageng (`china-leadership`). Zhou nianpu II, 410: "看望病中的陈嘉庚。△ 午夜，离京去邯郸". The departure is timed at the day's close (16:00Z). The visit has no hour, so without the link the sheet would sort it after the departure.
- May 16, Kennedy's tree planting at Ottawa (`transition-and-staff`) after his 10 a.m. meeting with Menshikov at the White House (`soviet-union`, FRUS V, doc. 69) and his meeting with Hallstein "at the White House on May 16" (`european-community`, 1961 Pub. Papers 380). The arrival remarks at Ottawa that day (Pub. Papers 380) speak of "this easy voyage ... from Washington". I did not name the Cuba Study Group's luncheon (`cuba`): Taylor dates it May 15, and the entry carries a Check on its day.
- May 31, Liu Shaoqi's speech (`china-leadership-2`) after Zhou's report (`-3`), Deng's speech (`-4`) and Zhu De's speech (`-5`). The Liu Shaoqi nianpu (II, 524, page image) says Liu "主持中共中央工作会议全体会议并作总结讲话": he presided at the plenary and gave the closing summary. The Peng Zhen and Deng nianpu put Zhou's report and Deng's speech at that afternoon plenary, Liu presiding. Yang's diary (II, 38) has Zhu De speak at the same meeting. I left Chen Yun's entry (`china-leadership`) out of the link, because its line also holds Liu's own warning about the Soviet Union in 1921 (below).

## Left unlinked, checked against the source

- May 2: The Secretariat (`-3`) and Zhou's return and study (`-2`): see above.
- May 8: The Secretariat (`-3`) and Zhou's return and talk with Chen Yi (`-2`). Yang's diary (II, 3) has the Secretariat meet in the morning and Zhou join it at noon, back from Handan. The talk with Chen Yi may be the Secretariat's own business, so no link.
- May 9: Zhou sees Chen Yi off "上午" and receives Chervonenko at the airport at 10 a.m. Neither the Zhou diplomatic chronology (311, page image) nor the Zhou or Chen Yi nianpu says which came first.
- May 28: Peng Zhen and the others at Zhou's on grain (`-7`), and Deng's meeting on grain (`china-leadership`). The Peng Zhen nianpu (IV, 131) prints these as two items. They are two meetings, not one as the reader thought. Yang's diary (II, 37) puts Deng's Secretariat at 4 to 7 p.m., but nothing places the meeting at Zhou's.
- May 30: The CIA station's cable and Trujillo's killing. Church report, 214: the cable speaks of "assassination preparation" and of withdrawing personnel. The page gives it no hour, and does not say it was sent before the killing.
- May 31: Deng's "少奇同志也说过" (Deng nianpu III, 1642) means Liu "has also said", at some earlier time. It does not point to Liu's speech of that day.
