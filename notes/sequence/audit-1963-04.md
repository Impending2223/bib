### The nine Zhu De links, now read against the nianpu

Each holds. The nianpu's items agree with the order at every point they speak to it, and the route goes the same way.

- Apr. 2, reaching Yongchuan (`china-leadership-9`) after the Luzhou inspections (`-8`), and hearing the Jiangjin heads (`-10`) after reaching Yongchuan. The nianpu (1870–71) has the Luzhou inspections under Apr. 2, then "抵达永川县", then the Jiangjin prefectural heads. Yongchuan lay in Jiangjin prefecture, and the entry places the hearing there.
- Apr. 4, reaching Chongqing (`-7`) after Qijiang (`-6`): "抵达重庆市。途中视察綦江县…" (1871), the inspections "on the way".
- Apr. 6, the night at Changshou (`-6`) after the boat trip and the reservoir (`-5`): "由重庆乘船去长寿县，视察狮子滩水库。夜宿水库招待所" (1871).
- Apr. 7, the boat back to Chongqing (`-5`) after the Changshou inspections (`-4`) (1871).
- Apr. 9, the poem "Southern Hot Springs" (`-4`) after the visit to the springs (`-3`): the poem is on the visit (its last line has the visitors climbing the steps), and the nianpu prints it after the visit (1871). A comment after the act it is on.
- Apr. 19, setting out south from Chengdu (`-9`) after the Chengdu items (`-8`) (1873).
- Apr. 20, the tour of Mount Emei and the ci (`-13`) after reaching it by way of Jiajiang (`-12`): "经夹江抵峨眉山" (1873). The nianpu does not say where he heard the Jiajiang heads, which `-12` also holds; the link rests on the arrival.
- Apr. 24, the Erya Terrace and its poem (`-16`) after reaching Leshan (`-15`) (1873). As on Apr. 20, `-15` also holds the inspections and the county heads, which the nianpu does not order against the terrace; the link rests on the arrival.
- Apr. 25, the return to Chengdu (`-18`) after the Meishan items (`-16`, `-17`): "返回成都市" last (1874).

But it is not shown: we do not know when Liu and Chen sent theirs, and the diary's word "agree" sits badly with the Zhou nianpu's account of what Peking asked (the entry's Check). No link.
- Apr. 3, Doar's agreement at Greenwood and the President's news conference on Greenwood; Apr. 25, the Rangoon items of the afternoon and Liu's embassy reception (*Chen Yi nianpu*, 964, gives the reception no hour).
