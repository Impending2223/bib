### Struck

The line says Harriman wrote on "the copy of Fulbright's speech ... the White House sent him." Woods, 386, says no more than that, and the note (386 n.40) dates the statement, "Statement by JWF on D.R., Sept. 15, 1965," not Harriman's writing on it.
