# Sequence pass, November 1966 (R25c)

Read all 30 days of `day_order.py 1966 --from 1966-11 --until 1966-11 --full`, a day at a time, and the full text (`./bib show`) of the 31 entries where the lines did not settle an order. Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1966-11.yaml`, 6 links, `patch-check` 0 errors. Sources opened: McNamara, *In Retrospect* (the Drive copy, text layer, for Nov. 7) and the FRUS text in `sources/frus-text/1966-11.json.xz` (docs 301 and 311). Nothing downloaded was kept. Most of the month is election-night returns, State and Chicago entries and Chinese nianpu items, where each entry stands alone or the clock already places it.

## Links written, by day

- Nov. 1: the Anchorage evening with Johnson's aides (elections-1966) follows the last night in Seoul (vietnam-asian-trip). The trip entry says the party went to Anchorage after Seoul "the same day, across the date line"; the Seoul entry is the last night of the stay. The Seoul entry carries "Check: the day", so this link is as good as that day.
- Nov. 3: Mao's sixth reception (china-leadership) follows Zhou's call to the heads of Peking's Red Guard bodies (china-leadership-2), which the entry places "before the sixth reception".
- Nov. 7: McNamara's dinner with Neustadt and the Harvard faculty (vietnam-2) follows the Mill Street crowd and his leaving for Kissinger's seminar (students). His account (*In Retrospect*, ch. 9) has the crowd, the seminar, then a half hour in a Harvard Square bookstore, then "Later I joined Dick Neustadt and other Harvard faculty friends for dinner." Both entries cite that chapter; the dinner's own entry gives only the evening.
- Nov. 8: Nixon's supper at El Morocco "after the last returns" (elections-1966-4) follows the night's returns (elections-1966). Both have the same election-night range; the entry's words give the order.
- Nov. 10: Zhou has Chen Boda wire the East China Bureau and the Anting workers (china-leadership-3) after he is "told of the Anting stoppage" (china-leadership-2, the stoppage itself). Without the link the call to the Bureau sorted first.
- Nov. 17: Komer's memorandum to Johnson (vietnam-3) follows Lodge's replies 11124 and 11125 (vietnam-2). FRUS 1964-68, IV, doc. 311, dated Nov. 17, opens: "Lodge is now in with three long replies (attached) which essentially buy our proposals."

Days with no link: all the others.

## Looked at and left unlinked

- Nov. 1: the Chen Yi receptions (china-leadership-5, -6) both fall in the evening; nothing says which came first.
- Nov. 3: the Clean Water and Demonstration Cities signings are both 1:06 p.m. and each says the other was signed with it.
- Nov. 4: Johnson's meeting with Rusk and McNamara (behind State's cable to Lodge, vietnam) and his meeting with McNamara and Wheeler on the 1967 request (vietnam-force-levels-2) may be one meeting or two; the notes (Gibbons IV, 463, 471) do not say.
- Nov. 11: Johnson's approval of McNamara's course (vietnam-force-levels) and McNamara's memorandum to Wheeler cutting the Chiefs' plan (vietnam). FRUS doc. 301 does not mention the meeting, so there is no basis for an order.
- Nov. 14: Mao's 3 p.m. meeting (china-leadership), where Chen Boda is told to draft a document on factories and mines, and Zhou's liaison meeting (china-leadership-2), where Gu Mu reports on the "Twelve Instructions" Chen Boda has already drafted. The second may come after the first, but the Zhou nianpu gives no hour, and the nianpu's order is no basis. The Zhou items -2 and -4 are "the item after" one another in the nianpu only.
- Nov. 21: Chen Boda's rebuke of Gu Mu and Yu Qiuli (china-leadership-2) and Li Fuchun's 10 p.m. call to Yu (china-leadership-3). Yang Jisheng (I, 334) has the two report to Zhou, who says he and Tao Zhu will hear them the next day, while the call already carries Mao's answer.
- Nov. 25: Ackley to Marshall and the Califano–Saunders call (transportation) and Edelman's call to Justice (transportation-2). Neither entry gives an hour.
- Nov. 30: Chen Yi at the Workers' Gymnasium (china-leadership-5), who passes on "the Center's and the Premier's instruction", and Zhou's collective reception (china-leadership-3), where Chen Boda calls it "the Premier's instruction". The Chen Yi nianpu says only "evening" (晚上); the Zhou entry has no hour. Not read further.
- Nov. 12, Nov. 17 (the Bethesda visits of Albert and Eisenhower, the "big four" and the anniversary party) and Nov. 28 (Chen Yi's cable and Mao's telegram to Albania): same day, no stated order.
