## Struck

- May 17, Macmillan's diary line that the summit "has blown up" (`soviet-union-u2-4`) after the three leaders' meeting at 3 p.m. (`u2-3`). Fursenko, 288, quotes the line without a day, in a paragraph on how each leader took Khrushchev's brushoff; it does not say when Macmillan wrote it, or that he wrote it after the meeting. The entry itself carries "Check: the day". The words "has blown up" could as well refer to the session of May 16. With the day in doubt and no order in the source, there is no basis for the link.

The lines show it: Zhou, Kim and Liu are at the meeting. Yang, I, 510: Mao's party in at 6:15 p.m., "金日成同志和少奇、恩来、小平等都到了", the talks from about 7 to midnight.
- May 27, Mao's reception of Montgomery (`china-leadership`) after Ke Qingshi's luncheon for him at noon (`-9`). Yang, I, 512: "柯老12时宴请蒙"; then "下午主席接见蒙". The chronology in the entry has it at dinner. Either way it is after noon.
- May 27, Zhou at Ulan Bator (`-2`) after his leaving Peking (`-3`). An arrival comes after the departure.
- May 28, the city tour (`-11`) after the 10 a.m. talk with Tsedenbal (`-2`). The Zhou nianpu, II, 323, says "会谈后参观乌兰巴托市容" and the factories. The Chen Yi nianpu, 819, prints the morning city tour before the morning talk. The order in which a chronology prints a day's items is no order by itself (R25c), and it does not speak to which came first. So the Zhou nianpu's "after the talk" stands. The *waijiao huodong*, 282, gives the talk at 10 a.m. and the factories in the afternoon, and has no morning tour. The link holds on the Zhou nianpu's words; the tension with the Chen Yi nianpu is noted here.
- May 29, Liu Shaoqi's and Deng's leave-taking of Lin Boqu's remains (`-3`, `-6`), the Secretariat on his funeral (`-7`) and Chen Yi's poem on hearing of his death (`-9`), each after the death (`-8`). The lines show it. Yang, I, 513: Lin died "今天上午10时40分".

- May 22, Mao's meeting at the Nanping pool (`china-leadership`) after the West Lake outing (`-4`). Yang, I, 511: back from the lake at 11:30; "午睡后，5时去游泳池主席处参加会议 ... 10时谈完".
