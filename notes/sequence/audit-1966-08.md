# Audit of the sequence pass, August 1966 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1966-08.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1966-08.md`. I read all 31 days of the month in the order the site now gives them (`day_order.py 1966 --from 1966-08 --until 1966-08 --full`). For each link in the patch I read the two entries in full. Where the lines did not settle the order, I read the sources. The corrected patch is in place: 30 `follows` ops and 16 `edit` ops. `./bib patch-check` gives 0 errors and 16 warnings. Fifteen warnings are for the edits that set only `n`, which is expected: each adds a sentence giving the basis of a link. The other warning is on Aug. 18 and is explained below. I disregarded nothing under R0.

## Links checked: 26. Kept: 23. Struck: 3. Turned: none.

### Struck

- Aug. 3, Mao's remarks at the plenum (`china-leadership`) after Song Renqiong's misgivings (`china-leadership-12`). Pu, 190, has the four regional heads speak in turn, Liu Lantao first, and Mao's remark to Liu Lantao comes right after Liu's speech. That is before Song spoke. The entry holds both of Mao's remarks, so as a whole it does not come after Song. If the remark to Song were split into an entry of its own (R25b), it could be linked, once a source places it.
- Aug. 3, Zhou Ziren's letter at Chongqing University (`china-leadership-10`) after the work group's announcement on Zheng Siqun (`china-leadership-9`). Yang, I, 490, dates both "the evening of Aug. 3" in two sentences side by side, with no word of which came first. The letter is about the suicide, not about the expulsion. That the letter answered the announcement is likely, but nothing shows it.
- Aug. 3, Zhou's calls to Wang Li and Guan Feng (`china-leadership-4`) after his message to Ye Lin (`china-leadership-3`). In Zhou's chronicle (III, 44) the second is only the next item of the day, marked △, with no word of sequence. The next △ item on that page speaks of "this afternoon's" meeting, so the items of a day are not always in the order of the clock.

### Kept, with the basis written into the later entry's note

In nine kept links the two lines alone do not show the order, so I added a sentence to the later entry's note naming the earlier entry and the page:

- Aug. 4, the New Friendship mass meeting (`open-housing`) after the YMCA meeting (`open-housing-2`). Ralph, 143, puts the YMCA meeting on Thursday morning; Branch, 509, puts the mass meeting on Thursday night.
- Aug. 18, Zhou's speech (`china-leadership-11`) after Lin Biao's (`china-leadership-6`), and the delegates' speeches (`china-leadership-17`) after Zhou's. The *Mao Zedong zhuan* (VI, ch. 75) has Lin speak, then Zhou "接着", then Nie Yuanzi and the students. The first of these links draws a warning, because Lin's speech is a part (a subentry) of the rally's entry and Zhou's is not. The warning asks whether Zhou's entry should follow the rally instead. I kept the link to Lin's speech, because Zhou's speech came directly after it.
- Aug. 20, the four links of Johnson's swing: Kingston, then Manchester, Burlington, Lewiston and Portland. Lady Bird Johnson's diary gives the sequence in so many words: "The first real speech of the day," "only thirty minutes before he spoke at a luncheon," "Our next stop," "Our next hop," "We motorcaded to Portland" (412–14).
- Aug. 31, Zhou's "salute" (`china-leadership-9`) after Lin's speech (`china-leadership-8`). MacFarquhar, 114: Zhou "concurred fully."
- Aug. 31, the Xibaituan secretary back from the meeting (`china-leadership-10`) after the commune's meeting (`china-leadership-5`). MacFarquhar, 129, says not all the brigade cadres at that meeting would carry out the order, which is the case of Xibaituan in Yang, II, 666.

Aug. 4 (the Standing Committee meeting after the Cultural Revolution Group's meeting, which Zhou's chronicle, III, 44, confirms; the small groups after the groups hear Mao's speech conveyed); Aug. 5 (Zhou's call after Liu received the Zambians: the line says "had that day received", and Pu, 194 n.13, agrees); Aug. 7 (the poster distributed under the title added at 3 a.m.); Aug. 8 (Mao meets Lin "at once" after the landing, which Yang, I, 224, bears out; the crowds the night the Decision is broadcast); Aug. 9 (the night meeting on the UAW's request after Johnston's talk with Raby); Aug. 18 (Xinhua's report after the rally); Aug. 19 (the night meeting on the injunction after the injunction); Aug. 22 (Yang Jingren receives the Red Guards after they arrive); Aug. 26 (Daxing relays Xie's speech after the speech); Aug. 28 and Aug. 30 (Zhou's acts that the lines say came after Mao's remarks and his own list); Aug. 31 (Ye Jianying meets the Pickets on the tower after they come up).

## Links added: 7, and one field set in place of a link

- Aug. 5, Mao's remarks at the plenum on the document "Bombard the Headquarters" (`china-leadership-14`) after his writing it that day (`china-leadership`). The lines show it: he speaks of the document by name. Pu, 194, dates the remarks Aug. 5.
- Aug. 18, Mao's words to Lin Biao "as they reviewed the parade" (`china-leadership-7`) after Lin's speech (`china-leadership-6`). The *Mao Zedong zhuan* (VI, ch. 75) has the parade pass the rostrum after the speeches, and Mao speak as he reviews it. The remark came directly after the delegates' speeches (`china-leadership-17`), but `patch-check` refuses that link, because Mao's words are a part of the rally's entry and the delegates' speeches are not; a part may follow only another part of the same entry. Lin's speech is a part, so the link names it. A sentence on the basis is added to the note.
- Aug. 21, King's march (`open-housing`) after *Meet the Press* (`civil-rights-organizations`). Branch, 520: King cut short his appearance, went to Liberty Baptist and led the afternoon march. A sentence is added to the note.
- Aug. 25, the foreign affairs bureau's march on the General Staff's Party committee (`china-leadership-16`) after Mao's reception of the Tanzanian delegation (`china-leadership`). Pu, 124: the bureau, on learning that Pan Zhenwu had been kept from the reception, put up its poster at once and marched that afternoon. A sentence is added.
- Aug. 26, Zhang's meeting with the Red Guard heads at Daxing (`china-leadership-14`) after the county bureau's meeting that relayed Xie Fuzhi's speech and put Zhang in charge of liaison (`china-leadership-6`). Yang, II, 664. A sentence is added.
- Aug. 27, the Zhongyue commune's meeting (`china-leadership-10`) after Changping's relay of Xie's speech (`china-leadership-9`). Yang, II, 663: upon the relay "於是" the county began to act. Next, the killings at the Yandan brick works "that evening" (`china-leadership-11`) after the commune's meeting, in the same paragraph. A sentence is added to each.
- Aug. 31, Lin Biao's speech (`china-leadership-8`) comes after Mao's reading of the draft (`china-leadership`), as the draft entry itself says. `patch-check` refuses the link, because Lin's speech is a part of the rally's entry. Instead the patch sets the hour of Mao's reading, 11 a.m. Peking time, from the Wengao's note as the entry already cites it (XVIII, 336). That places the reading before the rally's afternoon.
