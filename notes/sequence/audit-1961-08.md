The transcript: the opening statement praises the schools "desegregated today", and the first question on school aid asks of "the House action today". Basis written into the note of the conference.

The reader could not write the school-aid link from `cuba-bay-of-pigs`. The link belongs on the parent, the news conference, and `patch-check` suggests the same ("follow the parent?"). It is there now.
