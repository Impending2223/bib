# Sequence pass, February 1961 (R25c)

Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1961-02.yaml`, 5 ops. I disregarded nothing under R0. I read the notes of about forty entries (`./bib show`) and opened one source, Peng Zhen's nianpu, IV, 104 (Feb. 19), from the Drive scan; the copy is deleted.

## The links

- Feb. 12: Zhu De's poem "The new city of Sanming" (china-leadership-7) follows his arrival at Sanming and tour of its works (china-leadership-6). Basis: he writes it at Sanming, so after he arrives; the nianpu gives the poem "evening" and the tour no hour. The weakest link of the five: if the audit reads the poem's hour as no order against the tour, strike it.
- Feb. 13: Zhu De's talk with Yang Shangkui at Nanchang (china-leadership-7) follows his arrival there (china-leadership-6). The lines' own words.
- Feb. 23: Zhu De hears the Hengyang committees "on the way to Guangdong" (china-leadership-7) follows his leaving Nanchang (china-leadership-6).
- Feb. 28: Zhou's meeting at 9:30 p.m. on Khrushchev's letter (china-leadership-2) follows Chervonenko's handing the letter to Liu Shaoqi (china-leadership-8). Basis: the entry on Zhou says the meeting is on the letter; the Chen Yun nianpu's note, III, 76, which both notes cite, puts the handing "that afternoon". The Liu entry has no hour of its own, so the site sorted it after the 9:30 meeting.
- Feb. 28: Zhu De's talk with Dong Biwu and Nie Rongzhen at Wenquan (china-leadership-11) follows his arrival there (china-leadership-10); the entry says they were "there before him".

## The days with no link

- Feb. 1: the NSC meeting (defense) and Rusk's Congo paper and its NSC discussion (congo) are two entries on one meeting; the Congo entry holds its own NSC and approval. No order between the two. The Renmin ribao and Nhan Dan editorials follow the agreement signed Jan. 31, not the evening banquet.
- Feb. 2: the Albanian signing (5:30 p.m.) and the reception are placed by their hours.
- Feb. 4: Zhu De's poem "Visiting Mount Gu" and his climb of the mountain have no arrival or other word to put one first; left. Yang's train entries give no order beyond what they say.
- Feb. 6: McNamara's background talk, the gold message and Chen Yun's letter to Zhou have no stated order.
- Feb. 8: the news conference, the Laos meeting and the Hesburgh visit are placed by their hours or already linked (Wofford after Hesburgh, in the tree and right). Mao's talks with Mitterrand and the Zhejiang committee have no order.
- Feb. 10: the Dominican entries: the station's suggestion of the M3 machine gun is from testimony and a cable of Mar. 15 and is not tied to the meeting's day. No link.
- Feb. 14 and 15: the Special Group of Feb. 14 and Rusk's memorandum of Feb. 15 are on different days. The Feb. 15 meeting with the dissidents has no stated place against Rusk's memorandum.
- Feb. 19: Peng Zhen's morning meeting and his written report to the Standing Committee. The nianpu (IV, 104) prints the report after the meeting with a bare "△", and the report restates the meeting's matter. The nianpu's print order alone is no order (R25c); the meeting is already placed by "morning" and the report has no hour, so the site shows them in that order anyway. No link.
- Feb. 22: Zhou's revision of the notice on capital construction and the 10 p.m. Standing Committee meeting on the same plan: the notes give no order. No link.
- Other days: the ranges and hours place the entries (Yang Shangkun's days at Kunming and Chengdu, the Soviet and Albanian delegations' banquets, the Zhou receptions). Entries that answer each other across days (the Lumumba statements, Gizenga cables, Mohamed V's death and the condolence) are not one day.
