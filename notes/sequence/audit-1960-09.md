## The question the audit turned on

Most of the reader's links chain Kennedy's stops in the order the volume prints them. The reader opened no source. The audit of October 1960 found that the volume's printed order is not always the order of the day (`notes/sequence/audit-1960-10.md`; `notes/owner-rulings.md`, 55; R25c). So I read the September texts for anything that shows the order: a stop named as past or next, the part of the day ("this morning", "tonight", "at lunch"), a list of the day's stops, and the volume's notes on stops it has no text for.

On most days the texts agree with the printed order wherever they speak. On three days they do not:

- Sept. 9. The volume prints Turlock after Tulare and Burbank before Bakersfield. The train ran south down the valley line from Stockton, and Turlock lies between Modesto and Merced, a hundred and fifty miles north of Tulare. At Bakersfield Kennedy thanks the crowd "for coming and sending us off in the airplane from Bakersfield to Los Angeles" (p. 188). So Bakersfield came before Burbank, as O'Donnell has it, and Turlock came between Modesto and Merced.
- Sept. 13. The volume's note after Fort Worth says Kennedy "also spoke briefly at Arlington and Grand Prairie" (p. 222), and Arlington lies between Fort Worth and Grand Prairie. The note's place and the route agree, so Arlington comes after Fort Worth and before Grand Prairie. The reader had left Arlington out.
- Sept. 22. The volume prints the Fort Dodge airport rally before the City Square. At the airport Kennedy says, "We are about to depart and go to South Dakota, where I am supposed to speak this afternoon" (p. 316). So the airport rally came after the City Square, just before Sioux Falls.
