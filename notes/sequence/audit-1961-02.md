# Audit of the sequence pass, February 1961 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1961-02.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1961-02.md`. The reader wrote 5 links. I read all 28 days of the month in the order the site now gives them (`day_order.py 1961 --from 1961-02 --until 1961-02 --full`, every day), the American days with the same care as the Chinese. For each link in the patch, and for each pair the lines did not settle, I read both entries in full with `./bib show`, and then the sources. The corrected patch is in place: 8 `follows` ops and 1 `edit` op, the edit adding one sentence to a note that gives the basis of a link. `patch-check` shows 0 errors and 1 warning, because the edit sets only `n` (there is no additive op for a basis sentence; the audits of March 1960 and March 1964 did the same). I disregarded nothing under R0: the brief added nothing that narrows.

## Links checked: 5. Kept: 5. Struck: 0. Turned: 0. Repointed: 0.

- Feb. 12, Zhu De's poem "The new city of Sanming" after his arrival at Sanming and his tour of its works. The nianpu (III, 1797) has him reach Sanming and see the five works, then "△ 晚" the poem. The poem calls the place "this killing ground" (此杀人地), so he wrote it there, after he arrived. The reader called this the weakest of the five. It holds: the arrival is the first act of its entry, and the poem was written at the place reached.
- Feb. 13, Zhu De's talk with Yang Shangkui at Nanchang after his arrival there. The nianpu (III, 1797): "抵达江西省南昌市。和……杨尚奎谈话。" The lines show it.
- Feb. 23, Zhu De's hearing of the Hengyang committees after his leaving Nanchang. The nianpu (III, 1798): he left Nanchang for Guangdong and heard them "途经湖南省衡阳市时", passing through Hengyang. The lines show it.
- Feb. 28, Zhou's 9:30 p.m. meeting on Khrushchev's offer after Liu Shaoqi's talk with Chervonenko. The Chen Yun nianpu's note (III, 76 n.1) has the Soviet Ambassador hand Liu the letter "2月28日下午", that afternoon. Both entries cite that note, and the meeting's entry already says so in its note.
- Feb. 28, Zhu De's afternoon talk with Dong Biwu and Nie Rongzhen at Wenquan after his arrival there. The nianpu (III, 1798): "抵达从化县温泉。下午，和先到这里的董必武、聂荣臻等谈话。"

## Links added: 3

- Feb. 1, the NSC meeting (`defense`) after Rusk's Congo memorandum (`congo`). The Congo entry's line says Rusk "sums it up at the NSC that day", and FRUS XX, doc. 18 (cited there) has him summarize at the meeting "the new policy proposals sent to President Kennedy that day". The memorandum, the Congo entry's first act, was sent before the meeting took it up. The reader had these two as one meeting with no order; the memorandum is not the meeting.
- Feb. 4, Zhu De's poem "Visiting Mount Gu" (`-11`) after his inspection of the front and his climb of Mount Gu (`-10`). The nianpu (III, 1795) has the climb, then "△ 晚，赋诗《游鼓山》": the poem, written that evening, is on the visit of that day. The same kind of link as Feb. 12; if the owner rules against one, both go.
- Feb. 20, Peng Zhen's call to Yang at Chengdu (`-3`) after Yang's haircut and swim (`-4`). The diary (I, 655) writes the morning in the park, then "午睡后去理发，游泳", then the calls, Peng Zhen's among them, then the evening's film. The hours of the page stand in that order and nothing goes against it. This rests on the diary's order alone, the weakest kind of basis (owner-rulings 55), and the call's note now says so.

## Pairs read and left unlinked

- Feb. 4, Yang's day on the train (`-5`, `-6`, `-7`): the diary (I, 651) gives the whole day on the train, the talk, the reading and the rain, with no part of the day for any of them.
- Feb. 6, McNamara's background talk and the gold message; Kennedy with the Joint Chiefs (`defense-2`) and the Joint Chiefs' paper on Laos (`laos`): nothing ties the paper to the meeting.
- Feb. 8, the Cuba meeting (`cuba`), the Laos meeting at 2:45 (`laos`), the Hesburgh visit and the 10 a.m. news conference. Bundy's note (FRUS X, doc. 39) puts the Cuba meeting "this afternoon", after the news conference, which its range already shows; nothing places it against the Laos meeting. The Hesburgh visit has no hour, and its sources disagree on the day (Wofford and Branch, Feb. 7).
- Feb. 8, Zhu De's poem to Xie Juezai's rhymes (`-9`) and his visit to the Gutian reservoir (`-8`): the poem answers Xie's poem, and the nianpu (III, 1795–96) gives it no part of the day.
- Feb. 11, Chen Shaokuan's return call (`-8`) and Zhu De's arrival at Nanping (`-9`): the call was most likely at Fuzhou, before Zhu De left, but the nianpu (III, 1797) does not place it. Likelihood is not an order.
- Feb. 11, Chen Yun's letter to Zhou (`-11`) and his evening return to Peking (`-10`): the nianpu (III, 73) gives the letter no part of the day.
- Feb. 14, the government's statement on Lumumba (`china`) and Zhou's cable to Gizenga (`-2`): no order given.
- Feb. 17, Peng Zhen's evening at Zhou's (`-5`) and Zhou's reception of the Burmese Ambassador (`china-leadership`): both evening, no order.
- Feb. 19, Peng Zhen's morning meeting (`-5`) and his written report (`-6`). The nianpu (IV, 104) prints the report after the meeting with a bare "△" and no part of the day. The print order alone is no basis. I agree with the reader.
- Feb. 22, Zhou's revision of the urgent notice (`-2`) and the evening Standing Committee (`-3`). The Zhou nianpu (II, 392–93) gives the revision no part of the day and the meeting "△ 晚"; the Chen Yun nianpu (III, 75) does not name the notice. I agree with the reader.
- Feb. 26, Kang Sheng's forum (`-4`) against Yang's lunch (`-5`): the diary (I, 657) names the forum only as the reason Yang stayed in.
- The other American days (the messages of Feb. 2, 9, 20 and 23, the news conference of Feb. 15 at 7 p.m., the hearings, the papers and memoranda) have nothing in their lines or the daybook to put one after another of the same day. Replies across days (the Lumumba statements, Mohamed V's death and the condolence, Burke to Felt on the meeting of Feb. 23) are not one day.
