### Struck

- June 11, the decision to give the address (`civil-rights-bill-2`) after the schoolhouse door (`alabama`). The line says Kennedy spoke "as Wallace leaves the doorway," watching it on the Oval Office television. That is an act watched as it happens, which R25c says is of the same hour and no order. The door entry also holds the students' registration, which came after Wallace stood aside. The decision keeps its own record range (after 4:30 p.m. to 8 p.m.), which places it. The rest of the day's chain stands: the drafting after the decision, the address after the drafting, King's telegram after the address.
- June 26, the City Hall luncheon (`berlin-and-vienna-5`) after the Berlin day (`berlin-and-vienna`). The Berlin entry runs from 9:45 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and ends with the Free University speech that afternoon. The luncheon line says new material went into that afternoon speech, so the luncheon came before the end of the entry it was linked after. The reader left the arrival (`berlin-and-vienna-2`) unlinked for the same reason. The departure for Ireland still follows both the Berlin day and the luncheon, and the arrival at Dublin follows the departure.

### Kept, with the basis put in the note

- June 1, Johnson's request to O'Donnell (`civil-rights-bill-2`) after the strategy meeting (`civil-rights-bill`). Whalen, 76: Johnson asked O'Donnell "at the end of the June 1 meeting." The entry did not cite Whalen; the sentence is added to its note.
- June 3, Johnson's call to Sorensen (`civil-rights-bill`) after the leaders' meeting (`civil-rights-bill-3`). Whalen, 76–77: Johnson's silence "at the White House meeting that morning," then Schlei's visit to Johnson's office, then "Next, Ted Sorensen telephoned." Caro's parenthetical in the note already put a 10 a.m. meeting before the call. The Whalen sentence is added to the note.
- June 11, Rusk's cable to Trueheart (`vietnam-saigon`, 11:03 p.m. in Washington) after Thích Quảng Đức's burning (`vietnam`). Moyar, 220, puts the burning "At ten o'clock" that morning in Saigon, about a day before the cable. The burning had no time field, so the build put it after the cable. - June 16, the riot at Xá Lợi (`vietnam-2`) after the joint communiqué (`vietnam`). Moyar, 222: the riot "just a few hours after" the accord was announced. FRUS doc. 177 (Saigon, June 16, 4 p.m.) has the communiqué's text read over the radio that morning and the riot from about 9:15 to 11 a.m. near Xá Lợi. The edit puts FRUS doc. 177 first in the riot's note as its record, adds the Moyar sentence, and replaces the morning range with the hour (about 9:15 a.m., 01:15 UTC, `time_approx`).
- June 28, the Embassy luncheon (`-in-2`) after Cork (`-in`): "We flew back to Dublin for a luncheon" (O'Donnell, 415). The Dublin Castle reception (`-in-6`) after leaving the Dáil (`-in-5`): "The President then hurried to Dublin Castle" (417); the Papers' notes give 5 p.m. for the Castle. De Valera's dinner (`-in-7`) after the Castle: "That evening" (418). Each sentence is added to the later entry's note.
- June 29, Limerick (`-in-3`) after Eyre Square (`-in`): "On the way to Shannon, we made two stops," Galway and then Limerick (O'Donnell, 419–20). Shannon (`-in-4`) after Limerick: the helicopter left "the racecourse park in Limerick for the short hop to Shannon Airport" (421). The Papers' notes put Limerick at 1:30 p.m. and Shannon at 1:15 p.m., which cannot both be right if O'Donnell is. The link follows O'Donnell and the lines, and the edit adds a Check naming the two clock notes to Shannon's note. The reader had rested these two links on the Papers' print order; the print order alone is no basis (R25c), so the basis is now O'Donnell's.
