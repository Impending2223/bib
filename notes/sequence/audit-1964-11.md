## Links checked: 23. Kept: 20. Struck: 2. Pointed at another entry: 1.

### Struck

- Nov. 13, the delegation's departure (`china-leadership`) after the last meeting with the Soviet leaders (`china-leadership-2`). The entry holds two acts: Zhou's reception of the embassy staff, the students and the Dubna Chinese, then the departure that evening. The *Zhou Enlai nianpu* (II, 687–88) gives no hour for the reception, and the dashiji (429) does not list it. Wu Lengxi (878) gives only the evening departure. Nothing places the reception against the meeting, so a link would put an act after the meeting that may have come before it. The departure does come after the meeting. Split the entry under R25b, then link the departure.
- Nov. 23, Peng Zhen's cable to the Japanese party (`china-leadership-9`) after Mao's greeting (`china-leadership-4`). The *Peng Zhen nianpu* (IV, 373) has the cable say "现在，先将我党中央的贺电和我们代表团对大会的贺词发去": the cable sends the Central Committee's greeting, which is Mao's telegram. The greeting and the cable go out together. Nothing places the signing apart from the sending, and the two entries are of one moment.

### Pointed at another entry

- Nov. 15, Johnson's evening with the staff on the talent search (`transition-and-staff-2`) now follows the party for Heller (`transition-and-staff`), not the return to the White House (`succession`). The diary (203): after the welcome, "After shaking hands all around, we went to a party for Walter Heller"; then "Later that evening Lyndon met with some of the staff." The entry the line comes directly after is the party. Basis added to the note.

### Kept, with the basis put in the note

- Nov. 7, Liu's draft of the instruction on the poor peasants' associations (`china-leadership-5`) after Mao's note to Liu on the Hunan request (`china-leadership-10`). MacFarquhar's body text (III, 412) says only that Liu approved the Hunan move on Nov. 12. His note 71 (p. 631) says Liu drafted the approving directive and points to the Mao *wengao* "for Mao's prior approval", which is the note of Nov. 7. The *Liu Shaoqi nianpu* (II, 608) dates the draft Nov. 7 and says nothing of Mao. The basis is the note, cited "III, 412 n.71". The entry's own parenthetical gives "after Mao's approval" to p. 412; that belongs to the note (R35a).
- Nov. 16, Mao's talk with Kim Il Sung (`china-leadership`) after Zhou's talk with Kim at 5 p.m. (`china-leadership-3`). The dashiji (429): "会谈后，陪同金日成首相去毛泽东主席处". The *Zhou Enlai nianpu* (II, 688) has Mao's meeting "晚". The order holds; the time question the reader raised (Wu Lengxi's "afternoon") stays in the entry.
- Nov. 19, King's telegram to Hoover (`king-2`) after the Bimini statement (`king`). Branch (527): the telegram joined King's own tone to his advisers' call for a strong rebuttal, after Wachtel and Rustin had sent word from New York that his statement showed weakness. So the telegram comes after the statement. It is not the draft Wachtel wrote, as the reader supposed. Both entries still carry "Check: the day"; Branch puts the telegram "On Thursday", Nov. 19.
