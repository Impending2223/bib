## The links

- Jan. 2: the Brown Chapel meeting (voting-rights) follows King's morning drive from Atlanta to Selma (king). Branch 552–55: the drive, arrival at the Jacksons', then "later" the entrance to Brown Chapel.
- Jan. 3: He Long's speech to the Congress (china-leadership-3) follows Mao's changes to its draft (china-leadership-2). The draft is for the speech, sent by Zhou Jan. 2; Mao's note is dated "3rd, two o'clock", which reads as two in the morning (not set as a time field).
- Jan. 4: the House convening (congress) follows the Republican conference's choice of Ford as leader (congress-2). The Record has Laird nominate Ford for Speaker "by direction, and by unanimous vote of the Republican conference", so the conference had met before the roll call. The conference's hour is not given; the Record pages I opened do not show the 73–67 vote itself.
- Jan. 5: Peng Zhen at Deng's after the enlarged meeting (china-leadership-5) follows Mao's enlarged Standing Committee meeting (china-leadership). The entry's words are "after the enlarged meeting"; Peng Zhen nianpu IV, 385, puts him at that meeting that afternoon.
- Jan. 12: the state dinner for Satō (japan) follows his arrival and welcome, 11:23 a.m. (japan-and-korea). The Diary, 217–18: the arrival ceremony, then "This evening" the dinner.
- Jan. 18: the Hotel Albert scene (voting-rights-selma-2) follows the morning march to the courthouse (voting-rights-selma). Branch 560–61: the march and the alley, then the integrating of the hotels and restaurants, the Albert last. Selma-3 already follows both.
- Jan. 18: the pre-gala dinners (democratic-party) follow the ladies' reception at 3 p.m. (transition-and-staff-2), and the gala (transition-and-staff) follows the dinners. Diary 222–23: the reception "just before 3", back at the White House, then "the pre-gala dinners", then "arrived at the Inaugural gala on time".
- Jan. 20: the inauguration, 12:02 (transition-and-staff), follows the Interfaith Prayer Service (transition-and-staff-3), and the inaugural luncheon (transition-and-staff-4) follows the inauguration. Diary 224–27: the service at a quarter of 9, "after the service" the plans for the Capitol ceremony, the oath, then "went inside to the Inauguration luncheon". The parade (2:30) already has its range, and the Diary has it "after lunch".
- Jan. 24: the doctors' advice against Johnson's going to the funeral (succession) follows Churchill's death (europe-and-the-alliance). The advice is of the funeral, and the Diary, 231, has him dead "about 3 o'clock this morning" and the doctors calling Mrs. Johnson aside after she wakes at 10.
- Jan. 25: the night meeting at Tabernacle Baptist (voting-rights-selma-2) follows the line to the courthouse (voting-rights-selma). Branch 566: "By Monday morning, January 25" the march and Annie Lee Cooper, then "That night, in the first mass meeting at Tabernacle Baptist".
- Jan. 25: Hang Shangzeng's report at Doumen (china-leadership-3) follows Yang Shangkun's leaving Niujiao at noon (china-leadership). The diary's own words (II, 504, quoted in the note): "when we left the village many of the brigade were going to Doumen for the meeting". A diary is the weakest basis; the words agree with the day's hours and nothing goes against them.
- Jan. 28: Zhou's seeing Subandrio off at the airport (china-leadership-2) follows the signing of the joint statement and the agreements (china-leadership). Subandrio signed for his government (Chen Yi nianpu 1082), so he was in Peking at the signing; both entries are "morning", and the see-off is his departure.

The Jan. 28 entry wants a Check against the Jan. 27 one.
