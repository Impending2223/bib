# Sequence pass, October 1960 (R25c)

Read all 31 days of October 1960 as the site orders them (`day_order.py 1960 --from 1960-10 --until 1960-10 --full`), each day's entries together, asking of each: does it come after another entry of the same day? Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1960-10.yaml`, 152 ops, `./bib patch-check` 0 errors. I disregarded nothing under R0. No source was opened beyond the entries' own notes. Most links rest on one basis: the Senate Commerce Committee's *Freedom of Communications* (Part 1), cited in the notes of Kennedy's campaign stops, prints a day's remarks in the order given, and its page ranges run on without a gap from one remark to the next across the whole month (416 to 840). Where the notes cite its pages, the pages give the order of the stops. Where the lines or notes say more, that is the basis named. Stops with no page in that volume, and the two or three entries a day that are not stops (statements, letters, telegrams), are left out of the chains.
