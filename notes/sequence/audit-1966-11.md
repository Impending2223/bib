## Links checked: 6. Kept: 6. Struck: 0. Turned: 0. Repointed: 0.

- Nov. 1, the Anchorage evening (`elections-1966`) after the last night in Seoul (`vietnam-asian-trip`). Kept. McPherson (314–15) has the party at Walker Hill, "on the last night" the night out with Bundy and Rostow, then the flight to Anchorage, arriving in "the bleak twilight." The stay ran from Oct. 31 to Nov. 2, Seoul time, so the last night is the night of Nov. 1 in Seoul; the party left Seoul on Nov. 2 and, across the date line, reached Anchorage on the evening of Nov. 1, Alaska time. Both are Nov. 1 by the local calendar, and the Seoul night comes first by the clock (about 10 to 15 UTC on Nov. 1, against Anchorage's evening, from 4 UTC on Nov. 2). The link stands on the Seoul entry's day, which keeps its "Check: the day": McPherson does not date the night, and if it was Oct. 31 the link goes.
- Nov. 3, Mao's sixth reception (`china-leadership`) after Zhou's call to the heads of the Red Guard bodies (`-2`): "before the sixth reception" in the line. Kept.
- Nov. 7, McNamara's dinner with Neustadt (`vietnam-2`) after the Mill Street crowd (`students`). Kept. McNamara (ch. 9): the crowd, the tunnels, Kissinger's class, half an hour in a bookstore, then "Later I joined Dick Neustadt ... for dinner." The class and the bookstore are not entries, so the crowd is the entry the dinner comes directly after.
- Nov. 8, Nixon's supper at El Morocco "after the last returns" (`elections-1966-4`) after the night's returns (`elections-1966`). Kept, on the line's words.
- Nov. 10, Zhou's wires on the Anting stoppage (`china-leadership-3`) after the stoppage (`-2`): "told of the Anting stoppage." Kept.
- Nov. 17, Komer's memorandum (`vietnam-3`) after Lodge's 11124 and 11125 (`vietnam-2`). Kept. FRUS IV, doc. 311 opens "Lodge is now in with three long replies (attached)"; its note 4 names the attachments as telegrams 11124 and 11125 of Nov. 17.

## Links added: 3

- Nov. 8, the Cook County returns (`elections-1966-3`) after the Chicago election-day entry (`elections-1966-2`). The poll watchers thrown out and the New Breed member "held under arrest until the polls close" are acts of the voting; the count comes after the polls close. The day sorts an entry by its first act.
- Nov. 21, Li Fuchun's 10 p.m. call to Yu Qiuli (`china-leadership-3`) after Chen Boda's rebuke of Gu Mu and Yu (`-2`). Yang Jisheng, I, 334, cited on both: Chen sends for the two on Nov. 21; they report to Zhou, who says he and Tao Zhu will hear them the next day; then, "晚10點", Li calls Yu on "what you raised" (你講的問題). Without the link the untimed rebuke sorted after the call.
- Nov. 30, Chen Yi at the Workers' Gymnasium (`china-leadership-5`) after Zhou's speech at the collective reception (`-3`). The two are one meeting: the Chen Yi chronology (1176–77) has his speech in the evening to the workers come to Peking, "千言万语一句话 ... 周总理的指示"; the Zhou chronology (III, 94) has the same words from Chen Yi, "就是刚才周总理的指示", the Premier's instruction "just now", so Chen Yi spoke after Zhou.
