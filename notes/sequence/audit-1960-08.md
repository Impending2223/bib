I read every day of the month from the day sheet (`day_order.py 1960 --from 1960-08 --until 1960-08 --full`), each day's entries together, and held each of the reader's links against the two entries in full (`./bib show`) and, where the lines did not settle the order, against the source. So Seaton's remark comes before the farm conference, and the press conference comes directly after the conference, not after Seaton. The press conference now follows the farm conference and Scott (whose broadcast the question calls "today", with no part of the day); the farm conference (elections-1960-kennedy) now follows Seaton.

## Links added

- Aug. 1: china-leadership-2 (Zhou receives the Polish Ambassador at 10 a.m. at Xihuating, in Zhongnanhai) after china-leadership-6 (Zhou back in Peking). The Zhou chronology, II, 336, read: "8月1日 回到北京", then the reception. He could not receive the ambassador in Peking that morning before reaching Peking that day. The day's later acts in Peking are placed by their hours.
- Aug. 11: vietnam-diplomacy-2 (the Hanoi Home Service commentary) after laos-2 (Kong Le's announcement at the afternoon rally). Guan, 156, read: "Kong Lae's announcement was followed by" the commentary. The stored hours already put them in this order, but Guan gives "2:52 P.M." without saying GMT, as he does for the other broadcasts, so the link keeps the order whichever clock is meant.
- Aug. 28: china-leadership (Mao receives Balluku in the Qinzheng Hall, evening) after china-leadership-3 (Zhou at Liu Shaoqi's on the delegation to Vietnam). The Zhou chronology, II, 344, read: at Liu's, then "晚，到毛泽东处开会" (in the evening, to Mao's for a meeting), at which the Albanian delegation was received. The two entries' notes say the same: the Deng chronology, "then at Mao's"; the Peng Zhen chronology, "after Liu's meeting". Both entries carry the same evening range, so without the link they had no order.
- Aug. 30: jobs-and-wages (Kennedy announces the failure of the minimum wage conferees) after elections-1960-kennedy-2 (his remarks to the New York State AFL-CIO). *Freedom of Communications*, 64, read: the remarks say the bill "is now in conference" and still awaits "final action"; the announcement, 57, says the conferees have failed.

- Aug. 6: Kennedy's statements to the Lithuanian-American and Polish-American leaders and the national-origins meeting. *Freedom of Communications*, 4–12, prints each as a meeting at his home, "one of a series", with no hour or order. No link.
- Aug. 11: Zhou "back in Peking" and his seeing Hồ off. The chronology puts the seeing-off under Aug. 10 as "the next day" and does not say where; Yang's diary has it at Beidaihe on the 10th. No link.
- Aug. 12: the Peking papers and the NCNA report and Home Service bulletin. Guan, 157, gives the papers no hour and does not say they followed the bulletins. No link.
- Aug. 14: Kennedy's call on Mrs. Roosevelt and his Hyde Park speech. The speech's text (18) does not mention the call. No link.
- Aug. 23: the telegram to the VFW and the Senate's votes. The telegram says the Senate "is going to vote Tuesday", which reads as written before Tuesday; the volume dates it Aug. 23. If it was sent on the Monday, there is no link within the day. No link.
- Aug. 24: Zhou's report to the six parties (china-leadership) and Liu's symposium (china-leadership-2): very likely one sitting (Yang's diary, 3:30 to 8:30 p.m.), as the second entry's Check asks. No link; see below.

- Aug. 30: the press conference (ended 11:20 a.m.), the dinner at the Grahams', and the failure statement. Nothing in the texts gives their order, beyond the hour of the press conference and "dinner". The remedy is the time fields (below), not a link.
