# Audit of the sequence pass, September 1964 (R25c, R33b)

The patch audited is `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1964-09.yaml`, written by a Sonnet reader, with its note `notes/sequence/1964-09.md`. I read all 30 days of the month in the order the site now gives them (`day_order.py 1964 --from 1964-09 --until 1964-09 --full`, 936 lines), every entry of every day. For each link in the patch I read the two entries in full with `./bib show`. The reader opened no source and wrote 2 links; I read the sources its note named and the others the month's pairs needed. The corrected patch is in place: 6 `follows` ops, 5 `edit` ops and 1 `check` op; `patch-check` gives 0 errors and 4 warnings (each edit sets only `n`, to add an "Order:" sentence, as the audits of July 1968 and October 1964 did). I disregarded nothing under R0.

## Links checked: 2. Kept: 2. Struck: 0. Turned round: 0. Re-pointed: 0.

- Sept. 10, the Stadium inquiry (`baker`) after the cloture vote (`court`). The reader rested on the roll-call numbers, which R25c says are no order alone. The Digest settles it: it gives the cloture vote and the motion to table at the Record's pages 21885–901 and S. Res. 367 with its amendments at 21904–15 and 21920–39 (D471–72). Kept, with an "Order:" sentence in the later entry's note.
- Sept. 21, Westmoreland's message (`vietnam-tonkin-2`) after Sharp's (`vietnam-tonkin`). Marolda, 460, read on the page: n.57 gives Sharp's message as CP 210025Z Sep 1964, n.58 Westmoreland's as COMUSMACV 211422Z Sep 1964. Sharp's was sent some fourteen hours before. Kept, with an "Order:" sentence. The build's kind ranges put them the other way (Westmoreland's Saigon workday before Sharp's Honolulu workday), so the link matters.

## Links added: 4

- Sept. 8, Bundy's memorandum to Johnson (`vietnam-3`) after SNIE 53-64 (`vietnam`). FRUS 1964–68, I, doc. 342: "I also attach at Tab B a Special National Intelligence Estimate which was approved today"; its n.3 names doc. 341, the SNIE. An edit puts the basis in the note.
- Sept. 13, Mao's afternoon talk with Miyamoto at Hangzhou (`china-leadership`) after Chen Yi's farewell to Miyamoto at the airport (`china-leadership-3`). The Chen Yi nianpu, 1049, gives the farewell without an hour, and Chen Yi that evening at the Guinean chargé d'affaires' reception. The Peng Zhen nianpu, already in the Mao entry's note, has Miyamoto fly to Hangzhou in the morning and leave Hangzhou for Guangzhou that evening. Chen Yi could only have seen him off from Peking in the morning. An edit puts the basis in the note. This is the pair the reader left for p. 1049.
- Sept. 26, Chen Yi's banquet for Sihanouk at Guangzhou (`china-leadership-5`) after his welcome at the station (`china-leadership-4`). The lines show it: the banquet welcomes the Prince and his party come to Guangzhou; the welcome is his arrival. The Chen Yi nianpu, 1052, gives both "evening", the station first.
- Sept. 28, the Brown convocation (`program`) after Johnson's landing at Providence (`elections-1964`, 9:30 a.m.). The lines show it: he lands at 9:30 and reaches the convocation seventy minutes late.

## Placed instead of linked: 1

- Sept. 25, El Paso (`latin-america`), Eufaula (`elections-1964`) and Oklahoma City (`elections-1964-3`, already after Eufaula in the tree). At Oklahoma City Johnson says "This morning I stood with the President of Mexico" and, of the dam, "A few hours ago I dedicated the Eufaula Dam" (Pub. Papers 1128). That gives El Paso the morning, not an order against Eufaula, which has no hour. An edit gives El Paso the morning range in Mountain time (6 a.m. to noon, America/Denver, `time_band: morning`), with the words in its note; the build then puts it before the two unplaced Oklahoma entries. No `follows` link. This is the day the reader left for pp. 1117–28.

## Links in the tree

Three stand in September: Sept. 10, the Desoto authorization (`vietnam-tonkin`) after NSAM 314 (`vietnam`); Sept. 25, Oklahoma City after Eufaula; Sept. 28, the Senator and the press bus (`elections-1964-johnson`) after the landing at Providence. All three hold. The Public Papers bear out the second: at Oklahoma City, Eufaula is "a few hours ago."

## Other changes in the patch

- A Check on `cal.1964-09-28.china-leadership-4` (Mao with Sihanouk, Sept. 28, from the Wilson Center's *77 Conversations*, with the Mao nianpu behind "To check" for Sept. 28). The Mao nianpu has no meeting with Sihanouk on Sept. 28. It gives one on the evening of Sept. 27 at the Great Hall, Liu Shaoqi, Wang Guangmei, Dong Biwu and Zhou present, which is `cal.1964-09-27.china-leadership`. The two entries may be one meeting under two dates; the Check names the earlier entry.

## Pairs left without a link

- Sept. 4, Zhou's and Deng's notes on Lü Zhengcao's railway report. The Zhou nianpu, II, 666, and the Deng nianpu, III, 1827, each give only the man's own note; neither names the other's.
- Sept. 8, Bundy's memorandum and the Chiefs' talking paper. FRUS doc. 342 does not name the paper; doc. 343's n.3 dates the Chiefs' views (CM-124-64) Sept. 9, which bears on that entry's day, not on an order.
- Sept. 9, Johnson's ruling on the jets (`vietnam-macv`) and the 11 a.m. meeting (`vietnam`). Cosmas, 136, says only "on 9 September, in a decision not included in NSAM 314"; n.33 cites the messages of Aug. 29 and 31 and a J-3 briefing sheet of Oct. 13. FRUS doc. 343, the meeting's record, does not mention the jets. No link.
- Sept. 20, Mao's meeting with Boumaza (`china-leadership`) and Chen Yi's morning farewell to Boumaza at the airport (`china-leadership-3`). The Mao nianpu puts the meeting at Jinan, on Mao's special train, after he arrived there early that morning (晨). Nothing says whether the delegation flew to Jinan after Chen Yi saw it off or the farewell was from elsewhere. No link.
- Sept. 27, the evening of Sihanouk's arrival: Mao's meeting (Mao nianpu, evening), Liu's banquet, and Zhou's talks with Liu. The Zhou nianpu, II, 670, gives Zhou's reception of Sihanouk and the talks as Sept. 27 to Oct. 7, no hour; the Chen Yi nianpu, 1052, gives Liu and Zhou meeting the Prince that evening. Nothing orders them.
- Sept. 29, Mao with Massamba-Débat (evening, Hall 118), Chen Yi at the evening of arts, and the banquet for Keita: no order in the Mao or Chen Yi nianpu. Khrushchev's talk with Sukarno and his toast at the lunch: only the stenograms' print order, which is no order.
- Sept. 30, Mao with Keita (afternoon), Zhou accompanying Liu to return Keita's call, Zhou at the airport for the Moroccans: no order given.
- Sept. 24, Forrestal to McNaughton "sent on to Bundy" and Bundy to Johnson: Preston, 156–57, as cited, gives nothing to show that Bundy's memorandum followed Forrestal's, and the two may be different Bundys. Not read beyond the lines.
- The reader's other unlinked pairs (Sept. 2 signings, the primaries, Sept. 22 Presidium papers, the Peking receptions sharing the evening band) I read the same way and found no order.
