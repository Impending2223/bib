## The roll calls

Nine of the reader's links ordered one chamber's roll calls of a day by their numbers. R25c says a serial number alone is no order (owner-rulings 55), so I did not rest on the numbers. I held each link to the record of the day: the Daily Digest, which gives the Record's pages for each act, and, where the Digest was not enough, the Record's own pages. On every day I checked, the numbers run in the order of the Record's pages, so in 1968 each chamber's roll calls were numbered as they were taken. One case shows why the pages matter: on July 30 the Digest puts Sprague's confirmation at 24214, after the aid bill, but that is where the Senate prints its executive business; the vote itself is at 24199, before the *Pueblo* amendment at 24206, as the numbers say. Each link that rests on the record now has a sentence in the later entry's note, "Order: after ...", with the pages and the Digest or Record linked.
