# Sequence pass, May 1966 (R25c)

Read all 31 days of May 1966 as the site orders them (`day_order.py 1966 --from 1966-05 --until 1966-05 --full`), each day's entries together, asking of each: does it come after another entry of the same day? Patch: `inbox/2026-11-25-sequence-1966-05.yaml`, 10 ops (11 links), `./bib patch-check` 0 errors. I disregarded nothing under R0. I read the entries' own words and notes (`./bib show`); no source was opened. The one source I tried, the Wilson Center Digital Archive's page for the Romanian transcript of the Hanoi talks, was refused (a 403 on digitalarchive.umd.edu, a proxy 502 on digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org); I did not go round it.

## The days

- May 1 and 2: no link. The Chinese entries are placed by ranges or are unplaced with nothing to say which came first; Zhou's instruction to the press photographers (china-leadership) is probably before the festival, but the line gives no hour.
- May 3: china-leadership (Zhou at Daqing, hears reports in the evening) after china-leadership-2 (Zhou flies with the delegation to Harbin, the morning). Basis is the route and the second entry's note: the flight to Harbin comes first, and Daqing is reached from there. The Daqing entry's note records that the Waijiao huodong puts the delegation at Harbin on May 3 and does not name Daqing, so the link holds only if the nianpu's Daqing day stands. No link between the Alabama entry (the Lowndes County meeting) and the elections-1966 entry that summarizes it.
- May 4: china-leadership-6 (Liu approves Deng's notice) after china-leadership-5 (Deng's proposal, which it approves) and china-leadership-8 (Peng Zhen tells Liu and Deng he will stop work). Liu's own words in -6: "Today Comrade Peng Zhen has told me and Xiaoping." No link among Zhou's Harbin entries (-2 at Daqing and the Heilongjiang committees party that evening, -3 the factories, -4 the banquet at 19:00–22:00): none says which came first.
- May 5: no link. Mao's reception of the Albanian delegation at Shanghai (china-leadership) is given as the afternoon in its note, and Zhou's flight to Shanghai (china-leadership-2) as noon, but -2 is the whole trip to the 7th, so "after -2" would be wrong for the entry as a whole. Deng's trip (-6) has no hour. The entry china-leadership has no time field though its note says "afternoon" (R25a).
- May 6: no link. china-leadership-7 and -2 (both Zhang Chunqiao at the enlarged Politburo, the more than 10,000 letters) look like one speech; no order between them.
- May 7: no link. vietnam-hanoi-3 (a pause in the afternoon sitting) is part of the sittings vietnam-hanoi-2 records, not after them.
- May 8: no link.
- May 9: no link. vietnam-hanoi-2 (the first restricted meeting) and vietnam-hanoi (the fourth meeting, 9:30): the restricted meeting's note has Ionita to settle the battalion with Giáp "after lunch", which suggests the morning, but neither entry says which meeting came first; the transcript would. Left for a reader with the page.
- May 10: vietnam (the 557th NSC, 5:45 p.m.) after vietnam-2 (the NSC's briefing paper on the rules of engagement for Cambodia): a briefing paper is before its meeting; Gibbons's note 18 dates it May 10 and the paper's note says "See" the meeting. No link for vietnam-air-war-2 (Harriman to Rusk): its note points to the meeting but the line does not say it was written for it. No link between vietnam-diplomacy and -2 (Mezincescu's two exchanges of information; the lines give no order) or between Zhou's farewell banquet and the opera (china-leadership, -5, both 19:00–22:00).
- May 11: no link. Peng Zhen tells Li Xuefeng what to attend to on taking over (china-leadership-4) and Li leads the work group in (-5): the line does not say which came first.
- May 12: china (the Defense Ministry spokesman's protest) after vietnam-air-war (the MiG fight over Yunnan, which the protest is about). Both are May 12; a protest is after the act it protests.
- May 13 and 14: no link. The ranges place the Chinese entries; the Peking University entries of May 14 (Cao Yiou's team, Song Shuo's meeting) give no order.
- May 15: china-leadership-4 (the Center sets up the Capital Work Group) after china-leadership-2 (Zhou and Ye report jointly proposing it, with the same head, deputies and line to the Standing Committee). No link in the Vietnam group beyond those already in the tree: vietnam-struggle-5 (the meeting, 2:35 p.m.) put "the reply to Trí Quang" on its agenda, so the reply (vietnam-struggle-2, 3453) may follow it, but 3453 is numbered before 3455, which is "after the meeting", and the numbers alone are no order; 3450 (-3) is likewise unplaced. A reader with Gibbons, IV, 316–18, should settle both. The existing links hold.
- May 16: china-leadership-5 (Peng Zhen, "having read the May 16 Circular", speaks to his daughter) after china-leadership (the meeting adopts the Circular). Rusk's 3470 (vietnam-struggle-2) may come after the 12:20 meeting (-4); its note points to the May 15 cable, not the meeting; no link. china-leadership-7 (Liu on "not one word changed") may be the discussion at the adoption; no link.
- May 17: china-leadership-4 (Deng Tuo takes his own life, night) after china-leadership-12 (his last letter to his wife), which already follows -11 (the letter to Peng Zhen and Liu Ren). Both letters are written before the act. Pu Weihua (108) puts the act before dawn May 18; the entry is dated the 17th as it stands. No link between program (the Hirshhorn agreement signed) and program-2 (the Rose Garden, noon): the signing may be at the ceremony, but no note says so.
- May 18: no link. Walt on the bridge (vietnam-struggle) and the depot (vietnam-struggle-2, "evening"): Westmoreland's pages (173–74) are not clear on the order. china-leadership-2 and -3 (Zhou's report to Mao on the garrison, Mao's "do as proposed") look like one event, the first undated ("mid-May"), the second May 18.
- May 19: china-leadership-5 (the Peking University committee conveys the May 16 Notice that evening) after china-leadership-4 (the Center issues it as Zhongfa 267, to county and regiment level). A document is issued before it is conveyed; the first is unplaced and sorts after the second without the link. No link between Rusk's 3524 and Bundy's 3545 (vietnam-struggle, -2): the numbers are no order. No link among the Matsumura entries (Zhu De's meeting, Liao's talks, Chen Yi's farewell banquet at 19:00–22:00): no order in the lines.
- May 20: no link. The Vietnam entries are placed by hours, ranges or have none and no order in the lines.
- May 21 and 22: no new link. The existing links (vietnam-struggle-2 after vietnam-struggle; china-leadership-6 after -2 of May 22) hold. Johnson's news conference (vietnam-struggle-7, "see" vietnam-struggle) has no hour in the line, so no link to the Moyers call at 10:10 or the Fortas call.
- May 23: china-leadership-3 (Tian Jiaying takes his own life, the morning) after china-leadership-8 (his last talk with He Jun at about 8 a.m.): "last talk" and the act. china-leadership-7 (Li Xuefeng announces the Center's notice at the Peking Hotel) is left unlinked to the Politburo's decision (-2): the Center decided on the reorganization on May 10, so the notice need not be that decision.
- May 24: no link. The Case Examination Committee (china-leadership), the Center's Explanation (-3) and Chen Boda's speech (-2) give no order in the lines.
- May 25: china-leadership-8 (over a thousand posters follow Nie Yuanzi's) after china-leadership (the poster goes up, 14:00). The second entry's own words: "the day the poster goes up, over a thousand posters follow it." -9 (the committee's emergency meeting "that night") is placed by its range after the poster already.
- May 26 and 27: no link. Lansdale's two papers of May 27 (vietnam-3 "The SLO Role" and vietnam-4 to Bundy) point to one another and say nothing of order.
- May 28 to 31: no link. The Chinese entries are placed by hours or have none and say nothing of order; Zhou's report on the Capital Work Group's first meeting (May 31, china-leadership-2) is a later day than the meeting itself (May 26).
