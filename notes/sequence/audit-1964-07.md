## Links checked: 24. Kept: 22. Struck: 2. Turned round: 0.

### Struck

- July 2, the propaganda officials' meeting of cultural cadres (`china-leadership-5`) after the Standing Committee's decision to form the five-man group (`china-leadership-10`). MacFarquhar, III, 387, tells the meeting as the officials' answer to Mao's comment of June 27 ("Again the propaganda officials swung hastily into action, summoning another meeting of cultural cadres on 2 July"), and p. 388 has Mao suggest the small group only "at the beginning of July", with no day. Nothing on the page puts the meeting after the Standing Committee. The reader had called this the weakest link of the month.
- July 11, Zhou's signing of the joint communiqué with Burma (`china-leadership-2`) after his two talks with Ne Win (`china-leadership`). The Zhou nianpu, II, 656, says only "同日，签署中缅两国联合公报" (the same day); the dashiji, 413, gives the talks at 8 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. and says of the communiqué only "访缅期间，发表了中缅联合公报" (during the visit). The reader's basis, that a communiqué concludes the talks, is a likelihood, not an order.
