## The readers' open points

- Row 13. The Record of Feb. 25, 1960 (House), read on the page image of PDF page 8, printed 3490: Green's words are on 3490, not 3491, and the Record has a sentence between her two questions ("Has the President ever heard of the fact that some of these waterways border on more than one State?"), which the line dropped without a mark; it also prints "home State" and "Delaware River." An `edit`, `kind: correct`, base `baa29c8e`: the line's quotation takes "..." between the questions and the Record's capitals; the note's link goes to 3490 (`#page=8`) with the sentence "The Record has Green's words on 3490, not 3491, and a sentence between the two questions."; the map's row moves to the new link, for the whole line.
- Row 128. Fursenko and Naftali's n.29 (printed 593–94), attached at 345 to the sentence on the Albanian congress, says the Albanians attacked Khrushchev at their congress in February before a Soviet delegation headed by Pospelov. Its own text bears "the Albanian party's congress attacks Khrushchev before a Soviet delegation under Pospelov," not "The Presidium" or the order of the two. The protocol (I, 493) has Pospelov's report on the delegation, its tactics approved, and no attack.

The parenthetical "(the directive named)" says what the note bears; the cite stands. The record, the Center's letter (XIV, 225–27), is already cited on the part, its parenthetical excluding the printing.
- Row 193. Yang's n.2 (II, 3, image) says the May conference, held May 21–June 12 at Peking, had as its chief business the revision of the draft regulations for the rural communes, dropping the mess-hall and supply rules. It bears what the revision was and where it was done, not what the Secretariat did on May 8 (that is the body). The record the note names, the revised draft of June 15 (XIV, 385), cannot bear a meeting of May 8: choice 3.
