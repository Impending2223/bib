Patch `inbox/2026-11-25-nyt-cleanup-b.yaml`, corrected in place; dating rows `notes/nyt/dating-b.tsv`, corrected. Audited Nov. 25, 2026 (Opus). `./bib patch-check` ends with 0 errors; its warnings are the "sets only n" notices and the duplicate flags on new entries, for the merger. A scratch copy with the patch merged was read with `./bib claims show` for every entry that takes new words.

## What was read

All 18 clippings of the July 4 paper and all 40 of the July 5 paper were read as images, with an OCR pass (tesseract) only to find items. From the July 3 paper the Congo, Cuba (Szulc; "Cuban Seizure Studied"), Nepal and Algerian items were found and read; from the July 6 paper, Handler's "Khrushchev Looks to Reds' Triumph" and Phillips's jam-session story. Not read on a clipping: the July 3 items "Immigration Bill Voted," "Hussein Pleads for Arab Unity" and "Eisenhower Golfs," whose cites stand as the earlier readers gave them. Nothing on Drive was changed; the copies were deleted.

## For the merger

- Cleanup a was merged before this audit ended, and it already holds this patch's July 4 and 5 wording for the nine entries the two patches share (`cal.1960-07-01.cuba`, `cal.1960-07-02.china-leadership-2`, `congo`, `cuba-3`, `europe-and-the-alliance-france-3`, `immigration`, `latin-america-3`, `middle-east`, `white-house`). This patch's nine `edit` ops on them are therefore dropped, and the tree keeps a's July 3 parts. The additive ops on those entries (add-words, src, names, the Nepal Check) stay. They pass patch-check against the merged tree.
- The note parser splits an item at "; " inside a title ("Congress Passes Cuba Sugar Bill; Quits till August", "Dutch on Alert in New Guinea; Troops Guard Against Indonesia"; cleanup a has the same). Where such an entry gets footnotes, the first item shows cut. This is a fault in `claims.note_parts`, not in the cites.
