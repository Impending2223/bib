## The reader's doubts

- **The two cites on `cal.1960-01-06.congress`.** The `edit` is the right op. A `cite` op refuses anything but a work's card, and a `primary` op puts its item at the head of the note, ahead of the Congressional Record. That is against R26. An `add-words` with `text:` adds no cite when the issue's link is already in the note. So only an edit puts the paper after the Record. The base `ba2c8294` is the entry's current rev, so it is fresh. As the reader wrote it, though, the edit let Baker and "The Man of the House" stand for the whole line. Neither gives the Senate's count, Nixon in the chair, or the leaders. So I added parentheticals naming the parts each item bears out (R32). I also cited "Washington Proceedings", which gives the Senate's resolution, the swearing-in of Brunsdale and Kyl, and the hours. The `add-words` misread its item. "The Man of the House" says Rayburn was "in his sixteenth year as Speaker", not at its start. Baker gives a three-hour sitting. The words now follow the items and add the Senate's resolution.
