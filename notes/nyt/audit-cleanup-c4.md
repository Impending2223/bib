## What was read, and what was not

The clippings of these four days are not on the Drive. I searched the folders of the shared folder by name and by date of upload, and searched the text of the Drive for heads and words of these stories; nothing of these days came back. The folder named "NYT 1960-01-07" (41 screenshots, uploaded Nov. 16) is what its name says: I fetched all 41 and read the top of each. They are the paper of Thursday, Jan. 7, 1960 (the TimesMachine page shows that date; the datelines read "Jan. 6"). They are not a mislabelled copy of any day in this patch. No pass has read that folder yet; it is a day of its own, still to be read. The folder "NYT 1960-09-09" holds TimesMachine PDFs of Sept. 9, 1960, also another day. The Jan. 6, 1968 clippings were screenshots sent in the session of Nov. 14 and were never put on the Drive. TimesMachine and nytimes.com refuse this machine.

So no cite was checked against a clipping. Each cite was checked against the note it was made from: the entry's note as the audited passes of Nov. 12 and 14 left it, written then from the clippings. No place was set from a clipping, and no words were moved that the old notes did not already hold.
