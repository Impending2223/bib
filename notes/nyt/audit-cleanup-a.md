# Audit: NYT cleanup A (inbox/2026-11-25-nyt-cleanup-a.yaml)

Nov. 25, 2026. Opus audit of the Sonnet reader's patch that put the entries of the *New York Times* of July 3, 1960 into the form of R26a. Brief: `notes/briefs/nyt-cleanup-audit-a.md`.

## What was read

The owner's 56 screenshots of the paper of July 3 (Drive folder 1960-07-03, `inbox-2026-11-23.tsv`) were fetched and run through OCR to find each item. The items that bear on a change below were then read on the image itself: page 1 (Truman, Jorden, Olsen, Szulc, Handler), the Szulc runover on page 15 with the Esso section, the Hill and Kennedy items and the tornado, the Pentagon secrecy item, the Senate confirmations, Loan Fund, NAACP and Gravel items, the Soviet tankers and Harlem items, the Auriol runover with Panama and El Salvador, the California G.O.P. item, and the Schmeck and Elbrus headlines. For the other items the OCR text was checked against the cite: the author, the title and the dateline. The OCR text does not show the "Hussein Pleads for Arab Unity" item, and it was not found on an image; that cite stands as the reader wrote it. The papers of July 4 and 5 were not read: their cites are cleanup b's work.
