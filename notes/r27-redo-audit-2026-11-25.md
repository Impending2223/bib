# The R27 redo, audited, Nov. 25, 2026

The audit of `inbox/2026-11-25-r27-redo.yaml` (176 edits on notes) and `inbox/witnesses/2026-11-25-r27-redo.yaml` (59 amendment rows), against R27 as it stands, R22, R4 and R54a. The patch and the witness file were corrected in place. Nothing was merged, built or committed.

## What was checked

- Every op was compared, sentence by sentence, with the entry as it now stands in the tree. 174 ops only add sentences ("Made public", "Printed for internal circulation", "Circulated in China") or change the year of "Made public". Two did more: they were built on an older text of the entry.
- Every "Made public" year was set against the year of every work the entry cites (read or behind "To check:"), leaving out the works typed as internal or as circulated printings.
- Every work the reader marked "marking not seen" was looked for on the Drive and in the CiNii Books catalogue. The Drive holds none of them except the Liu Shaoqi chronology of 1996 (vol. 2), whose copyright page was read.
- *Current Background* no. 891 was dated from a citation that prints its date.
- The 59 witness rows were checked against the rows they amend, in the pending readers' files.
