## The points the brief named

- MAC 1858 is kept on Feb. 9 with Saigon's zone. FRUS prints the cable as "Saigon, February 9, 1968, 1633Z," and R8 dates a paper by its own date. A record would have to settle a change beyond doubt (R22), and none does: the text speaks of "today" and "tomorrow" as if written on the 9th. The note now says that 1633Z is 11:33 a.m. Feb. 9 in Washington and 12:33 a.m. Feb. 10 in Saigon. A Check names the Saigon day.
- JCS 1590 is kept on Feb. 8, 7:21 p.m., Washington. This follows the tree's practice for a Washington cable whose date-time group falls on the next day in Greenwich time: JCS 7729 of 050043Z is dated Aug. 4, 1964. Drea's text puts both of Wheeler's cables on Feb. 8, and so does his note 35. His notes 37 and 39, and FRUS's note 2, give Feb. 9, which is the date in Greenwich time. The note says so.
- Two cites were wrong. Barrett, 115, quotes JCS 1529, not JCS 1590: Barrett stays on the new entry for JCS 1529 and is struck from the one for JCS 1590. Clifford's note 12 gives the quoted words to JCS 01589 of Feb. 9: that is now a Check. Westmoreland, 351, was added as a cite. The reader's pin "Drea, 183 n.36" was wrong: it is now 182 and 182 n.41 on the Westmoreland entry, and 182 and 181 n.35 on the Wheeler entries.
- The reserve call-up of Feb. 13. FRUS VI, doc. 74, note 2, gives JCSM 96–68, sent on Feb. 13, as 46,000 men at once and 137,000 readied. Cosmas, 157, gives 46,300 and 137,000. Drea, 183, gives 44,000 and 138,000 "at the luncheon," and assigns 46,300 and 137,000 to JCSM-99-68 of Feb. 15. At the luncheon, by doc. 74, Wheeler said the Chiefs had no recommendation yet. JCSM 96–68 was already in the tree, in `cal.1968-02-13.vietnam-205000-4` (Cosmas). The reader's new entry was therefore struck. Its sources went onto `-4` as a cite of FRUS, a variant for Drea, a Check, and a `follows` after the luncheon. `-4` also held the Chiefs' act of Feb. 15; that was split out as `cal.1968-02-15.vietnam-205000`. The Feb. 12 line lost its Feb. 13 and Feb. 15 figures.
- Fletcher. Califano's text puts the day at Aug. 23 (Aug. 21, then "the next day," then "the next morning"). His notes 17–19 cite the President's Daily Diary and the Daily Guide of Aug. 24. "A week later, on September 6" would make it Aug. 30. The Diary could not be fetched (discoverlbj.org answered 403; lbjlibrary.net 522), so nothing settles the day. Aug. 23 is kept, with a Check on all three entries of the episode. The Diary and the Guide were added under "To check:". Two further acts on the same page were entered: Johnson naming Fletcher to Califano that morning, and the jet taking Fletcher back the next morning (Aug. 24). A Check was also added on whose clock "about eleven" is.
- Resolution 242. The resolution's own text bears parts 3, 4 and 5. It also bears the first three items of part 6 (navigation, refugees, the Special Representative), but not U Thant's naming of Jarring on Nov. 23. So the narrowing to 3, 4 and 5 stands. A `src` op names the words of part 6 that the resolution bears, so the merge will cut there. FRUS XIX, doc. 534, is Kosygin's undated letter: it does not bear the Council's vote, yet its row still stands under all six parts. That is left for the map's next reader.
- The five rows that named the wrong id were confirmed. The Civilian Service Awards are `cal.1967-08-16.vietnam-support`; Fletcher is `cal.1967-08-23.states-and-cities`; Trại Thủy is `cal.1968-01-29.vietnam-tet-4`; the 44,000 figure now sits on `cal.1968-02-13.vietnam-205000-4`; the Ripon minutes are `cal.1968-02-27.republican-party`.
- The two items that waited. For the Embassy's American clock, the Drive held Wirtz's *Tet Offensive*. On p. 229, from MACV J-2's daily journal, Wirtz has the wall breached at 0247, the defense almost out of ammunition at 0636, and the compound secured by 0900. The MACV history (1968, II, 899) gives the assault no hour; it has a helicopter driven off at 0500, the landing on the roof at 0810, and the grounds secured by 0900. These now stand as a variant and a Check on `cal.1968-01-31.vietnam-tet-11`. Oberdorfer's *Tet!* is on archive.org as a restricted loan, and its search refused this machine. Braestrup's *Big Story* is neither on the Drive nor on archive.org. Both were put under "To check:".
