# The audits' leftovers, gathered Nov. 26, 2026

The table is `notes/leftovers/2026-11-26.tsv`: 1369 rows, one an item, under the header `id month kind what source from`. It covers 1248 entries and 71 missing entries (id `new`; the day is the first words of `what`). It gathers what 128 audit notes left for a later pass, so that readers can be given it by month. Nothing in `lists/` or `sources/` was changed, and nothing was merged, built, committed or pushed.

## How it was made

Each note was read for what it leaves: the sections headed "What is left", "Left for a later pass", "What else the reading showed", "For the merger", "Left for an edit", "Not read, and why", "Events found and not entered", "Entries that hold more than one event", and the like. Pairs a note read and left unlinked for want of any ordering source are not work for a later pass, so they are not in the table. Nor are the "ops kept", "ops struck" and "rows left with no field, and why" lists.

Each item was checked against the tree as it stands. Where the audit named an entry only by its place or day, the entry was found by the day and its words (253 rows); the `what` column says what was meant, and a reader should confirm the entry before editing. The item was left out where the tree already shows it done (the 59 rows listed below). A split, a duplicate, a day, a variant or a cite was left in unless the entry shows it plainly done.

Two kinds are beyond the ones the brief lists. `order` is a pair whose order only a page not yet read can settle. `variant` is two sources that disagree, to be recorded as a variant (R41). Both are things the audits left for a later pass.

## Notes for whoever gives out the batches

- The splits (R25b, R17) and the possible duplicates are the heavy items, and a split changes ids and links, so it should be done in the same batch as the hours and zones of the same entry. The hours and zones are light.
- The `thread` rows are placings the thread audits left as close calls or found outside their batch. They are the owner's to rule, or for the session, and are not reading work. They are in the table so that nothing is lost.
- The `cite`, `map-row` and `registry` rows from the via-read audits are narrow edits of a claim map or a link and need no new reading, except the rows marked "not read", which name a record the series does not hold or could not fetch.
- Ids that no longer match their dates (`cal.1960-09-08.elections-1960`, `cal.1963-06-20.civil-rights-bill`, `cal.1965-12-27.vietnam-2` and the like) are `day` rows. An id is never changed, so the item is the entry's date and its Check, not a rename.
- Under R0 the brief and every audit note were read for what they leave undone, and nothing was left out because a note called the item minor, a lead, partial, for the owner or "not this pass's". What was disregarded is each audit's own limit on its scope ("not a sequence matter", "no op rests on it", "not mended"), since each such limit only marks what that audit did not do.
