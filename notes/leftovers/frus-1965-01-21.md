## The date and the hour

FRUS: the source text lists Jan. 22; the editors, from the President's Daily Diary, give Jan. 21, 10:07 a.m.–12:45 p.m. Gibbons: Jan. 22, 10:00 a.m.–12:40 p.m. (his note 101 cites the notes of Jan. 22). Branch: "Friday morning" (Jan. 22). Logevall and Mann: Jan. 21. Mrs. Johnson, Jan. 21: a meeting of the Leadership at 10, in the Yellow Room, the House leadership and the committee chairmen, on the legislative program. The entry keeps Jan. 21 and 10:07 on the editors' reading of the Diary, with a Check that sets out each. The Diary is not in the daybook for these days (the Catalog has 50 days) and discoverlbj.org refuses this host, so it is under "To check" and the record is not seen. Two things the record here does not settle: whether Mrs. Johnson's 10 a.m. meeting is the same sitting (her list shares McCormack, Albert, Boggs, Humphrey, Busby, Valenti, Moyers, Cater and O'Brien with the notes' list, but has the House chairmen, who are not in it) or a second meeting at the same hour; and Gibbons's Jan. 22 hours. The Senate chairmen's meeting is dated by Gibbons to "that afternoon" and to the Diary Backup of Jan. 22; its entry carries a Check on the day.
