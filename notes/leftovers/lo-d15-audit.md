## A fault in every map the reader wrote

Every `edit` that set both `c` and `src` stored as `src.c` the hash of the line with its cut marks in it. The tree stores the hash of the line without them. Merged as written, all 20 of those maps would have been stale. Each `src.c` is now the hash of the bare line (`claims.chash` of `cuts.strip`). The same check found the tree's map on `cal.1965-07-28.defense` already stale; the edit there now sets a fresh one.

## The clocks

The Chinese hours of events in China are Peking's. Every op in the patch already set `Asia/Shanghai` for them, and none carried a Check, so there was nothing to drop. The reader added seven Checks ("the book does not say whose clock") on Zhou's hours at Rawalpindi, Cairo and Alexandria. They were struck. The reader's brief, R25, takes an act's hour to be its place's clock unless the source names another. The chronology's hours fit the local clock on each day's itinerary: an 8 a.m. arrival at Rawalpindi after a morning departure from Peking the day before, and a 5 p.m. arrival at Bucharest after a morning stop at Karachi. The Bucharest notes no longer say "the book not saying whose".
