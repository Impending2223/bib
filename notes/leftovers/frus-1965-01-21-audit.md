## The edit of cal.1965-01-21.vietnam: what was checked and changed

Each sentence of the line was read against doc. 30. Who said what, the order of speakers, and every figure match the notes, except for these changes:

- The `kind: correct` on Dirksen's words is struck. The reader changed "Dirksen asks why not bring the dependents home" to "cannot understand why". The notes end Dirksen's remark with a question, "Why do we have to send all our civilization to war". Branch (563) quotes it as a question ("he asked"). So no record settles the change (R22). The old words are back, word for word, and the note's sentence about it is gone. The edit now only adds parts and states no kind.
- The attendance sentence is back in its place after part 5, with the rest of those present (part 19) straight after it. Before, it sat at the end of the line.
- Johnson's interruption of Rusk, on allies who hold back aid, now comes before Rusk on Hanoi. That is its place in the notes.
- These are now in the line: Johnson's ratio of about 5 to 1 (part 21) and "The war must be fought by the South Vietnamese ... count on their fighting their war" (part 22). Both are in the notes, and Gibbons and Logevall cite the second. Also added: McCone kept in government; "Never in recent history has there been greater willingness" to weigh foreign policy and defense by the national interest; the Pathet Lao movement into the Panhandle; the antiaircraft guns at Ban Ken; the semi-covert nature of the Laos flights.
- "Hopefully ... 87,000" is now "hoped up by 87,000", not a plain forecast.
- A new part (20) says what else was talked about, taken from the editors' brackets. The reader's list in the note had left out West New Guinea, Burundi, the Sudan and the Alliance for Progress. The note now says that FRUS gives these topics in brackets only: the editors left them out, not Bromley Smith.
- "The text is attached to the notes, not printed" is about the record, so it moved from the line to the note.
- The note no longer says "no answer to Ford's question is recorded". It now says FRUS prints no answer, and that FRUS gives the talk after his question only in brackets. An answer may be in the part FRUS left out.

Changes to the note, the map and the fields:

- "To check: Bromley Smith minutes ... (cited by Branch, 563)" is gone. That record is the same notes that FRUS prints as doc. 30. - Made public: Gibbons, III (1988, the GPO printing); in full, FRUS (1996).

The Logevall paragraph now follows his words more closely ("in full swing", not "began") and adds his points on Geneva and the fourteen-power conference and on Mansfield's silence. Mann gains his "not so much to inform, but to inflame".
- Two Checks added: Branch gives the allies' fear to Rusk; Logevall makes the war "must be fought" quotation Johnson's closing words. - Pearson is named and tagged. Vandenberg has no name entry.
