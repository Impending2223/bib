## The owner's question

The owner asked, Nov. 27, 2026, whether the May 29 entry on the broken windows is rightly filed under the Poor People's Campaign. The audit keeps it there, with `also: [court]`. CQ files the arrests under the Campaign and says "the campaigners" protested "in behalf of the American Indian contingent"; McKnight puts the demonstration in the Campaign's week. The Indians who protested were the Campaign's own contingent, and their spokesman, Hank Adams, spoke for them again at the Press Club on June 3. There is no thread for Indian affairs. This is a question for the owner; the session should record it in `notes/owner-rulings.md` (this audit did not touch that file).
