The patch `inbox/2026-11-27-lo-d08.yaml` was audited against its pages and corrected in place. Each old note now says in a sentence with no link that the other act "is entered on its own", naming the source, and the new entry points back. To take a work out of an old note I used the rule of Nov. 27 in `notes/guide/changing.md`, "Splitting an entry": the same patch adds the new entry and cites the work there, so patch-check counts the drop as a move. Fursenko, MacFarquhar and Sorensen 293 left the Clay entry this way, Yang's diary II, 130 left the flight of Mar. 3, and MacFarquhar, Peng Zhen and the Chen Yun nianpu left the return of Mar. 18. The *Mao Zedong zhuan* stays on the return, since its text, ch. 69, has the three men back on Mar. 18.
