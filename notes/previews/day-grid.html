The day grid inside the year and month navigator

Mock-ups only; the links go nowhere. The page being read is July 1960, the day July 14 (32 entries, the month's busiest). Real entry counts for July 1960.

The navigator runs on above and below. The year 1960 opens; the row holding July opens again into July's weeks, Sunday first, the day being read boxed. The grid starts under the months, past the year column, so it reads as unfolding out of the row above.
