The day grid for a whole page

Mock-ups; links go nowhere. Two real pages: a long one, Cal. 1960, first half (Jan. 6–June 30, 1960; day being read Mar. 15), and a short one, Cal. 1964, third quarter (July–Sept. 1964; day being read Aug. 7, the Tonkin resolution). Entry counts are the calendar's own. Each grid unfolds under the navigator row holding the page's last month; the navigator carries on below.

A. One continuous grid of the page's weeks

Weeks run straight through, Sunday first; a week that straddles two months shows both. Each month's name sits in the left column on the week it begins, its first day bold, and alternate months are faintly shaded so the boundaries read.
