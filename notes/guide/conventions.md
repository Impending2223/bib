# National conventions

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

`elections/conventions.yaml`, kept by hand (header there: the schema), one block a convention (`1968-R`): the Call's apportionment, State by State, from the convention's *Official Report of the Proceedings*; how and when each delegation was chosen, from the source its `methods` names, with that source's Check; and the roll calls as the Proceedings tabulate them. `tools/bib/conventions.py` renders them (STYLE notes there); `drawQ` in `templates/congress.html` draws the maps, as for the primaries.

- A calendar entry tagged `conventions:<YYYY>-<R|D>` (the opening, beside `primaries:`) carries the delegations: a summary, a map by how chosen (primary; the districts in a primary, at large otherwise; district and State conventions; a State convention; a State committee; not found), a map by the first-ballot leader, and the table (each delegation's first-ballot vote, its votes, the Call's at large and districts, the bodies and dates of choosing, a primary's date linked to its day). Its `gloss` sets out the Call's formula, quoted and cited by page.
- An entry tagged `rollcall:<YYYY>-<R|D>-<ballot>` (the nomination) carries that roll call by State: the first tabulation, the final where the delegations changed their votes after the roll, the Proceedings' starred notes, the printed totals. Now Miami Beach, 1968: `pres1` (Aug. 7) and `vp` (Aug. 8). No procedural roll call was taken there: the credentials report and the platform went by voice vote.
- `check` fails where the Call's at large and districts do not make a State's votes, the States do not add to the Call's total, a State's ballot does not add to its votes, or the columns do not add to the printed totals.
- Sources and access: the Republican Proceedings of 1968 open on archive.org (`sim_republican-national-convention- proceedings_august-5-8-1968`; 1972 on too); 1964 both parties, Bain and Parris's *Convention Decisions and Voting Records*, CQ's *National Party Conventions* and the Senate Library's *Nomination and Election of the President and Vice President* (1968) borrow only; 1960 both and Democratic 1968 not found (`notes/leads.md`).
- A convention not yet read keeps only a source's table of how its delegations were chosen (`hughes`): Democratic 1968, the Hughes Commission's Table I (*The Democratic Choice*, 114 Cong. Rec. 31549, Oct. 14, 1968), each State's marks as printed, `check` holding them to the printed column totals. No block until the Call and the roll call are in.
