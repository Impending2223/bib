# Maps of Indochina

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

`tools/bib/vnmap.py` (STYLE notes at the top): a map of Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia for each year, 1960–68, "Southeast Asia 1966", set immediately above the year's first Southeast Asia table, or, in a year without one, in the year's first calendar entry tagged with a place in the Republic of Vietnam (vnmap STYLE 8; the owner, Nov. 4, 2026), showing every entry of the year tagged with a place: a dot a place, larger by the year's entries there, a tap listing them, each linked; under the map, folded, the year's placed entries; names placed by the library's collision, the year's places first, then the gazetteer's rank. The provinces as of late 1966, tinted by corps zone (the default), today's region, or plain; or the borders alone; or land and water. Boxes: Names; North Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia (their provinces, today's); Relief, Elevation, Contours, Rivers, Climate, Rain (by month), Population (1960, 1970: modelled, later) and Cities (the UN *Demographic Yearbook*'s figures).

- Drawn by MapLibre GL JS (the owner's spouse, Oct. 22, 2026: "Use libraries and don't reinvent the wheel"), vendored in `sources/vendor/` at a pinned version (its README: versions, licenses, how to update) and copied to `build/vnmap/`; no CDN. One client for the annual maps and the page, `tools/bib/vnmap.js` and `vnmap.css` (copied hashed to `build/vnmap/`, one `<script type="module">` a page), which loads the library only when a map nears the screen. Its gestures, label collision, hillshade (`raster-dem`, `hillshade`) and colour relief (`color-relief`) are the library's: write no projection, gesture or label placement of our own. The build computes no geometry (CI has only pyyaml): it copies the files and writes a manifest (`build/geo/vn-manifest-<hash>.json`).
- The data, made once by `tools/vnmap/` (each script's docstring names its downloads; they need shapely >= 2.1, pyproj, pyogrio, rasterio, mercantile, contourpy, pmtiles): one border geometry for everything, `sources/indochina/admin.geojson`, from geoBoundaries CGAZ (`base.py`: the countries, Vietnam's districts, Laos's and Cambodia's provinces as one coverage, simplified with shared edges, so neighbours meet; the 1966 units dissolved from the districts; each line drawn once at its rank). Rasters as PMTiles (`dem.pmtiles`, `koppen.pmtiles`; `tiles.py`), vectors as GeoJSON. Serving PMTiles needs HTTP range requests: GitHub Pages answers them; locally use `npx http-server build`, not `python3 -m http.server`. `check` fails on a map file over 50 MB, the map's files over 60 MB together, or a vendored file missing.
- Tags, hidden: `place:<id>`, a place in `sources/vn-provinces-1966/places.json` (the gazetteer: name with its diacritics, plain form, kind, Wikipedia's coordinates, 1966 province, country, rank for names); `province:<id>`, a 1966 unit, where an entry names a province and no finer place. An event in Indochina or off its coast takes them; a decision about it made in Washington, Honolulu or Manila does not. `check` fails on a tag that names nothing.
- A place not in the gazetteer: add it, with Wikipedia's coordinates (never guessed) and its province by point in polygon. The lines are approximate (the README there); the gazetteer's notes are working notes, not the series'.
- A city's population (`sources/indochina/cities.yaml`, kept by hand): each figure as the Yearbook prints it, with its table and page; `check` fails on a row without them.
- The map on a page of its own (the owner's request, Oct. 22, 2026): `build/indochina.html`, "Indochina" in the top bar (`templates/list.html`), the reader's Pages and its front page; `./bib build indochina` alone. The same figure and script (vnmap STYLE 6), as tall as the screen, every placed entry, with a year selector (All years, 1960–68), each year anchored (`indochina.html#y1965`); then the gazetteer as a table (place, plain form, kind, 1966 province, corps zone, the entries tagged there by year, each row anchored `pl-<id>`) and the sources and licenses, HydroSHEDS's statement in full, the software's licenses (`tools/bib/indochina.py`, its STYLE notes). Each annual map closes with a muted line to it.
