# Ebooks and feeds

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

The owner's request (Oct. 22, 2026): "ebooks of documents, arranged chronologically, and RSS feeds of accessible recordings, dated and sorted chronologically ... to go through this material systematically." `build/ebooks.html` lists both, by year (the series index's Pages, README).

- Ebooks (`tools/bib/ebooks.py`, its STYLE): an EPUB 3 a month, `build/ebooks/documents-<YYYY-MM>.epub`, Jan. 1960 to Jan. 1969: every FRUS document the daybook files that month, in full with its footnotes, and the President's Public Papers in full (above, "The Public Papers"; each item's text with its pages marked and linked to the PDF, the documents the Papers do not print by title, linked to APP), day by day in the daybook's order; a title page (contents, order, sources, terms), the contents by day, each document's citation and links to history.state.gov and to its day in the calendar. The text from `sources/frus-text/` (`python3 tools/ebooks/make_frus_text.py --cache DIR`, the TEI make_daybook.py reads; rerun after make_daybook.py; its STYLE the conversion). The build needs neither TEI nor network. The Papers' text from `sources/ppp-text/` (GPO's scans, their OCR cleaned), never APP's transcriptions (its site's; its robots.txt refuses automated readers, ClaudeBot by name). `ebooks.validate` checks each book's structure (the mimetype first and stored, container, manifest, spine, nav, every file well-formed, every inner link); epubcheck 5.1.0 passes them (not in the workflow: it needs Java).
- Feeds (`tools/bib/feeds.py`, its STYLE): RSS 2.0, a feed a year (the owner's choice, Oct. 22, 2026), `build/feeds/recordings-<YYYY>.xml`, and `feeds/recordings.opml` for all: every recording the Miller Center gives free (PRDE-only recordings left out), its page the link and its audio the enclosure, linked, never copied; dated by the recording (the catalogue's time, Eastern; noon without one), in date order, numbered, `itunes:type` serial; the description catalogue data only. Each calendar page's head names its years' feeds, and its lede points to the page.
- `./bib build ebooks` (the books, the feeds, the page), `./bib build feeds` (the feeds and the page); the full build does both. `check` fails on a file in `sources/frus-text/` that is not a daybook month's xz, and warns on a daybook month without one [ebooks].
