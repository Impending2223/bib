# Elections

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

Every general election from 1956 to 1974, in `elections/<year>.yaml`: the House and Senate race by race, and in presidential years the vote for President by State with the electoral votes as cast. 1956 is a base for swing and is not shown. `build/congress.html` puts each election from 1958 to 1974 before the Congress it chose (the 86th and 94th have no rosters); a calendar entry tagged `election:<year>` carries its block too (now every November election, 1960–68). A block: the electoral and popular vote first, then by chamber the seats won, the net change from the close of the last Congress, pickups, and new members; one view switch for every map (Result; Party, the parties' flat colors; Margin, the winner's lead over the runner-up in points of all the votes, by the quantile rule; Swing, continuous); the President's map above the House and Senate, with a States | Electors | Chamber switch (Electors: a dot an elector, as voted); the Senate map with a Senators toggle (a dot a seat, larger than the House's: `PB`, `RB`); a Chamber under every map but the specials' (a semicircle, a dot a seat or an elector as cast; the Democrats and their allies left, the Republicans and theirs right, a third ticket between; each side from its end in by margin, the narrowest in the middle, the Senate's seats not at stake gray at the ends; filled in a snake whose midpoint is the middle dot on the center line; a faithless or unpledged elector by his vote, in its color; its Result held and picked up, the President's too: STYLE 12 in `tools/bib/elections.py`); and every race in tables (the candidate first, in bold where he won, his party under or after him; under 520px the share under the count and the note on a line of its own): candidates with party, votes and share, the margin in votes and in points of all the votes cast, and a note. Pickups are shaded in the winner's color, other member changes in gray. Members of neither major party count with the party they caucused with, and are labeled so ("Conservative, caucusing with the Republicans"; `caucus:` in `elections/facts.yaml`), here and in the rosters and bars at each opening. Definitions are the STYLE notes at the top of `tools/bib/elections.py`.

```
tools/elections/make_elections.py   writes elections/<year>.yaml and senate-prior.yaml (needs network, pdftoppm, tesseract)
tools/elections/president.py        the presidential vote by State (electors, names, notes from elections/facts.yaml)
tools/elections/pres_margins.py     elections/pres-margins.csv: every contested elector race, 1824–2024 (needs network, pandas)
tools/elections/read.py             loads elections/readings/; its docstring is the readings schema
tools/elections/review.py           the by-eye loop: left, sheets, pages, locate, verify, merge, audit (docstring)
tools/elections/review/             READER.md and PRESIDENT.md: instructions to hand a reader, person or agent
tools/bib/elections.py              renders the blocks and checks the data (STYLE notes; "Map records")
```

- Votes: the Clerk's *Statistics of the Presidential and Congressional Election* (clerk.house.gov), OCR'd; the OCR's misread party labels and names put right by `tools/elections/labels.py`. Each figure is read three times and settled against the State's recapitulation total and Wikipedia's percentages (`reconcile.py`); a race that does not settle is read by eye into `elections/readings/<year>.yaml`. Where a race page and its recapitulation differ, the figure the Clerk's totals add up with is used, and the reading's `printed` note says so: shown in the race's note, as is any other disagreement among the Clerk's own figures (a misprinted total, transposed figures, a party printed one way and counted another). A reading's `note` (other rulings) is kept in the file and not shown. Where the Clerk and Wikipedia disagree after a reading, the Clerk stands (`wikipedia_differs`), names included, unless the State's own canvass, read off its page, says otherwise: then the State's figure stands, the reading carrying `source` (the State's cite) and `clerk` (the Clerk's figures), both shown in the race's note (Md. 1958, Colo. 1960, Fla. and Mo. 1962; President: Wyo. 1960, Cal., Colo., Ga. 1968, Cal. 1972). A later State compilation is not a canvass. CQ's *Guide to U.S. Elections* (6th ed., 2010) is compared in `elections/cq.yaml`; where it differs from the figures shown, its figures are printed in the race's note ("CQ Guide 6th (2010) 1275: Rivers 64,804."; `shown` there, read by the build); it fills a race only where the Clerk prints no vote (`source` without `clerk`: Pa.-6, 1964).
- Reading keys: `h NY 9` (district; 0 at large), `s MD 1` (Senate class), `h NM 0-1` (an at-large position), `s OR 2 special` (a special held with a regular race for the same seat). A special Wikipedia lists that the Clerk's November volume does not print goes under `not_in_volume` with the reason. `check` fails on a malformed or stale key.
- Candidates, incumbents, and their fates: Wikipedia's race tables. Names: the OCR's are for matching only (it garbles names), so a race takes Wikipedia's name where the surname matches; in a race read by eye, only where Wikipedia's given names agree with the printed ones (`wiki_names`): a nickname is no disagreement (Bob and Robert; '"Pete"' as printed), nor a first name or initial one drops; a first name nothing matches (Russell and Thomas S. Kleppe, N.D. 1970) or initials that differ (Gladys E. and Gladys L. Davis, Ohio-6, 1960; CQ prints E. too) is, and the printed name stands. Wikipedia's also mends the Clerk's surname misprints ('Zelenki'). New York's fusion candidates carry their party lines and the Clerk's total.
- President: each slate's vote (its highest elector), assigned to a candidate by Wikipedia's results by State; electoral votes from the same table, with `CAST`.
- Margins are ordinary: the leader's votes less the runner-up's, over all the votes. The two-party share (D over D+R) serves only for swing.
- Swing: House, the same district where its lines did not change between the two Congresses (the same shape key in `congress/geo.json`), otherwise the State's House vote; Senate, the seat's last regular election, six years before (1952 and 1954 from `elections/senate-prior.yaml`, Wikipedia's percentages); President, the last presidential election.
- The partisan voting index (the owner, Oct. 28, 2026: "If we can calculate the CD numbers, we should take the extension off the shelf"): the States' from these returns, the districts' from the Census's *Congressional District Data Books*, read by page into `elections/readings/districts/` (1952-60 on the 87th, 88th and 89th Congresses' lines; 1968 on the 93d's), each district row held to its State column and each State column set against these returns (`vs_clerk`); `python3 -I tools/elections/make_pvi.py` (its STYLE; `check` [pvi], `tools/bib/pvi.py`). District PVIs: 1960 419, 1962 321, 1964 302, 1966 228, 1968 114, 1970 386, 1972 15, 1974 418 (the 1966 Supplements, the 90th's lines, and CDD-92, the 92d's, the owner's copies, read Oct. 31, 2026, gave 1964 and 1968 by district; Jacobson's file, where no Census volume gives a district, 1964 for 50 districts of 1966 and 2 of 1972 for 316 of 1974, each row's basis naming it; CDD-94, the 94th's three reports (Texas, California, New York; the owner's copies, read Oct. 31, 2026), 1968 and 1972 for those States' 102: make_pvi.py STYLE 13; 1964 on the 93d's lines is still to find: the memo, notes/extensions/memos/pvi-districts.md). The same volumes' vote for Representative, retabulated onto each Congress's lines, is in `elections/readings/districts-house/` (1963, 1965, 1966s, 1971, 1973, 1974 volumes; and CQ's recomputed shares where the Census gives none: `cq-1962`, `cq-1964`, `cq-1966` (CQ's "Winning Representatives' Vote Percentages" charts, the leading party's percent of the total, read by eye, the party by the type weight) and `cq-1968` (Missouri's 1966 vote on the 1968 lines); make_baseline.py STYLE 8), the baseline's lags. Every seat's lag where its lines changed, the cell as printed, its page, Jacobson's beside it, and every seat with none and why: `notes/house-lags.md` (generated, `python3 -I tools/elections/lag_listing.py`). Shown (the owner, Oct. 28, 2026: "Show both. Be sure to cite and attribute"), in each election's block and the calendar's `election:` entries: the index under each race's seat (the district's; a Senate race and the President's table, the State's), a map view "PVI", and, folded under the race tables, the States' and the districts' indexes with their inputs and the vote by district as each Data Book prints it, every table citing its volume by page and naming the compilers the Census names (the Republican Congressional Committee, CQ, *America Votes*, the NRCC); the vote taken as fact, not CQ's selection or arrangement (*Feist*): STYLE 3-5 in `tools/bib/pvi.py`.
- The baseline (the owner, Oct. 30-31, 2026: the PVI's "primary use is to set that baseline"; the swing "against prior Congressional results in each election-day district"; "Run the numbers twice, once with [the incumbency term], and once without"): `elections/baseline.yaml`, written by `python3 -I tools/elections/make_baseline.py [--print]` (its STYLE; generated, never edit): Gelman and King's regression a year at a time over the contested seats with a lagged House vote, the Democratic two-party share on the seat's last share and the party holding it, with and without the incumbent's running; each race's residual in points (how far the Democrat ran ahead of the seat's baseline, the Republican the negative), each year's coefficients and the mean district swing beside them. The lag: the Clerk's returns where the lines did not change; across a redistricting, the Census's retabulation of the earlier House vote onto the new lines (`elections/readings/districts-house/`). Run on three lags, each fitted apart (the owner, Oct. 31, 2026: "go ahead with 1-3"): that one (`census`), Jacobson's (`jacobson`, his `dvp`, the redrawn seats included) and the Clerk's alone where the lines held (`clerk`); STYLE 6-7 in the script. Not shown yet. To check: the literature behind it, recalled, in `notes/extensions/memos/pvi-districts.md`, "The baseline, and what to read for it".
- Jacobson's district file (`sources/jacobson/`, its README: Gary C. Jacobson's replication data for "It's Nothing Personal," Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/29559, CC0; fetched Oct. 31, 2026), 1946-2014, a row a seat a year: `tools/elections/jacobson.py` (its STYLE) holds his shares, lags and redrawn flags against these returns seat by seat and writes `notes/jacobson-compare.md` (generated; `check` warns when stale [jacobson]); the Clerk's figures stand, each difference a lead to the page. His presidential figure (`dpres`) fills a district no Census volume gives (make_pvi.py STYLE 13).
- Hand facts (who caucused with whom, electors who broke, the 1960 unpledged slates, the Alabama reckonings) live in `elections/facts.yaml`, not in code. A third-party member missing from `caucus:` fails `check`.
- To add or correct an election, follow the loop in the docstring of `tools/elections/review.py`: `make_elections.py YEAR --cache DIR`; `review.py left` and `sheets`/`pages`; readers with `review/READER.md`; `review.py verify`, then `merge`; rerun `make_elections.py` (it should leave nothing); `review.py audit YEAR`; `./bib check`; then tag the calendar entry.
- Colors are tokens at the top of `templates/congress.html` (its CSS is in commented sections); the parties' are the *New York Times*'s election colors (the owner's choice, Oct. 22, 2026: #1375B7, #C93135). Result: four steps a side (`--eR1`–`--eR4`, `--eB1`–`--eB4`). Margin: a straight line in OKLab from `--rW` (near white) at a tie to `--rR`/`--rB`, the darkest steps, by the quantile rule: a margin's position is the share of the 2,291 contested races for presidential electors, 1824–2024, decided by less (`elections/pres-margins.csv`, written by `tools/elections/pres_margins.py`; the build puts its percentiles into `QM`). Swing: from `--eN` (gray) at none to `--eR4`/`--eB4` at `SWING` points. The keys draw the same scales as gradient bars.
- "Check" in a note (as in the calendar) marks a detail still to verify: `grep -rn Check elections/facts.yaml elections/readings`.
