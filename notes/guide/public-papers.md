# The Public Papers

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

The owner's request (Oct. 22, 2026): "Yes to govinfo (be sure to clean them, indicate their pagination in-text, and replace our APP links with PPP links where possible, while leaving the APP links where it's not)." The *Public Papers of the Presidents* (Office of the Federal Register), 1960–69, from GPO's volumes on govinfo (collection PPP): Eisenhower 1960–61 (`PPP-1960-book1`), Kennedy 1961, 1962, 1963 (`PPP-1961-book1` … `PPP-1963-book1`), Johnson 1963–64 Books I and II (`PPP-1963-book2`, `PPP-1964-book1`), 1965–1968–69 Books I and II (`PPP-1965-book1` … `PPP-1968-book2`): fourteen scanned books, each one PDF with ABBYY's OCR layer and no granules. Public domain (17 U.S.C. § 105). Nothing is taken from presidency.ucsb.edu (its robots.txt bars AI crawlers): APP's own pages are linked, never read.

```
tools/ppp/make_ppp_text.py   fetches each PDF (585–825 MB) into --cache, reads its words with their boxes (pdftotext
                             -bbox, kept gzipped there, ~12 MB), deletes the PDF; segments and cleans (its docstring:
                             the reading and the cleaning, STYLE 1–7); writes sources/ppp-text/<package>.json.xz and
                             sources/ppp-text/rules.json (each cleaning rule and how often it fired, by volume)
tools/ppp/match_app.py       matches APP's documents (the daybook's President rows, sources/app-index.jsonl) to the items
                             (its STYLE); writes sources/ppp-matches.json: the matches, the unmatched on each side, each
                             volume's particulars and an index of its items
tools/ppp/relink.py          rewrites the lists' links to matched APP documents as Public Papers cites (prints each change)
tools/bib/ppp.py             the cite, the link, the texts for the ebooks, and check (STYLE notes at the top)
```

- An item: its number, heading (title and date as printed; a date the OCR lost is `month` alone or `filed` under the item before), first and last printed page, first and last PDF page, and its text as blocks: paragraphs, set-off lines (salutations' closes, signatures), the editors' bracketed notes, the "NOTE:" after the text, footnotes, small type. "[p. 123]" marks where each printed page begins; every item's text opens with its first page's marker. A year's two books are paginated through, so a page finds its item without the book. 5,414 items (three of 1968 lost by the OCR, `missing`), 8.5 MB compressed, the matches file 1.4 MB.
- The cite, the Bluebook's: the volume's years, "Pub. Papers", the item's first page ("1961 Pub. Papers 1", "1963–64 Pub. Papers 842", "1960–61 Pub. Papers 589"), linked to the PDF at that page (`…/PPP-1961-book1.pdf#page=59`); in a calendar note after the title and before the date: "Inaugural Address, [1961 Pub. Papers 1](…) (Jan. 20, 1961)". The hover names the volume, item and pages. Write new notes so; the APP link only where the Papers do not print the document (executive orders, proclamations, platforms, the campaign of 1960) or no item matches.
- After each Papers cite, " (APP)", the word linked to the APP document the item matches (the owner, Nov. 6, 2026: "After each Public Papers link, give a parenthetical ' (APP)' hyperlinked to the coordinate document in the American Presidency Project"): shown "Inaugural Address, 1961 Pub. Papers 1 (APP) (Jan. 20, 1961)". Made at render, never written into the data (write the note as above): the notes (`markup.to_html` asks `ppp.note_paren`), the daybook's President list, the Names' presidential documents, the Executive roster's APP sources, the ebooks' items. A note's link names its item by its first page; where several items begin on that page, the title written before the cite settles which (else the date after it), and where nothing does, or the item has no APP match, nothing is added; a note already giving an APP link right after the cite is not doubled. `check` warns on a Papers link whose page is no item's first page, or one the words around it cannot settle [ppp-app]. STYLE 5 in `tools/bib/ppp.py`.
- Where the series showed APP, the Papers now stand where an item matches (STYLE 3 in `tools/bib/ppp.py`): the daybook's President list (daybook STYLE 13), the calendar's and the lists' notes (rewritten in the data by `relink.py`: 1,284 links in 109 files, Oct. 22, 2026; 176 left on APP), the Names' presidential documents, the Executive roster's APP sources (an APP address, or `APP YYYY-MM-DD`: the day's items that name the holder). Of APP's 7,505 documents of Jan. 1960–Jan. 20, 1969, 5,326 match an item (98.5 percent of the 5,405 the Papers could print; 1,163 are orders, proclamations and platforms, 937 a candidate's or Nixon's); 5,325 of the 5,414 items match.
- Accuracy, checked by eye against the page images (Oct. 22, 2026: 13 pages, 31 items, every volume but 1965 Book II and 1966 Book II, read as text only): item numbers, headings, dates, boundaries and page markers right on every page; the words as the OCR read them (an italic salutation can come out garbled: "My jello tv Americans"), its misreadings put right by the rules logged in `rules.json` (digits in numbers and years, J read as I, "£", split small capitals, hyphens at line ends, a fixed table of letter confusions against the volumes' own vocabulary). A misreading no rule meets stands.
- `check` [ppp]: fails on a file in `sources/ppp-text/` that is not a volume's or `rules.json`, an item out of order, pages or markers out of order, a PDF page outside the volume, a date outside the volume's span, an item with no text or not opening with its page's marker, a match naming no item; warns on items the OCR lost (`missing`) and on a list's APP link the Papers print (run `relink.py`). Rerun `make_ppp_text.py --cache DIR` (DIR holding the boxes, or the network) and then `match_app.py` after changing either; the build needs neither.
