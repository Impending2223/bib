# The works catalogue

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

Every work the lists cite is a card in `works/`, once; the lists point to it (`work:`), so a correction to the card changes every listing, short form and page. `tools/bib/works.py` is the only code that reads the cards; everything else asks it (its docstring has the fields and the STYLE notes). A card is structured (author, title, year, and the rest as fields; the citation is rendered from them) or verbatim (`c`, the citation as written, where the fields cannot yet render it; author, title and year read from it for sorting and search).

```yaml
- id: w.beschloss-crisis-years          # permanent; w. and the first author's surname and the title's words
  author: [Michael R. Beschloss]         # running order; editor, translator, director the same
  title: 'The Crisis Years: Kennedy and Khrushchev, 1960–1963'
  year: '1991'
  n: Interviews with nearly everyone.    # the card note
  tags: [topic:berlin]
```

- Derivative cards (the owner, Nov. 12, 2026: translations, in 17 U.S.C. § 101's sense): a translation's card names its original, `translates: w.x`. The works page shows on the original "Translated in [Y]" and on the translation "Translation of [X]"; a citation shows only the work cited. A calendar entry that reads the translation as a source carries "Check: [[X]] ..." until it cites the original too, in the parallel form "[[w.y]], 123, translating [[w.x]], 123" (or X as an item of its own), or says "Not in [[X]]"; `check` holds it (an error, `[translation]`). Pairs so far: Bảo Ninh's *Nỗi buồn chiến tranh* and *The Sorrow of War*; *Vremya. Lyudi. Vlast'* and *Khrushchev Remembers*. The foreign periodicals have cards too (Nov. 12): `w.pravda`, `w.izvestia`, `w.nhan-dan`, `w.hoc-tap`, `w.le-monde`, `w.der-spiegel`, `w.current-digest-of-the-soviet-press`, `w.peking-review`, `w.guardian`, `w.times-london`, `w.frankfurter-allgemeine-zeitung`, `w.economist`, `w.china-quarterly` (`kind: periodical`), cited "[[w.pravda]], Oct. 25, 1962".
- Editions, translations and chapters are cards of their own. A chapter or a volume points to its container (`in:`) and takes from it what it lacks; a correction to the container changes every chapter. The short form of a multi-volume work is Bluebook's ("Logevall, Title, 2 CHVW 123"). `edition_of:` and `translates:` join an edition or a translation to its original.
- Under a person's subject line the author it names is not repeated (the citation starts at the title); `byline: true` (or `byline: [w.…]`, some of several) keeps it. A listing of several works naming their author at the first only writes `ditto: true`.
- Queries (`./bib works list`): `-t` every one of, `--any` at least one of, `--not` none of. Stored tags pass down from a container to what is in it. Worked out for every card: `in:kja`, `section:vn.II.C.1`, `author:logevall`, `year:1991`, `pub:1990s`, `kind:film`, `form:verbatim`; `in:` alone is any list, `section:vn.II.C*` a prefix.
- Periodicals by the Bluebook's abbreviations, the 21st edition's where the Indigo Book differs ("Int'l Sec." for *International Security*: the Bluebook abbreviates "Security" and "Securities" both "Sec."). A dissertation's or thesis's title in roman, as any unpublished work's (`kind: thesis`, verbatim, no italics). STYLE 7 and 8 in `tools/bib/works.py`.
- No publisher in a citation, as Bluebook's: "(2007)", not "(University of North Carolina Press, 2007)"; a work published before 1900 keeps its place of publication and its publisher ("(Venice, Vitale Mascardi 1665)"); a publisher's edition named as such ("Random House ed. 1966") and a film's network stay. A count of volumes after the title: "*Reporting Vietnam*, 2 vols. (1998)". STYLE 5 and 6 in `tools/bib/works.py`; `check` warns [publisher].
- Change a card with `./bib works set ID FIELD=VALUE` or by hand; tag it with `./bib works tag ID +TAG`. To fix a citation, fix the card, not the listing: a listing's `c` written over cuts its pointer.
- `check` fails on a pointer to no card, a card neither structured nor verbatim, an `in` that comes round to itself; warns on a card nothing points to and on a work cited as text (`./bib works adopt`).
- `notes/works-inventory.md` (`./bib works inventory`): the works written differently that are probably one, the editions, the works listed in several places with differing notes, the calendar's citations, suspected errors. `tools/works/make_cards.py` made the catalogue from the lists, once (Oct. 13, 2026), and `make_chapters.py` the chapters and their volumes (34 chapters, 28 volumes); don't rerun either.
- Not yet in the catalogue: primary documents (cases, laws, orders, hearings, speeches), periodicals as such (newspapers, the *Pa. Manual*; the *Department of State Bulletin* excepted, `w.department-of-state-bulletin`, `kind: periodical`, cited with its issue and page linked, "[[w.department-of-state-bulletin]], [July 9, 1962, 64](…)"; and *American Foreign Policy: Current Documents*, a card a year, `w.current-documents-1956` … `-1967`), and a few works the calendar names in passing ("translated as *The Feast of the Goat*"). One-time scripts, Oct. 13, 2026 (don't rerun): `tools/works/convert_calendar.py` turned the calendar's short citations into pointers (2,287, with a second pass for several titles under one author, "and", a bare surname, a chapter); `adopt_calendar.py` made cards of the 85 works the calendar cited in full and found nowhere else, and pointed those citations (and their "see [[…]]" short forms) at them; `merge_duplicates.py` folded 80 cards into the work they repeat (one first author, main title and year).
- A work on two cards: `./bib works combine KEEP DROP` (works.combine): DROP's id stays an alias, listings and `[[w.…]]` links follow, the notes are joined (a bare "Also …" pointer to a list now holding the card gives way).
- The A–Z bibliography, `build/works.html` (`./bib build works`; `tools/bib/bibliography.py`, its STYLE notes): every card, A to Z, built beside the themed lists, which stay until it replaces their Parts I–II (Part III stays). Filed by the first-listed author's surname; a film under its director's; an organization only where the card names it as the author (cards are not edited to add one); a compilation of one writer's texts under the writer (`files_under`: Ladejinsky's *Selected Papers*, Walinsky ed.; the Public Papers and the edited White House tapes under each President, though many speak in them); recordings and records of talks under the one or few named persons they record (the Abrams tapes under Abrams, *The Kissinger Transcripts* under Kissinger), not a study that draws on them, nor a film cut from them (a film files as a film: *The Lady Bird Diaries* under Porter); otherwise the short title, its leading article and subtitle dropped (edited volumes included), the heading in italics; then the year; then the short title (FILING in `tools/bib/works.py`; `./bib works list` sorts the same way). A work is entered again, a ghost, under its other authors, the writer it was written "with", its editors and translators, and any name in `also_under` (Cincinnatus [Cecil B. Currey] under Currey): its citation in the muted gray, the whole of it a link to the work's own entry, not underlined, and nothing more (no "Filed under"; the owner's ruling, Oct. 20, 2026). One person written two ways is one heading: by the marks alone (Andrade, Andradé) under the marked form; otherwise by `SAME` (William Colby, William E. Colby); each citation keeps its card's form. An author's works under the name, inverted ("Beschloss, Michael R."; a particle with the surname, "De Palma, Brian"; a Vietnamese, Chinese or Japanese name in its own order, as written; `OWN_ORDER`, `RUNNING`, `SURNAME` for what the rules cannot tell), linked to the name entry where one person fits; a work without an author under its title as the heading; a leading article dropped, another language's too (*La Guerre du Vietnam* under G); a title beginning with a number under the number spelled out ("The 30-Year War" under T). Each work's full citation, its author in running order, nothing indented; no notes on this page; a book's, a film's or an edited collection's title in plain roman, an article's or a chapter's title in italics, its journal or collection in roman (Bluebook's small caps tried and set aside), here only; the type of the series' entries (18px, line height 1.55). After it, on its line, muted, no closing stop: the list sections holding it, linked to the listing, and the calendar entries pointing to it (`[[w.…]]`, or a short title and list not yet converted), by year. The anchor is the card's id (`works.html#w.beschloss-crisis-years`), lit as a roster row is: white, an en dash past it on either side, plain again at a click outside.
