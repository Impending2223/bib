Parts: I portrayals, II sources, III names. A reference like "K–J Adm. II.D" is `lists/kja/II.D.yaml`. The calendar's files are named by month, year and month, `YYYYMM.yaml` (`196104.yaml`; any year: `178904.yaml` would do), and so are their section ids (`196104`; the old ids, `apr`, `apr60`, kept as `old_id`, which the headings still anchor); every section a calendar month (`196001.yaml` from Jan. 6, 1960, the calendar's first day; `196901.yaml` to Jan. 20, 1969, its last; January 1966 two, `196601.yaml` and `196601b.yaml`, at the third part's opening), plus `th.yaml` (threads); the primary seasons after Jan. 1969, `197203.yaml`–`197608.yaml`, hold only the primaries and the conventions' openings for now. Its sections are numbered straight through, I to CXXIV, in date order, in the data (`num`); the headings show the dates alone.
