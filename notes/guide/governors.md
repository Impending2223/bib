# Governors

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md, Nov. 10, 2026 (the owner: "I approve both"); CLAUDE.md keeps the core and an index of these guides.

`elections/governors.yaml`, written by `tools/elections/make_governors.py` from Wikipedia (needs the network; the build does not): every general election for governor, 1960–68, odd years included (New Jersey and Virginia, 1961 and 1965; Kentucky and Mississippi, 1963 and 1967; Louisiana, Mar. 3, 1964, and Feb. 6, 1968). The races and incumbents from Wikipedia's tables of each year; the figures from each race's article, its election box, with the source the article gives for them (most the State's canvass or *America Votes*; Our Campaigns, or none, marks Check). What the articles cannot settle is `HAND` in the script (Georgia, 1966: the legislature elected Maddox; Minnesota, 1962: the recount). Never edit the file by hand. `tools/bib/governors.py` renders them (STYLE notes there):

- A calendar entry tagged `governors:<YYYY-MM-DD>` carries the day's races (on a November election day, inside the day's "Elections of" block, the entry pointing to it): a summary (races, wins, each party's gains, the governorships after), a map (Result: the winner's party, dark where it took the office; Margin: the election maps' quantile rule), and the table, one race a `tbody`: the candidates with votes and shares, the margin, and under them the note (the incumbent and his fate, the hold or gain, the source). Now Nov. 8, 1960, Nov. 6, 1962, Nov. 3, 1964, Nov. 8, 1966, Nov. 7, 1967, Feb. 6, 1968 (Louisiana) and Nov. 5, 1968 (thread "Governors"); the other odd years' races are entries of their own.
- Names: each race a row in the candidate's record (`governor_rows` in `tools/bib/lives.py`), the date linked to the day's table.
- The primaries for governor are calendar entries (thread "Governors"), their figures cited in each entry.
- The bars on re-election (the owner's request, Oct. 23, 2026: "the authority and reason for the bar, and its scope"): `elections/term-limits.yaml`, kept by hand (header the schema), a State an entry for each State whose incumbent was barred, 1960-68: each provision in force over the span (cite, words, scope, from and to), each read by page (Thorpe's *Federal and State Constitutions* of 1909 for the old texts, the States' manuals, session laws, codes and election returns for the rest), the changes since, the text today, and a Check for what is not settled. A race the tables call "term-limited" shows "barred from another term (Mo. Const. art. IV, § 17 (1945, amended 1965): not eligible for election as his own successor)", the cite linked, and the rule's Check (STYLE 9 in `tools/bib/governors.py`; `tools/bib/termlimits.py`, its STYLE). `check` fails on a barred race with no provision, a rule without its words, scope or a linked source, a span out of order.
