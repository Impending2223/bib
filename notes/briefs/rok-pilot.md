## Reader A: the junta's log (Opus, page images)
*Ilchi* 『일지』 [Log], the Supreme Council chairman's office, vol. 1, May 16–Aug. 14, 1961, the Presidential Archives (대통령기록관), record no. 1A00614175030660: the scan, free, https://dams.pa.go.kr/dams/DOCUMENT/2019/04/29/DOC/SRC/0104201904291352007271611010636.pdf (126 pages, 18 MB; no text layer). Fetch it into your scratch directory, render the pages (`pdftoppm -r 110 -png`), and read May 16–June 30 from the images: handwritten, Korean with Chinese characters, dated by the Tangun era (4294 = 1961), each day's items under heads (革命 the revolution; 指示命令 directives and orders; 國內外 at home and abroad). The page images and the scan are a government record; delete them when done.
- Make the card first (`./bib works new`, then edit to the form of works.py STYLE 10): editor `Kukka Chaegŏn Ch'oego Hoeŭi Ŭijangsil 국가재건최고회의 의장실`, title `Ilchi`, script `일지`, trans `Log`, paren `10 vols., May 16, 1961–Nov. 25, 1963, Presidential Archives, record nos. 1A00614175030660–74`, `online:` the archive's collection page https://www.pa.go.kr/portal/online_contents/instant_record/instantRecordDetail.do?seq=10. Cite it by volume and the log's day and item: `[[w.ilchi-...]], I, May 18, 1961, item 4`. "Made public: 2019." (the scan's year; ? if the archive gives an earlier release, use it).
- An item is an entry where it records an act of the leadership (a decree, an appointment, a directive, a decision, a meeting, a statement); the log's routine (callers received, inspections) named in a sentence on the day's chief entry or passed over and listed. One event an entry; a day's several acts are several entries. Quote nothing beyond a few words; translate plainly.
