## A and B. Subentries (the design: `tools/bib/subentries.py`, its STYLE; `notes/guide/entries.md`, "Subentries")

One event with several matters (a news conference, a Presidium session) is a parent entry and its subentries. The parent states what is true of all its parts and carries their common bibliography; each part keeps its own id, thread, `c`, sources and claims, with `part_of: <parent id>` and `follows: [<the sibling before it>]` where the order is known (the transcript's order; the protocol's items). One level only. The models, already in the tree: `./bib show cal.1961-07-19.press-and-broadcasting cal.1961-07-19.school-aid cal.1961-07-19.berlin-and-vienna-4 cal.1961-07-19.transition-and-staff cal.1961-07-19.berlin-and-vienna-5`, and Feb. 17 and Sept. 7, 1960 (`./bib find -l cal --since 1960-02-17 --until 1960-02-17`).

What to do, for each case:
1. Read every calendar entry of the day (`./bib find -l cal --since D --until D`) and the source (the Public Papers' text, `sources/ppp-text/PPP-YYYY-book1.json.xz`: items with n, title, page, pdf, text; the note at the item's end gives the conference's number, place and hour). Rule whether the entries are parts of the one event. An entry that only mentions the conference but records another event (the breakfast to prepare it; Robert Kennedy watching it; a city praised there for a deed of its own) is not a part: leave it.
2. A parent: an `add` op (`./bib new-id cal D press-and-broadcasting`; `./bib ledger add ...` with your patch's name). News conference: thread `press-and-broadcasting`; `c` "Kennedy's fourteenth news conference, the State Department Auditorium, 10 a.m." (number, place, hour as the note gives them; Johnson's likewise; where the note gives none, say only what it gives); `n`: the Public Papers cite in full, the pinpoint range, and the parenthetical for the note ("(the note, 521: the fourteenth, the place and the hour)"), then the event's common bibliography (a work that treats the whole conference), `Names:`; `time` (UTC, `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ`) and `time_zone` from the note; `tags: [name:...]` for the President. Presidium session: thread `soviet-union-leadership`; `c` "The Presidium in session" with what the volume gives of all items (the protocol's number, who presided, who attended where the editors list them, the place where given); `n` the volume and pages of the session's protocol and notes (`[[w.prezidium-tsk-kpss-1954]], I, 556–58`), then FRUS or other works on the whole session.
3. Each part: an `edit` op with `base:` (the rev `./bib show` prints), `set:` `part_of`, `follows` (the sibling before it in the transcript or the protocol; the first part takes none), and an `n` whose first cite is shortened to the short form plus the part's pinpoint ("[1961 Pub. Papers 513](link), at 516") with the rest of its note kept as it stands. Do not change `c`, the thread, the claims (`src`) or tags, except: a part whose `c` opens "News conference." or "Press conference:" loses those words (the parent says it); then its `src.c` hash must be refreshed, so say so in your report and leave `src` alone (the merger refreshes it). Do not change a part's `time`.
4. A same-session pair that are really one item entered twice (one decision): do not combine; report it.
5. Not in scope: adding the conference's other matters that no entry holds. List them in your report (page, matter) as leads.
