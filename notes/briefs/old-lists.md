## For each item, rule
- **One event entered twice**: a `combine: {keep: ID, drop: ID}` op (keep the entry with the primary record or the fuller sources; combine unions them and turns differing text into a conflict the lead settles, so say which wording to keep).
- **Two events, distinct**: no op; say why (the day, the persons, the act differ).
- **Two events in one entry**: split. An `add` op for the second event (`./bib new-id cal DATE THREAD`, then `./bib ledger add DATE THREAD ID "words" PATCH`), with its own `c` and only the sources that bear on it, their pinpoints; an `edit` op (with `base:`) taking it out of the first entry's `c` and `n`. Where a span holds a day that has an entry of its own, edit the span to drop that day.
- **The wrong day**: where the record settles it (the Public Papers, the Record, a dated document, the Daily Diary), an `edit` op setting `date` and `when`, and the source behind it in `n`; where the sources disagree and nothing settles it, a `check` op (the text begins "Check:") or a `variant`, naming the sources and their days.
- **A word or number that disagrees with the page** (Dallek's "thirty"): read the page image where the text layer may fail; a `variant` or a `check`, or an `edit` where the entry is plainly wrong.
- **A `follows` the held patches left undone**: a `follows` op where the entries are still unordered.
- **A true judgment call** (what the owner's rules do not settle): no op; put it in the report, with the facts.
Never change an `id`. Read every entry whole (`./bib show ID`) and the sources it cites where the item turns on them; the Drive's books by curl (`curl -sSL -o DIR/x.pdf "https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=ID&export=download&confirm=t"`), deleted when done; quote no book text beyond a few words.
