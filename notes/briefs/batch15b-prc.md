## The notes pass: the faults the audit of *Origins* III run 1 found (Nov. 18), to avoid
1. Anchors drifting in runs of notes (each note carrying the next note's items, from OCR splits): check each note's number against the image of the notes page.
2. Items missing: every item of every note is registered (each interview, document, press issue, study).
3. Documents standing only as their collections: each directive, letter, speech or report a note names is a witness row of its own (`part_of` its collection or card); the press is the day's issue, not the paper.
4. Edges carry `pin` (the page of the cited work) where the note gives one.
5. "To check:" lines name works by their cards (`[[w.…]]`, R53) and list every unread item of the notes that bear on that entry: the notes called in the passage the entry rests on (R3.3, "its 'To check:' line on each entry it bears on"; R35, "each witness the work's note gives for that passage"; R29). Every item of every note on a cited page is registered (a row or an edge), but an item goes on an entry's line, and its `via` row into the entry's map, only where its note bears on that entry. Corrected Nov. 18: this line first read "every unread item of the page's notes", my wording, not the brief's; it put 25 to 97 items on single entries of *Origins* III run 1.
6. Witness rows give names and titles in full, and a dated document's year from the note.
The cite form for *Origins*: the card `w.macfarquhar-origins-of-the-cultural` becomes the three-volume set (the audit's recommendation, works.py STYLE 9): cite `III, 152`, anchor `III, 152 n.4`; run 1's cites and anchors to be converted at its notes redo.

## What is an entry, and the form
As the first wave (batch15-prc.md): a session of the Politburo, its Standing Committee, the Secretariat, a Central Work Conference or a plenum (one entry a session, its matters in order); a talk with a named person (a foreign leader above all); an instruction, comment, letter, speech, document drafted or revised; a reception, a tour, an inspection; now also each day's reading, travel and minor acts the chronology records (R5 as amended). One event an entry. Thread `china-leadership` (`also:` `china`, `vietnam-hanoi`, `soviet-union`, `south-asia`, `laos`, `indonesia` ... where the act bears on it); `polity:prc`; `name:` for persons with a name entry (`./bib find -l kja SURNAME`; FRUS's persons, `sources/frus-names/persons.json`); Chinese in Pinyin without tones, the calendar's spelling where it has one; Vietnamese with full diacritics. Visibility (tools/bib/vis.py STYLE 8): an act inside the leadership unknown outside at the time `vis:only:prc`; with a foreign party present `vis:only:prc+<polity>`; an act published at the time no ruling. "Made public: YEAR." (the volume's year) on what was secret.

**Already in the calendar:** read your window's entries first (`./bib find -l cal --thread china-leadership --since YYYY-MM --until YYYY-MM`, and `--thread china`, `vietnam-hanoi`, the Soviet threads). Where the Mao chronology or another source already holds the act (a meeting Zhou attended, a talk Mao and Zhou held together), add a `cite` (one pinpoint and one parenthetical per cite op) and a `variant` or `check` where Zhou's or Liu's chronology differs; a new entry only for what the calendar lacks. Before adding, `./bib ledger find DATE` for the other readers' claims.
