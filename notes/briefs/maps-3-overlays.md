Where the work stands. The Indochina map has, beside its provinces and places, these boxes: Names; Relief; Elevation; Contours; Rivers (with lakes); Climate; Rain, by month; Population (1960, 1970, modelled); Cities (the UN *Demographic Yearbook*'s figures). Each is made once by a script in `tools/vnmap/` from a source that covers the world or Asia (ETOPO 2022; HydroRIVERS, SWORD and HydroLAKES; Beck et al.'s Köppen maps; CRU CL 2.0; ISIMIP3a's population; the Yearbook). The China map (`china.html`, and its figures in the calendar's and the threads' pages) has Names and Counties only. One client draws both maps.

What to build: every one of those overlays on the China map, by the same sources and methods, in the same boxes, with the same looks.
1. For each overlay, make China's data with the Indochina script's own method, by making that script serve a second frame (or, where that would bend it badly, a sibling in `tools/cnmap/` that calls its functions): do not copy a script. The data goes to `sources/china/`, each file's source, method, licence and limits stated in `sources/china/README.md` in the form the Indochina README uses.
2. China is some thirty times Indochina's area. Choose each layer's detail (the tiles' deepest zoom, the rivers' least size, the contours' steps, the grid of the population and the rain) so that the map reads well at the zooms the China map allows and the files stay small: aim at 40 MB or less for all of China's overlay files together, no single file over 20 MB, and say what you chose and why. The site as built is already about 1.1 GB; report the bytes you add. Extend `check`'s size rules for the map's files to China's.
3. Cities: the UN *Demographic Yearbook*'s figures for China's cities, as the Yearbooks of the 1960s print them, each with its date, table and page, in a file of the form of `sources/indochina/cities.yaml`. Find out from `sources/indochina/README.md` and the guide how the Indochina figures were read and from which copies, and read China's the same way (`notes/guide/reading-a-copy.md` for getting a copy and reading a page). Where the Yearbooks of the span print no figure for China, say so and bring no figure from elsewhere; a city is listed only with a printed figure.
4. The overlays that are tints of the units (the corps zones, today's regions) have no like on the China map; do not invent one. Say in your report what a like tint could be (the six great regions of the party's bureaus, the military regions) and what source would be needed, for the owner.
5. The overlays load as the Indochina map's do: nothing until the map nears the screen, a layer's file only when its box is ticked. They are offered wherever the China map stands (its page, the calendar's pages, the threads' pages), as the Indochina map's are offered wherever it stands.
6. Tests on the model of the maps' present ones; the golden files rewritten where the output changes as meant, the diff read; the fast suite passes; `./bib check` ends in 0 errors. The guides say what is now true.
