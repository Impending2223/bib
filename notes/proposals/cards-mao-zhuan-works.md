## Notes for the linking patch

- *Mao Zedong wenji*: the plain cites write "vol. 8, 150"; as a card, "[[w.mao-zedong-wenji]], VIII, 150". `cal.1966-08-15.china-leadership-5` cites vol. 3 ("III, 81–84") inside a Wengao parenthetical, the same set.
- *Jianguo yilai zhongyao wenxian xuanbian*: vols. 13–19 dated by the plain cites (May 1996 to Mar. 1998); vol. 20's date is not confirmed, so the card's year 1992–98 rests on vols. 1 and 19.
- *Mao Zedong he ta de mishu Tian Jiaying*: every cite in the calendar is the enlarged edition of Aug. 1996, the new card; the 1989 card (`w.bian-mao-zedong-he-tade`) stays for MacFarquhar's cites.
- *Mao Zedong shici ji*: most of the 22 clauses are the Wengao's statement of its source ("printed from *Mao Zedong shici ji*") inside a parenthetical or a Check; whether those become links is the lead's call. The cites with a page (`cal.1965-05.china-leadership-2`, 212–213) are the ones that need the card.
- Bo Yibo: the one occurrence is a link to the 1993 card with "rev. ed. (1997)" written after it; it becomes "[[w.yibo-ruogan-zhongda-juece-yu-1997]], II, 1166–1167". Note that `w.yibo-ruogan-zhongda-juece-yu` (1993) and `w.yibo-ruogan-zhongda-juece-yu-1991-93` look like one work on two cards (both rest on CiNii NCID BN11526536): a `./bib works combine`, not done here.
- *Mao zhuxi lun jiaoyu geming*: most clauses are the Wengao's statement of its source; `cal.1967-12-07.china-leadership` names the book in `c` (the event is its issue), and `cal.1967-12-06.china-leadership` has it in "To check" as text; `cal.1964-06-16.china-leadership` cites a page (18–21).
- *Mao Zedong du shehuizhuyi ...*: the one cite is to Deng Liqun's postscript in it.
