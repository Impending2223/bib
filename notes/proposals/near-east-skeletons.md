# Skeletons for the Near East, the Arab states and Israel: what they would need (Nov. 15, 2026)

The owner, Nov. 15: "What would we need to build skeletons for the Near Eastern states, and particularly the Arab states and Israel, in our span?"

A skeleton, as batches 12 and 14–16 built them: each state's or party's dated acts from its own record (a government's, a leadership's, a party's), one entry an act, cited where the calendar already holds it, with the Americans', British and Soviets' records of the same acts as cites and variants. Nothing below has been read for this; the titles marked "to check" are from memory.

## What the calendar has now (Nov. 15)
- The thread `middle-east` and its subthreads (Dimona, Iran's arms and oil, the Johnson Plan, Kuwait, Johnson's trip, Samu, the Six-Day War and its aftermath, Syria's secession, Yemen): about 20–36 entries a year, 1960–68, three in 1969, nearly all from the American side.
- Polities and leaders in `sources/polities.yaml`: the UAR, Israel, Jordan, Syria, Iraq, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Kuwait, Libya, Sudan and the Maghreb, with name entries for the heads of state and government (Nasser, Amer, Ben-Gurion, Eshkol, Meir, Dayan, Eban, Rabin, Hussein, Faisal, Saud, Qassim, the Arifs, Barzani, al-Sallal, al-Badr, Shukairy).

## What each skeleton would rest on
**A seed for every state: the chronologies of the time.**
- The *Middle East Journal*'s quarterly "Chronology" (from 1947), by country and day: the nearest thing to a ready skeleton (JSTOR).
- *Middle East Record* (the Shiloah Center, Tel Aviv): an annual day-by-day record with documents; volumes for 1960, 1961, 1967, 1968 and 1969–70 (to check which exist).
- *Arab Political Documents* (American University of Beirut, annual from 1963; to check): the Arab governments' and parties' statements, communiqués, summit resolutions.
- *Keesing's Contemporary Archives* (weekly) and the BBC's *Summary of World Broadcasts*, Part 4 (the Middle East: Cairo, Damascus, Baghdad, Amman radio, daily); FBIS's daily reports (Readex, by subscription).

**Israel: the state's own record is largely open.**
- The Israel State Archives (archives.gov.il): the government's (cabinet) minutes and the Ministerial Committee on Defense, released in part (the May–June 1967 minutes among them); the ISA's documentary publications (to check how far into the 1960s the *Documents on the Foreign Policy of Israel* run).
- The Knesset's proceedings (*Divrei ha-Knesset*), online at the Knesset's site; the elections of Aug. 15, 1961, Nov. 2, 1965 and Oct. 28, 1969.
- Medzini, ed., *Israel's Foreign Relations: Selected Documents*, vols. 1–2 (1947–74; on the Foreign Ministry's site; to check).
- Ben-Gurion's diary (the Ben-Gurion Archives, Sde Boker; digitized, to check), Sharett's diary (to 1957, before the span), Eshkol's and Dayan's papers through the biographies.
- Works: Segev, *1967*; Oren, *Six Days of War*; Cohen, *Israel and the Bomb* (Dimona); Shlaim, *The Iron Wall*; Bar-Zohar's Ben-Gurion; Gluska, *The Israeli Military and the Origins of the 1967 War*.

**The UAR (Egypt) and Syria to Sept. 1961: no open archive; the public word and the memoirs.**
- Nasser's speeches and documents (the Bibliotheca Alexandrina's Nasser archive, nasser.bibalex.org; to check what it holds and whether it answers this host).
- *al-Ahram*, Heikal's Friday column "Bi-saraha"; Heikal's books (*The Cairo Documents*; *1967: al-Infijar*).
- The Arab Socialist Union's and the National Assembly's acts; the Charter (May 1962).
- Works: Ferris (held); Kerr; Nutting's and Aburish's Nasser; Sadat's *In Search of Identity*; Fawzi's and Murtagi's memoirs of the 1967 war (Arabic; to check).

**Syria and Iraq: the Ba'th's own documents, and the coups' records.**
- *Nidal al-Ba'th* (the party's documents, Arabic, many volumes; to check): the National and Regional Commands' resolutions and congresses (the sixth National Congress, Oct. 1963; Feb. 23, 1966).
- Works: Seale, *The Struggle for Syria* and *Asad*; Rabinovich, *Syria under the Ba'th, 1963–66*; Van Dam; Batatu (Iraq, the coups of Feb. 8 and Nov. 18, 1963 and July 1968); Khadduri, *Republican Iraq* and *Socialist Iraq*; Tripp; on the Kurds, McDowall.
- The Ba'th Regional Command records (Hoover Institution) begin after the span's end for Iraq's party in power (to check).

**Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Lebanon, the Gulf.**
- Hussein's own books (*Uneasy Lies the Head*, 1962; *My "War" with Israel*, 1969); Shlaim; Ashton.
- Yemen and the Saudis: Ferris; Orkaby; Safran, *Saudi Arabia*; Vitalis; Witty's and Jones's work on the war; the British records of Aden and the Federation of South Arabia (to the withdrawal, Nov. 29–30, 1967).
- Lebanon: the Chehab era; the Intra bank crash (Oct. 1966); the Cairo Agreement (Nov. 3, 1969).

**The Palestinians.** The PLO's founding (the Palestine National Council, Jerusalem, May 28–June 2, 1964); Fatah's first claimed action (Jan. 1, 1965); Karameh (Mar. 21, 1968); Arafat's chairmanship (Feb. 1969). Sayigh (card held) is the standard work; the PLO's and Fatah's published statements in *Arab Political Documents*.

**The Arab League and the summits.** Cairo (Jan. 1964), Alexandria (Sept. 1964), Casablanca (Sept. 1965), Khartoum (Aug.–Sept. 1967), Rabat (Dec. 1969): their resolutions (the League's, and *Arab Political Documents*).

**The others' records of the region, beyond FRUS.**
- The British: the National Archives, Kew (CAB 128 and 129, PREM 11 and 13, FO 371): the Kuwait crisis (1961), Yemen, Aden, the June war. The UK pilot of `foreign-leadership-reading.md` would carry these.
- The Soviets: the Presidium and Brezhnev records already read; Naumkin, ed., *Blizhnevostochnyi konflikt 1947–1967* (Soviet documents, 2 vols., 2003; to check); the CWIHP's 1967 documents (batch 17 has some).
- The French: the DDF volumes held; the embargo of June 1967 and January 1969.
- The UN: the Security Council's records (S/RES 242, Nov. 22, 1967), UNEF's withdrawal (May 1967), UNTSO and the Mixed Armistice Commissions; the Jarring mission (from Nov. 1967).

## An order (a suggestion)
1. The *Middle East Journal*'s chronology, 1960–69, as the seed for every state: dated, sourced to the press, quick to read against the calendar.
2. Israel from its own records (the ISA's minutes, the Knesset, the documents), the one state of the region with an open archive.
3. The UAR, Syria and Iraq from the published documents and the standard works; Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Yemen and Lebanon from the works.
4. The British records, with the UK pilot.
