# The thread pass

The proposal for the owner to rule (notes/extensions/memos/volume.md, section 2): for every thread `check` [thread-size] names (more than 150 entries of its own in one year's page), the subthreads proposed and the entries moved to them. The patch: `inbox/2026-11-04-thread-pass.yaml` (not merged; `./bib patch-check` 0 errors, 0 warnings; `./bib merge --dry-run` 60 added, 2,747 edited, no conflicts; merged in a scratch copy, `./bib check` 0 errors and no [thread-size], [thread-order] or [statements] warning left).

Rules of the pass. Entries were assigned by reading their `c`. An entry carrying `short:` (an event's first entry, named in the parent's statement) stays in the parent. An entry already listed in an existing subthread of its own thread by `also:` moves to that subthread (and leaves `also:`), but not into Vietnam: Congress and the President or 1968 elections: Chicago, which were themselves over the line. What a subthread does not take stays in the parent: its headline entries and what no strand holds. A subthread with a statement names its own events in the index; a ghost (no statement) leaves its events in its parent's. "Counts" are own entries (`thread:`) a year's page, after the pass; in brackets, before.
