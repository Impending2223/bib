# Other IMF / bond-market numbers worth tracking, 1960-69

"Kept": already in indicators/. FRED spans checked Oct. 20, 2026 (CSV downloads); others marked Check.

| Measure | Reported then, and where | Available now | Kept? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monetary gold stock | Monthly: Treasury Bulletin; Federal Reserve Bulletin | FRED M1476CUSM144NNBR (NBER) | Kept (gold-stock) |
| London gold price | Daily fixing, $35.0x until the gold pool's end and the two-tier market (Mar. 17, 1968); Bulletin and IMF *International Financial Statistics* (IFS) monthly | LBMA's own data; FRED's LBMA series withdrawn. 1960s monthly from IFS or the Bulletin (Check) | No |
| Treasury yield-curve spread (10-year less 3-month bill) | Not as a spread; both legs monthly in the Bulletin and H.15 | Computable from GS10 and TB3MS, which we keep; FRED's T10Y3M only from 1982 | Legs kept; spread not |
| Average interest rate on the public debt ("computed annual interest rate") | Monthly: Treasury Bulletin, Debt Outstanding, Table 2, on the pages read for the public debt | Treasury's historical average interest rates (Check span) | No (easy next: same pages) |
| Average maturity of the marketable debt | Treasury Bulletin; ERP (Table B-66 in Feb. 1968). Bears on Operation Twist and the advance refundings | Treasury Bulletin archive only | No |
| Ownership of the debt (Federal Reserve, foreign official, trust funds) | Treasury Bulletin (Treasury Survey of Ownership), monthly; ERP Table B-65 | Treasury Bulletin; OMB Table 7.1 (Fed holdings, fiscal years) | Fed share only inside OMB's held-by-public figure |
| Corporate bond yields, Moody's Aaa and Baa | Monthly: Bulletin; ERP "Bond yields and interest rates" | FRED AAA, BAA, monthly from 1919 | No |
| Mortgage rates | FHA new-home yields in the ERP bond-yields table; FHLBB conventional rates from 1963 | FRED MORTGAGE30US only from Apr. 1971; 1960s from the ERP or FHLBB (Check) | No |
| Money stock M1 (currency plus demand deposits), and with time deposits | Bulletin, monthly; ERP Table B-50. "M2" named only from 1971 | FRED M1SL, M2SL from 1959 (today's definitions, revised) | No |
| Free (or net borrowed) reserves | Weekly and monthly: Bulletin, H.4.1; the FOMC's working gauge of "money market conditions" | FRED NFORBRES, 1959-2013 (discontinued); BORROW, TOTRESNS from 1959 | No |
| Exchange rates: sterling, the mark, the franc | Noon buying rates in New York, monthly averages in the Bulletin; IFS. Sterling devalued Nov. 18, 1967; mark revalued Mar. 1961 | FRED's H.10 series (EXUSUK, EXGEUS, EXFRUS) start 1971; 1960s from the Bulletin or IFS | No |
| U.S. reserve assets (gold, convertible currencies, IMF gold tranche) | Monthly: Treasury Bulletin, Bulletin; quarterly in the ERP's balance-of-payments tables | FRED TRESEGUSM052N (reserves less gold, IFS) from 1950 | Gold only |
| Liquid liabilities to foreign official holders (the overhang against gold) | Monthly: Treasury Bulletin, Capital Movements; Bulletin | Treasury's TIC historical tables (Check) | No (inside the two BoP balances) |
| Current account and trade balance | Quarterly: Commerce, *Survey of Current Business*; monthly merchandise trade from Census; ERP | BEA's international transactions accounts, quarterly from 1960; FRED NETEXP (NIPA net exports) from 1947, BOPBCA annual from 1960 (discontinued) | No (overall BoP balances kept) |
| Output gap | CEA, in the Economic Reports | CBO via FRED GDPPOT | Kept (gap-cea, gap-cbo) |
| General government (federal, state, local) balance and debt | NIPA government sector; Census *Governmental Finances* (fiscal years) | BEA; OMB; IMF historical public debt database (Check) | No: federal only |
| Unit labor costs and compensation per hour (the guideposts' test) | BLS productivity releases, quarterly from the mid-1960s; ERP | FRED ULCNFB, COMPNFB (nonfarm business) | No |
| Interest-rate differential with London (the Interest Equalization Tax, 1963) | Bulletin, "open market rates" abroad; covered-arbitrage tables | IFS; Bank of England (Check) | No |
