# China threads: the scheme revised on the owner's points

Nov. 25, 2026, about 7:30 p.m. PT. For the owner. This answers the six points of about 7 p.m. and sets out the scheme as it now stands. Nothing in the calendar has been changed yet. The counts are from the measurement as the audit left it (`notes/threads/china-assign-audit.md`); they are by word lists, so each is good to a few percent.

## 1. Famine

Done. The words "as the provinces reported it" are out. The thread holds the famine whole: what happened in the provinces and what the Center heard and did.

The statement now reads: "The famine: the deaths and the reports of them, the grain taken, moved and bought abroad, the relief, the Center's measures."

## 2. A title for "Polemic"

Three candidates.

- **Polemic with Moscow.** It says who the quarrel is with. It reads "China: Polemic with Moscow". This is the one I suggest if the thread stands alone.
- **Open polemic, 1961–64.** It says the years but not the other party.
- **The Nine Commentaries.** It is the name the period gave the main texts. It covers only September 1963 to July 1964, so it is too narrow.

There is a second way to fix it. The calendar's China threads hold three episodes of the quarrel: Bucharest and Moscow (1960, 158 entries), the polemic (1961–64, 425) and the border war (1969, 72). They could sit under one parent, "Split with Moscow". Then the short name is clear from its place: "China: Split with Moscow: Polemic". I suggest this, for the reason in section 4.

## 3. One primary thread, any number of secondary threads

Agreed, and it changes the scheme more than any other point. The rule now is:

- Every thread an entry fits is recorded.
- The order (episode, then person, then system) picks the primary thread, the one that goes in `thread:`. It no longer keeps an entry out of anything.
- The others go in `also:`. The site already shows an entry on the page of every thread it carries.

What this does to the three person threads, by the present rules:

| person | primary | every entry where he acts |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mao Zedong | 1,583 | 2,654 |
| Zhou Enlai | 4,366 | 6,076 |
| Liu Shaoqi | 573 | 1,094 |

The statements drop "outside the episodes". Mao's becomes: "Mao Zedong's acts: his comments and instructions, letters, talks, audiences, poems, swims, travels."

By the number of threads an entry fits: 8,064 entries fit one, 9,554 fit two, 5,424 fit three, 1,793 fit four or more, and 1,512 fit none.

One caution. The word lists for the systems were tested only on the entries they place first, and three of them tested badly there (Propaganda and culture 15 of 25, Army 18, Political-legal work 20). As second and third threads they will be wrong more often. So a system goes into `also:` only where its score is strong, and the audit reads a sample of the secondary placings as well as the primary ones.

## 5. The bomb and nuclear diplomacy

Yes, there is enough for a second thread, and I suggest the parent you describe. A rough count over every entry that touches the China threads (28,464), by word lists not yet tested by reading:

| matter | entries | years |
|---|---:|---|
| the weapon: the programme, the tests, the missiles | 160 | 1960–69, most in 1962–66 |
| the test ban treaty of 1963 | 58 | 41 of them in 1963 |
| China's proposals: no first use, a summit of all countries, complete prohibition | 23 | 1963–65 mostly |
| the conferences against atomic and hydrogen bombs (Japan) | 21 | 1960–66 |
| non-proliferation and the "nuclear umbrella" | 17 | 1961–68 |
| talk of nuclear war, "nuclear blackmail", the "paper tiger" | about 70, loosely | 1960–69 |

The four middle rows together are 112 entries, 13 of which are also in the first row. So the tree would be:

- **Nuclear weapons** (parent). Its own entries are those about the weapon in general: what Mao and others said of nuclear war, the guarding against a strike in 1969.
- **Nuclear weapons: Bomb.** The programme and the tests, as now, about 160.
- **Nuclear weapons: Nuclear diplomacy.** The test ban refused, the proposals for prohibition and no first use, the conferences against the bomb, non-proliferation. About 110.

"Nuclear diplomacy" is my suggestion for the name. "Test ban and disarmament" is the other candidate; it is plainer but leaves out the conferences. The word lists for this thread are new and need the same test by reading as the rest.
