# The reading plan's rows: one work a row, the right card

Nov. 27, 2026. This note reports a check of `notes/proposals/drive-reading-plan.tsv`. The check covered every row of more than one file and every row whose card match was weak. That was 173 rows, since the two groups overlap. The TSV was rewritten in place. Nothing else was changed, and nothing was fetched from the Drive or the web.

## The rule I used

A row is one work. The volumes, parts and copies of one work stay in one row. A row is split where its files are different books, or two editions that the catalogue holds as two cards. Where a series has one card for the whole set (the French and German diplomatic documents, the MACV histories, the nine-volume Vietnamese war history), that card is the row's card. Where each volume has its own card and the row holds several of them, the row keeps one card and stays weak.

Nothing in this check turned on what is an entry, so R0 did not come into play and I disregarded nothing.
