## What the audit found

The reader read the pages carefully and quoted them accurately; the faults were in what was done with them. About a third of the additions put another act into an entry's line (R17, R25b): an act of another day or hour, or of other persons, that the source tells beside the entry's own. Those were taken out of the line and made entries of their own, or moved to the entry already in the tree that records them. A few cites stood "on" words their page does not hold, and one name tag named the wrong man.

- Struck from the line and made new entries (R5, R17): the leaders' morning meeting and the committees' meeting of Apr. 10 (both before the rail-strike message, which now takes the record's "that afternoon"); Johnson's word on Ottinger after the debt-limit vote; Johnson's hot-line message to Kosygin of 10:58 a.m., June 10 (it was in the cease-fire's line); Hoover's note "I assume we have to do it"; Ginsburg's recall as staff director; Johnson's instruction to Bailey, Sept. 5 (it was in the line of Bailey's statement of Sept. 6); Johnson's marginal notes on McNamara's memorandum; the reception of the United Action members, Apr. 22; Liang Xingchu's report of Apr. 20; the Qiqihar army delegates' command of Mar. 18; Zhou on a chair at the Great Hall, May 13; the arrival of Xie Fuzhi, Yu Lijin and Wang Li at Wuhan, July 14, about noon; the decision of the evening of July 17 and Qi Benyu's meeting in the small hours of July 18 (both were in the line of the struggle of July 18); Zhou's charge to the Wuhan air force before leaving, July 18; Ye Jianying's and Li Fuchun's telling of Mao's rebuke, after Feb. 10; *Hongqi* no. 3 of Feb. 3.

## The reader's points

- The Chen Yi nianpu, 1184 (page image read): those not to be told are Chen Yun, Zhu De and Nie Rongzhen (陈云、朱德、聂荣臻), as in the Ye nianpu, 959. The note of `cal.1967-02-10.china-leadership` said Liu Shaoqi; corrected by edit. The reader's Check, which the edit settles, was struck.
- Yang's note 20 (I, 324; page image read): it prints 王立德, Wang Lide, but cites Wang Li's *Fansi lu*, II, 251, with a misprint in the same line (班 for 版). The account is Wang Li's; the name is a misprint. The Check on `cal.1967-07-13.china-leadership-2` now says so. MacFarquhar's words of Mao "on the eve of his departure" (206) were taken out of the line: the page does not tie them to the meeting of July 13, and the entry's Check on that point stands.
- Branch, 629, on Johnson and Carmichael: Branch has Johnson urge DeLoach to arrange leaks against Carmichael and not King; the line, from Garrow 570, has him ask how King's tie to Levison could be made public. The reader's Check stands; Hoover's comment is now its own entry.
- Drea (read in the DTIC text): 138 bears out Enthoven's Program 4 ceiling of 470,000 (May 1); 173 has 43,000 more men in the message of Aug. 3, now in the Check beside Branch's 55,000; 142 bears out Program 5, approved Aug. 14, 525,000. McNamara, *In Retrospect*: read (ch. 10). The MACV *Command History 1967*, 359 (image read): bears out "for the first time" and the evacuation of civilians, as the note has them. The Zhu De nianpu, 1964, and the Zhou *waijiao dashiji*, 517: read; Zhu De's presence and the 500,000 are now in the line of Oct. 1. MacFarquhar 207–08: read; the walk of July 14 is "late on the evening that Mao arrived", and the arrival at noon is now its own entry. DeBenedetti 194–96: the text layer reads well; it bears out the 1,100 cards, Boston's 4,000 and Oakland's 3,500 and 10,000, and gives 87 cards burned (a variant).
- The three leads: Kissinger leaving Romney's staff is entered on Aug. 31, the day of the taping, with a Check on the day (Seidman: "the day that Romney made his brainwashed remark"); Wang Li's reports to Jiang Qing and Chen Boda are entered on Aug. 8 (Zhou nianpu III, 173, "次日"). Johnson's "Are we going to be able to win this goddamned war?" is not a separate act of July 11: Dallek's note (470 n.75) cites *In Retrospect*, 283, which puts the question at the report of July 12. It is entered in the line of the July 12 meeting, with a Check giving Dallek's date. This is the one place the audit did not do what its brief said (enter it as an entry of July 11), because the sources make it the same act (R17).
