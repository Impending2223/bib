## Leads

### National
- Apollo 204: the House NASA Oversight subcommittee heard Webb, Thompson (the Review Board) and Seamans on Apr. 10 (113 Cong. Rec. D144). The Senate Aeronautical and Space Sciences Committee heard the Board on Apr. 11 (D145). Neither is in the calendar; the Board's report going to Congress (`cal.1967-04-09.space`) has no transmittal found.
- Mills's statement on federal spending (`cal.1967-10-06.economy-surcharge`): Robison, 113 Cong. Rec. 28500 (Oct. 10), has it "released last weekend", which would be Oct. 7–8, not Oct. 6. Settle the day from the press.
- S. Res. 180: the Digest has Foreign Relations approving it on Nov. 16, in executive session (D594); it was reported Nov. 21 (D602) and adopted by the Senate Nov. 30, 82–0 (FRUS V, doc. 421). The Senate vote may want an entry.
- The hearings on S. Con. Res. 44: the Digest names that resolution alone on Oct. 26 and 27 (D549, D553), with S. Res. 180 added on Nov. 2 (D565).
- The Record not yet searched for insertions: Robert Kennedy's Chicago speech of Feb. 8; Ryan's letter of Oct. 12 with its 29 cosigners; Bunker and Westmoreland on *Meet the Press*, Nov. 19; McCarthy's statement of Nov. 30; Tower on the Policy Committee's paper, May 2 (it is not in the Senate's Record that day, which prints Miller's remarks at 11437).
- State session laws of 1967, every copy found bot-checked (HathiTrust) or refused: Ark. Acts No. 93 (freedom of information); Colo. H.B. 1426 (abortion); Tenn. ch. 237 (repeal of the Butler Act); Or. H.B. 1601 (the beach bill); Nev. Stats. 1967 (corporate gaming).
- The Daily Diary's pages are open on the National Archives Catalog for only 11 days of 1967 (Apr. 11–14, Apr. 24–26, June 23–25, Dec. 23). The entries that rest on the Diary for other days (May 12, June 12, July 26, July 31 and others) wait on discoverlbj.org, which refuses this environment.
