# Oral histories at the JFK and LBJ Libraries that our sources cite (Nov. 15, 2026)

The owner, Nov. 15: "Could you give me a hyperlinks to each and every other JFK Library oral history we might want to read? Not just for this item, but for anything else in our span. And the same for LBJ Library oral histories."

Made from the witness registry (`sources/witnesses/`): every oral history at the two libraries that a work we have read cites, 369 citations, here by person. Neither library's site answers this host (jfklibrary.org and discoverlbj.org refuse it), so no link below was opened from here. Each link is a search confined to the library's own site, which should put the transcript first; the one direct link is the one the calendar already uses (Robert Kennedy, JFKOH-RFK-01). Where a source gave only a surname, the person is named from the context of the citation (Bissell, Hilsman, Lansdale and the like); a bare "Smith" is left out. "Cited" counts the citations; the dates are the interviews' where the source gives them.

Not here: the libraries' interviews no source of ours has cited. For those, the libraries' full lists matched against the calendar's name entries (see the end).
