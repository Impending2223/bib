What the book is: Robert Kennedy's press officer's memoir, 1956–68 (at Justice, 1961–65): the rackets committee (chs. 1–4, before the span), the 1960 convention as Guthman covered it for the *Seattle Times*, the Justice Department and its men, the Bay of Pigs prisoners, the missile crisis as seen from Justice, the Keogh case, the Freedom Rides, Oxford, Wallace, Johnson and Hoover, the Senate race of 1964, and the campaign of 1968. No source notes: a few footnotes, asides and figures. Guthman's own account where he was present (the Keogh meetings, the prisoners, the steel night, the Corbin call, the Senate race); his account of others' where not. The records his text prints or quotes for the passages the calendar cites are witnesses: Philip Potter's draft article on the choice of Johnson, with Robert Kennedy's changes (Apr. 1964, 78–79), and Johnson's telegram of Jan. 1–2, 1964, printed in full (253): `inbox/witnesses/2026-11-03-b3-guthman-2.yaml` (one row, one amendment adding the print as a copy), the edges in `sources/witnesses/by-work/guthman-we-band-of-brothers.yaml`.
