# Book-check: Deng Xiaoping nianpu, 1904–1974 邓小平年谱 (card w.deng-xiaoping-nianpu-1904)

## Run 2: 1963 to Jan. 1970

Reader b15c-deng-2 (Nov. 19, 2026). Patch `inbox/2026-11-19-b15c-deng-2.yaml`; witness rows `inbox/witnesses/2026-11-19-b15c-deng-2.yaml`; edges `sources/witnesses/by-work/deng-xiaoping-nianpu-1904.yaml` (under `# run 2`). Run 1 has 1960–62 (`inbox/2026-11-19-b15c-deng-1.yaml`).

**The copy.** *Deng Xiaoping nianpu, 1904–1974* 邓小平年谱（一九〇四—一九七四）, vol. 下 (III), Zhongyang Wenxian Chubanshe, 2009; the Drive PDF (753 pp., a scan; text by tesseract chi_sim on the page images). Offset: printed page = PDF page + 1328 (folio checked at PDF 416 = 1744, 611 = 1939). The nianpu is a day-by-day chronology, one item to a day, "△" for the same day as the item before; the editors' notes (注释) stand at each page's foot.

**What was read.** Every page of 1963 through Jan. 1970: printed 1743 to 1952 (PDF 415–624), by OCR, each date checked on a montage of the page headings, each doubtful name or figure on the page image. Stopped after the item "1月 ... 停发工资" of Jan. 1970 (printed 1952, PDF 624). Next run starts at Feb. 9, 1970 (letter to Wang Dongxing on Deng Lin's posting and the living allowance; PDF 624 bottom, printed 1952), and goes on to Dec. 1974.

**The notes.** Nearly all identify persons (posts held) or say who "朱, 彭, 谭" are. Those that name a document are registered: 17 new witness rows and 20 edges (one to a row already registered, Mao's comment of Dec. 12, 1963; two to rows registered by others, the Luo Ruiqing report of Apr. 30, 1966 and the Center's explanation on Lu Dingyi and Yang Shangkun). The tie to units is a `via` row (`src` ops, 12). The nianpu gives no archive and no "据…" source for its items in these years. It quotes Deng's writings and Mao's comments and cites none of them by place, so the *Wenxuan* texts the items mention ("Base on reality, look to the future"; "Build a mature, fighting party") are leads (below).

**Counts.** 676 new entries (`china-leadership`, `polity:prc`; Deng's acts and those of the leadership he attends, the visits he receives, his tours, his letters, the persecution in 1967–69); 262 with a time (`time`, `time_range` or band), 127 with a place; 389 cites on entries the calendar already holds (the Mao, Zhou, Liu, *Wengao*, MLR, MacFarquhar and Yang Shangkun records), each with units; 21 variants; 6 checks and 1 to-check on existing entries; 17 witness rows; 20 edges; 12 `src` ops.

**Variants (21).** Dates and hours: the Politburo of Jan. 8, 1963 (Zhou's call the 8th, not the 9th); the Standing Committee of Mar. 7, 1963 (morning); Deng at Pyongyang May 2–4, 1963 (the calendar had him at Hangzhou, May 3); Chengdu June 23, 1963; the Moscow talks' fifth meeting July 13, 1963 and the cables of the 13th; the Apr. 5, 1964 start of the Northwest tour and Changsha Apr. 13; the Northeast tour from June 22, 1964 (two entries); Oct. 27, 1964 (evening); the editorial meeting Nov. 17, 1964; Mar. 2, 1965 (Deng presides at the Secretariat, not Liu); the title of the Mar. 1965 article (Mar. 18); May 31, 1965 (Politburo, not the Standing Committee); Deng's departure for Romania July 17, 1965 and the arrival hour; the airport send-off Aug. 6, 1965; the talk with Luo Ruiqing Dec. 10, 1965.

**Checks on existing entries (6).** Jan. 10 and Jan. 17, 1963 (the Secretariat's sitting against the nianpu's SED talk; morning against Yang Shangkun's 4:30 p.m.); Dec. 27, 1963 (the Secretariat's session on Daqing); May 28, 1964 (which meeting Liu presides over); Oct. 1968 ("My Account", written Jun. 20–Jul. 5 in the nianpu, handed in on the plenum's eve in MacFarquhar); Oct. 19, 1969 (Xinzhou in the calendar, 赣州 in the nianpu).

**OCR faults found.** Date digits are garbled throughout (1, 11, 14, 4 and 7 swap; "1月S日" for Nov. 5; "也月" for Nov.; "蔚月", "世月" for Nov.), so every date was taken from the heading montage; names: 袁宝华 (OCR 圳宝华, 南宝华; image checked), 杜克洛 (OCR 福克洛), 邓楠 (OCR 邓杭, image checked), 陈昌奉 (OCR 陈昌素; the image not checked, the known name taken), 赣州 (OCR 闵州, image checked), 斋藤卯助 (my first reading from the OCR was wrong; corrected from the image), 林彪 (林此, 林闹, 林彤, 恩来 for 周恩来 in quotations). The nianpu's own misprint: "三届全国人大四次会议" at III, 1779 (Dec. 3, 1963), for the Second NPC (image checked; a `check` on the entry).

**Passed over (R44; R18's grounds only).** No day-by-day list was kept while reading, so the table cannot be given here and a recount by day is needed. The rules applied while reading: a transmittal or covering note of another body's paper where the nianpu gives no matter ("批转", "转发" alone) was not entered (R18); private family matters were entered only where they touch the persecution or a record of the Center (1967–70); everything else the nianpu dates and describes was entered or cited on the calendar's entry.

**Doubts.** (1) Entry grain: where the nianpu gives a day with several acts (a meeting, then a talk, then a letter), each is an entry; some are short because the nianpu names the act and seldom its matter. (2) Hours: a `time_band` only where the nianpu gives the part of the day ("上午", "下午", "晚"), the exact hour where it is printed, in UTC from Asia/Shanghai. (3) Month-level entries for 1967–69 persecution items carry an `at:` placing them in the month; the nianpu's "夏" (1969) is placed before the solstice (June 21) by the loose-date rule. (4) Variants rest on one source against another record already in the calendar; for Deng at Pyongyang, May 2–4, 1963, the nianpu agrees with Yang Shangkun's diary. (5) "Made public: 2009 (the nianpu)" is given on every new entry; the nianpu prints many of Mao's and Deng's texts known earlier (the *Wengao*, the *Wenxuan*); where a cited work printed an item before 2009 the calendar's entry carries it already.

**Persons with no name entry** (in `c` and "Names:", tagged only where a key exists): Wu Xiuquan, Yang Shangkun, Liu Ningyi, Wu Lengxi, Njoto, Kim Il Sung, Williams, Wilcox, Hakamada, Kurahara, Sanmugathasan, Baluku, Andropov, McCarra, Qiao Guanhua, Gu Mu, Pilai, Geerdelai, Amazonas, Duclos, Takenaka, Cardenas, Munoz, De la Puente, Nesti Nase, Nakajima, Gollan, Huang Zhen, Togliatti, Hoàng Văn Hoan, Li Tianyou, Liao Chengzhi, Wang Feng, Yang Jingren, Lü Dong, Wang Lin, Liu Lantao, Wang Dongxing, Xu Yefu, Qi Benyu, Zhuo Lin, Wu Yuzhang, Wei Wenbo, Qian Xinzhong, Nie Yuanzi, Huang Yongsheng, Wu Faxian, Deng Pufang, Deng Nan, Deng Lin, Deng Rong, Deng Zhifang, Xia Bogen, Wang Liang'en, Cheng Huiyuan, Yang Dongliang, Cheng Shiqing, Chen Changfeng, Nan Ping, Chen Liyun, and others.

**Leads.** (1) The *Wenxuan* texts the nianpu names (vol. 1, "Base on reality, look to the future" and "Build a mature, fighting party", and the 1963–64 reports and talks it prints in part): read the texts and set them as primary records.
