# Joseph A. Palermo, *In His Own Right: The Political Odyssey of Senator Robert F. Kennedy* (2001): the calendar against the book

Card `w.palermo-in-his-own-right` (made by this reading; Columbia University Press, 2001, the first edition). Read Oct.
29, 2026, from the owner's copy on the Drive (a text layer; no OCR needed). Printed page = PDF page − 19 throughout,
checked at pp. 2, 12, 82, 132, 182, 232, 251, and in the notes (260, 282) and the bibliography (324). Text pp. 1–258,
endnotes pp. 259–322, numbered by chapter (Prologue, 1–10, Conclusion); each note's page is the printed page of its mark
in the text, as `sources/cited-records/palermo-in-his-own-right.yaml` keys it. Patch:
`inbox/2026-10-29-book-palermo.yaml` (book-palermo). Records: `sources/cited-records/palermo-in-his-own-right.yaml`.

What the book is for the calendar: Kennedy's Senate years from the archives, the RFK Papers and the Dolan, Walinsky and
vanden Heuvel Papers at the JFK Library and the Watson, Sanders, Roche and Tom Johnson files at the LBJ Library, many of
them quoted at length. Its thesis, as the calendar states it: Kennedy's dissent on Vietnam was one of substance, held in
check by party loyalty until Tet; the Johnson White House and the Democratic National Committee worked against him and
the party's peace wing, with the FBI's help. The book has no prior citations in the series (none to pin).

How the list was made: every dated event in the text was taken in page order, pp. 1–248, and set against the entries
of its day in a fresh dump of the calendar (all threads), and against the pending patches in `inbox/`. Where the event
was in, the page was added (with a parenthetical where it bears part, and a Check where it differs); where not, an
entry was made from the page and its note's record; the rest is "Passed over", below.
