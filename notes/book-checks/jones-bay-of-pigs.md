# Jones, *The Bay of Pigs* (2008): the calendar's citations, and what the book adds

`w.jones-bay-of-pigs`. Howard Jones, *The Bay of Pigs* (Oxford, 2008), read from the owner's copy (a PDF with a good text layer), Oct. 29, 2026; the PDF deleted once its text was taken. Pages are the printed folios: PDF page = printed page + 19 throughout (checked at pp. 1, 117, 175, 200). Narrative pp. 1-174, notes 175-214. Patch: `inbox/2026-10-29-b3-jones.yaml` (`b3-jones`, 103 ops: 48 edits, 53 additions, 2 tag ops). Records: `sources/cited-records/jones-bay-of-pigs.yaml` (174 records; 123 found and read, 11 found and not read, 40 not found: mostly the Church Committee's unpublished testimony, the Kennedy Library's oral histories, Kirkpatrick's survey, interviews).

How the run went: every calendar entry citing the card (behind "To check:" or without a pinpoint) was read against the page and the note's sources; then every dated event Jones treats was looked for in the calendar and in the pending patches (`./bib find`, the inbox), and FRUS 1958-60, VI and 1961-63, X were read for each one first. Jones's notes are thorough and cite FRUS by page: each FRUS page was mapped to its document (the TEI), and the document read, so most of the book's records are the FRUS documents themselves, read and linked. The owner's ruling of Oct. 28 (the papers of government are entries) came in mid-run: the staff papers, cables and intelligence memoranda first passed over were then entered (Jan. 18, 1961; Apr. 18-19, the cables of the battle; Stevenson's of Apr. 19).

## Held as written

Most of the 48 entries edited hold and take their pages (with a parenthetical where Jones stands for part of the entry only). Six had their `c` extended or rewritten from the record Jones led to: Jan. 3, 1961 (the meeting, doc. 3); Jan. 25 (Lemnitzer's words); Mar. 16 (Burke's 50 percent, doc. 66); Apr. 14 (Bissell's cut from sixteen B-26s to eight, doc. 103 n.2); Apr. 18 (Burke's account, docs. 121, 124); Apr. 6 rewritten from doc. 84.

- `cal.1961-01-03.cuba`: Jones has Eisenhower approve the break and direct the brigade's enlargement (40); FRUS's list of those present, from a CIA chronology of May 24, 1961, does not name him. - `cal.1961-04-19.cuba`: Jones has Bissell get the President's approval for the hour's cover about 3:30 a.m. (118).
- `cal.1961-04-19.cuba-2`: Jones gives the last message as "I have nothing to fight with" (121); the entry's "nothing left to fight with" stands on its other record.
- `cal.1961-04-26.cuba`: Jones has Havana held within a week and a withdrawal in ninety days (149); the Chiefs' table, Havana D+6 to D+8, combat forces out D+60 to D+90.
- `cal.1961-04-26.cuba-2`: Jones has $100 million (149); the CIA's paper, $50 to $100 million.

- `cal.1961-05-05.cuba`: Reeves's five hundred more troops for Vietnam that day (125) against FRUS's up to 100 for the MAAG; a Check.
- New entries: `cal.1960-09.cuba-2` (the Committee's report names no September meeting with the exiles; Jones dates it late September, 25, and puts it on the Venetian Causeway); `cal.1961-01-25.cuba-3` (Jones has two calls from the White House within three days of the inauguration and Bundy's encouragement, 47-48; the Committee found the testimony in conflict, 183-84); `cal.1961-03.cuba` (Jones names the official, Orta, and has him break with Castro in January and take refuge in the Mexican embassy, 91); `cal.1961-04-13.cuba-5` (Hawkins's cable: Jones dates it Apr. 14); `cal.1961-04-09.cuba-bay-of-pigs` (Jones notes Bissell's memoir does not mention the meeting, 78: it does not).
