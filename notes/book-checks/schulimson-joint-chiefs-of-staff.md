# Shulimson, *JCS and the War in Vietnam*, pt. 1, 1960–1963: the calendar reread (stage 3)

Jack Shulimson, *The Joint Chiefs of Staff and the War in Vietnam, 1960–1968*, pt. 1 (2011), card `w.schulimson-joint-chiefs-of-staff` (alias `w.shulimson-…`). Read from DTIC's scan on archive.org (DTIC_ADA543332), by its OCR; page numbers the printed folios. The title page by the OCR reads "Jack Schulimson"; the author is Shulimson, as the card's alias has it: reported, not changed. Endnotes by chapter at the back (pp. 409 ff. of the scan).

Every pinpoint to this volume in the calendar was read against the entry's units (`./bib claims show ID`), the page sentence by sentence. The patch `inbox/2026-10-29-histories-jcs.yaml` (patch `histories-jcs`) writes each entry's claim map (`src`): this volume's rows by page and units, a parenthetical in the note where the page bears out only part of the entry, a page put right where it was off, and a `via` row for each record the page's note gives for the units (described in `sources/cited-records/schulimson-joint-chiefs-of-staff.yaml`; the found ones read, FRUS and the Public Papers cited first where they bear the entry out). Secondary works and the Pentagon Papers' narrative cited in the notes are not logged as records. Where another volume read in the same stage is cited in the same entry, the patch keeps its rows and parentheticals.

Entries mapped: 28; records logged: 57 (found and read 38, found and not read or not bearing on the entry 9, not found 10).
