## Audit, Nov. 18 (Opus; patch `inbox/2026-11-18-b15b-wulengxi.yaml`)

**Settled from the image.** The debt (p. 336): 152亿, 72亿, 79亿 rubles, that is 15.2, 7.2 and 7.9 billion ("nearly 8 billion"); p. 337: at 5亿 (500 million) a year sixteen years, at 8亿 (800 million) ten; the Politburo decides five; three-quarters of the 15.2 billion for Korean War arms at half price.
