The book's thesis, as the calendar states it: Diệm's government was viable and winning in 1962–63; the American press,
the Buddhist militants and the State Department's opponents of Diệm misread it; the coup of Nov. 1963, which Lodge
pressed past Kennedy's instructions, was the error that undid the war effort; the limits Johnson set in 1964–65 invited
Hanoi's escalation. Where its account differs from FRUS or another reading the entries carry a Check naming Moyar.

## The six citations it had

All six were in "To check" without pages; each is now read and pinned, out of To check:

- `cal.1963-05-08.vietnam` (Huế): 212–13, the units A–D, F. The book gives nine dead and fourteen wounded and
  leaves the cause open (troops, an agitator's explosives, or both); the entry's "eight killed by the consul's first
  count" and "troops and armored cars fire" stand on FRUS. Check added.

  embassy's and the CIA's reports (FRUS III, docs. 261, 275; IV, doc. 80), has them proposed by the generals (by Đôn's
  account decided by ten of them Aug. 18) and run largely by them, Đính in command in Saigon, Nhu and Tung with
  parts; the entry has "Special Forces under Nhu" from FRUS and Miller. Check added; new entry Aug. 18 (below).
- `cal.1963-08-24.vietnam` (cable 243): 236–38, B–G. Moyar adds that Kennedy's approval was conditional on Rusk's and
  Gilpatric's concurrence and that the condition was not passed on. Check added.
- `cal.1963-11-01.vietnam` (the coup): 265–73. Three Checks: Flott's account of Lodge offering Diệm a plane (Lodge's
  record, doc. 259, has none); Dunn's account of a second call from the church; Minh's signal for the killing.
