## Doubts

- The notes' misprints, kept as printed in the rows with a gloss: Zhou to Ne Win "December 22, 1960" (1959); "June 15, 1965" (1962); "October 24 [and 25], 1964" for the Faure talks (1963); "October 30, 1965" twice (1964); Zhou's Tenth Plenum speech "September 16, 1962" (the plenum sat Sept. 24–27); Guevara's talk "October 18, 1960" (a `check`); "MBP-B," "CMFA," "GPA," "Rui Ruiqing," "Chen Yuan zhuan," "LNP-B"; MNP-B "4:358" for 5:358. The text's own: "Li Fuchen" (Li Fuchun); "the spring of 1962" for the Tenth Plenum; "October 12" for Nov. 12, 1964 (a Check on the new entry); "the State Planning Council."
- Cards that do not fit the book's citation exactly, the edge's `pin` saying so: *Luo Ruiqing zhuan* (the book's 1991 printing, the card 1996); Gopal's *Nehru* (the book's one-volume Oxford edition of 2014, the card vol. 3, 1984); Li Lianqing's *Da waijiaojia Zhou Enlai* (the book cites vols. 4 and 5; the card one volume); ZZ-B cited "3:588" (two volumes); Bo Yibo and CYNP cited at times without a volume. The book dates ZNP-B 1998 (the card 1997). *Wang Jiaxiang nianpu* ("Xu et al.") has no fuller reference in the book: a study row, not a card.
- Mao's words on Zhou's Eighth Congress speech "two days before the meeting ended": the Mao chronology has them June 14, 1960; left in the cite's parenthetical, no variant.
- Pan Zili's report "that day" (July 21 or 22, 1962): a Check on the entry.
- The Kunming call (Dec. 30 by the book's "a few hours later," Dec. 31 by Zhou's itinerary): dated Dec. 31 with a Check.
- Persons named in the new entries with no name entry: Ye Jizhuang, Lei Renmin, Yang Chengwu, Wang Bingnan, Yang Shangkun, Pan Zili, Zhang Zhizhong, Fu Zuoyi, Zhang Xiqun, Chiang Ching-kuo, Li Qingquan, Jacques de Beaumarchais, Wu Xiuquan, Wu Jieping, Nguyễn Chí Thanh; in the cites besides, Song Renqiong, Tian Jiaying, Liu Xiao, Hoxha.
