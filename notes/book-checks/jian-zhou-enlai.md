**Rules for run 2.**
1. Anchor a note by the text page that carries its call, and the note's number: "518 n.3". Where a sentence runs over a page break, use the page the numeral stands on. Never the notes' own page.
2. Letter every note of more than one item in the note's own order: "520 n.7a", "n.7b". Every item counts, cards included. Write the same letter in the edge, in the "(cited by …)" of the "To check:" line, and in the witness's `where`.
3. Put on each entry's "To check:" and `via` rows only the items of the note for its own act. A note listing several days' records gives each day its own record.
4. Before you make a card, search the catalogue by normalized title: words joined or split, Pinyin with or without spaces. Search the author too. `works new` misses "Womende" against "Women de". For a book cited in several volumes, make a set card. Give an edition its own card with `edition_of`. Give a translation its own card with `translates`.
5. Card ids are surname first for Chinese authors (w.wang-bingnan-…). Put the translation in `trans`, not `n`. A nianpu's compilers are its editors. Add characters only from a card or entry in the repo.
6. "Made public:" takes the year of the volume actually cited. If that year is unknown, write "Made public: Check (…)". Do not take a sibling volume's year.
7. Before adding an act, search the tree for its words and persons over the whole month, and over month- and year-dated entries too. The chronology often holds the act without the person's name ("persons concerned"). Where it does, cite the entry. Correct its visibility by a `tag` op.
8. Date an act by the record the note names, not by the narrative's "that day". Where they disagree, enter a Check.
9. A misprinted date the book's own sequence refutes is not a variant. Give it in the cite's parenthetical ("printed 'June 6'").
10. Look up each witness row against every `inbox/witnesses/` file by archive file number and day before writing it.
