## Held in part, or the book gives another date, figure or account

- `cal.1960-07-12.elections-1960` (the Democratic platform). The book treats only the civil rights plank: Bowles and
  Wofford's "maximum" plank, which Robert Kennedy told the organizers to back "unequivocally" (215-16). Nothing on the
  minority report, Medicare or growth. Ruling: holds in part; 215-16.
- `cal.1961-05-06.robert-kennedy` (Law Day, Athens). Schlesinger, 293, quotes "We will not stand by and be aloof." The
  speech's text at justice.gov (the PDF the entry cites, read) has "stand by or be aloof", as the entry does. Ruling:
  the entry stands; 293-94.
- `cal.1964-01-17.robert-kennedy` (Sukarno in Tokyo). Schlesinger, 634: "He met Sukarno in Tokyo on January 16."
  FRUS 1964-68, XXVI, doc. 17 (Tokyo, Jan. 17, 8 p.m.) reports the hour and a half's talk and Sukarno's agreement; doc.
  19 (Jan. 18, 3 p.m.) the Saturday breakfast. Ruling: the entry's Jan. 17-18 stands; docs. 17 and 19 added as its record;
  633-35.
- `cal.1964-08-25.elections-1964` (the Senate candidacy). Schlesinger, 666: Kennedy announced "On August 22, 1964, two
  days before the Atlantic City convention." The statement the entry cites (justice.gov) is dated Aug. 25, 1964, and the
  convention opened Aug. 24. Ruling: the entry stands; 666-67.
- `cal.1965-11-10.robert-kennedy` (South America). The entry had no dates by country and left out Venezuela.
  Schlesinger, 694-98: left Miami for Lima Nov. 10; to Chile Nov. 13 (Frei the next day, Concepción the day after);
  Argentina "the next morning" for forty-eight hours; to Brazil Nov. 20, Bahia Nov. 22, Rio Nov. 24, the Amazon; "two
  busy days in Caracas"; "At the end of the month they were back in Washington." Ruling: `c` now gives Peru from Nov. 10,
  Chile from Nov. 13, Brazil from Nov. 20, Venezuela, and the return at the month's end; the Check on the end date and
  Argentina's dates stays. 692-98 (the State Department's briefing, 693-94).
- `cal.1966-06-04.africa` (South Africa). The entry: Luthuli visited at Groutville (Check). Schlesinger, 747: Kennedy
  "left by helicopter before dawn the next day for Luthuli's farm"; the place is not named. Ruling: `c` now "visited on
  his farm, by helicopter (Groutville: Check)"; 743-48.
- `cal.1966-06-28.states-and-cities-nyc` (the Surrogate primary). The book (750-55) tells the campaign (Rose's tip, the
  reformers' meeting of May 22, the motorcade, "Silverman won easily, even almost carrying Harlem") but gives neither
  the date nor the vote; it calls it a "special election for the surrogate court." Ruling: holds; the entry's Checks
  stay; 750-55.
- `cal.1966-12-09.poverty-nyc` (Bedford-Stuyvesant). Schlesinger, 788: "On December 10, 1966 ... a thousand people
  gathered in the auditorium of a Bedford-Stuyvesant school." The entry has Dec. 9; the New York Preservation Archive
  Project's page also gives Dec. 9. No primary record reached. Ruling: "Check: Schlesinger gives Dec. 10" added; 786-88.
- `cal.1967-02-06.robert-kennedy` (the Newsweek story and the meeting). The entry: Katzenbach present (Check).
  Schlesinger, 767-68: the meeting "Monday afternoon, February 6," Katzenbach and Rostow present; the Newsweek story to
  the Times on Sunday evening, Feb. 5; the day of the Manac'h talk not given. Ruling: Rostow added to `c`, Names and tags,
  the Check kept (the book's account rests on Mankiewicz's and Edelman's recollections, 768); 764-69.
- `cal.1967-04-10.poverty` (Jackson and the Delta). Schlesinger, 794: Marian Wright testified at the subcommittee's

- `cal.1968-03-14.robert-kennedy` (the commission). The entry: Kennedy among its members (Check the terms).
