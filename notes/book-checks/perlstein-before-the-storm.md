## The twenty-three citations it had

Eight hold and take their pages: July 22, 1960 (81–85); Apr. 8, 1963 (192–94); Jan. 3, 1964 (258–61); Jan. 7, 1964
(269–70, 274); July 12, 1964 (377–78); July 13, 1964 (379–80); Oct. 20–22, 1964 (494–96); Oct. 27, 1964 (499–503).
Eleven hold in part and take their pages with a parenthetical saying what the book stands for. Three take a Check.
One the book does not treat. The cases that are not a plain "holds":

- `cal.1960-07-22.elections-1960`. Holds. Perlstein, 81, has Nixon arrive in New York "Friday, July 23"; July 23,
  1960 was a Saturday, and his next page dates the statement "Dawn, Saturday, July 23." A misprint; the entry stands.
- `cal.1961-06-19.welfare-ny` (Newburgh). The points, the State's ruling of June 22 and July 15 (130–31), and
  Goldwater's meeting with Mitchell in Washington (133, citing the *Herald Tribune*, July 19, 1961) hold. The
  council's vote, four ayes, is the minutes' alone.
- `cal.1961-10-08.goldwater` (the Chicago motel). The meeting, the date, the secrecy, White's three hours (177–79)
  hold. Perlstein: twenty-six invited, twenty-two came (178); White "didn't dare mention the name Goldwater" (179);
  the strategy without the Northeast, the map, at the next meeting, in December (179–80). The entry's "the South and
  the Midwest enough, without New York and New England" belongs to December by his account. Added "Check: Perlstein,
  178, gives twenty-six invited and twenty-two present; Goldwater's name not spoken, 179." The entry's own "Check the
  number" stays.
- `cal.1961-11-18.goldwater` ("where the ducks are"). Perlstein, 190, gives the words, to Georgia Republican
  activists, undated in the text (his note cites the *New York Times* of November [1961]). Holds for the words; the
  date and the *Courier-Journal* are not his. The entry's Check stays.
- `cal.1962-03-07.goldwater` (Madison Square Garden). The rally, the crowd turned away, Tower the next-to-last
  speaker, Goldwater last (163–65). Thurmond and "the wave of the future" are not in Perlstein.
- `cal.1963-04-08.elections-1964`. Holds: "It was April 8, 1963," the Mayflower, O'Donnell and Mrs. Harrington,
  White behind the scenes, Goldwater not endorsing (192–94). The entry's "Check the date" taken out on his date.
- `cal.1963-07-04.elections-1964` (the Armory rally). The rally, the crowd, the buses, White's slides (222).
  Perlstein names Fannin, Zimbalist and Chill Wills on the platform, not Tower; no police count. Goldwater rides in
  the Prescott parade every July 4 (194). Parentheticals so.
- `cal.1963-11-22.goldwater` (Muncie). Muncie, the funeral (248); Kennedy's talk of debating at every stop (253–54).
  Perlstein has Goldwater tell Kitchel "within days" to spread word he would not run (253); the entry has December,
  by Goldwater's memoir. Added the Check.
- `cal.1964-02-29.elections-1964-state-conventions` (Oklahoma's convention). Not treated: Perlstein's Oklahoma
  convention is the resolution of Apr. 20, 1963 (198). Taken out of the To check.
- `cal.1964-03-10.elections-1964` (New Hampshire). Lodge's write-in win with 35 percent holds (297–98). Perlstein
  gives Goldwater 23 percent; the *Manual*'s 22.3, cited first, stands.
- `cal.1964-05-24.goldwater` (defoliation). The interview, Sunday, May 24, Howard K. Smith, the words (346–47). The
  later "competent military people" is not in Perlstein.
- `cal.1964-06-19.civil-rights-bill`. Goldwater's reasons, his speech and vote, Dirksen's close of June 19 (363–64).
  The roll calls are the Record's.
- `cal.1964-09-03.goldwater` (Prescott). Prescott, where he always opened (409–11); the courthouse steps are the
  1952 opening's (25), not said of 1964.
- `cal.1964-09-07.elections-1964`. The spot (412–14) and Cadillac Square (418–20). Johnson's Seattle speech is not
  treated (Perlstein's Seattle, 423, is Goldwater's).
- `cal.1964-09.goldwater` (*Fact*). Ginzburg and the poll (438–39), no figures. Perlstein calls it the magazine's
  "first and only issue"; *Fact* ran to 1967. Not carried into the entry.
- `cal.1964-10-19.goldwater` (Jenkins). Perlstein treats Johnson's side (489–93): Jenkins in Goldwater's reserve
  squadron (491). Not Goldwater's request to Hoover. Against the entry's "no more than an allusion ... his staff
  told to make no use of the arrest": "Goldwater's snide references to the 'curious crew' ... were stepped up" and
  the bumper stickers (493). Added the Check.
- `cal.1964-11-03.elections-1964`. Goldwater's six States and the popular vote (510–13); the House and Senate
  figures are the Clerk's.
