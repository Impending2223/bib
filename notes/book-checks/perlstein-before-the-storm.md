- `cal.1960-07-22.elections-1960`. Holds. Perlstein, 81, has Nixon arrive in New York "Friday, July 23"; July 23, 1960 was a Saturday, and his next page dates the statement "Dawn, Saturday, July 23." A misprint; the entry stands.

Perlstein: twenty-six invited, twenty-two came (178); White "didn't dare mention the name Goldwater" (179); the strategy without the Northeast, the map, at the next meeting, in December (179–80). Added "Check: Perlstein, 178, gives twenty-six invited and twenty-two present; Goldwater's name not spoken, 179." The entry's own "Check the number" stays.
