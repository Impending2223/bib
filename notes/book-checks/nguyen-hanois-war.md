- `cal.1960-09-05.vietnam` (the Third Congress): holds, 51–53 (Sept. 5–10; Hồ re-elected chairman; Lê Duẩn first
  secretary; "Develop the North to give to the South").
- `cal.1965-02-07.vietnam` (Pleiku): 74–75 give the attack in two sentences (Bundy's "streetcar"); cited "(the
  attack)".
- `cal.1967-01-04.vietnam` (Rusk to Dobrynin): not treated (no Sunflower in the book). Taken out.
- `cal.1967-12-29.vietnam` (Nguyễn Duy Trinh's statement of Dec. 29): not treated. Taken out. The book does treat
  Trinh's statement of January 1967 (90), and is added to `cal.1967-01-28.vietnam`, the Burchett interview.
- `cal.1968-01-30.vietnam` (Tet opens): 108, 113 hold for the offensive's opening, "some of the central region
  provinces launched their attacks one day early"; cited for that.
- `cal.1968-04-03.vietnam` (Hanoi's statement): 117, the announcement of Apr. 3; holds.
- `cal.1968-05-13.vietnam` (the first session): 115 (May 13, the Majestic), 121 (Thủy, Lâu, Harriman, Vance); holds.
- `cal.1968-12-03.vietnam` (the shape of the table): 129 has only "squabble over ... the shape of the table" after the
  election; cited for that.
- `cal.1969-01-15.vietnam` (the round table agreed): not treated beyond the line at 129 cited under Dec. 3. Taken out.

The cases that are not a plain "holds":

- `cal.1965-03-28.vietnam` (the Eleventh Plenum's resolution). Nguyen names the March meeting twice differently: "the
  Eleventh Plenum in March" (75), "the special session of the Tenth Plenum on 25–27 March 1965" (76). Check added.
- `cal.1964-09.vietnam` (Thanh sent south). Nguyen, 63, 65, has Thanh appointed to COSVN after the Ninth Plenum, leaving
  in mid-1964 and at COSVN's headquarters in Tây Ninh by October (73); Asselin, 198–99, has him go in September after
  the Politburo of Sept. 25–29. Check added.
- `cal.1967-07.vietnam-tet` (the General Staff's plan put to the Politburo). Nguyen, 101–2, dates the meeting July 18–19,
  Lê Duẩn and Văn Tiến Dũng presenting, Hồ Chí Minh against. Added to `c` as Nguyen's account.
- `cal.1967-10.vietnam-tet` (Tet set). Nguyen, 105: the Politburo reviewed the plan Oct. 20–24, Trường Chinh presiding,
  Lê Duẩn absent, and put off approving the general uprising. Not the entry's claim (the offensive moved to Tet); Check
  added, the book not cited as a source for it.
- `cal.1968-01.vietnam-tet` (the Fourteenth Plenum, "in Hanoi"). Nguyen, 108: "the Fourteenth Plenum took place outside
  the capital, in Hoa Binh province, in early January." Check added; the `place:hanoi` tag left until it is settled.
- `cal.1968-05-03.vietnam`. Nguyen, 118, from Hanoi's file of May 3: talks "on 5 May or a few days after." The entry's
  May 10 rests on FRUS 1964–68, VI, doc. 221 and the President's news conference; it stands. Nguyen cited for Hanoi's
  proposal of Paris only.
- `cal.1968-09-07.vietnam` (the first private meeting, three hours of Thọ). Nguyen, 122, dates it Sept. 8 at
  Vitry-sur-Seine (Hanoi's record, n.48) and, on the same page, Thọ's lecture Sept. 7, "for nearly an hour." FRUS VII,
  doc. 7 stands; Check added.
- `cal.1967-07-27.vietnam` (new). Nguyen dates Hoàng Minh Chính's arrest July 27 (91) and July 25 (102). The entry takes
  27; Check added.
