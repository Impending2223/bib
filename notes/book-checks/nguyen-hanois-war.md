  the Politburo of Sept. 25–29. Check added.
- `cal.1967-07.vietnam-tet` (the General Staff's plan put to the Politburo). Nguyen, 101–2, dates the meeting July 18–19,
  Lê Duẩn and Văn Tiến Dũng presenting, Hồ Chí Minh against. Added to `c` as Nguyen's account.
- `cal.1967-10.vietnam-tet` (Tet set). Nguyen, 105: the Politburo reviewed the plan Oct. 20–24, Trường Chinh presiding,
  Lê Duẩn absent, and put off approving the general uprising. Not the entry's claim (the offensive moved to Tet); Check
  added, the book not cited as a source for it.
- `cal.1968-01.vietnam-tet` (the Fourteenth Plenum, "in Hanoi"). Nguyen, 108: "the Fourteenth Plenum took place outside
  the capital, in Hoa Binh province, in early January." Check added; the `place:hanoi` tag left until it is settled.
- `cal.1968-05-03.vietnam`. Nguyen, 118, from Hanoi's file of May 3: talks "on 5 May or a few days after." The entry's
  May 10 rests on FRUS 1964–68, VI, doc. 221 and the President's news conference; it stands. Nguyen cited for Hanoi's
  proposal of Paris only.
- `cal.1968-09-07.vietnam` (the first private meeting, three hours of Thọ). Nguyen, 122, dates it Sept. 8 at
  Vitry-sur-Seine (Hanoi's record, n.48) and, on the same page, Thọ's lecture Sept. 7, "for nearly an hour." FRUS VII,
  doc. 7 stands; Check added.
- `cal.1967-07-27.vietnam` (new). Nguyen dates Hoàng Minh Chính's arrest July 27 (91) and July 25 (102). The entry takes
  27; Check added.
