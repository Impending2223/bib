### Doubts

- The 1965 report dates none of its meetings. The eight meetings are year-dated (`date: '1965'`, "1965, June–July", `at: before 1965-07`) except Kim Il Sung's (July, after July 4, from the paper's own reference to the American national-day party) and the report (after July 13, Zhou Enlai's talk the last on the trip). The calendar holds Liu on June 3 and Zhou on July 13; the report does not name those days, so both edits carry a Check. The eight `vietnam-hanoi` entries of 1965 raise the merge's duplicate flag, though each is a distinct talk (`./bib resolve ... --take keep`). The Soviet talks may fall in July (the Presidium's July 8 on Brezhnev's report; the aid agreement of July 10); then the `at` is wrong for them.
- Six existing entries are edited (an added sentence of `c`, a new primary record, a re-lettered map): Oct. 9, 1964 (base `bf51ff16`), June 3, 1965 (`fd03b406`), July 13, 1965 (`4f467da1`), June 27, 1966 (`76a9bdb9`), Dec. 15, 1966 (`ca49bbb5`), July 4, 1968 (`746cdb64`). Any other patch touching them conflicts at the merge. Two of the six hold several events in one entry ("Dec. 15–16", "July 4–10"): not split here, since the brief says to cite where the calendar holds the act; the owner may want them split.
- The July 20, 1965 record: the Archive's title and date give July 25; the text says July 20; the Ninth Congress ran July 19–24. Entered on the 20th. The record names no place beyond the Soviet delegation's residence near Bucharest (`place: bucharest`).
- The H1 report (May 30, 1965) names no dateline and no addressee; `place: pyongyang` is from the Archive's heading ("Hungarian Embassy in the DPRK"). It repeats what Romanian, East German and Cuban ambassadors told the ambassador; the Chinese ambassador's own words are not in it.
- Dec. 16, 1966: the record lists Brezhnev, Kosygin, Suslov, Andropov and Gromyko; the existing entry has "Kosygin and Andropov with him" (a variant, not a correction).
- The July 6, 1968 minutes have four unreadable pages, flagged by the translator, at the point where Nasser's reply on a defense agreement passes to the press.
- The Albanian record of June 27, 1966 places the talk before Zhou's rally speech "this afternoon"; the existing entry's 9:30 a.m. (the Chinese book) is not contradicted.
- Visibility: the Soviet–Vietnamese, Soviet–Romanian and Soviet–Korean records take `vis:only` of the two polities; the Albanian one adds `vis:only:prc+albania` to the Tirana entry; no entry here is a talk with a party out of power.
