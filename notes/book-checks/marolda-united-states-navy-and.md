# Marolda and Fitzgerald, *From Military Assistance to Combat* (1986): the calendar reread (stage 3)

Edward J. Marolda and Oscar P. Fitzgerald, *The United States Navy and the Vietnam Conflict*, vol. II, *From Military Assistance to Combat, 1959–1965* (1986), card `w.marolda-united-states-navy-and`. Read Oct. 28, 2026, from archive.org's copy (`unitedstatesnavy02maro`): its OCR text by page, each page's folio from the running head (leaf less 20 throughout the text), the footnotes' numbers read off the page images where the OCR mangled them. Footnotes at the foot of each page: the Navy's files (OPNAV memoranda, CNO minutes, fleet messages, ships' logs and reports, oral histories), CINCPAC's histories, *U.S.-Vietnam Relations*.

Every pinpoint to this volume in the calendar was read against the entry's units (`./bib claims show ID`), the page sentence by sentence. The patch `inbox/2026-10-29-histories-af-navy.yaml` (patch `histories-af-navy`) writes each entry's claim map (`src`): this volume's rows by page and units, a parenthetical in the note where the page bears out only some of the entry, and a `via` row for each record the page's note gives for the units (described in `sources/cited-records/marolda-united-states-navy-and.yaml`; the unread ones join the note's "To check:"). Secondary works and the Pentagon Papers' narrative cited in the notes are not logged as records.
