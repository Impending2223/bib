## Disagreements with the American record the entries give (Check added, no side taken)

- `cal.1963-01-02.vietnam` Ấp Bắc: entry, five helicopters, three Americans killed, a VC battalion. Book: 450 casualties
  incl. nine Americans, 16 aircraft shot down or badly damaged; the defenders two companies and a platoon (119–20,
  460 n.50).
- `cal.1964-08-02.vietnam` Tonkin: entry, the Maddox and planes hit all three boats. Book: the boats drove off the
  Maddox and shot down one aircraft (132).
- `cal.1964-08-04.vietnam`: the book records no engagement on Aug. 4 and calls the incident fabricated (132). Cited, no Check.
- `cal.1964-08-05.vietnam` Pierce Arrow: entry, two planes lost. Book: eight of 64 sorties (132, 461 n.20).
- `cal.1964-11-01.vietnam` Biên Hòa: entry, 4 Americans killed, 27 aircraft destroyed or damaged. Book: 59 aircraft
  destroyed, hundreds of pilots and technicians killed (136).
- `cal.1965-02-07.vietnam` Pleiku: entry, 7 killed, 109 wounded. Book: 100 killed, 20 aircraft (142).
- `cal.1965-05-29.vietnam-operations` Ba Gia: entry, May 29–30. Book: opened the evening of May 25; 51 days; 2,054
  (144–45, 462 n.32).
- `cal.1965-08-18.vietnam` Starlite: entry, 614 enemy dead, 45 Marines killed. Book: 900 Americans killed or
  wounded, 13 aircraft, 22 armored vehicles (158).
- `cal.1965-11-14.vietnam` Ia Drang: entry, 79 Americans at X-Ray. Book, for the Plei Me campaign Oct. 19–Nov. 19:
  1,700 Americans and 1,270 South Vietnamese, 59 helicopters (159).
- `cal.1966-01-24.vietnam` Masher: entry, Jan. 25–Mar. 6. Book: Jan. 28–Mar. 7; ~8,000 incl. 500 American and Korean;
  236 aircraft (178–79, 463 n.7).
- `cal.1966-03-09.vietnam-operations` A Shau: entry, the 95th Regiment, 325th Division. Book: taken by province and
  district local forces breaking a sweep of Feb. 17–Mar. 3 (180).
- `cal.1966-09-14.vietnam-operations` Attleboro: entry, from Sept. 14; ends Nov. 25. Book: from a landing Nov. 4 to the
  withdrawal Nov. 26; 22,000 Americans (196, 465 n.28).
- `cal.1967-01-08.vietnam` Cedar Falls: dates agree (Jan. 8–26); book, over 3,000 casualties (196–97).
- `cal.1967-02-22.vietnam` Junction City: book, 45,000 men; losses 14,000, 112 guns, 775 vehicles, 160 aircraft; its
  own dead 280 (198–99, 465 n.32). Entry `cal.1967-05-14` gives 282 American dead (Rogers): different sides' dead.
- `cal.1967-03-20.vietnam-operations` Ấp Bàu Bàng: entry, from 10:50 p.m. Mar. 19, 227 enemy dead, 3 Americans killed.
  Book: Mar. 19; 400 and 63 armored vehicles (198).
- `cal.1967-03-21.vietnam-operations` Suối Tre: entry, Mar. 21, 31 Americans killed. Book: Mar. 20; the 3d Brigade,
  4th Division, over 1,200 casualties (198–99). The book's place name for it is not the entry's.
- `cal.1967-03-31.vietnam-operations` Ấp Gù: entry, Haig's 1/26 and 1/16 Infantry. Book: an infantry and an artillery
  battalion of the 2d Brigade, 1st Division (199). Check which brigade.
- `cal.1967-10-29.vietnam-operations` Lộc Ninh: book, a campaign Oct. 26–Dec. 5, 4,700 incl. 3,000 Americans (212).
- `cal.1968-01-21.vietnam` Khe Sanh: entry, before dawn Jan. 21. Book: the Route 9 front opened the night of Jan. 20;
  siege from Feb. 10; 17,000 incl. 13,000 Americans and 480 aircraft over 170 days (216–17, 222, 230).
- `cal.1968-01-31.vietnam-tet`: the entry's "Check the hours" for the embassy: the book has military police on the roof
  at 9 a.m., Jan. 31; seventeen attackers (220). Not settling: a second account, not the record.
- `cal.1968-02-07.vietnam` Lang Vei: entry, Feb. 7 before dawn. Book: Feb. 6; 400 killed incl. three American advisers,
  253 captured (222, 467 n.19).
- `cal.1968-02-24.vietnam` Huế: entry, the flag raised Feb. 24. Book: withdrawal ordered Feb. 23, after 25 days (219).
- `cal.1968-04-01.vietnam-2` Pegasus: book, 17 battalions; relief force withdrawn Apr. 16 (222–23, 467 n.21). The
  entry `cal.1968-04-08.vietnam-khe-sanh` ("Check the day") is not answered by the book.
- `cal.1968-04-19.vietnam-operations` Delaware: book, almost 1,000 casualties, Apr. 19–May 16 (229).
- `cal.1968-05-25.vietnam-may-offensive` ("Check the day"): the book gives May 25 (228). Supports, does not settle.
- `cal.1968-06-27.vietnam` ("Check the day of the announcement"): the book has it June 26, the withdrawal 17 days, the
  base its own July 15 (229). A second date, not the record: the Check stays.

## Internal inconsistencies of the book

- Full-time forces in the South at the end of 1966: 219,640 (192; 166,248 in 1965, 191) against 204,000 (211).
- Nam Bắc (Laos): the campaign "ended on 27 January 1967" (214), plainly 1968.
- Tet's first night: the text dates the general offensive the night of Jan. 29–30 (217), the note explains the
  postponement to Jan. 30–31 and the four provinces not told (466 n.15).
