## Internal inconsistencies of the book

- Full-time forces in the South at the end of 1966: 219,640 (192; 166,248 in 1965, 191) against 204,000 (211).
- Nam Bắc (Laos): the campaign "ended on 27 January 1967" (214), plainly 1968.
- Tet's first night: the text dates the general offensive the night of Jan. 29–30 (217), the note explains the
  postponement to Jan. 30–31 and the four provinces not told (466 n.15).
