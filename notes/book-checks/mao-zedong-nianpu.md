**Doubts.** (1) The nianpu's accounts are of the 2013 compilers, from the Central Committee's archives; the dates are the compilers', and where a day carries two acts the entries split them. (2) Entries on the Politburo's decisions in 1965 on Vietnam (Mar. 22) rest on the single paragraph; the calendar's later entries (the engineers, June 6; the antiaircraft divisions, Aug. 1) are not cited from them (a patch here may not point forward). (3) The Feb. 13, 1964 forum, the Jan. 4–5, 1965 and Jan. 14 speeches and others quote Mao at length; the entries compress them to the sense, in translation, quoting no sentence of the Chinese beyond a word or two. (4) Same-day entries (eleven dates) are distinct events and left as such; the merge may flag them. (5) The nianpu's "see" notes and footnotes give the names of the Chinese transliterations of foreign delegates (Ke Fulan, Oke, Pu Jinzhe and so on); each is marked Check where the reading is a guess. (6) `Made public: 2013.` is put on every inside act; the nianpu may have drawn on documents printed earlier (the *Wengao*, vols. 10 and 11; the *Xuanji*): not checked. (7) The Jan. 9, 1965 Snow talk has Snow's own contemporary account; not compared.
