# Poole, *JCS and National Policy*, vol. 8, 1961–1964: the calendar reread (stage 3)

Walter S. Poole, *The Joint Chiefs of Staff and National Policy, 1961–1964* (2011), card `w.poole-joint-chiefs-of-staff`. Read from the Defense Technical Information Center's scan on archive.org (DTIC_ADA551509), by its OCR, every page number the printed folio. The title page (checked on the page image) prints the author "Willard S. Poole"; the card has Walter S. Poole: reported, not changed. Endnotes at the back (pp. 313 ff.): JCS papers by JMF file, CM and JCSM memoranda, FRUS, the Public Papers.

Every pinpoint to this volume in the calendar was read against the entry's units (`./bib claims show ID`), the page sentence by sentence. The patch `inbox/2026-10-29-histories-jcs.yaml` (patch `histories-jcs`) writes each entry's claim map (`src`): this volume's rows by page and units, a parenthetical in the note where the page bears out only part of the entry, a page put right where it was off, and a `via` row for each record the page's note gives for the units (described in `sources/cited-records/poole-joint-chiefs-of-staff.yaml`; the found ones read, FRUS and the Public Papers cited first where they bear the entry out). Secondary works and the Pentagon Papers' narrative cited in the notes are not logged as records. Where another volume read in the same stage is cited in the same entry, the patch keeps its rows and parentheticals.

Entries mapped: 37; records logged: 34 (found and read 16, found and not read or not bearing on the entry 5, not found 13).
