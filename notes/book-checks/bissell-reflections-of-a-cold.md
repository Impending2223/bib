# Bissell, *Reflections of a Cold Warrior* (1996): the calendar's citations, and Bissell's account

`w.bissell-reflections-of-a-cold`. Richard M. Bissell Jr., with Jonathan E. Lewis and Frances T. Pudlo, read from the
owner's copy (a PDF of 276 pages with a text layer), Oct. 29, 2026; the PDF deleted once its text was taken. Pages are
the printed folios, at the foot of each page: PDF page = printed page + 8 throughout (checked at pp. 152, 192, 238,
251). Read: ch. 5 from 1959 (pp. 121-40), ch. 6 (141-51), ch. 7 (152-204), and the notes (251-61). Patch:
`inbox/2026-10-29-b3-bissell.yaml` (`b3-bissell`, 55 ops: 7 edits, 48 additions, the owner's rulings of Oct. 28 and
Oct. 28 late applied from the start). Records: `sources/cited-records/bissell-reflections-of-a-cold.yaml` (66 records:
6 found and read, 15 found and not read, 45 not found: the Eisenhower Library's files, the Lewis interviews, Bissell's
papers).

A memoir of the operator, written late with Lewis's research: its notes cite the Eisenhower Library's files and
FRUS by page, and many of its Cuba pages restate the record already in the calendar; Bissell's own acts and talks
(Palm Beach, the cable to Guatemala, Robert Kennedy's briefing, the night of Apr. 16, the calls to Hyannis Port, his
leaving) are entered as "By Bissell's account," the record first wherever FRUS prints it (the Willauer memorandum,
SNIE 85-3-60, the luncheon of Oct. 3, 1961, his answer to the Inspector General).

## Disagreements (Checks written)

- `cal.1960-08-25.congo`: Bissell has the President himself express the "extremely strong feelings" at a meeting with
  the Special Group (143); by the minutes, Gray reports them.
- Jan. 13, 1960 (`cal.1960-01-13.cuba-bay-of-pigs`, in `b3-jones-more`): Bissell has the Special Group decide formally
  that the regime "must be overthrown" (153); the minutes (S. Rep. No. 94-465, 93): contingency planning "might be in
  order," no "quick elimination." A Check there.
- Jan. 3, 1961: Bissell (161-62) puts Eisenhower at the meeting and quotes him ("of course go in with our own forces";
  "manufacturing something"), from Gray's memorandum of Jan. 9; FRUS's list of those present does not name him (the
  Jones book-check). For the owner: the Check in `cal.1961-01-03.cuba` stands; Bissell's note supports Jones.
- His notes date the NSC of Oct. 20, 1960 "24 October" (155 n.5) and the NSC of Nov. 17 "21 November" (159 n.8): the
  memoranda's dates, not the meetings'.
- Mar. 14 or 15, 1961: Bissell gives the move from Trinidad to the Bay of Pigs "March 11 to ... March 14" (172); the
  calendar's Mar. 15.
