# *Zhou Enlai waijiao huodong dashiji, 1949–1975* 周恩来外交活动大事记 (1993): the cite pass

Card `w.zhou-enlai-waijiao-huodong` (made Nov. 18, 2026, with `script`, `trans` and `paren`). Zhonghua Renmin Gongheguo Waijiaobu Waijiaoshi Yanjiushi 中华人民共和国外交部外交史研究室 (ed.), Shijie Zhishi Chubanshe 世界知识出版社, Beijing, first edition and first printing, March 1993 (title, title-verso and copyright pages: PDF pp. 1–3 of the scan). A day-by-day chronology of Zhou's diplomatic acts, 1949–1975, in Chinese; 22.5 printed sheets, about 580,000 characters, 11,000 copies printed. **The chronology has no notes**: no footnotes, endnotes or source lines; an item gives a date, often an hour, a place and the act, sometimes a few sentences of what Zhou said. So there is no notes pass, no `notes-coverage` and no witness row beyond the work itself; each cite stands on the dashiji's own line, and its unread sources are not named by the book. An item that says "Made public" is every item: the volume (1993) is the earliest publication of those acts found here, so new entries of closed talks carry "Made public: 1993."
