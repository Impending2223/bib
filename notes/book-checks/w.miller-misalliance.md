# Edward Miller, *Misalliance* (2013): the calendar's citations held up

Card `w.miller-misalliance`. Read Oct. 23, 2026, from the owner's copy (428 PDF pages; printed page = PDF page − 9
throughout, checked against the page images at pp. 205 and 261). Chapters 6–9 and the Conclusion (pp. 185–324) carry
1960–63; the book ends with the coup of Nov. 1–2, 1963. Patch: `inbox/2026-10-23-book-miller.yaml` (book-miller).

## The ten citations it had

Eight hold and take their pages: Jan. 26, 1960 (205); Mar. 7, 1960 (204–05); Oct. 14, 1960 (212); Nov. 11–12, 1960
(202–04, 207–13); Feb. 27, 1962 (280); Aug. 21, 1963 (275–77, 287–90); Aug. 24, 1963 (290–91); Nov. 1–2, 1963
(315–24). Two hold in part and take a Check. The cases that are not a plain "holds":

- `cal.1960-03-07.vietnam` (Durbrow sends the Country Team's report). Miller does not discuss the report as such; he
  cites it (Desp. 278, Mar. 7, 1960, n. 57) for the rise in attacks from the autumn of 1959, 204–05. Kept, with those
  pages: the book uses the document the entry records.
- `cal.1960-04-26.vietnam` (Caravelle). The entry: the eighteen meet at the Caravelle Apr. 26 and petition Diệm.
  Miller, 206: they "wrote an open letter to Diem on April 19, 1960," citing Tel. 2981 of Apr. 19 (n. 64). That
  telegram is FRUS 1958–60, I, doc. 142, which the entry already cites: it reports a draft of the letter "to be handed
  Diem by opposition group at meeting which they planning for April 25." So Miller's date is the telegram's; the
  meeting came later. Ruling: entry's date stands; added "Check: Miller, 206, dates the letter Apr. 19, the day of doc.
  142, which reports a draft to be handed Diệm at a meeting set for Apr. 25." The day of the meeting and the release
  (Apr. 26?) wants a record of its own (the *Times of Viet Nam*, or Durbrow's later telegrams).
- `cal.1962-02-27.vietnam` (the palace bombed). Miller gives it one sentence, 280 ("a pair of South Vietnamese air
  force pilots tried to kill the president by bombing Independence Palace"), and points to Diệm's account in FRUS
  1961–63, II, 187–93 (n. 2). Holds, thinly; kept as the entry's only work.
- `cal.1963-05-08.vietnam` (Huế). The entry: troops and armored cars fire; eight killed by the consul's first count.
  Miller, 260–61: soldiers and police under Maj. Đặng Sĩ; an armored car pushed through the crowd; "at least one loud
  explosion and a series of gunshots"; seven dead at the scene, two more within hours; the government blamed a
  communist bomb, most witnesses Đặng Sĩ's men. 266–67: the flag ban by Diệm's telegram of May 6, enforced in Huế May 7
  after Thục's complaint; the crowd came for the recording of Trí Quang's morning remarks. The consul's first count
  (FRUS III, doc. 112) and Miller's nine do not contradict; Miller's account of who fired is less certain than the
  entry's. Ruling: pinpoint 260–68; added "Check: Miller, 261, gives nine dead, seven at the scene and two within
  hours, and an explosion with the gunfire." The UN mission's report (cited in the entry) is the third record to read.
- `cal.1963-11-01.vietnam` (the coup). Holds: the Diệm–Lodge call at 4:30 p.m. (322: "4:30 in the morning in
  Washington"), the flight to Cholon, the Cha Tam church, the armored car (323–24). Miller does not narrate the
  killing itself; the book stops at the brothers' hands tied in the carrier.
- `cal.1963-08-21.vietnam`. Holds: twelve pagodas in Saigon, more than 700 held, Huế and Đà Nẵng, martial law
  (275–76); the raids the work of the Special Forces and the National Police, a Civil Guard unit with them, the regular
  army only in Huế (289–90). Lodge in Saigon Aug. 22 (290).

doc. 135) where Miller, 227, has him beheaded; Nhu wanting half the Americans out (the *Post*, as FRUS III, doc. 122,
n. 2, quotes it) where Miller, 258, has "at least 50 percent" of the advisers "not absolutely necessary"; the
guidance of Oct. 9 (FRUS IV, doc. 192), which Miller, 311, calls a White House cable. The book's own inconsistency on
the Don–Conein talk (July 4 at 284–85, "three days" before July 11 at 286) is a lead, not an entry.
