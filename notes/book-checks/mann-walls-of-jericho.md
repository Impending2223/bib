# Mann, *The Walls of Jericho* (1996): the calendar read against the book

Robert Mann, *The Walls of Jericho: Lyndon Johnson, Hubert Humphrey, Richard Russell, and the Struggle for Civil Rights* (1996), card `w.mann-walls-of-jericho`. Read Oct. 29, 2026, from the owner's Drive copy (file id 1Q5WXirErb1AgAw2bUkXGw3hPGJkt-b-D), a scan with a text layer, by its printed pages: the PDF's page is the printed page + 22 through p. 374, + 30 from p. 375 (the plates between); checked on pp. 238, 380 and 577. The PDF was deleted once its text was taken; the text was kept outside the repo, and nothing of it is here but the short quotations the entries carry.

The span read is the book's 1960 to Jan. 20, 1969: ch. 15 (the bill of 1960) through ch. 24 and the epilogue's opening (Russell and Johnson, Jan. 1969); the epilogue's later years are outside the calendar. The book's source notes are unnumbered, keyed by page and phrase; they were read with the text. Each dated event was looked for in the calendar on its day, in every thread, and among the adds of the other pending patches (`inbox/2026-10-29-b3-*.yaml`): an event the calendar holds took the page (an edit, the existing cites, sentences and rows kept); one it lacks became an entry; one a pending patch adds is listed below for the merger. Every quotation from the floor was set against the bound *Congressional Record* on govinfo where Mann's note gives its page (or the day's granule was searched), and the President's against the Public Papers. The recordings named (the Miller Center's and PRDE's catalogues) were not heard: they stand behind "To check:". The patch is `inbox/2026-10-29-b3-walls-of-jericho.yaml` (patch `b3-walls-of-jericho`); the records the book cites for the calendar's claims are in `sources/cited-records/mann-walls-of-jericho.yaml`.
