## How it was read

Pages 1–41 and 85–155 were read in full, page by page, with their notes; pp. 156–186 in full for the Mississippi summer's days (June 20–July 21, 1964) and by their dated passages otherwise; pp. 42–84 (the background to 1963, already the calendar's from Branch, Garrow and Mann) and pp. 187–434 (Atlantic City to 1968) by their dated passages only: every sentence of the text holding a date, a weekday or a word of meeting, call, memorandum, letter, speech or statement, with the sentence after, and, where an event was entered or cited, the page around it and its notes. Each dated event was looked for on its day in the calendar, every thread, and in the ledger of pending entries.
