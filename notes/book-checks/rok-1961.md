### What the log is

Not the chairman's engagements: a digest of the regime's acts as announced, with the news. Most items read as the day's public record (proclamations, laws promulgated, appointments, statements to the press), several with the newspapers' turn of phrase; the news head carries foreign items (Algeria, Laos, Berlin, the Dominican Republic) that have nothing to do with the office. Its value here is the dated sequence of the junta's acts in one place. It names Kim Chong-p'il only by office ("金中央情報部長", June 4 and 5, before the agency's law of June 10), Wŏn Ch'ung-yŏn first as "元大領" (June 2), and calls Park "朴議長" on June 19 and (with 副 written in) June 26, before he was chairman. Nothing in these pages is marked secret, and nothing records a private talk beyond "talks with" (Park and Magruder, May 23; Berger, June 28 and 30). So no entry carries "Made public": the acts were public at the time, as far as the log shows; the visibility pass will want the Gazette or the press beside the log (Reader B).
