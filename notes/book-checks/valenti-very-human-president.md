The book is a memoir by Johnson's appointments secretary (Nov. 1963 to mid-1966), arranged by topic, not by date: the dated events are in Part I (Nov. 21-22, 1963), Part II (the Kennedy matter, June-July 1964; the campaign; Valenti's memoranda of 1964-66), Part III (two backgrounders and dinners) and Part IV (the Vietnam meetings of July 1965). Everything else is character sketch and undated anecdote. Every entry says it is Valenti's account.
