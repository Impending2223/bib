**The text layer.** The PDF's text layer is not usable as it stands: the Chinese characters come out (a CID font, GBK) but every digit, the punctuation and the Latin letters are glyphs of a per-page subset Type 1 font whose encoding is the order of first appearance on the page, so `pdftotext` gives "$%月 %&日" for "12月 23日" and OCR (tesseract chi_sim) was too slow and lost the glyphs. Read instead by decoding the content stream: the Chinese by GBK, each Type 1 glyph by the hash of its charstring against a table of 59 glyph hashes labeled from three pages checked against the page image (the table in the reader's scratch, deleted with the copy). Rare characters (袴, 崎, a name's second character) come out missing; named from context and noted "Check: the Japanese forms" in a few entries. The decoding was checked against the page image on PDF pp. 626, 631, 700, 701.
