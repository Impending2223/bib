**The card.** The calendar had a card for the 1986 edition only (`w.zhu-de-nianpu`, Renmin chubanshe, one volume). This is another edition, with its own pagination and much new matter, so a card of its own: `w.zhu-de-nianpu-xinbian`, made by `./bib works new` from the copyright page (PDF 4): 朱德年谱（新编本）（1886—1976）, 编者 中共中央文献研究室, 主编 吴殿尧 (Wu Dianyao), 副主编 庹平 (Tuo Ping), 中央文献出版社, 2016年11月第2版, 2016年11月第1次印刷, 126 印张, 1,470 千字, 新华书店经销, no 内部发行; CIP 朱德年谱：新编本/中共中央文献研究室编; `script` and `trans` added to the card by hand (the command takes neither). The postscript (后记, 2004–2005) says the new edition was begun in 1999 on the base of the 1986 edition, with some 1.2 million of its 1.47 million characters newly written, and names the writers of each stretch: 1955.12–1960.12 Li Hongcai 李洪才; 1961.1–1963.5 Yu Jundao 于俊道; 1963.5–1976.7 Wang Jiyi 王纪一 (read on the image). **The cite form:** "[[w.zhu-de-nianpu-xinbian]], III, 1760", the date and Zhu De's part in a cite's parenthetical. **Made public:** 2016 (this printing, the "2nd edition", the 1986 book being the first); where the 1986 edition already printed an item the year is 1986, which this run could not check (see Doubts).

**The notes.** The chronology has a few footnotes (marked [1] etc.) glossing a meeting or a term (p. 1776, the "West Lake meeting" of May 21–22, 1960); they name no work. It gives no archive or source for its items; documents it names with a date (letters and reports to the Center and to Mao) are in the entries' notes, the nianpu their only witness here.

**How the run was made.** Each dated item of the nianpu, in the book's order, was set against the calendar's entries of its day (tree and pending inbox, all threads, by a script over the calendar files and the inbox's patches: the conditions restore, *Mubei*, Dikötter's *Cultural Revolution*, Chang and Bu Weihua among them), the spans covering it, and every entry of the day naming Zhu De; then every add scripted again against the day before and after by shared names and runs of characters, the candidates read. Where the act is in (chiefly the Mao, Zhou, Liu, Peng Zhen and Chen Yi chronologies' and Yang Shangkun's diary's entries of meetings, receptions and send-offs where Zhu De is present), a `cite` with the page and his part in the parenthetical (the hour, the place, who else); an `edit` setting the band or hour the page gives where the entry had none; a `variant` where the page differs (a day, an hour, a session's number). Where not, a new entry (`china-leadership`, `also:` the region's thread, `polity:prc` and the foreign party's polity; `name:` tags only for persons the series holds). **Visibility:** sessions of the NPC Standing Committee, receptions, banquets, airport welcomes and send-offs, rallies, signings, credentials, inscriptions and bare inspections and sightseeing on tours carry no ruling (public, as the Chen Yi and Liu audits ruled); the Politburo and its Standing Committee, meetings Mao or Liu call, the Military Commission, the work conferences, reports heard from provincial and city committees, letters and reports to the Center or to Mao, talks with the General Staff's Third Department and speeches to it, the NPC Standing Committee's working meeting on South Korea, visits to Mao and to Soong Ching Ling, the private talks (Wang Shaonan on Zhang Guotao, Jin Handing), the visit to his physician, the secret plants (784, 719) and the exhibition of advanced technology are `vis:only:prc` with "Made public: 2016."; where a public inspection carries a remark to the hosts, the remark's unit alone is closed (`vis:only:prc@B`); talks with substance with a foreign government's man `vis:only:prc+<polity>` (Krim Belkacem, May 2, 1960; Bušniak, May 12; the Iraqi Ambassador, May 14). **Times:** 上午 morning, 下午 afternoon, 晚/晚上 evening, 夜 night, 傍晚 after-workday daytime, 早晨 early morning, as bands with `time_zone: Asia/Shanghai` and "The words:" in the note; a clock hour as `time` (7 p.m., Jan. 5, 1961). **Places:** `place:` where the item names the city or a building of it, or the nianpu's sequence puts the tour in a named city (Canton, Jan. 20–Feb. 7, 1960; Conghua county; Peking for the NPC Standing Committee's sessions, the seat); a bare reception, letter or poem unplaced (`ok:fields-place`). Three new place keys: `maanchang` (Ma'anchang, Yilong county, Zhu De's birthplace), `yilong` and `qinhuangdao`.
