What the book is: the Kennedy Library's recorded interviews with Robert Kennedy, the transcript re-edited against the tapes (Editorial Note, xi): Schlesinger, Washington, Feb. 27, 1965 (pp. 5–32); Martin, McLean, Feb. 29, 1964 (33–57); Lewis, Marshall present, New York, Dec. 4, and McLean, Dec. 6 and 22, 1964 (66–230); Martin, Mar. 1 (McLean), Apr. 13, Apr. 30 and May 14, 1964 (New York) (239–423); Stewart, July 20 and Aug. 1, 1967, on 1952–56, printed apart without notes (431–53). The book does not mark where each session within the Lewis set or the Martin set begins. The editors' notes (58–60, 231–34, 424–27) identify persons and date events; their brackets in the text are theirs. Every cite is Robert Kennedy's account, and the entries say so where it is one man's.
