### Held in part

Throughout, where Gibbons's account covers only part of an entry, the cite carries a parenthetical (the meeting,
the vote, the final count); where his source is a document not read (a memorandum in the LBJ Library, a committee
transcript, Tom Johnson's or Jim Jones's notes), the note names it after the pinpoint, and the document stands under
"To check" where it is to be read. FRUS is the primary record wherever it prints the same notes. Earlier rulings:
Ia Drang (IV, 100–102) cited for the battles generally; SANE's march and Oglesby's speech (IV, 93–94), Morrison and
LaPorte (IV, 92–93), Katzenbach's inquiry (IV, 90), each with its Check; the fourteen points (IV, 130) against the
*Bulletin*'s index (no news conference of Dec. 29); the supplemental of Mar. 22, 1966 (IV, 263), the House's vote
misdescribed.
