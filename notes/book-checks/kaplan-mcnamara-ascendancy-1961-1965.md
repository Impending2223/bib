# Kaplan, Landa and Drea, *The McNamara Ascendancy* (2006): the calendar reread (stage 3)

Lawrence S. Kaplan, Ronald D. Landa and Edward J. Drea, *The McNamara Ascendancy, 1961–1965* (2006; History of the Office of the Secretary of Defense, V), card `w.kaplan-mcnamara-ascendancy-1961-1965`. Read Oct. 28–29, 2026, from the OSD Historical Office's PDF (history.defense.gov, OSDSeries_Vol5.pdf), by the printed folios of its running heads, not the PDF's pages; the endnotes (pp. 555–628) by chapter and printed note number. OSD's files (the Secretary's, the Comptroller's, ISA's, accessions at the Washington National Records Center, OSD Hist), the JCS files, the Kennedy and Johnson Libraries' NSF, FRUS (often cited by volume and page), the hearings, the *McNamara Public Statements*, the Pentagon's news releases, interviews by the OSD historians, and the studies.

Every pinpoint to this volume in the calendar was read against the entry's units (`./bib claims show ID`), the page sentence by sentence. The patch `inbox/2026-10-29-histories-osd-a.yaml` (patch `histories-osd-a`) writes each entry's claim map (`src`): this volume's rows by page and units, a parenthetical in the note where the page bears out only some of the entry, and a `via` row for each record its note gives, described once in `sources/cited-records/kaplan-mcnamara-ascendancy-1961-1965.yaml`; where FRUS or the Public Papers print the record, it was read and is cited first (the FRUS page Kaplan gives turned into its document). A record not read joins "To check:" with the page and note it comes from.

Entries mapped: 115, 100 of them finer than the whole entry (one cite taken out: `cal.1963-08-29.vietnam`). Records logged: 173 under 123 notes: 89 found and read (FRUS, the Public Papers, the Directive in Cole et al.), 7 found and not read (a recording, hearings), 77 not found open (OSD's files, news releases, interviews).

New entries citing it (12): `cal.1961-03-06.space` (Directive 5160.32, read in Cole et al., *Documents on Establishment and Organization*, a new card, `w.cole-department-of-defense-documents`); `cal.1961-04-26.laos`, `cal.1961-04-29.laos`, `cal.1961-05-01.laos` (FRUS 1961–63, XXIV, docs. 62, 67, 73); `cal.1961-08-31.defense` (the Defense Supply Agency); `cal.1962-11-24.defense` (the TFX award); the base closures, `cal.1961-03-30.defense`, `cal.1962-03-31.defense`, `cal.1963-12-07.defense`, `cal.1963-12-12.defense`, `cal.1964-04-24.defense`, `cal.1964-11-19.defense` (the Pentagon's releases and McNamara's statements not open: each in "To check:").
