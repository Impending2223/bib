# John Dittmer, *Local People* (1994): the calendar's citations held up

Read Oct. 23, 2026, from the owner's copy (archive.org's scan, 566 PDF pages). Printed pages: PDF page less 13 for
pp. 1–142, less 21 for pp. 143–314 (plates after 142), less 29 from p. 315 (plates after 314); checked against the page
images at pp. 205 and 351. All changes are in `inbox/2026-10-23-book-dittmer.yaml` (patch `book-dittmer`).

43 calendar entries cited the book. 26 hold and now carry pages; 13 hold in part (pages added; a "Check" in 9, where
the book gives another date, figure or account); 4 are not treated by the book. Below, every case that is not a plain "holds", and the pinpoints of the
partial ones.

## Held in part, or the book gives another date, figure or account

- `cal.1960-04-24.mississippi-biloxi`. Entry: Mason arrested Apr. 17 for swimming there; some 125 at the wade-in.
  Dittmer, 86: Mason and a few friends went for a swim Apr. 14 and a policeman ordered them off (no arrest); Apr. 24 a
  mob of forty white men with pipes, chains and bats; no count of the swimmers. Third record: the Fifth Circuit's
  opinion (cited) does not settle the April days. Ruling: Check added.
- `cal.1962-02.civil-rights-organizations`. Entry: COFO revived at Clarksdale; Moses voter registration director.
  Dittmer, 118–19: Moses, Gaither, Henry and Evers met in Jackson, Feb. 1962; Moses program director. The SNCC Digital
  Gateway page cited may say Clarksdale. Ruling: Check added.
- `cal.1963-03-27.mississippi`. Entry: police set a dog on citizens walking home from the registrar; a minister
  bitten. Dittmer, 151–52: Mar. 27 the march past city hall, the dog bites Matthew Hughes and tears Moses's trousers,
  ten leaders jailed; "the day after ... The following morning" (Mar. 29 by his telling) the Rev. D. L. Tucker bitten
  leading forty-two applicants back from the courthouse. The entry joins two events of two days. Third record: the
  Justice Department's memorandum of Apr. 8 (crmvet.org) refused this environment (406). Ruling: Check added; the
  entry may need splitting once the memorandum is read (lead).
- `cal.1963-05-28.mississippi`. Entry: the crowd beats them for three hours. Dittmer, 162: "for two hours." Ruling:
  Check added.
- `cal.1963-08-06.mississippi-ms`. The book gives no figures for the primary; it says Johnson beat Coleman (189) and
  that 733 Negroes voted by affidavit (200). Holds in part: 189, 199–200.
- `cal.1963-11-02.mississippi-ms`. Entry: some 80,000 ballots (Check). Dittmer, 205: more than 83,000. Ruling: the
  figure added to the entry's Check.
- `cal.1964-01-22.mississippi-hattiesburg`. Entry: some fifty pickets and some thirty Northern clergymen (Check the
  numbers). Dittmer, 220–21: nearly 200 at the courthouse by 10:30, fifty clergymen from out of the State; Moses the one
  arrest there. Ruling: Check added.
- `cal.1965-09-17.mississippi`. Dittmer, 351, dates the House's vote, 228 to 143, Sept. 16. The entry's Record (the
  bound Record's House part for Sept. 17) and Voteview's roll call give Sept. 17. Ruling: the entry stands; pinpoint 351.
- `cal.1966-01-31.mississippi`. Entry: some seventy Delta people (Check). Dittmer, 366–67: about forty poor people and
  ten civil rights workers at 6:30 a.m., some fifteen more at noon, almost 100 by evening; some 150 air policemen
  removed them Feb. 1. Ruling: Check added.
- `cal.1966-06-06.mississippi`. Entry: wounded by birdshot. Dittmer, 392: three loads of buckshot, sixty to seventy
  pellets, the wounds superficial. Dittmer, 389, also has Meredith set out "Sunday, June 4, 1966"; Sunday was June 5,
  as the entry has it. Ruling: Check added on the shot; the date stands.
- `cal.1966-09-12.mississippi`. Entry: about 250 children of 450 enrolled go to class. Dittmer, 404–06: nearly 300
  applied; about 150 entered Sept. 12, some forty turned away; more than thirty children beaten; Judge Clayton's
  injunction that Friday; thirteen men charged by the FBI Sept. 17; eight acquitted in June 1967. Ruling: Check added.
- `cal.1961-02-01.civil-rights-organizations`. Dittmer, 91, says only that Farmer was CORE's national director in
  1961; nothing on his taking office. Cited for the thread. Ruling: kept, pinpoint 91.
- `cal.1962-05.mississippi-ms`. Dittmer does not treat the registration laws of 1962; he treats the Lynd suit and the
  Fifth Circuit's injunction the entry starts from (179–80). Ruling: pinpoint 179–80.

## Not treated by the book

- `cal.1964-01-21.mississippi-ms` (Paul Johnson's inaugural). Dittmer quotes the words only from Hoover's report of
  July 1964 (250–51), not the inaugural. Left without pinpoint.
- `cal.1964-03-06.voting-rights` (*United States v. Mississippi*, the three-judge court). Not in the book. Left
  without pinpoint; the entry has other works.
- `cal.1964-08-24.mississippi` (*Gray v. Mississippi*, the unpledged-elector law). Not in the book (it mentions only
  Alabama's unpledged electors, 290). It is the entry's only work; left in place rather than leave the entry with no
  bibliography. A work on the unpledged electors should replace it.
- `cal.1964-12-04.mississippi` (the Neshoba arrests). The book treats the federal trial of 1967 (398, 418) but not the
  arrests of December 1964. Left without pinpoint.
