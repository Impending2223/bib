## Doubts

1. The thread-wide glosses: the famine has no thread of its own; its toll is set on `china-leadership`'s first entry (the Standing Committee of Jan. 6, 1960), the Cultural Revolution's on the last entry of the four-entry subthread `india-and-china-cultural-revolution` (most of the Cultural Revolution is filed under `china-leadership`). The owner may prefer another anchor.
2. Files with two dates in one note (Dikötter 206 n.43, n.44; 246 n.5; 296 n.36; 277 n.10, n.16, n.19; *Tiandi* II, 601 n.30): dated by one, "Check: the day" naming the other.
