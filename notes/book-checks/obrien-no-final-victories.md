The book is a memoir by the man who ran Kennedy's campaigns, the congressional liaison office for Kennedy and Johnson, the Post Office, and Humphrey's campaign of 1968. It is partly by topic, partly by date, and few events carry a day. Every new entry says it is O'Brien's account, and where the work gives only a weekday, a week or a month the entry says "Check: the day."
