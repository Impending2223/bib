What the book is: Johnson's year, Jan. 1968–Jan. 20, 1969, by episode (the State of the Union, the *Pueblo*, Tet, Mar.
31, King, Robert Kennedy, Fortas, Czechoslovakia, Chicago, Chennault, the last day). Its notes rest on Tom Johnson's
meeting notes, the Daily Diary, White House memoranda (Manatos, Califano, Sanders, Rostow) and above all the memoirs
(Busby, Lady Bird Johnson's diary, Califano, Clifford, Christian, Watson, Humphrey, Vantage Point). It is mostly the
informal record; where a meeting is in FRUS, FRUS was read and cited first.

## Where the book differs (each noted in the entry, "Check:")

- `cal.1968-01-31.vietnam-tet-2`: Longley names Rivers at the breakfast (45, 57); FRUS VI doc. 39's list does not.
- `cal.1968-02-06.vietnam-tet`: Longley puts Harry Byrd at the breakfast (46); FRUS and Longley's own p. 60, Robert Byrd.
- `cal.1968-02-09.vietnam`: Longley dates Wheeler's "do not refrain from asking" Feb. 8 (62, from Clifford); the entry Feb. 7 and 9.
- `cal.1968-04-10.open-housing`: Longley gives 229–195 as the bill's passage (134); the entry, the previous question
  (229–195) and adoption (250–172).
- `cal.1968-08-23.soviet-union-czechoslovakia`: Longley gives Clifford's words on the Soviet buildup as Wheeler's, and
  Wheeler's "less than prudent" on Central Europe as Johnson's (199); FRUS XVII doc. 85.
- `cal.1968-08-28.elections-1968`: Longley gives the minority plank's defeat as 1,527¾ to 1,041¼ (225, Witcover); the
  entry 1,567¾ (already "Check").
- `cal.1968-10-22.elections-1968`: Longley's note dates Farrell's *Times* article Dec. 31, 1968 (236 n.13), for 2016.
- `cal.1968-10-23.elections-1968`: Longley has Walt Rostow hand Johnson his brother's memorandum early on Oct. 29, and the
  FBI's tap ordered that day (233, 239); the entry has both under Oct. 23 with "Check the day."
- `cal.1968-10-26.elections-1968-2`: Longley dates Nixon's statement Oct. 25 (236, from Ambrose); *RN*, Oct. 26.
- `cal.1969-01-20.transition-and-staff-2`: Longley puts the Cliffords' house in Georgetown (266) and in northwest
  Washington (267); the entry, Bethesda (Vantage Point).
- `cal.1968-07-31.court-fortas`: Longley gives as Johnson's own the words on Thornberry ("a very rare thing") that Johnson
  quotes from Christopher (177; the Papers).
- `cal.1968-07-22.japan-and-korea-pueblo`: Longley quotes Reid's words of July 22 ("182 days") but cites 18098, her
  remarks of June 20 ("150 days later"); both read in the bound Record.
- Longley's notes cite every *Pueblo* meeting of Jan. 23–25 as FRUS XXIX:1 "doc. 213," by page; the records file gives
  the documents that hold the words (218, 223, 224).
- `cal.1968-01-26.japan-and-korea-pueblo-2`: Longley gives no source for Johnson's talk with Sidey and Horner (41–42).
