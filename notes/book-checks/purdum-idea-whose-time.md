## Disagreements of the page with the calendar (variant and check)

- `cal.1963-06-20.civil-rights-bill` (A): Purdum, 78–80, puts Robert Kennedy's first appearance before the subcommittee on Wednesday, June 26, the President in Germany; the entry's day is June 20. Baker's count of the next day (June 27) agrees with Purdum.
- `cal.1963-07-04.civil-rights-bill` (A): Purdum, 117–18, has Marshall and McCulloch meeting in Piqua on Tuesday, July 2, the day of the Roosevelt Hotel meeting, not July 4. Purdum gives two conditions of McCulloch's (119), not four.
- `cal.1963-08-28.civil-rights-bill` (A): Purdum, 111, has the meeting of the leaders with Kennedy in the Cabinet Room, not the Oval Office.
- `cal.1963-10-29.civil-rights-bill` (A): Purdum, 144, has the Judiciary Committee passing the substitute 20 to 14 at the noon bell, after Moore's motion was beaten 19–15; the entry has 20–12.
- `cal.1963-10-29.republican-party-congress` (A): Purdum, 139, puts the 12:45 p.m. call, Halleck: "I've got you the votes," on Oct. 24, and a second call of thanks after the vote on Oct. 29 (144–45).
- `cal.1963-12-04.civil-rights-bill-2` (A): Purdum, 165, has Johnson's breakfast with Dirksen on Wednesday, Dec. 4, and with Halleck, picked up in the limousine, the next day, Dec. 5.
- `cal.1963-12-23.foreign-aid` (B): Purdum, 172, has the reception in the East Room, not the State Dining Room.
- `cal.1963-12-23.civil-rights-the-executive-3` (A): Purdum, 174, has Whittington arriving at 11:00 p.m.; the entry has 10:50.
- `cal.1963-11-25.succession` (A): Purdum, 154, has thirty-five governors, not forty.
- `cal.1964-01-18.civil-rights-bill` (C): Purdum, 179, has Halleck by Johnson's side during the call to Webb, not before him.
- `cal.1964-02-07.civil-rights-bill` (B): Purdum, 193–95, has Harris's amendment restoring the Administration's original Title VI language (the President's discretion, no court review), not striking the cutoff; Boggs spoke for it; beaten 80 to 206.
- `cal.1964-02-10.civil-rights-bill-2` (A): Purdum, 198–99, has Johnson's call to Rauh about four o'clock, hours before the vote, and a call to Wilkins minutes later; the entry has it minutes after the vote.
- `cal.1963-08-28.march-on-washington-5` (A): Purdum, 111, has "He's damned good ... Damned good!"
- `cal.1964-03-26.civil-rights-bill` (D): Purdum, 238, gives Russell's words as "A skirmish has been lost. We shall now begin to fight the war."
- `cal.1964-04-09.civil-rights-bill` (A): Purdum, 257, has the call with Byrd on Apr. 10; the entry has Apr. 9, 4:55 p.m.
- `cal.1963-02-12.civil-rights-the-executive-2`: Purdum, 13, spells the name May Britt.
- `cal.1963-10-24.republican-party-congress`: the day. Purdum, 137, has Halleck's meeting with the committee's Republicans on Oct. 23, before the White House meeting; he was to poll them by noon on Oct. 24 (139).
- `cal.1963-06-11.republican-party-congress`: the day. Purdum, 55, has Kennedy's meeting with Dirksen and Halleck before the breakfast of June 11.
- `cal.1963-07-18.civil-rights-bill`: Purdum, 81, has Robert Kennedy at the Judiciary Committee on July 16 (Ervin spoke and he was not allowed to testify) and July 17, his testimony beginning on July 18.
- `cal.1964-02-03.civil-rights-bill`: Purdum, 189–90, calls Poff a Virginia Democrat; Poff was a Republican. Purdum has the Poff amendment passing 100 to 68 on a standing vote.
- `cal.1964-02-04.civil-rights-bill`: Purdum, 190–91, has the Goodell amendment (adding "enforced by officials") and Willis's amendment to it (striking "fostered") agreed by voice vote on Feb. 4, and Meader's amendment beaten 68 to 153 on Feb. 5.
- `cal.1964-02-05.civil-rights-bill`: Purdum, 191, has Smith on the podiatrist and his Thirteenth Amendment amendment (107 to 149) on Feb. 4.
- `cal.1964-04-04.civil-rights-bill`: Purdum, 248–49, has more than forty Democrats and a dozen Republicans absent and the Senate in recess forty-one minutes after it met at eleven; the vote on the recess is not given.
