## The copy

The owner's PDF from Drive (Atheneum, 1965, first edition; the Internet Archive's scan, `isbn_9780224…`, with its
text layer), 456 PDF pages; printed page = PDF page − 20 throughout (the running heads at PDF 24, 60, 142, 240, 390,
420; the page image of p. 122 checked against the text, the Harris table). Read with `pdftotext`. Deleted when done.

The book has no source notes: a journalist's narrative, White's own reporting (he was with Rockefeller in New Hampshire
and Oregon, at Hollis, in White's trailer at the Cow Palace, with Johnson on Oct. 27) and his interviews; its few
footnotes are asides. The witnesses registered are those the text names (the *Concord Daily Monitor*, Loye Miller's
file, Harris's California polls, Reichley's memorandum, the *Times*'s story of Dec. 8, 1963) and Graham's memorandum,
which app. B prints whole (407–15). Every entry it alone grounds says "by White's account" where it is his account of a
private meeting.
