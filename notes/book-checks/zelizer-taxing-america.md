# Julian E. Zelizer, *Taxing America* (1998): the calendar's citations held up

Card `w.zelizer-taxing-america`. Read Oct. 24, 2026, from the owner's copy (408 PDF pages, a text layer; printed page =
PDF page − 20 throughout, checked against the page images at pp. 241 and 245). Chapters 6–9 (pp. 179–311) carry
1961–69: the Revenue Acts of 1962 and 1964 (ch. 6), Medicare (ch. 7), the surcharge and the Revenue and Expenditure
Control Act (ch. 8), tax expenditures and the reform of 1969 (ch. 9). Much of chs. 6–8 rests on the White House
recordings and the LBJ and JFK Libraries' memoranda, which Zelizer cites by date; where the Miller Center's catalogue
holds the recording, the new entries cite it first. Patch: `inbox/2026-10-24-book-zelizer-stein.yaml`
(book-zelizer-stein), with Stein's *Fiscal Revolution* (`notes/book-checks/stein-fiscal-revolution.md`).

The cases that are not a plain "holds":

- `cal.1966-11-08.economy` (the investment credit suspended). The entry said the credit was "restored by law in January
  1968." Zelizer, 260: the administration asked Ways and Means to restore it "within a few months." The calendar's own
  entry of June 13, 1967 (81 Stat. 57) has the suspension ended as of Mar. 9, 1967, instead of Dec. 31. Proposed: "the
  suspension, set to end Dec. 31, 1967, ended as of Mar. 9, 1967, by the act of June 13, 1967."
- `cal.1967-10-03.economy` (Ways and Means sets the surcharge aside). The entry carried "Check the day and the vote."
  The Daily Digest (113 Cong. Rec. D505) settles the day, Oct. 3, and gives the motion, Watts's, in its words; Zelizer,
  265, agrees on the day and the motion. The vote stays a Check: the entry says 20–5; Zelizer counts Mills, ten
  Republicans and eight Democrats for the motion (nineteen, or twenty with Watts). The Digest prints no count.
- `cal.1967-11-29.economy` (hearings after the devaluation). "Check the dates" settled by the Digest: Nov. 29 and 30,
  Fowler, Schultze and Martin (D616, D619). Schultze added.
- `cal.1968-06-20.economy` (the House adopts the conference report). Zelizer, 276, gives 268–159; the entry's 268–150
  is Voteview's roll call 357 (`congress/bills.yaml`). The entry stands.
- `cal.1963-02-06.economy` (Ways and Means opens hearings). Zelizer, 201, has the hearings run from Feb. 6 "until
  September 10"; the entry (with the Digest and `bills.yaml`) has the hearings to Mar. 27. Sept. 10 is the day Mills
  introduced H.R. 8363 (Stein, 440). Added as a Check.
- `cal.1969-01-17.economy` (Barr to the Joint Economic Committee). The entry has Barr give 155 taxpayers over $200,000,
  "21 of them over $1 million." Zelizer, 298, has the 155 from Barr and the 21 millionaires found afterward, when
  Mills's staff examined the returns (*New York Times*, Mar. 22, 1969). Added as a Check. Zelizer's text says the
  warning made headlines Jan. 20; his note dates the statement Jan. 17, as the entry does.
- `cal.1962-07-11.economy` (the depreciation guidelines). Zelizer, 186, says the Treasury announced liberalized
  depreciation "in July 1961"; 190 has it in 1962. The guidelines are Rev. Proc. 62-21, July 1962. Cited for 186, 190
  without a change.
- `cal.1965-05-17.economy` (the excise message). Zelizer, 250, gives "a $4.8 billion excise tax reduction in mid-June"
  (the act); the message's figures differ in kind (two stages of $1.75 billion and more by 1969). Cited for the cut only.
- Thin citations, kept with a parenthetical because the book bears on the entry's matter but not its particulars:
  Feb. 21, 1963 (King–Anderson in Ways and Means, not the message); Jan. 19 and Mar. 15, 1966 (one sentence on the
  1966 act); Dec. 31, 1966 (Johnson's refusal of a general increase in 1966); Jan. 29, 1968 (the unified budget).
- Recording cites: Zelizer gives the June 9, 1964 call as Tape WH6406.08, #3642; PRDE and the Miller Center have
  WH6406.03, #3642. The new entry follows the catalogues.
