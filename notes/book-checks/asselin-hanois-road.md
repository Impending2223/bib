## The two citations it had

Both stood behind "To check"; both move out with their pages.

- `cal.1960-09-05.vietnam` (the Third Congress). Holds in part, 83–87: the congress in September, the five-year plan,
  the two revolutions North and South "pursued separately" (85–86), Lê Duẩn first secretary (86). Asselin gives no
  days and does not say Hồ Chí Minh was re-elected chairman; Nguyen, 51, gives both (Sept. 5–10). Cited "(all but the
  days and Hồ Chí Minh's chair)".
- `cal.1963-11-01.vietnam` (the coup). Asselin treats Hanoi's reading of the coup, 160–62, and gives the event in one
  sentence: Diệm and Nhu killed Nov. 2, Dương Văn Minh at the head (160). Cited for that only. (Nguyen, 63, writes
  that the brothers were shot Nov. 1; not added there.)

The cases that are not a plain "holds":

- `cal.1963-12.vietnam` (the Ninth Plenum, dated December). Asselin, 162: "The plenum opened on 22 November," citing
  Nguyen's 2006 article and Grossheim (n.139), and adding that it is not known when it opened or closed; Hồ Chí Minh
  told the Soviet ambassador Dec. 25 "while the plenum was still in session" (163). Nguyen, *Hanoi's War*, 64, 66, has
  it in December. Ruling: the entry's month stands; a Check gives Asselin's Nov. 22. Made public added: the communiqué
  on the world situation, Jan. 20, 1964, the resolution on the South kept secret (164; Nguyen, 66).
- `cal.1964-06-18.vietnam` (Seaborn's first meeting with Phạm Văn Đồng). The entry said "Check the date." Asselin, 191,
  gives "an 18 June meeting," from Seaborn's report of June 20 (n.126). Ruling: the Check taken out; Asselin cited for
  the date.
- `cal.1965-02-07.vietnam` (Pleiku). The entry: 7 Americans killed, 109 wounded, by the first count. Asselin, 206: "a
  U.S. Special Forces camp," eight killed, "more than one hundred" wounded. Ruling: a second Check beside Frankum's (8
  and 128).
