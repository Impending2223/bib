## The copy

The Wilson Center's PDF (`https://www.wilsoncenter.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/publication/ACFB39.pdf`), 194 pages, a text layer (`pdftotext -layout`). Edited by Odd Arne Westad, Chen Jian, Stein Tønnesson, Nguyen Vu Tung and James G. Hershberg, Washington, D.C., May 1998. **Printed page = PDF page + 2** (checked against the page images: PDF 64 prints 66, PDF 135 prints 137; the folios in the text layer agree throughout). The cites link `#page=` the PDF page. Front matter i–iii and the abbreviations list (7); Westad's essay 8–18, Chen Jian's 20–31, Tønnesson's 32–43, Nguyen Vu Tung's 44–65, the 77 documents 66–194. A document is a conversation's record in English translation, headed by its number, the persons and the place and date, mostly excerpts (the editors' ellipses); the editors' notes are footnotes, numbered 108–260 through the documents. The book names no archive for the records; it says only (72 n.117) that the notes add "information from the same sources as the documents themselves". So every witness row says `open: closed archive` and the provenance is "the Chinese record, no repository named". No note numbers carry a document or a source except those listed below; the rest of the notes in the span are glosses (a person's career, an office, a place).
