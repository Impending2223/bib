## The copy, and how much of it was read

The owner's copy in Drive (file id 1MNuABSuml4ne3RlmYmr7p3YukbJ6mpzV, 15 MB): the Internet Archive's scan of the Northern
Illinois University Press edition (item `centercannothold0000giff`), 264 PDF pages with the scan's OCR text layer, read
with `pdftotext -layout`; kept in the scratchpad only. **Printed page = PDF page − 14 throughout**, the eight pages of
plates (pp. 103–12) counted in the folios: checked by script on every page's running head (text 3–203, notes 205–29,
bibliography 231–36, index 237–42), and by eye on the page images of pp. 65 and 81 (PDF 79, 95). Cited by the printed
folio. The OCR garbles some folios ("1738" for 73, "TI" for 77) and superscript note numbers; the notes were matched to
the text by their order.

Read in full: the Introduction (pp. 3–20, historiography), the six chapters (Rockefeller, pp. 21–56; the Negro vote,
57–90; *National Review*, 91–123; the young Republicans, 124–45; South Carolina, 146–67; the nationality groups,
168–96), the Conclusion (197–203), and the notes to every passage cited (pp. 205–29). Nothing is entered from memory.

The book rests on archives: the Rockefeller Archive Center (Rockefeller Papers, RG 4 and 15), the Nixon pre-presidential
papers as Gifford cites them ("Nixon Papers, NALN", Series 320 and 207), the Goldwater Papers (Arizona Historical
Foundation), the Buckley Papers (Yale), the Workman and Shorey Papers (South Caroliniana Library), the Byrnes and
Thurmond papers (Clemson); and the press, the Negro press above all. Some passages carry no note: Nixon's putting off
Wilkins, Jan. 6–12, 1960 (64–65); McCaffrey to Shipley, Aug. 30 (81); Henderson's telegram of Oct. 26 (189–90). Those
entries cite the page alone.

  dates a Goldwater for President meeting at Chicago Jan. 23 by its minutes. Whether one meeting: Check. The entry has
  Wannamaker "South Carolina's new Republican chairman"; Gifford, 150, 153, has Shorey State chairman, 1956–62, and
  Wannamaker elected national committeeman at the convention of Mar. 26. Check.
- `cal.1960-03-08.elections-1960`: Gifford, 43, Nixon 65,077 to Rockefeller 2,890 write-ins; the N.H. *Manual*, 65,204 to
  2,745. The *Manual* stands.

  party's plan, "designed to call attention of GOP bigwigs and Nixon personally," by Milliken's account to Waring, Mar.
  21; Manion not named. Both stand, as accounts.
- `cal.1960-07-22.elections-1960`: Gifford's two dates for the meeting (52, 76); the calendar's July 22 stands.
- `cal.1960-07-26.elections-1960`: the words of the withdrawal. Gifford, 136, from the *Proceedings*, 289–91, has
  conservatives told to "grow up" and "If we want to take this Party back, and I think we can someday, let's get to
  work"; the entry, "Let's grow up, conservatives, if we want to take this party back." Check against the *Proceedings*.
- `cal.1960-10-12.elections-1960`: Gifford, Harlem; the entry, East Harlem.
- `cal.1963-07-04.elections-1964`: Gifford, 200, almost 9,000 from forty-four States, two busloads from New York;
  Perlstein, over 7,000; Young, almost 9,000.
- In the book itself: Nixon's press conference of Aug. 17 is at Greensboro (80) and "Greenville, N.C." (161); the new
  entry has Greensboro, with APP's title, and a Check. Gifford's own note, 227 n.67: Nixon's immigration statement gave
  the 1920 census as the quotas' base, the act of 1924 having set 1890's (a Check on the new entry).
- `cal.1960-04-09.conservative-movement` (new): *National Review* (by Gifford, 115) has all 450 college delegates for
  Goldwater; Evans (133) a resolution the conservatives carried, the chairman a liberal by five votes. Check.
