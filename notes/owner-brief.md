>
> (Implication by omission permitted if disciplined and determinate. If an entry reads "ABCD" and the citation block reads "X; Y; Z (C)", I assume that X stands for ABD but not C, Y stands for ABD but not C, and that Z stands for C, and for C in collocation with ABD, but not necessarily for ABD in full. )

>
> Second, because the immediate traces may be unidentified or unidentifiable, unecovered or unrecoverable, the actual body of identifiable and recoverable matter may include multiple items "from which no material is upstream" for any (express, implied, inferred, or notional) immediate trace.
