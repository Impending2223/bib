# The calendar's threads: what the statements leave unsaid

For whoever writes or extends a thread (person or agent). Not shown on the pages.

Every thread is its matter as the nation or state met it (the owner, Nov. 15, 2026, R2 of the reader's brief; before it, Oct. 29, "as Washington and its counterparts met it"): what its government, its parties, its legislature, its courts and its people in politics did about it, said of it or were told of it, and what those they dealt with did and said. Write a thread's statement as what the thread is and its stations, without "as Washington met it" or "as the nation met it".

Scopes taken out of the statements, kept here:

- Soviet Union: the Soviet Union, apart from Berlin, Cuba, and Arms control and testing (each its own thread).
- South Asia: India and Pakistan; China and Indonesia are threads of their own (the owner's ruling, Oct. 21, 2026, parting India and China). The Sino-Indian war takes South Asia, also China.
- Cyprus: its own thread, out of Europe and the alliance and the Middle East (Oct. 21, 2026).
- Oil: the import program (Proclamation 3279 and its amendments, Interior's quotas), the world's oil as it touched policy (OPEC, Libya, the 1967 embargo, the OECD's emergency machinery), out of the Middle East and Economy threads (Oct. 21, 2026); an event that is also the Middle East's (OPEC, Law 80, the embargo) takes Oil in `also` or the reverse. The month's figures are the indicator group Oil (`tools/bib/oil.py`).
- King: Martin Luther King, Jr. (the owner, Nov. 15, 2026: "The MLK thread should drop 'and Washington.'"; the statement's "The President, the Attorney General, the Bureau" dropped with it).
- Black nationalism: Black nationalism (the statement opens "Malcolm X and the Nation of Islam").
- Civil rights organizations: their campaigns (Albany, Birmingham, the Freedom Rides, Freedom Summer, Selma, the March) are in the place and law threads; King has his own; Malcolm X and the Nation of Islam are in Black nationalism.
- Africa: Africa, apart from the Congo (its own thread): South Africa, decolonization at the UN.
- Sit-ins: the student sit-ins of 1960 at lunch counters, an event of their own; other segregation in public places is in Desegregation: public places, the Freedom Rides in their own thread.
- Japan; Korea: each country, its governments and its alliance: the treaties, the visits (one thread until Oct. 21, 2026). A matter between the two (the treaty of 1965, Ōhira's offer) takes Korea, also Japan.
- Labor disputes: strikes and lockouts, the emergency boards, the Taft-Hartley injunctions, the settlements, taken out of Jobs and wages (Oct. 21, 2026), the railroads' disputes its subthread; a strike in a State's or a city's thread, or in Steel, King or Students, takes Labor disputes in `also`. Jobs and wages keeps unemployment, depressed areas, wages, the unions' law (14(b), the Taylor Law).
- Students: college, university and high school students in politics: their organizations, demonstrations, and manifestos (the statement keeps the first clause; the high-school walkouts, McComb and the East Side, Oct. 29, 2026).
- The States and the cities (Oct. 14, 2026, at the owner's request for State and local elections and politics): each event takes the narrowest thread. A race for governor: Governors, also the year's elections thread (Elections of 1961, 1963, 1965 for the off years). A race for mayor, or for the body that governs a city: Mayors, likewise. A legislature's elections, control, apportionment and districting: Legislatures. A State constitution, its amendments, conventions, referenda and initiatives: State constitutions and referenda, also the topic's thread. Taxes, budgets, borrowing: State and city finance. Committees, laws, oaths and speaker bans against subversion: Antisubversion. Open housing, welfare, the schools and the rest: the topic's thread. What none of these holds: States and cities. An event of Alabama, Georgia, Mississippi or South Carolina alone takes that State's thread first. Each entry carries hidden tags for the States and cities it concerns (`state:ny`, `city:nyc`), for State threads or lists later; none yet beyond the four.

- Republican Party; Democratic Party; Conservative movement (Oct. 29, 2026, the owner's rule on the parties and the members: CLAUDE.md): a party and its organs (out of the White House, or, for the Republicans of 1960, its convention, platform and national committee in the campaign) (the national committee and its chairman, the governors' association and meetings, the coordinating committee, the platform's and the leadership's papers), its factions and their papers, its figures out of office on policy or politics; its members of Congress as a party (the leaders, conferences, caucuses, policy committees, task forces and their papers) in the subthread "Republican Party: In Congress", and for the Democrats in Democratic Party itself, Congress as an institution staying in Congress. A member's word on the war is Vietnam: Congress and the President, also the party's thread where he speaks as his party (a conference paper, a leadership statement), not where he speaks for himself. The President's own party as he leads it (the national committee he runs, his campaign) is in the year's elections thread, also Democratic Party. The conservative movement's organized groups and third party (YAF, the Conservative Party of New York, the Free Society Association, the ACU, *National Review*); the radical right and the officers who spoke for it stay in Right and the military.

- Intelligence; Antisubversion: surveillance at home (the owner, Nov. 15, 2026: "Please add the list to the threads file"). The FBI's, the CIA's, the Army's and the NSA's watch on Americans, 1960–69, and where each part is filed. A surveillance act takes the thread of its target's matter (King, Black nationalism, Opposition to the war, Robert Kennedy, the year's elections), `also: [intelligence]`; the agencies' programs as such (a COINTELPRO opened, CHAOS created, a report on foreign influence) take Intelligence; the laws, committees and prosecutions against subversion take Antisubversion.
  - In the calendar, Nov. 15: King, his advisers and SCLC (Levison, Clarence Jones, O'Dell, Rustin; the taps of 1962–63, the Willard recordings, the suicide package; Hoover to Johnson on Riverside, Apr. 7, 1967; the tax inquiry on the Poor People's Campaign, Dec. 1967); Malcolm X, Elijah Muhammad and the Nation of Islam (the Chicago taps, 1964); the black nationalist counterintelligence program (Aug. 25, 1967; widened Mar. 4, 1968); the New Left program (May 1968); the Freedom Democrats and Robert Kennedy at Atlantic City (Aug. 22–25, 1964); Robert Kennedy's campaign (the FBI's offices' reports, Apr.–May 1968); the Fulbright hearings (Johnson to Hoover, Feb. 19, 1966; the memoranda to Dirksen, Mar. 7); Goldwater's staff (Moyers's name checks, Oct. 27, 1964); Anna Chennault and the South Vietnamese embassy (Oct.–Nov. 1968); the steel crisis's calls on reporters (Apr. 1962); Campbell, Rosselli and Giancana (Feb.–Mar. 1962); Ellen Rometsch (Aug.–Oct. 1963); the CIA's Special Operations Group (CHAOS, Aug. 1967), Project Resistance (Oct. 1967), "International Connections of the U.S. Peace Movement" (Nov. 15, 1967) and its sequel (Dec. 21), "Restless Youth" (Sept. 18, 1968); the Army's CONUS intelligence (Sept. 1967), its agents at the march on the Pentagon (Oct. 1967) and the Civil Disturbance Plan (Dec. 1967); the National Student Association's CIA money (Feb. 1967) and the Katzenbach committee (Mar. 29, 1967).
  - Not found, Nov. 15 (leads, `notes/leads.md`, "Surveillance at home"): the counterintelligence programs against the Communist Party (from 1956), the Socialist Workers Party (Oct. 1961) and the "White Hate Groups" (Sept. 1964) as opened; the wiretaps in the sugar-lobby inquiry (1962); the taps on Hanson Baldwin and Lloyd Norman after their defense stories (1962); the CIA's taps on Robert Allen and Paul Scott (Project Mockingbird, 1962–63); the NSA's watch lists (from 1967; later Minaret); the FBI's check of Agnew's calls in the Chennault affair (Nov. 1968); the Justice Department's Interdivisional Intelligence Unit (Dec. 1967).

Names: no thread or subthread name begins with "The" (the owner's rule, Oct. 21, 2026): "Bay of Pigs", "Air war".

Order: th.yaml in alphabetical order of the threads' names, as shown; War on poverty under "poverty", Bobby Baker under "Baker", Robert Kennedy under "Kennedy" (`THREAD_SORT_AS` in tools/bib/check.py; `check` warns otherwise [thread-order]).
