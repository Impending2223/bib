# The calendar's threads: what the statements leave unsaid

For whoever writes or extends a thread (person or agent). Not shown on the pages.

Every thread is its matter as Washington met it: what the President, the executive departments and agencies, the
Congress, and the courts did about it, said of it, or were told of it (the Public Papers, FRUS, the Congressional
Record, the Statutes). The owner found the phrase redundant in the statements, since the calendar's whole frame (the
Congresses, the Executive, the APP and FRUS lists) says it; it is kept here instead. Write a thread's statement as
what the thread is and its stations, without "as Washington met it", "and Washington", "for the administration".

Scopes taken out of the statements, kept here:

- Soviet Union: the Soviet Union as Washington met it, apart from Berlin, Cuba, and Arms control and testing (each its own thread).
- South Asia: India and Pakistan as Washington met them; China and Indonesia are threads of their own (the owner's
  ruling, Oct. 21, 2026, parting India and China). The Sino-Indian war takes South Asia, also China.
- Cyprus: its own thread, out of Europe and the alliance and the Middle East (Oct. 21, 2026).
- Oil: the import program (Proclamation 3279 and its amendments, Interior's quotas), the world's oil as it touched
  policy (OPEC, Libya, the 1967 embargo, the OECD's emergency machinery), out of the Middle East and Economy threads
  (Oct. 21, 2026); an event that is also the Middle East's (OPEC, Law 80, the embargo) takes Oil in `also` or the
  reverse. The month's figures are the indicator group Oil (`tools/bib/oil.py`).
- King: Martin Luther King, Jr., and Washington: the President, the Attorney General, the Bureau (the statement
  keeps "The President, the Attorney General, the Bureau").
- Black nationalism: Black nationalism as Washington met it (the statement opens "Malcolm X and the Nation of Islam").
- Civil rights organizations: their campaigns (Albany, Birmingham, the Freedom Rides, Freedom Summer, Selma, the March) are in
  the place and law threads; King has his own; Malcolm X and the Nation of Islam are in Black nationalism.
- Africa: Africa as Washington met it, apart from the Congo (its own thread): South Africa, decolonization at the UN.
- Sit-ins: the student sit-ins of 1960 at lunch counters, an event of their own; other segregation in public places is
  in Desegregation: public places, the Freedom Rides in their own thread.
- Japan; Korea: each alliance as Washington met it: the treaties, the governments, the visits (one thread until Oct. 21,
  2026). A matter between the two (the treaty of 1965, Ōhira's offer) takes Korea, also Japan.
- Labor disputes: strikes and lockouts, the emergency boards, the Taft-Hartley injunctions, the settlements, taken out of
  Jobs and wages (Oct. 21, 2026), the railroads' disputes its subthread; a strike in a State's or a city's thread, or in
  Steel, King or Students, takes Labor disputes in `also`. Jobs and wages keeps unemployment, depressed areas, wages,
  the unions' law (14(b), the Taylor Law).
- Students: college, university and high school students in politics: their organizations, demonstrations, and
  manifestos (the statement keeps the first clause; the high-school walkouts, McComb and the East Side, Oct. 29, 2026).
- The States and the cities (Oct. 14, 2026, at the owner's request for State and local elections and politics): each
  event takes the narrowest thread. A race for governor: Governors, also the year's elections thread (Elections of
  1961, 1963, 1965 for the off years). A race for mayor, or for the body that governs a city: Mayors, likewise. A
  legislature's elections, control, apportionment and districting: Legislatures. A State constitution, its amendments,
  conventions, referenda and initiatives: State constitutions and referenda, also the topic's thread. Taxes, budgets,
  borrowing: State and city finance. Committees, laws, oaths and speaker bans against subversion: Antisubversion. Open
  housing, welfare, the schools and the rest: the topic's thread. What none of these holds: States and cities. An event
  of Alabama, Georgia, Mississippi or South Carolina alone takes that State's thread first. Each entry carries hidden
  tags for the States and cities it concerns (`state:ny`, `city:nyc`), for State threads or lists later; none yet beyond
  the four.

- Republican Party; Democratic Party; Conservative movement (Oct. 29, 2026, the owner's rule on the parties and the
  members: CLAUDE.md): a party and its organs (out of the White House, or, for the Republicans of 1960, its convention, platform
  and national committee in the campaign) (the national committee and its chairman, the
  governors' association and meetings, the coordinating committee, the platform's and the leadership's papers), its
  factions and their papers, its figures out of office on policy or politics; its members of Congress as a party (the
  leaders, conferences, caucuses, policy committees, task forces and their papers) in the subthread "Republican Party:
  In Congress", and for the Democrats in Democratic Party itself, Congress as an institution staying in Congress. A
  member's word on the war is Vietnam: Congress and the President, also the party's thread where he speaks as his
  party (a conference paper, a leadership statement), not where he speaks for himself. The President's own party as he
  leads it (the national committee he runs, his campaign) is in the year's elections thread, also Democratic Party.
  The conservative movement's organized groups and third party (YAF, the Conservative Party of New York, the Free
  Society Association, the ACU, *National Review*); the radical right and the officers who spoke for it stay in Right
  and the military.

Names: no thread or subthread name begins with "The" (the owner's rule, Oct. 21, 2026): "Bay of Pigs", "Air war".

Order: th.yaml in alphabetical order of the threads' names, as shown; War on poverty under "poverty", Bobby Baker under
"Baker", Robert Kennedy under "Kennedy" (`THREAD_SORT_AS` in tools/bib/check.py; `check` warns otherwise [thread-order]).
