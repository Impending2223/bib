# The disagreements, Oct. 28, 2026: the books against the records the series holds

For the owner. Stage 2 of the second reading pass (approved Oct. 28, 2026). The population: every "Check:" in a calendar note that sets a book's (or another record's) account against the entry's, as the book-reading runs, the official-histories run, the Daily Diary run and the spot check left them (`notes/book-checks/`, `notes/official-histories-discrepancies.md`, `notes/diary-discrepancies.md`, `notes/spot-check-2026-10-28.md`); their tables' open rulings all stand as Checks in the entries, so the entries are the list. A Check that only names a source still to read ("Check: the record") is not a disagreement and is left; so are six such that came up in the search.

Each was searched for a third record, in the brief's order: the daybook's FRUS documents (their text, `sources/frus-text/`), the Public Papers (`sources/ppp-text/`), the recordings' catalogues (`daybook/recordings/`), the Daily Diary days (`daybook/diary.yaml`), the bound *Congressional Record* (govinfo, the day's part read with `pdftotext`) and Voteview, the Daily Digest's committee rows (`daybook/committees/`), the *Bulletin* (`daybook/bulletin/`, archive.org's text). For every open row a mechanical pass also listed the FRUS documents, Public Papers items and *Bulletin* items of the entry's days (two before to five after) that name its persons; the rows say where a record was read. Nothing from presidency.ucsb.edu or discoverlbj.org.

Counts: 191 disagreements in 180 entries at the start; settled 57 (in 56 entries); open 134 (two of them narrowed with the record found). The patch: `inbox/2026-10-28-disagreements.yaml` (patch `disagreements`, 58 edits with their bases, one tag, one move; claim maps, `src`, on 11 entries whose new citations ground part of `c`).

Rulings. Where the record settles it for the entry, the Check is taken out and the book's version kept as a plain note, the record named ("Gibbons, IV, 602, dates the House's passage May 16, a misprint: Voteview and the Record's day, Mar. 16."). Where it settles it against the entry, the entry is corrected. FRUS's editorial notes and source notes are taken as the record where they state it (several from the Daily Diary). Two Hanoi and Washington accounts of the same fight, or two participants' accounts, with no third record, stay open, no side taken.
