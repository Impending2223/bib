# One event, one entry: the folds of Oct. 28, 2026

The owner's ruling, Oct. 28, 2026 ("One entry, listed in both threads"): a meeting, a call, a document or an address
whose matter falls in several threads is one entry, filed under its chief thread, the others in `also:`. The patch:
`inbox/2026-10-28-folds.yaml` (patch `folds`).

## Method

- The cases: the four the duplicates sweep put to the owner (`notes/duplicates-2026-10-28.md`, "ASK"), and every pair
  of dated calendar entries in different threads, on the same date, citing one Public Papers item (the item found from
  the page linked, by `sources/ppp-matches.json`), one FRUS document or one recording (the conversation number), run on
  the tree of Oct. 28, after the stage 2 patches: 35 pairs on a Public Papers item, 11 on a FRUS document, 1 on a
  recording, 47 in all (the sweep's 37 and 8, with some news conferences counted pairwise); and the same-day pairs
  sharing any other link (48, all elections' returns citing one State or Clerk's volume, different races: none read
  further). Each pair read, and the document read where the question turned on it (FRUS's text in
  `sources/frus-text/`, the Papers' in `sources/ppp-text/`).
- The chief thread: the event's chief matter; for a news conference whose subjects are equal, the subject the Public
  Papers' text takes up first.
- A fold: the kept entry's `c` rewritten to cover the event's matters, the notes' sources joined (the primary record
  once, every pinpoint, parenthetical and Check kept; a source that stood for one entry labelled for its part), the
  `Names:` and tags joined, the other threads in `also:`; the other entry made identical and folded in by `combine`
  (its id an alias; links follow).
- Where the folded entry bundled other events (another day's act, a later reply), those went to entries of their own
  rather than into the kept entry (the spans rule): four new entries, below.
