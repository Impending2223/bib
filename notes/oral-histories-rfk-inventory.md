# The RFK Oral History Collection (JFK Library): what we should have (Nov. 16, 2026)

The owner's folder `RFK OH` (Drive `1sChFLbuSMINeixPDqb26oieHlHynxNs_`), Nov. 16: the finding aid (`RFKOH-FA.xml`, `.html`) and the directory snapshot. Held already, in `JFK OH`: RFKOH-KPO-02 (Kenneth O'Donnell's second).

**The collection:** the Robert F. Kennedy Oral History Program, "interviews conducted with individuals who have recollections of events and people associated with Robert F. Kennedy": 266 interviews of 118 people (the finding aid; "approximately 300" by its own abstract), recorded mostly in 1969–70 (168), the rest to 1981. Each has a link in the finding aid; which transcripts are online shows only on each interview's page.

**What we should have: all of it.** Every interviewee is there for Robert Kennedy, and nearly all of what they recall falls in the span: the Justice Department, the Senate years, New York, the campaign of 1968. In order:
1. **The 38 people our sources already cite** (the "not in the finding aid" names of the JFK inventory: Walinsky, Edelman, Greenfield, Mankiewicz, Dunfey, Eldridge, Hackett, Gifford, Sheridan, Lowenstein, Chavez, Orrick and the rest, with Seigenthaler, Marshall, Katzenbach, Salinger, Gwirtzman, Sorensen, McGovern and Tydings, who have interviews in both collections): 116 interviews.
2. **The other people the calendar tags by name:** 35 people, 64 interviews (Humphrey, McGeorge Bundy, Maxwell Taylor, Harriman, Javits, Lindsay, Dillon, Celler, Wagner, Dutton, Joseph Alsop, Unruh, Edith Green, Courtney Evans, Mondale, Douglas, Shriver ...).
3. **The rest:** 45 people, 86 interviews (Roy Cohn, Paul Corbin, Lord Harlech, Charles Spalding, Sander Vanocur, Art Buchwald, Gerard Doherty, William Haddad, Barrett Prettyman ...).

Cited by our sources and not in this finding aid either: Clark Mollenhoff, George Plimpton, Joseph Dolan, Walter Reuther, and the group interview "The Federal Government and Urban Poverty" (another library's, or another collection, or a source's slip; Dolan has JFK interviews, JFKOH-JOFD-01 and -02).
