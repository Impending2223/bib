# Audit of the time splits, Nov. 25, 2026

The patch is `inbox/2026-11-25-time-splits.yaml`, corrected in place. The reader's own copy is kept outside the repository for comparison only. `./bib patch-check` now shows 0 errors; its warnings are the same-day and "one event?" flags, which the merger settles.

## What the audit found, in general

- Three of the reader's new entries were already in the calendar: the leaders' meeting at 6:45 p.m. on Aug. 4, 1964 (`cal.1964-08-04.vietnam-congress-2`), Kosygin's hot-line message at 8:48 a.m. on June 10, 1967 (`cal.1967-06-10.middle-east-six-day-war`), and Ball in the Security Council on Aug. 21, 1968 (`cal.1968-08-21.soviet-union-czechoslovakia-2`). Those adds are struck and their ledger claims released. What the reader brought to the 1967 entry goes in by additive ops: Dobrynin's page, the list of those present from FRUS XIX, doc. 244, and their names.
- Several first entries kept names in "Names:" or in tags after the person's act had moved out (Omaha, Eban, the June 5 and June 9 entries of 1967, Robert Kennedy on May 27, 1968, Mao on July 22 and 25, 1967). These are now matched to the line.
- The reader's split notes did not name a record, so the edits did not meet R22. Each edit now says which record gives the moved act its own hour or day, by page or document.
- The reader took out the Chinese characters that the Zhou Enlai entry of Mar. 9, 1966 had from the page, leaving gaps in the note. They are restored, from the page image.
- `follows:` was missing where the record gives the order (the Nam Định cable after the strike, Dahmer's death after the morning visits, Salinger after the telegram, the arraignment after Yorty, and others). It is added, with the basis in each note.
- Some hours rested on a book's note, not its text. The cite now names the note: Califano, 104 n.62 for the briefing at 2:15 p.m.; Palermo, 215 n.130 for Salinger at 10:30 a.m.
- Four unused ledger claims in the reader's name (June 10, 1967, -3 to -5; June 11, 1967) are released.

## Each split

- Berlin, Oct. 10–12, 1963: the four hours checked in FRUS XV, docs. 220, 221, 223 and 226. All held. The first entry now takes the first day's hour, 8:59 a.m. at Marienborn (doc. 220 n.1), with a Check on whose clock that is.
- Steel, Jan. 2, 1966: Califano, 104 read. The 2:15 p.m. is in note 62, not the text, so the cite now says so.
- Dahmer, Jan. 10, 1966: Branch, *Pillar of Fire*, 607 read. The death at 3:45 p.m. held. It now follows the morning visits.
- Zhou Enlai, Mar. 9, 1966: the nianpu, III, 19–20, read as page images. The 4 p.m. talk with the Xingtai leaders gives no place, so its place is now Hebei, not Xingtai. It follows the afternoon trip to Shijiazhuang. The characters are restored.
- Nam Định, Apr. 14, 1966: *Văn kiện Đảng*, XXVII, 118 read. The cable says "two enemy planes" at 6:27 a.m., not "American", so the line now says "two planes". The cable follows the strike.
- Albany, June 14–16, 1966: Smith, 478–79 read. The 4:10 a.m. announcement held. The reader had changed the talks to June 14–15 without a record; that change is undone, because the talks ran into the morning of June 16.
- King, Sept. 9, 1966: Branch, *At Canaan's Edge*, 526–27 read. The words held. The hour is the wiretap transcript's, in the note. The registry rows for the call moved off the retreat entry.
- Omaha, July 2–5, 1966: Howard's article read online (pp. 84–85). It gives more hours than the old line: the evening of July 2, 12:49 a.m., 1 a.m.; on July 5, 12:30 a.m., the Guard leaving the armory at 1:10 and arriving at 1:40. The July 5 entry is split into the call for the Guard, the Guard's march, and the mayor's order. The YMCA meeting has no day in the source, so it is an entry of July, after the 5th. The first entry's "Names: Sorensen" is gone.
- Daley, Aug. 9, 1966: Ralph, 149–50 and Anderson, 230 read. Ralph gives a third act, the Commission taking its proposal to Daley "later that afternoon", now an entry between the noon meeting and Daley's call to Beatty. The reader's "Sun-Times, Aug. 11 (Anderson 230 n.60)" was the wrong note: n.60 is the UAW paragraph. The right note is n.59 (the Commission's minutes; *Sun-Times*, Aug. 10).
- Tao Zhu and Mao, Sept. 11, 1966: the Mao nianpu read (epub). The 1 a.m. sending and Mao's reading at dawn held.
- San Francisco, Sept. 27–Oct. 2, 1966: the Wikipedia article the entry rests on was read (Nov. 25, 2026). It gives the shooting at 2:30 p.m., the riot declared at 7:35 p.m., Shelley's call at 11:39 p.m. and Brown's order at 11:44 p.m. Brown's order and the riot are now entries of their own. The Bayview shooting takes the range the article gives, 5:43 to 7:15 p.m. The article no longer gives Oct. 2, 457 arrested or 160 hurt. It gives Sept. 27–Oct. 1 and, after Agee, 359 and 51. The Oct. 2 entry stays, with a Check saying this.
- Archives, Nov. 15–16, 1966: the Mao nianpu and the Zhou nianpu, III, 89 read. "当晚十二时" places Mao's reply at the end of Nov. 16. The hour is exact, so the approximate flag and its Check are gone.
- Jiang Qing's speech, Nov. 1966: the Wengao, XVIII, 396 read as a page image, and the nianpu. The 1 a.m. note of Nov. 28 held. The Wengao dates the item "November 1966", not Nov. 24, so the parenthetical is corrected and the first entry carries "Check: the day".
- Stand-down, Jan. 2, 1967: MACV, 1255 read. It says "0700H", which is Saigon's UTC+8. The Check is replaced with that reading.
- Eban, May 25–26, 1967: FRUS XIX, docs. 72 and 77 read. Harman is now named in the evening entry. The Dallek variant is moved there. The evening entry follows the 1:30 p.m. meeting, because Johnson spoke from that meeting's notes.
- June 5, 1967: the Miller Center catalogue (7:57) and FRUS XIX, docs. 155–159 read, and Califano, 204–05 for McCloskey. All held.
- June 6, 1967: FRUS XXXIV, doc. 233 and Drea, 443 read. Radio Cairo at 7:40 a.m. held. Neither source names the closing of the Suez Canal. That entry now cites neither and carries a Check, with Yergin to check. Drea gives the breaks in relations without days.
- June 9–11, 1967: FRUS XIX, docs. 243, 244, 252 and 255, and Dobrynin, 165–66 read. The Soviet break (Dobrynin, 165) and the cease-fire (166: "late on June 10") are entries. The cease-fire's evening range was redrawn from the band. The first entry is now June 9–10.
- Côn Thiên, July 5, 1967: MACV, 360 read as a page image (folio 360 of 1340). The hours 0745 to 1345 held.
- Wuhan, July 22, 1967: the nianpu read. It also gives Zhou's call its own hour ("八时二十分") and two later talks of Mao, at 1:30 and 2:40 p.m. Both are now entries, the two talks as one entry because the nianpu gives their words once.
- Wuhan, July 25, 1967: the Wengao, XIX, 128 and 130, read as page images, and the nianpu. The 10 p.m. revision is of a letter, not "the open telegram". The line now says so, with the paragraph Mao added. Lin Biao's tag is off the afternoon entry.
- Chamizal, Oct. 26–28, 1967: the Public Papers, 952–61 read (govinfo). The note to item 454 gives a further timed act, the signing of the final act at the White House at about 5:30 p.m. on Oct. 27. That is a new entry. The 1:10 p.m. ceremony was at Ciudad Juárez, so it has a new place key and its own zone. The bridges are in the declaration, so the reader's Check on them is gone. The 12:01 a.m. entry names no one, so its name tags are gone.
- Oregon, May 27, 1968: Palermo, 215–16 and notes 129–30 read. The 10:30 a.m. and the Benson Hotel are in note 130 only. The claim rows were corrected: n.129 is for the spots and the wire.
- Sirhan, June 5, 1968: Witcover, 286 read. The 7:40 a.m. Pacific time held.
- Tonkin Gulf, Aug. 4, 1964: FRUS I, docs. 272, 276, 278, 280 and 286, and the Public Papers, 927 read. Doc. 276 gives Herrick's report at 1:27 p.m., so his doubts are now an entry. The first entry takes the Maddox's first report, 7:40 a.m. EDT (doc. 272). The reader's "strikes begin" entry was really Sharp's call at 11:20 p.m., so it is now that call. McNamara's call to Johnson after it is its own entry. The launch itself is the Aug. 5 Pierce Arrow entry. The works earlier readers had read for the moved parts are carried to the new entries.
- Czechoslovakia, Aug. 20–21, 1968: FRUS XVII, docs. 80 and 81, XIV, doc. 289, the Miller Center catalogue and the Public Papers, 905 read. Doc. 81 bears out the line on the visit, so the reader's Check is gone. Walt Rostow is named in the Dobrynin entry. The earlier readers' works and their Check go with the moved acts.
